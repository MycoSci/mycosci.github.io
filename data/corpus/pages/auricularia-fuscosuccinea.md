## Summary

Auricularia fuscosuccinea (Mont.) Henn. is a species of Auriculariales in the family Auriculariaceae. It was first described in 1842 by Camille Montagne as a species of Exidia, and Paul Christoph Hennings transferred it to Auricularia in 1893. The supplied literature on this species consists of transmission-electron-microscopy studies of its basidia (1980, 1981). *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*
