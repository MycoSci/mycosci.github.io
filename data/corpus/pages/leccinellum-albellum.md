## Summary

Leccinellum albellum (Peck) Bresinsky & Manfr. Binder is a species of bolete fungus in the family Boletaceae. The current combination dates from 2003, when the species was transferred to the genus Leccinellum. GBIF occurrence records come from the US, Canada, Mexico, China and Costa Rica. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described by Charles Horton Peck as a species of Boletus. After 1945 it was usually treated as a species of Leccinum, and in 2003 it was transferred to the newly created genus Leccinellum. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
