## Summary

Bovistella radicata Pat. (1899) is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae, known in English as the rooting puffball. One encyclopedia source, which treats the species under the name Lycoperdon radicatum, describes a fruiting body up to 10 cm across with a rigid pseudoroot 4-8 cm long. Three laboratory papers from 2017-2020 report in vitro antifungal activity of extracts and compounds from the species against Trichophyton rubrum and T. mentagrophytes. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia, which treats this species under the name Lycoperdon radicatum, states that it was described in 1848 by Michel Charles Durieu de Maisonneuve and Camille Montagne in Algeria, and that it was previously placed in Bovistella because of its Bovista-like capillitium. A 2020 paper in BMC Microbiology cites the name as Bovistella radicata (Mont.) Pat. This is the only account of the naming found in the supplied sources, and it is not reconciled here with the spine's 1899 date. *[wikipedia, lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (a single source) states that the species is found in grassy areas, pastures, and in acidic soils in open woods, and that it is considered endangered in Sweden. *[wikipedia]*
