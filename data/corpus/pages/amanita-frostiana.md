## Summary

Amanita frostiana (Peck) Sacc. (1887), known as Frost's Amanita, is a member of the Amanitaceae. Sources describe it as a small mushroom with a yellow to orange or reddish cap and a yellow to cream universal veil, recorded mainly in eastern North America. Most of the descriptive detail comes from one Wikipedia article and one 2021 student repository record. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

A 2021 Binghamton University repository record reports specimens growing scattered in hemlock pine duff and in hardwood and coniferous forests, from June to September. It describes the range as widespread but most common in the northeastern USA and southeastern Canada. Wikipedia describes the species as rare and native to the eastern U.S. and southeastern Canada, normally found in mixed forests with oaks and conifers. GBIF descriptions list Vermont. A 1962 record from Thailand has been attributed to A. rubrovolvata rather than to this species. *[lit_1, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
