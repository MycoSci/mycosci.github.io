## Summary

Guepiniopsis buccina (Pers.) L.L. Kenn. (1959) is a jelly fungus in the family Dacrymycetaceae, order Dacrymycetales. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as very tiny, with a top-shaped fruiting body 2–8 mm across and 2–5 mm high, an orange to yellow upper surface, and gelatinous flesh.
