## Summary

Lactocollybia epia (Berk. & Broome) Pegler, 1986, is a species in the family Marasmiaceae (Agaricales), with 268 GBIF occurrence records. The GBIF-hosted description, which lists a Colombian collection from Tolima (Gómez-Montoya et al. 2022), gives a small collybioid fruiting body with a white to yellowish-white, translucent-striate, hygrophanous cap 4–28 mm across. The lamellae are white, crowded and adnate to subdecurrent, turning greyish yellow on drying and bruising, and the stipe is 10–15 × 1.5–2.5 mm and slightly eccentric. Under the microscope the spores are 5.3–8.4 × 3.9–4.7 μm, broadly ellipsoid to subamygdaliform, with abundant gloeocystidia and common clamp connections. *[gbif_desc]*
