## Summary

Fomes fasciatus (Sw.) Cooke, 1885, is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae with the common name Southern Clam Shell. Wikipedia describes it as a white rot wood-decay fungus found on hardwood trees in the southeastern United States and Central and South America. Its fruiting body is described there as a sessile, semicircular, clam-shaped conk 7-18 cm wide, with concentric gray, brown or black zones on the upper surface. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the genus name Fomes translates to "tinder", because the genus's type species was historically used to start fires. It states that the epithet fasciatus translates to "banded", in reference to the bands of brown to grey and black on the top of the fruiting body. This derivation comes from a single source. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by the Swedish botanist Olof Swartz in Jamaica in 1788 as Boletus fasciatus, and that Cooke later transferred it to Fomes fasciatus in 1885. iNaturalist repeats the Swartz and Jamaica details, but it is a declared derivative of Wikipedia and is not independent evidence. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as growing on various dead and living hardwood trees in the southeastern United States and Central and South America. It reports the fungus as documented as far north as the coastal plains of North Carolina and as far south as southern South America, with specimens also recorded in Australia, southern Asia and Indonesia. It says that in North America the fungus is commonly found on hackberry, hickory, maple and oak, and that it has been recorded on at least ten host genera worldwide. A GBIF-hosted description citing Ryvarden (2015) gives 'growing on deciduous wood' and calls it an American species ranging from south-eastern United States to northern Argentina. That description also lists Brazilian state records, including a new record for Goiás. No supplied source states a fruiting season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
