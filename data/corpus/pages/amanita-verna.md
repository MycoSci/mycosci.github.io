## Summary

Amanita verna Bull. ex Lam. (1783) is a member of the Amanitaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a deadly poisonous fungus known as the fool's mushroom or spring destroying angel. It says the cap, stipe and gills are all white, and that it is reported in Europe in spring. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the species name verna is derived from the Latin word for "spring". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Jean Bulliard first mentioned the taxon in 1780 as form vernus of Agaricus bulbosus. Jean-Baptiste Lamarck gave it distinct species status three years later in his Encyclopédie Méthodique, Botanique. This account comes from a single independent source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it occurs in European woodlands and hardwood forests in spring, associating with various deciduous and coniferous trees. This is a single-source claim. *[wikipedia]*
