## Summary

Galerina hypnorum (Schrank) Kühner 1935, known in English as Moss Bell, is a species of agaric fungus in the family Hymenogastraceae. Wikipedia describes a hygrophanous cap 4-15 mm in diameter, initially conical and becoming broadly convex with age. The cap is honey-yellow to ochraceous tawny when moist, fading to buff or tawny brown as it dries, and is striate when wet and glabrous to faintly fibrillose. GBIF's distribution listing for the species includes Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Madeira Island and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
