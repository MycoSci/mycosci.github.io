## Summary

Hygrocybe singeri (A.H. Sm. & Hesler) Singer, 1958, is a species in the family Hygrophoraceae, known by the common name western witch's hat. Wikipedia describes it as a species from Northwestern California that is very similar to Hygrocybe conica but differs in having a viscid stipe. MushroomExpert.Com describes a 1-3 cm conical, reddish orange to orange cap, a slimy stem, and flesh, gills and stem that blacken with age or when bruised. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as appearing under conifers, usually growing scattered, from spring through fall, or over winter in warmer climates.
