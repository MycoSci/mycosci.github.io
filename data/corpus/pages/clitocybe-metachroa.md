## Summary

Clitocybe metachroa (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is placed in the Tricholomataceae and is known in English as the Twotone Funnel; Index Fungorum lists the current name as Pseudolyophyllum metachroum. Mushroom World describes a smallish species with a hygrophanous cap 3-5 cm across that is initially convex and becomes funnel-shaped, with whitish-grey decurrent gills, a stem lacking a ring, and a white spore print. The GBIF description record lists a distribution that includes Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Madeira. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
