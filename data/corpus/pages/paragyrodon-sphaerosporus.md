## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both associate the species with white oak, and in Colorado with Gambel oak. Wikipedia says it is distributed mostly around the Great Lakes region, with observations further west in Kansas, Iowa and Colorado. MushroomExpert.Com (single-author field guide) describes it as presumably mycorrhizal and as growing gregariously, with a summer and a fall fruiting period. It gives the range as the Great Lakes region to New York, Iowa, Kansas and Colorado, and calls it an uncommon find in Colorado. The same source says it frequently appears under oaks in lawns in the Great Lakes region. It quotes Smith & Thiers (1971) calling it one of the most frequently collected species in Michigan 'suburbia', where original forest trees have been left as shade trees. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
