## Summary

Aureoboletus gentilis (Quél.) Pouzar, 1957, is a bolete in the family Boletaceae. Wikipedia reports that it is considered vulnerable in the Czech Republic. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Lucien Quélet first described the species as Boletus sanguineus var. gentilis in 1884 (Compt. Rend. Assoc. Franç. Avancem. Sci. 12: 504). Zdeněk Pouzar transferred it to Aureoboletus in 1957 (Česká Mykol. 11(1): 48). Wikispecies also lists earlier transfers to Viscipellis, Ixocomus, Boletus, Xerocomus and Pulveroboletus. The sources give no reasons for any of these transfers. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*
