## Summary

Lycoperdon nigrescens Pers. (1794), the dusky puffball, is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae. Its current name in Index Fungorum is Fuscospina nigrescens. The fruit body is described as spiny and as darkening with age. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it appears from summer to fall in conifer and hardwood forests, and in alpine areas. Mushroom World separately says it typically grows in troops on acidic soil in heaths, coniferous forests and mixed woodlands. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
