## Summary

Coltricia montagnei (Fr.) Murrill, 1920, is a species in the family Hymenochaetaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a polypore with a velvety, concentrically zoned brown and orangish-brown cap 3–12 cm wide. Its pore surface can develop concentrically arranged, gill-like tubes with maturity. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as apparently saprobic, growing on the ground alone or gregariously under hardwoods or conifers. It gives the range as widely distributed east of the Rocky Mountains but more common in the southern Appalachians, with fruiting in summer and fall. The collections it illustrates and describes are from Ohio and North Carolina. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*
