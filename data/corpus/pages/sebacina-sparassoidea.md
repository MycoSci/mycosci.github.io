## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described in 1873 by Miles Joseph Berkeley as a variety, var. reticulatum, of Corticium tremellinum. In 1908 William Gilson Farlow raised it to species rank in Tremella, as Tremella reticulata. In 2003 Peter Roberts re-examined the species and transferred it to Sebacina. A different species, Sebacina reticulata Pat., already carried the epithet reticulata, so Wikipedia says the new combination was applied to the earliest available synonym, giving Sebacina sparassoidea. This account rests on a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*
