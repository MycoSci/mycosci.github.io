## Summary

Coprinus comatus (O.F. Müll.) Pers. 1797 is a species in the family Agaricaceae, commonly called the shaggy ink cap, lawyer's wig or shaggy mane. It has a white, shaggy-scaled, almost cylindrical cap. Its free gills pass from white to pink to black and deliquesce into black, spore-laden liquid as the fruit body matures. It is recorded widely across Europe and North America, and elsewhere. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific name derives from coma, 'hair', hence comatus, 'hairy' or 'shaggy'. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia says the white cap is covered with scales, and that this is the origin of its common names. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by the Danish naturalist Otto Friedrich Müller as Agaricus comatus, and was given its current binomial by Christiaan Hendrik Persoon in 1797. Wikipedia says it is the type species of the genus Coprinus. It also says DNA sequence analysis showed that the species formerly placed in Coprinus belong in two families, Agaricaceae and Psathyrellaceae. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) says DNA studies show C. comatus is fairly closely related to species of Agaricus and Lepiota and only distantly related to most other mushrooms whose gills turn to ink. It says the genus now holds only C. comatus and a few similar mushrooms. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Cultural and culinary history

Wikipedia notes that in Australia the species is common enough to have featured on a postage stamp issued by Australia Post in 1981. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or in clusters, lines or fairy rings on lawns, wood chips or hard-packed ground in summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. It also says the species is frequently found in disturbed ground, including the edges of dirt roads. Wikipedia says it grows in groups in grasslands and meadows in North America and Europe, from June through November in the United Kingdom. It says the species appears to have been introduced to Australia, New Zealand and Iceland. Mushroom World says it grows solitary, scattered or in small clusters on soil in short grass. Wikipedia also describes it as a nematophagous fungus able to kill and digest the nematodes Panagrellus redivivus and Meloidogyne arenaria. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
