## Summary

Tulosesus subdisseminatus (M. Lange) D. Wächt. & A. Melzer (2020) is a mushroom-producing species in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was previously known as Coprinellus subdisseminatus, and originally as Coprinus subdisseminatus. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Coprinus subdisseminatus in 1952. A phylogenetic study in 2001 led to a major reorganization of that genus, and the species was transferred to Coprinellus. It was known as Coprinellus subdisseminatus until 2020, when the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many species in the Psathyrellaceae on the basis of phylogenetic analysis.
