## Summary

Pholiotina striipes (Cooke) M.M. Moser (1967) is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae, with occurrence records from Great Britain, continental Europe and the US. A 2026 publication proposes moving it to the genus Conobolbitina as Conobolbitina striipes, and Index Fungorum already lists that as the current name. A Turkish macrofungi survey reports it as new to the Turkish mycobiota. *[lit_2, lit_1]*

## Discovery and naming

The basionym is Agaricus striipes Cooke (spelled 'striaepes' in the original), published in Illustrations of British Fungi 4, plate 478 (1885). The combinations listed in the 2026 source are Naucoria striipes (Sacc., 1887), Conocybe striipes (Lundell, 1953), Pholiotina striipes (Moser, 1967) and Conobolbitina striipes (T. Bau & H. B. Song, 2026, comb. nov.). That source states that both Hausknecht's (2009) morphological description and Tóth et al.'s (2013) phylogenetic analysis support placing the species in sect. Conobolbitina. *[lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

GBIF lists a distribution entry for the Flemish Region of Belgium. A Turkish study of macrofungi collected around Pozantı (Adana) between 2003 and 2012 reports the species as a first record for the Turkish mycobiota. *[gbif_desc, lit_1]*
