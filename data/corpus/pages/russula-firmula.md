## Summary

Russula firmula Jul. Schäff. (1940) is a species in the family Russulaceae, published in Index of Fungi 1: 60. Wikipedia describes it as found in Europe and parts of Asia, with a dark red-purple to red-brown cap and a yellow spore print. The same source says it was placed in the Rubrinae clade in 2017, together with R. rubra, R. rutila and R. veternos. *[wikipedia]*
