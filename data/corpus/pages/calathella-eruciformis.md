## Summary

Calathella eruciformis (P. Micheli ex Batsch) D.A. Reid, 1964, is a cyphelloid fungus in the family Marasmiaceae (Agaricales). A 2019 paper reported it as new to Poland and describes it as a wood-inhabiting saprotroph. The same paper's ITS rDNA data indicate that Calathella is not monophyletic and that this species, the type of the genus, groups with Flagelloscypha and is closely related to Sphaerobasidioscypha citrispora. *[lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single source, a 2019 study from north-eastern Poland, found it at four localities as a wood-inhabiting saprotroph on dead, decorticated but still attached twigs and branches of living Populus tremula, in an oak-hornbeam forest (Carpinion betuli). *[lit_1]*
