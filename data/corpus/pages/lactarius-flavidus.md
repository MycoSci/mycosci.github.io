## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies credits the name to Boudier (1887) and lists Boudier's 'Champignons nouveaux rares ou peu connus de France' (Bulletin de la Société mycologique de France) among its references. It records these later combinations and rank changes: Lactifluus flavidus (Kuntze, 1898), Lactarius uvidus var. flavidus (Bataille, 1908) and Lactarius aspideus var. flavidus (Neuhoff, 1956). It also lists Lactarius aspideus sensu Bresadola (1929) and sensu Konrad & Maublanc (1937) as misapplied names. The sources do not say what material the description was based on or why the transfers were made. *[wikispecies]*
