## Summary

Tricholoma orirubens Quél. (1872) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, with France as its type locality. GBIF holds occurrence records from Europe and from several countries outside it. Wikipedia describes it as an ectomycorrhizal species of deciduous and coniferous woodland. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (the only independent source with habitat text; iNaturalist copies it) describes the species as widespread across Europe but not common, and rare in the Netherlands. It says fruit bodies are found singly or in small groups in deciduous and coniferous woodland in autumn, that the species prefers clayey or chalky soils, and that it is ectomycorrhizal. Wikipedia also reports that it can form fairy rings, and gives one in Germany about 80 m in diameter with an estimated 10,000 mushrooms. *[wikipedia]*
