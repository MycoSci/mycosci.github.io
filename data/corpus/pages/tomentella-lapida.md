## Habitat and ecology

In a 2010 study of a monospecific, old-growth beech (Fagus sylvatica) forest, Tomentella lapida was recorded as an ectomycorrhizal fungus on roots. Together with Cenococcum geophilum and Lactarius blennius it was dominant, and the three colonized about 70% of root tips. Its colonization was unaffected by girdling of the trees. The excerpt of that study does not name the country or site. A GBIF descriptions record lists the distribution as DK; NO; Vermont. *[lit_1, gbif_desc]*
