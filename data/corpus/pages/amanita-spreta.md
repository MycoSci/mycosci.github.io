## Summary

Amanita spreta (Peck) Sacc. (1887) is a species in the family Amanitaceae, recorded in GBIF from many countries. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com, which the pipeline counts as one independent source because their text overlaps, describe it as distinguished by a grayish brown cap with dark radial streaks, a ring on the upper stem, and a white sac-like volva at a stem base that is not prominently swollen. They also note ellipsoid, inamyloid spores. MushroomExpert.Com adds that the name may represent a group of similar North American species rather than a single one. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods and conifers and often reported from sandy soil. It fruits in summer and fall, and is common in the southeastern United States and not infrequent in the Great Lakes region and the northeast. Separately, a primary-literature paper lists it among eight ectomycorrhizal basidiomycetes collected in Cofre de Perote National Park, Veracruz, Mexico. A second study names it as one of the dominant ectomycorrhizal fungi in the Labanan research forest, East Kalimantan, Indonesia. *[mushroomexpert, lit_1, lit_3]*
