## Summary

Mutinus bambusinus (Zoll.) E. Fisch. is a stinkhorn in the family Phallaceae, and Wikipedia gives it the common name "bamboo dipstick". MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tropical stinkhorn with no separate cap: the spore slime is spread over the surface of the stem itself. The same source describes mature fruiting bodies 6–10 cm high, narrow and sharply pointed, pink to nearly white below and dark red over roughly the upper half to two-thirds of the stem. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Saprobic. Wikipedia says it grows in decaying wood and bamboo groves and is found in the tropics, most commonly in Asia. MushroomExpert.Com describes it growing alone or gregariously in gardens, flowerbeds and cultivated areas, and also in bamboo groves, from May through October, and lists records from Africa, Asia, Australia, South America, Central America and, in North America, Florida, Mexico and Puerto Rico. The season and the garden and flowerbed habitat come from MushroomExpert.Com alone. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
