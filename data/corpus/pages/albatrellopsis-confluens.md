## Summary

Albatrellopsis confluens (Alb. & Schwein.) Teixeira, 1993, is a polypore in the family Albatrellaceae, commonly called the fused polypore. Wikipedia describes it as similar to Albatrellus ovinus but bitter, tending toward a salmon colour with age. GBIF holds about 7,500 occurrence records, and the catalog spine lists occurrences in Europe, North America and Asia. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was moved from Albatrellus to Albatrellopsis in 1993 on the basis of morphological characteristics and phylogenetic analysis. It adds that a later molecular study found high sequence similarity with Albatrellopsis flettii and confirmed the reclassification. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat text, says it occurs sporadically in coniferous and mixed forests and is most often found in mountain spruce forests. It grows on the ground, singly or in crowded clumps, from July to October. Wikipedia describes the species as widespread in Europe and North America, and also recorded in Japan and on the Yorke Peninsula in Australia. A GBIF description record lists Vermont as a distribution. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
