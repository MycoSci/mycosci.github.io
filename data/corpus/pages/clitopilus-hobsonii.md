## Summary

Clitopilus hobsonii (Berk.) P.D. Orton, 1960, is a species in the family Entolomataceae, known in English as Miller's Oysterling. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tiny white mushroom, usually pleurotoid in shape, whose gills turn pinkish with age. ## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on various plant substrates, including deadwood, bark, grasses, and plant stems. It gives the season as summer and fall, or over winter in warm climates. The same source says it was originally described from England and found throughout Europe. It reports the species in North America east of the Great Plains and on the West Coast, and also in Oceania, Central America, and South America. The illustrated and described collection is from California. GBIF's description record lists a distribution of Belgium, Flemish Region, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
