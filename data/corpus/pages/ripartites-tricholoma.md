## Summary

Ripartites tricholoma (Alb. & Schwein.) P. Karst. is a species of fungus in the family Tricholomataceae, commonly known as the bearded seamine. Wikipedia reports it from North America and Europe and as also collected in Costa Rica. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described scientifically as Agaricus tricholoma by Albertini and Lewis David von Schweinitz in 1805, and later transferred into the genus Ripartites by Petter Karsten in 1879. *[wikipedia]*
