## Summary

Cystodermella granulosa (Batsch) Harmaja, 2002 is a mushroom in the family Agaricaceae, called the brickbrown powdercap in English by Wikipedia. Wikipedia describes a reddish-brown cap 2–5 cm wide with a scaly stipe, while MushroomExpert.Com describes a brown to orangish-brown cap 1–5 cm across, densely covered with granular scales, on a stem with no true ring. Both sources give a white spore print. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from Germany (Batsch 1783) and epitypified from Estonia (Saar et al. 2009). It lists Cystoderma granulosum as a synonym and cites Harmaja, 2002 for the current combination. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously under conifers or hardwoods in summer and fall, and fairly widely distributed in Europe and North America. This is a single-source description; its illustrated collection is from Illinois. GBIF's description record lists only the distribution entries DK, NO, SE and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
