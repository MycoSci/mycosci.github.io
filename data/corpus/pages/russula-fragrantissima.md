## Summary

Russula fragrantissima Romagn. (1967) is a species of Russulaceae with a type locality in France, known in English as the almond-scented russula. MushroomExpert.Com places it among the foetid russulas and describes it as a large mushroom with dull yellow colors, a slimy cap when wet, and a sickly sweet odor that turns foul with age. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a mycorrhizal fungus found under both hardwoods and conifers. MushroomExpert.Com likewise describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods or conifers, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in summer and fall, and apparently widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
