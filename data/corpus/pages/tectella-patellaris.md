## Summary

Tectella patellaris (Fr.) Murrill, 1915, commonly called the veiled panus, is a species of mushroom in the family Mycenaceae. It is a small, dull brown mushroom that grows in groups or clusters on decaying wood of broad-leaved trees. Young specimens are described as easily recognized by a partial veil, since no other agarics that lack a stipe have one. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet patellaris means "dish shaped". *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as saprobic, found in groups or clusters on decaying wood of broad-leaved trees and on fallen logs of hardwoods in North America and Europe. It is described as widely distributed in those regions and possibly more common than reported because it is inconspicuous. A GBIF distribution record lists Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
