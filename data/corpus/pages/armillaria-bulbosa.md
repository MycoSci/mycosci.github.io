## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia's article on Armillaria gallica (a single source), the French mycologist Henri Romagnesi published a description of Armillariella bulbosa in 1973, based on specimens he had found near Compiègne and Saint-Sauveur-le-Vicomte in France. *[wikipedia]*
