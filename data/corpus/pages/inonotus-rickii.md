## Summary

Inonotus rickii (Pat.) D.A. Reid (1957) is a species in the family Hymenochaetaceae, with GBIF occurrence records from many countries. The one source available describes two 2019 collections from Recife, Pernambuco, Brazil, found on living Fabaceae trees in the anamorphic stage (Ptychogaster cubensis Pat.). In an ITS phylogeny in that source, the species is closely related to I. patouillardii, and the source suggests the two may be the same species but says type material or material from the type locality would need to be sequenced to confirm this. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single source (a GBIF-hosted description) reports two specimens from Recife, Pernambuco State, Brazil, collected in 2019 on living Clitoria fairchildiana (native) and Delonix regia (exotic), both Fabaceae. The source states the specimens were recorded as parasitic on urban trees, and that both were found in the anamorphic stage (Ptychogaster cubensis Pat.). *[gbif_desc]*
