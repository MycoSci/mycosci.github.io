## Summary

Tricharina praecox (P. Karst.) Dennis (1971) is a species in the family Pyronemataceae (order Pezizales, Ascomycota). It was first published as Peziza praecox by P. A. Karsten in 1869 and was transferred to Tricharina by Dennis in 1971. The GBIF description record lists Denmark, Norway and Sweden as its distribution. *[wikispecies, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Peziza praecox P. Karst., published in Notiser ur Sällskapets pro Fauna et Flora Fennica Förhandlingar 10: 124 (1869). It records the original typification as not indicated, and a lectotype (H 6055460, P. A. Karsten #1176) designated by Yang & Korf in Mycotaxon 24: 504 (1985). The same source lists homotypic combinations in Humaria (Karsten, 1885), Lachnea (Saccardo, 1889), Scutellinia (Kuntze, 1891) and Tricharia (Boudier, 1904), and the combination in Tricharina by Dennis in Kew Bulletin 25(2): 338 (1971). It also lists Ascorhizoctonia praecox Yang & Korf (1985), whose holotype is recorded as coming from an ascoma of Tricharina praecox in Norway. The sources give no reasons for any of the transfers. *[wikispecies]*
