## Summary

Lycoperdon molle Pers. (1800) is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae, known in English as the smooth puffball or soft puffball. Mushroom World describes the fruiting body as pear-shaped to turbinate, with a pleated sterile base attached by fine rhizoids. It gives the outer surface as cream to pale buff when young, becoming tan to greyish-brown with age, and covered in fine granules and short spines. It gives the size as 2.5-5.0 cm across and 2.5-6.0 cm tall. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
