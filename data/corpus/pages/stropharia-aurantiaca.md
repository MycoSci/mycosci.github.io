## Summary

Stropharia aurantiaca (Cooke) M. Imai, 1938, is placed in the family Strophariaceae, and Index Fungorum lists Psilocybe aurantiaca as its current name. Wikipedia states that the name Stropharia aurantiaca has been used extensively but incorrectly for the mushroom now called Leratiomyces ceres. Descriptions and photographs filed under this name may therefore belong to a different species. The supplied sources give no description of the taxon this name properly refers to. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that around 1885 Mordecai Cubitt Cooke originated the names Agaricus squamosus f. aurantiacus and Agaricus thraustus var. aurantiacus, which later gave rise to the name Stropharia aurantiaca. According to that article, the name is defined by Cooke's illustration in his Handbook of British Fungi. In 2004 Richard Fortey found that the illustration did not show Leratiomyces ceres, as had generally been assumed, but L. squamosus var. thaustus. The article concludes that the name aurantiaca is best avoided when applied to L. ceres. This account comes from a single source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
