## Summary

Exidia saccharina Fr. (1822) is a jelly fungus in the family Auriculariaceae, known in English as Pine Jelly and recorded mainly across northern and central Europe. Wikipedia describes the fruit bodies as gelatinous and button-shaped at first, later coalescing into irregular, effused, often ridged or folded masses up to 20 cm across and 2 cm thick. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the taxon was first described in 1805 from Germany as Tremella spiculosa var. saccharina by Albertini and Schweinitz, and was raised to species rank in Exidia by Fries in 1822. Wikipedia, the only supplied source discussing this, adds that molecular analyses place the species in a clade distinct from Exidia and refer it to Ulocolla, and it treats the species under that name. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a saprotroph growing on dead branches of pine in Europe, most common in Scandinavia but also found elsewhere in Europe. This is a single-source statement; no season is given in any supplied source. *[wikipedia]*
