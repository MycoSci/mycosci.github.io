## Summary

Inocybe lanuginosa (Bull.) Kalchbr. (1867) is a species in the family Inocybaceae, known in English as the woolly inocybe (Wikipedia) or Woolly Fibrecap. Wikipedia describes a brown cap 1–5 cm across, covered in fibers and scales, and a brown spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows under conifers, sometimes under hardwoods, and occasionally on decaying wood, and that it fruits in fall. This is a single-source claim, since the iNaturalist entry derives from Wikipedia. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Brussels-Capital Region and Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
