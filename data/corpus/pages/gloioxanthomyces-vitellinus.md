## Summary

Gloioxanthomyces vitellinus (Fr.) Lodge, Vizzini, Ercole & Boertm. is a species of agaric in the family Hygrophoraceae, with the recommended English name glistening waxcap. Wikipedia describes its distribution as European and mainly in agriculturally unimproved grassland. It notes that threats to that habitat have led to the species being assessed as globally "endangered" on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Elias Magnus Fries first described the species in 1863 as Hygrophorus vitellinus, and Petter Adolf Karsten transferred it to Hygrocybe in 1879. Wikipedia says molecular research based on cladistic analysis of DNA sequences showed it is not closely related to the type of Hygrocybe and belongs in the related genus Gloioxanthomyces. iNaturalist's taxon summary, a declared derivative of the Wikipedia text, says it was made the type species of the newly created genus Gloioxanthomyces in 2013. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the glistening waxcap as rare but widespread in Europe. Like many other European waxcaps, it occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns), and it is described as typical of waxcap grasslands. iNaturalist's taxon summary states that it fruits among mosses in late summer and autumn. That seasonal statement comes from a single source cluster and was not corroborated independently. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*
