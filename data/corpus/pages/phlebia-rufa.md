## Summary

Phlebia rufa (Pers.) M.P. Christ. (1960) is a fungus in the family Meruliaceae, order Polyporales, with occurrence records from many countries including Great Britain, Sweden, Denmark, Switzerland and Norway. In a 1983 study of decaying oak branches in south-west England it was classed as a white-rot fungus. It seemed to act as a pioneer only in obviously weakened branches. *[lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single 1983 study of attached oak (Quercus robur) branches at sites in south-west England reported Phlebia rufa as one of twelve basidiomycetes characterising the decay communities. There it seemed to act as a pioneer causing white rot, but only in obviously weakened branches. *[lit_2]*
