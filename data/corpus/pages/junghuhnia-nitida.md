## Summary

Junghuhnia nitida (Pers.) Ryvarden is a crust fungus in the family Steccherinaceae, order Polyporales. Wikipedia describes it as widespread and says Leif Ryvarden relocated it to the genus Junghuhnia in 1972. Wikipedia reports it from Asia, Africa, Europe, North America and South America. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus grows on the fallen wood of deciduous trees, where it causes a white rot. A GBIF description record lists specimens from Yunnan Province, China, collected from fallen trunks of angiosperms in broad-leaved forest, and gives the substrate as a fallen twig of a dicotyledonous plant. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
