## Summary

Fabisporus sanguineus (L.) Zmitr. (2001) is a member of the Polyporaceae (Polyporales), recorded in GBIF occurrence data from many tropical and subtropical countries. The only descriptive source available, English Wikipedia (which uses the name Pycnoporus sanguineus), describes a thin, dry bracket fungus with an orange-red to orange cap that fades to salmon or buff with age.
