## Summary

Calcipostia guttulata (Sacc.) B.K. Cui, L.L. Shen & Y.C. Dai (2018) is a polypore in the order Polyporales. Wikipedia places its genus, the monotypic Calcipostia, in the family Fomitopsidaceae. A 2025 paper in Doklady Biological Sciences describes it as a widespread but rare Holarctic polypore. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Denmark and Vermont. *[wikipedia, lit_1, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single 2025 study (Doklady Biological Sciences, abstract only) reports that C. guttulata is confined to the early stages of drying of coniferous stands, primarily spruce forests. It describes the species as a poorly studied saprotroph that colonizes coniferous deadwood and, less often, fallen trees. The same abstract calls it widespread but rare in the Holarctic. *[lit_1]*
