## Summary

Peziza repanda Wahlenb. ex Fr. (1822) is a cup fungus in the family Pezizaceae, with its type locality in Sweden. Mushroom World, an aggregator, describes a sessile, cup-shaped fruiting body 6-12 cm across and 1-4 cm tall. It gives the hymenium as light brown to chestnut-brown and the exterior as white to ash-grey and finely tomentose, with smooth, elliptical spores of 11-16 x 6-10 µm. *[mushroomworld]*
