## Summary

Oudemansiella canarii (Jungh.) Höhn. is a gilled mushroom in the family Physalacriaceae, published in 1909 in Saccardo's Sylloge fungorum XXI. Wikipedia describes it as occurring in tropical America, southeast Asia and Australia, where it grows as a saprotroph on hardwood logs. GBIF holds 968 occurrence records from about 40 countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a saprotroph on hardwood logs, on fallen, decaying and wet logs, in tropical America, southeast Asia and Australia. A GBIF description record from Colombia (Tolima, Murillo, 2350 m a.s.l., collected 24 November 2005; citing Gómez-Montoya et al. 2022) gives the habit as gregarious. It gives the substrate as living or decaying trunks or branches, burnt trunk, wood, and decaying trunk.
