## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Agaricus hysginus Fr., 1815, with later combinations Galorrheus hysginus (Fr.: Fr.) Fr., 1825 and Lactifluus hysginus (Fr.) Kuntze, 1891; the 1838 combination in Lactarius is cited to Fries's Epicrisis Systematis mycologici. It also lists Lactarius curtus (in the sense of several authors), L. chrysophyllus Z.Schaef., 1957 and L. pseudohysginus J.Blum, 1976 as taxonomic synonyms, and excludes L. hysginus in the sense of J.Blum, 1966, which it assigns to Lactarius trivialis. The source gives no reasons for these transfers and no information on the original material. *[wikispecies]*
