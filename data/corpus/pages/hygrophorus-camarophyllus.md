## Summary

Hygrophorus camarophyllus (Alb. & Schwein.) Dumée, Grandjean & Maire (1912), the arched woodwax, is a species in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes a grayish to brownish cap about 3–10 cm across that is umbonate or convex with an inrolled margin when young, whitish adnate to slightly decurrent gills, and a white spore print. Mushroom World independently describes a carbon-brown to grey-black, radially streaked cap with waxy, decurrent gills, and gives a cap size of 2–7 cm. GBIF's distribution listing names Flanders, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found in coniferous forests, growing under pine, spruce and hemlock, in the Northern Hemisphere. Mushroom World, an independent source, describes it growing in mossy pine forests throughout Scandinavia. Neither source gives a season or a substrate. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
