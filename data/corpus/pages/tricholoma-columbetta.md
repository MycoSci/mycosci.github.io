## Summary

Tricholoma columbetta (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, recorded from Europe and elsewhere in the Northern Hemisphere. Wikipedia places it in Tricholoma section Albata and describes it as forming mycorrhizal relationships with oak. The same source quotes Roger Phillips in 2010 reporting what "seems to be a first record of this species in North America." *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Elias Magnus Fries described the species in 1821 as Agaricus columbetta. Paul Kummer placed it in the genus Tricholoma in 1871, where it is classified in the Section Albata. No supplied source gives the type material or the reasoning behind the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, describes it as widespread across Europe. It states that the species forms mycorrhizal relationships with oak (Quercus). It is found in woodlands, parks, and rarely sand dunes, on sandy, mildly acidic soils. Fruiting is given as August to November, and in southern Finland as August and September. *[wikipedia]*
