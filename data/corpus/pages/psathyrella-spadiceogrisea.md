## Summary

Psathyrella spadiceogrisea (Schaeff.) Maire, 1937, is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae, recorded from many countries. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a large, springtime Psathyrella with brown caps, wisps of veil material along the cap margin, a dark brown spore print and snap-able white stems. That source says that apart from its larger size it is virtually indistinguishable from Psathyrella pseudovernalis in macroscopic features. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com notes that Kits van Waveren (1985) reduced a number of species to mere forms of Psathyrella spadiceogrisea, including Psathyrella vernalis. The site's author says they disagree with this approach, as do Orton (1986) and Hansen & Knudsen (1992), whom the source names as agreeing with that view. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing scattered to gregariously or in small clusters in hardwood or conifer forests, often near woody debris, in spring and early summer, and apparently widely distributed in North America. This is a single-source description, and it refers to North America only. *[mushroomexpert]*
