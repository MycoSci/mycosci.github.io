According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1826 as a species of Polyporus by the German botanists Carl Ludwig Blume and Theodor Nees. Otto Kuntze transferred it to Microporus in 1898. The supplied sources do not say why the transfer was made. *[wikipedia]*
