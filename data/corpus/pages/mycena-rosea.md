## Summary

Mycena rosea, the rosy bonnet, is a bioluminescent mushroom in the family Mycenaceae, given its present name by Gramberg in 1912. Wikipedia describes the cap as initially convex before flattening, up to 6 cm in diameter. It also reports that the fruit bodies contain two red alkaloid pigments, mycenarubin A and mycenarubin B, described as unique to this species. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was first named Agaricus roseus in 1803 by the Danish botanist Heinrich Christian Friedrich Schumacher, and was given its present name in 1912 by Gramberg. The iNaturalist text repeats this Wikipedia wording and is not independent of it. *[wikipedia]*
