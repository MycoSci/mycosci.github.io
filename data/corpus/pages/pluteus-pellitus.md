## Summary

Pluteus pellitus (Pers.) P. Kumm., the ghost shield, is a species of mushroom-forming fungus in the family Pluteaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a wood-dwelling fungus that decomposes dead plant material, with a whitish cap that is pale brown towards the centre, and as widespread but rare in Europe. Mushroom World independently describes a cap 4-10 cm across that is white with an ocher-cream centre, gills that are white and then pink, and a pale pink spore print. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a wood-dwelling mushroom that decomposes dead plant material. Mushroom World says it typically grows alone or in small clusters on decaying wood and is found in various forested environments. Wikipedia calls it widespread but rare in Europe, Mushroom World lists Europe and North America as its location, and the GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
