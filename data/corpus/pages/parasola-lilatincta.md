## Summary

Parasola lilatincta (Bender & Uljé) Redhead, Vilgalys & Hopple, published in 2001, is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. Its English name is Lilac Inkcap, and it was previously named Coprinus lilatinctus. The only descriptive source supplied, a GBIF description based on specimens from Pakistan, gives a cap 20–30 mm across, deeply plicate toward the margin, with free lamellae that turn black and collapse as they age, and a white, fragile stipe without an annulus. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The one supplied source records Pakistani collections in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province: scattered under herbaceous plants at 430 m altitude (Malakand, Qaldara; collected 11 August 2014), and on road trails at 1023 m altitude (Swat, Kanju Township; collected 27 July 2017). The same source lists distribution records for Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden, without habitat detail for those. *[gbif_desc]*
