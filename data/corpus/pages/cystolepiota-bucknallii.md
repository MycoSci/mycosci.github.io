## Summary

Cystolepiota bucknallii (Berk. & Broome) Singer & Clémençon, 1972, is a species of Agaricaceae, known in English as the Lilac Dapperling. Wikipedia describes it as a small, rare agaric with a distinctive smell of coal gas, recorded in central and northern Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a terrestrial decomposer occurring in deciduous forests on moist, calcareous soils rich in nutrients, especially nitrogen. Fruit bodies appear in autumn. It is recorded in France, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Germany, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Russia, and is described as uncommon or rare.
