## Summary

Tricholoma sejunctum (Sowerby) Quél., 1872, is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as the Deceiving Knight. Wikipedia describes it as occurring across much of the Northern Hemisphere. MushroomExpert.Com reports that in North America the name has been applied broadly to a number of sejunctum-like mushrooms, so its application there is unsettled. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states the species was first described from England and cites the authorities as (Sowerby, 1799) Quélet, 1872. It also says that the author has narrowed his own use of the name in North America to hardwood-associated, greenish yellow collections, and treats conifer-associated sejunctum-like North American collections separately. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Sources disagree on the host association, and the disagreement may reflect different species concepts. Wikipedia says the species is associated with pine forests and, in North America, appears on the Northwest coast and in the east. MushroomExpert.Com, using a narrowed concept of the name, describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods in temperate zones, growing scattered or gregariously in late summer and fall. It says its North American distribution is uncertain, and its illustrated collections come from Illinois, Kentucky, Michigan and Missouri. GBIF's distribution listing names Belgium (including the Walloon Region and Flanders), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
