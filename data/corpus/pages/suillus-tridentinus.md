## Summary

Suillus tridentinus (Bres.) Singer, 1945, the orange larch bolete, is a member of the Suillaceae (Boletales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with European larch, with an orange cap and an orange pore surface. Wikipedia describes it as uncommon and found mainly at higher altitudes beneath larches on chalk. It is recorded from many European countries. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
