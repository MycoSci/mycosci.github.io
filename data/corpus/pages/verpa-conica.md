## Summary

Verpa conica (O.F. Müll.) Sw. is a species in the family Morchellaceae, known in English as the early morel, thimble morel or bell morel. Sources describe it as a springtime mushroom whose cap hangs free from the stem, like a thimble on the end of a pencil. It is sometimes mistaken for a true morel but is not one, because its cap is not attached to the stem along its sides. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Phallus conicus by Otto Friedrich Müller in 1775. It was later moved to Leotia by Persoon (1801), to Relhanum by Samuel Frederick Gray (1821) and to Monka by Otto Kuntze (1898), and was transferred to Verpa by Olof Swartz. Michael Kuo (MushroomExpert.Com) notes that at least two potentially distinct North American species are currently passing under this name, a large-spored western one and a smaller-spored eastern one. He says the eastern version's spore sizes match the European species and the western version's are too large. He also says he knows of no investigation into whether either is the same as the true European species. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as found in North America, from the West Coast (March to June) to the Northeast (April to May). It says the species grows singly, scattered or in groups on the ground in both hardwood and coniferous forests, often in river valleys or along stream banks. It fruits mainly in late spring, usually near morel season, and has been reported fruiting abundantly in chaparral scrubland in southern California. MushroomExpert.Com (Kuo) says it is probably mycorrhizal and is found under hardwoods in spring, or over winter in California, where it also grows under conifers. Kuo describes it as fairly widely distributed in North America and cites collections from Michigan, Indiana and California. GBIF's distribution listing for the species includes Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
