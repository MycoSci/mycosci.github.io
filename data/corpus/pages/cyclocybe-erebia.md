## Summary

Cyclocybe erebia (Fr.) Vizzini & Matheny, 2014, the dark fieldcap, is a brown-spored agaric in the family Tubariaceae. Wikipedia describes it as widely distributed, occurring in the Americas, Europe and parts of Oceania. MushroomExpert.Com describes a dark brown, sticky cap 1.5–5 cm across, decurrent-toothed gills, a flaring white ring on the stem, and a brown spore print. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Agaricus erebius Fr., published in Systema Mycologicum 1: 246 (1821), with typification not indicated. The same record gives 'On soil, associated with Poaceae: Sweden' as its host-substratum/locality entry. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both note that the species was long placed in Agrocybe (as Agrocybe erebia). MushroomExpert.Com attributes the move to recent DNA research (Vizzini, Angelini & Ercole 2014), which found it is not closely related to Agrocybe. Wikispecies cites the combination in Cyclocybe to Vizzini & Matheny in Vizzini, Index Fungorum 154: 1 (2014). *[wikispecies, wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, growing scattered or gregariously on the ground in woods under hardwoods or conifers, and sometimes in urban settings such as gardens and parks, in summer and fall. It reports the species as widely distributed east of the Rocky Mountains in North America, also reported from the Pacific Northwest, and found in Europe and Oceania. It says the species seems most common in the upper Midwest and mid-Atlantic states. Wikipedia gives a fruiting period of August to October in most of North America and September to December on the West Coast. The GBIF description record lists only the distribution entries DK, NO, SE and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
