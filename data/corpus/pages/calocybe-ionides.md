## Summary

Calocybe ionides (Bull.) Donk, 1962, is a fungus in the family Lyophyllaceae, listed in the site catalog under the English name Violet Domecap. Wikipedia gives its range as Europe, North America and Japan. MushroomExpert.Com describes a purple to brownish purple cap 2–6 cm across, white to cream gills, a purple to lilac stem and a white spore print. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com says the species was originally described from France and gives the basionym authority as J. B. F. Bulliard, 1792, with the combination by M. A. Donk, 1962. It also notes that Alexander Smith (1941, 1944) thought a mealy odor was not typical of North American collections, and named the variety Tricholoma ionides farinaceum to account for mealy collections. The same source lists Rugosomyces ionides and Tricholoma ionides as synonyms. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as probably saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously under hardwoods in summer and fall. It calls the species widespread in Europe and apparently widely distributed in North America. The collection illustrated and described there is from Indiana. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*
