## Summary

Macrolepiota excoriata (Schaeff.) Wasser, 1978, is a mushroom in the family Agaricaceae, known in English as the Frayed Parasol. English Wikipedia describes it as white to cream, 5 to 15 cm tall, with a convex to shield-shaped cap 6 to 10 cm across. The cap has a brownish center and ochre-yellow to pale-brown scales. The stipe is smooth and cylindrical with a bulbous base and a ring. This description comes from a single encyclopedia entry. *[wikipedia]*
