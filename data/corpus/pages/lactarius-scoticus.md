## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by the British mycologists Miles Joseph Berkeley and Christopher Edmund Broome in 1879, from a type collection made near Aboyne, a village near the edge of the Scottish Highlands, in 1862. Wikispecies lists later combinations and synonyms, including Lactifluus scoticus (Berk. & Broome) Kuntze 1891 and Lactarius pubescens var. scoticus (Berk. & Broome) Krieglst. 1991.
