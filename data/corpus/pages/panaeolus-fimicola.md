## Summary

Panaeolus fimicola (Pers.) Gillet, the turf mottlegill, is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a widespread but rarely identified "little brown mushroom" which sometimes contains small amounts of the hallucinogen psilocybin. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it growing solitary to scattered in soil or dung, fertilized lawns and other grassy places, from late spring to mid-fall, and often appearing during or after cold rain. The same source calls it widespread and common across the Americas, Europe and Africa, and notes it has also been found in Turkey. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
