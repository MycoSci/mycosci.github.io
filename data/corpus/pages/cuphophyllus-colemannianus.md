## Summary

Cuphophyllus colemannianus (A. Bloxam) Bon is a gilled mushroom in the family Hygrophoraceae, with the recommended English name toasted waxcap. Wikipedia describes it as having a European distribution, occurring mainly in agriculturally unimproved grassland. It is assessed as globally "vulnerable" on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species because its habitat is under threat. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described from Britain in 1854 by the naturalist Andrew Bloxam as Hygrophorus colemannianus, and was transferred to the genus Cuphophyllus by the French mycologist Marcel Bon in 1985. The same source states that molecular research based on cladistic analysis of DNA sequences has confirmed it as a distinct species. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as widespread but generally rare throughout Europe, and also known from Greenland. It states that the species occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns), with a marked preference for calcareous sites. The same source adds that recent research suggests waxcaps in general are neither mycorrhizal nor saprotrophic but may be associated with mosses. This is a single-source account and no season is stated. *[wikipedia]*
