## Summary

Hemistropharia albocrenulata (Peck) Jacobsson & E. Larss. is the only species in the monotypic genus Hemistropharia, placed in the family Tubariaceae. Wikipedia describes the genus's classification as unclear, and says it most closely resembles Pholiota, the genus where it was previously placed. It says H. albocrenulata differs from Pholiota in lacking chrysocystidia and in having darker basidiospores. Wikipedia also gives an orangish cap up to 9 cm wide, a stem up to 12 cm long, and a brown spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally named Agaricus albocrenulatus by the American mycologist Charles Horton Peck in 1873. It lists Pholiota albocrenulata (Peck) Sacc. and Stropharia albocrenulata (Peck) Kreisel among its synonyms. The supplied sources do not say what material Peck described it from or why it was later transferred between genera. *[wikipedia]*
