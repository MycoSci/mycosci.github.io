## Summary

Tarzetta catinus (Holmsk.) Korf & J.K. Rogers, 1971, is a cup fungus in the family Tarzettaceae (Pezizales). Wikipedia describes it as a largely European species with a few records from Mexico and the United States, appearing as cream-coloured cups up to 5 cm across. The English name Greater Toothed Cup is in the catalog. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (the only independent source with descriptive text) states that it appears from spring to autumn, usually in small groups among broad-leaved trees, especially beech. It describes the species as largely European, with a few records from Mexico and the United States. *[wikipedia]*
