## Summary

Coprinellus disseminatus (Pers.) J.E. Lange is a species of agaric fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It is known in English as the fairy inkcap, fairies' bonnets or trooping crumble cap. Unlike most coprinoid mushrooms, its gills do not turn to black ink (deliquesce) at maturity, and MushroomExpert.Com describes it as extremely fragile, with caps that crumble quickly when handled. Sources place it on dead wood in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as growing on dead wood such as rotting stumps, widespread across Europe. It gives a North American fruiting season of May to November in the East and October to March near the West Coast. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing in clusters, often by the hundreds, on decaying wood, especially near the bases of stumps, in spring, summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. Mushroom World gives North America and Europe as its location and describes it as growing on and around stumps and dying deciduous trees. Specimen records in the GBIF descriptions list a gregarious habit on decomposing trunks or logs, including a Colombian collection at 2540 m elevation. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
