## Summary

Lacrymaria lacrymabunda (Bull.) Pat. 1887 is a species of fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, commonly known as the weeping widow. It is described as a medium-sized agaric with a yellowish to brownish cap, dark brown gills and a blackish spore print. It is recorded from North America, Central America, Europe, northern Asia and New Zealand. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows on disturbed ground in woodland, gardens, and parks. Mushroom World describes it as growing solitary or in tufts, on soil among grass by paths in woods and on roadside verges. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
