## Summary

Clavicorona taxophila (Thom) Doty, 1947, is a fungus in the family Clavariaceae, with the English common name Yew Club. It is one of the species Maxwell Stanford Doty included when he circumscribed the genus Clavicorona in 1947, and Species Fungorum accepted it as of March 2024. It is among the fungi listed in the Red Data Book of the Murmansk Region, and a 2021 article reports new findings of it there. GBIF distribution records list Belgium (including the Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, lit_1, gbif_desc]*
