## Summary

Rickenella swartzii (Fr.) Kuyper, published in 1984, is a species in the family Rickenellaceae (order Hymenochaetales) and is known in English as the Collared Mosscap. Mushroom World describes it as a tiny agaric with a pale grey to grey-brown cap, a nearly black central navel, a cream-coloured cap margin, and a stem top tinged dark violet. Its cap is given as 0.5-1 cm across and its stem as 2-4 cm tall. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

The GBIF descriptions record list its distribution as Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. Neither source gives a season, a host tree, or a type locality. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
