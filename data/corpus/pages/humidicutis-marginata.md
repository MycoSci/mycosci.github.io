## Summary

Humidicutis marginata (Peck) Singer is a gilled fungus in the waxcap family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes it as found in North America under trees, and a GBIF record lists Vermont as a distribution. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Hygrophorus marginatus by Charles Horton Peck in 1876, and that William Alphonso Murrill called it Hygrocybe marginata in 1916. It was later transferred to the new genus Humidicutis by Rolf Singer, who had previously placed it in Tricholoma. This account comes from a single source cluster (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
