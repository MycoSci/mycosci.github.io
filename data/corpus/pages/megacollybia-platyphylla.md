## Summary

Megacollybia platyphylla (Pers.) Kotl. & Pouzar (1972) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae. Mushroom World, a single aggregator source, describes it as a medium to large, fleshy agaric with a pale brown, fibrillose cap and whitish gills and stem, with a cap 4-12 cm across and a stem 6-15 cm tall. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World (a single aggregator source) states that it grows solitarily or in small groups on and near deciduous hardwood trunks, branches or woody debris, and less commonly on conifer timber. It gives the location as Europe. The same source adds that material from the United States was once thought to belong to this species but has since been classified as a separate species. The GBIF description record lists distribution entries for Belgium (including the Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway, Madeira Island and Vermont, without further detail. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
