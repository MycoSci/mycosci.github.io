## Summary

Russula nobilis Velen. (1920) is a species of Russulaceae, known in English as the beechwood sickener, with its type locality in the Czech Republic. One source describes it as mycorrhizal with beech (Fagus). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was formerly known as Russula mairei, a name given by Rolf Singer in 1929 in honour of the French mycologist René Maire. It was later found to be the same taxon as the earlier Russula nobilis of 1920, which has naming priority. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as mycorrhizal with beech (Fagus) in woodland areas, and as widespread and common in Europe, Asia and North America where these trees grow. This is a single-source statement. No supplied source gives season or substrate detail. *[wikipedia]*
