## Habitat and ecology

Found under conifers in western North America. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, especially Bishop pine, Ponderosa pine, sugar pine and Douglas-fir, growing alone, scattered or gregariously in fall and winter. The same source says the illustrated collections are from northern California and that the precise range is uncertain, though possibly widespread in the Pacific Northwest and northern Rocky Mountains. Wikipedia gives the season as September to March. Wikipedia also notes that the parasitic ascomycete Hypomyces cervinigenus may colonise it, often producing a white moldy appearance. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
