## Summary

Hebeloma radicosum (Bull.) Ricken is a species of agaric fungus in the family Hymenogastraceae, known in English as the rooting poison pie or Rooting Poisonpie. Wikipedia describes the fruit bodies as identifiable by a tapering, root-like stipe base and an almond-like odor. Wikipedia also states that the mushroom is poisonous. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by Jean Baptiste François Pierre Bulliard in 1784 as Agaricus radicosus, and that it was later transferred to Hebeloma by Adalbert Ricken. It also lists transfers to Pholiota (Kummer), Dryophila (Quélet), Myxocybe (Fayod) and Roumeguerites (Locquin). No source supplied here explains the reasons for these transfers. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with descriptive text, states that the species is found in Japan, Europe and North America. It calls it an ammonia fungus that fruits on the latrines of moles, wood mice and shrews. Fruit bodies are said to occur on the ground, scattered or in groups, on soil or grassy areas, and also near bushes and hedges in residential areas. A GBIF descriptions record lists distribution entries for Belgium, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. No source supplied here gives a fruiting season or host trees. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
