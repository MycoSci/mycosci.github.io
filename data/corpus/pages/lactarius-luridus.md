## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Agaricus luridus Pers., 1801 (as Agaricus (Lactifluus) luridus). It lists later combinations: Galorrheus luridus (Pers.) P.Kumm., 1871, and Lactifluus luridus (Pers.: Fr.), 1891. It also lists Lactarius acris var. luridus (Bataille, 1908) and Lactarius uvidus var. luridus (Kavina, 1929). Wikispecies further records that the name Lactarius luridus was misapplied by Bat., Sacc., Ricken and Nüesch to what is now Lactarius violascens. It gives no reasons for any of these transfers. *[wikispecies]*
