## Summary

Pleurotus opuntiae (Durieu & Lév.) Sacc. 1887 is a species in the family Pleurotaceae (Agaricales). Wikipedia describes it as growing in the semi-arid climate of central Mexico and in New Zealand, with beige or cream mushrooms whose caps are 1 to 6 cm across, quite flat and funnel-shaped with slightly rolled edges. It also describes very decurrent gills and a stipe that is very short or often basically nonexistent. A GBIF description record lists a distribution of France. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the binomial name derives from the plant Opuntia megacantha, on whose dead remains the fungus fruits. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
