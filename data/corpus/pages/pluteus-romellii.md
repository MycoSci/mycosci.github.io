## Summary

Pluteus romellii (Britzelm.) Lapl. (1894) is a species in the family Pluteaceae. A 2022 revision in the Journal of Fungi accepts it as an exclusively Eurasian species. MushroomExpert.Com applies the name to North American collections, following studies from 2010 and 2011, whereas the 2022 revision restricts it to Eurasia. *[lit_1, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

A 2022 study using morphological and molecular data (nrITS, TEF1-α) lectotypified and epitypified Pluteus romellii and treated Pluteus lutescens and P. pallescens as synonyms of it. The same study treats P. fulvibadius as a related but separate North American species and describes five new species in the /romellii clade of section Celluloderma. MushroomExpert.Com notes that North American collections had gone under several other names, including P. lutescens, P. nanus var. lutescens, P. fulvibadius and P. melleipes, before it applied the name romellii to them. *[lit_1, mushroomexpert]*
