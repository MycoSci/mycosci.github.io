## Summary

Conocybe rickenii (Jul. Schäff.) Kühner is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a small brown mushroom with a conical cap and a long, thin stem, growing on dung and very rich soil. It says the species differs from other dung-loving Conocybe in having two-spored basidia, large spores, and no lecythiform (flask-shaped) caulocystidia. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by the German mycologist Julius Schäffer as Galera rickenii, and that Robert Kühner later placed it in the genus Conocybe. No supplied source gives the type material or the reasoning behind the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Conocybe rickenii as coprophilous, growing on extremely rich soil, especially on dung and compost heaps. It says the species can be found in very large numbers in gardens where horse manure has been used to enrich the soil. Wikipedia gives its range as Europe, Australia, Pacific islands and the United States. The GBIF species description lists its distribution as Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
