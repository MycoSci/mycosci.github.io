## Summary

Gymnopilus picreus (Pers.) P. Karst. 1879 is a species of mushroom in the family Hymenogastraceae, recorded from Europe and North America. Mushroom World describes it as a saprotrophic agaric with an orange-brown cap, and says mycologists disagree about what the species looks like and that the name may have been applied to more than one species. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology
