## Summary

Leccinum versipelle (Fr.) Snell, the orange birch bolete, is a bolete in the family Boletaceae. It is recorded mainly across Europe and also in North America and parts of Asia. Sources describe it as growing in association with birch. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as fruiting on the ground, solitary or gregarious, in an ectomycorrhizal association with Betula in forests and heathland, on sandy, slightly acidic, loamy soils. Mushroom World likewise says it grows solitary or in small scattered groups on soil under birch and on heaths, sometimes together with Leccinum scabrum. Wikipedia gives the fruiting period as July through November in one place and August to November across Europe in another. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
