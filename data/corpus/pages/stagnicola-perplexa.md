## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the genus name Stagnicola refers to the fungus colonizing (-cola) its habitat of stagnant pools. No supplied source discusses the derivation of the epithet perplexa. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the authors were unaware when they coined the genus name Stagnicola that a genus of snails already had the same name. Because the zoological and botanical/fungal nomenclature codes are independent, both names are valid. Wikipedia also reports that the genus appeared phylogenetically isolated, was closest to Mythicomyces, and was placed with it in the new family Mythicomycetaceae in 2019. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

It gives the range as North America (Canada, USA) and Europe. *[wikipedia]*
