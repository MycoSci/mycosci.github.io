## Summary

Tricholoma robustum (Alb. & Schwein.) Ricken, 1914, is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, with 355 GBIF occurrence records across 24 countries. Wikipedia describes it as an ectomycorrhizal mushroom of coniferous forests, associated with pine. In Japan it is called matsutake-modoki. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia reports that in Japan the species is called matsutake-modoki ("pseudo-matsutake") and colloquially obasan no matsutake ("old lady's matsutake"), because it resembles the matsutake mushroom while being smaller and less aromatic. This is a single-source account. It concerns the Japanese names only, and no source supplied here explains the English name "Robust Knight". *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, itself a single independent source, describes T. robustum as an ectomycorrhizal fungus found in coniferous forests, forming a relationship with pine trees (especially Pinus densiflora), on sandy soils. It states that the species is distributed throughout the northern hemisphere, especially the northern temperate zone, including Polesia and the forest steppes of Ukraine. It also states that the species often grows in the same forests as matsutake but fruits later, from August to October. *[wikipedia]*
