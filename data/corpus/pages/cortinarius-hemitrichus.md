## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Agaricus hemitrichus by Christian Hendrik Persoon in 1801, and Elias Magnus Fries transferred it to Cortinarius in 1838. The same source notes that the name Hydrocybe hemitricha, published by M.M. Moser in 1953, reflects a different placement of the species.
