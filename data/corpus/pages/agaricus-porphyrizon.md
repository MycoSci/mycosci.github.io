## Summary

Agaricus porphyrizon P.D. Orton (1960), the Lilac Mushroom, is a species in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia describes it as rare. A 2008 survey of Itapuã State Park in Rio Grande do Sul reported it as new for Brazil, and a 2021 paper reported it as new for Azerbaijan, from Shaki District field trips in 2016–2019. *[wikipedia, lit_2, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found in mixed and deciduous woodland, as well as grassy areas. A study of Shaki District, Azerbaijan, classes it as a humus saprotroph, one of three such species among the 18 taxa it reports. Both habitat statements are single-source. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*
