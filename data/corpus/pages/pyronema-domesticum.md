## Habitat and ecology

A 2021 Frontiers in Microbiology paper calls P. domesticum a post-fire fungus. Its isolate came from the 2013 Rim Fire (California, USA). In laboratory culture it grew on agar where the sole carbon source was pine wood pyrolyzed organic matter (PyOM) produced at 750°C, and it mineralized that PyOM to CO2. The same paper states that Pyronema species (a genus-level statement) strongly dominate soil fungal communities within weeks to months after fire. This is a single-source account, and it does not give host, season or field substrate for wild collections. *[lit_2]*
