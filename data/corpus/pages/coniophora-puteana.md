## Summary

Coniophora puteana (Schumach.) P. Karst. (1865) is a species in the family Coniophoraceae, order Boletales, with the common names cellar fungus and wet rot. Wikipedia describes it as a wood-decaying brown rot fungus that can cause structural damage to timber in buildings, especially where the wood is damp. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as typically found in humid areas on pine as well as hardwood. It states that the fungus needs high humidity (given as averaging around 50-60%), so it occurs in wood near water leaks or in naturally humid places, not only in cellars. The same article calls its distribution widespread, on most continents, most predominantly in eastern North America and western Europe, and says it is most commonly found in autumn, in October.
