## Summary

Baeospora myosura (Fr.) Singer, 1938, is a small gilled mushroom in the family Marasmiaceae, known in English as the conifercone cap. Sources describe it as growing on decaying conifer cones, with a convex cap 1-3 cm across, very crowded whitish gills, a white spore print and amyloid spores. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic on the fallen cones of spruces and pines, especially Norway spruce, eastern white pine, Douglas-fir and Sitka spruce. It grows alone or gregariously in fall and is described as widely distributed in North America. Wikipedia likewise says it grows on decaying conifer cones and is common in North America and Europe. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
