## Summary

Faerberia carbonaria (Alb. & Schwein.) Pouzar, 1981, is the only species in the monotypic genus Faerberia, family Polyporaceae, and is commonly called the firesite funnel. Wikipedia describes it as a small, funnel-shaped, gilled polypore that grows on burned soil. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the common name 'firesite funnel' is due to the species' habitat of burned soil. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described in 1805 as Merulius carbonarius by Johannes Baptista von Albertini and Lewis David de Schweinitz. The genus Faerberia was circumscribed in 1981 by the Czech mycologist Zdeněk Pouzar. *[wikipedia]*
