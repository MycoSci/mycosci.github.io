## Summary

Tricholoma acerbum (Bull. ex Pers.) Quél. 1872 is a mushroom in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as Bitter Knight. Wikipedia describes it as found in Europe and North America. The same source reports it as listed as endangered to critically endangered in various countries in northern, western and central Europe, and names deforestation, loss of forest grazing and the shift from oak to conifer silviculture as threats. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as found in temperate, deciduous oak forests, and reports mycorrhizal associations with oak, chestnut, linden and hazel. In southern Europe it has been reported on mesotrophic to base-rich soils, though not calcareous ones. In Norway and Russia it has been observed on calcareous soils. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia); no season or substrate detail beyond this is stated in the supplied sources. *[wikipedia]*
