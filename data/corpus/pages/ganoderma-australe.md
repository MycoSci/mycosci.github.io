## Summary

Ganoderma australe (Fr.) Pat., 1889, is a bracket fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a fungal plant pathogen and a polypore, and it is recorded from many countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that G. australe is distributed widely throughout the tropics and appears to be one of the most common Ganoderma species there. It names south of the Sahara, the Pacific shores of Canada, north-west India and Pakistan, and the range from the Philippines to New Caledonia and Papua. The same source says it is not clear whether the species occurs in South America, because few specimens are available. It says the species parasitizes oil palms and has a variety of hosts compared with other Ganoderma species. A GBIF descriptions record lists the substrate as dead or living trunks in sunny places, and separately as a decaying trunk. These substrate notes come from a single database record. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
