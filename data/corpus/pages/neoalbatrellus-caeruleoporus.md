## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Polyporus caeruleoporus Peck, published in the Annual Report of the New York State Museum of Natural History 26: 68 (1874, dated 1873). The typification is listed as Peck s.n., with locality New York. The species was later placed in Scutiger by Murrill (1903) and in Albatrellus by Pouzar (1966). Audet moved it to the new genus Neoalbatrellus in Mycotaxon 111: 443 (2010). The supplied sources do not give the reasons for these transfers. *[wikispecies]*
