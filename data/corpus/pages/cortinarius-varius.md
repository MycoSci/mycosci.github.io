## Summary

Cortinarius varius (Schaeff.) Fr., 1838, the contrary webcap, is a species of Cortinariaceae. Wikipedia describes it as having a sticky, orangish-yellow cap up to 10 cm across, with gills that start cornflower blue and age through lilac to ochre-cinnamon. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus varius by Jacob Christian Schäffer in 1774, and that Elias Magnus Fries gave it its current name in 1838. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruit bodies grow in groups in coniferous forests, and also in glades and at the edge of woods, from the end of summer until late autumn when the frosts set in. It says the species is common in some places and quite rare in others, and that it prefers calcareous soils. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
