## Summary

Psilocybe caerulipes (Peck) Sacc. (1887) is a species in the family Hymenogastraceae, based on the earlier name Agaricus caerulipes and known in English as blue-foot. Wikipedia describes it as a small mushroom with a cap 1–3.5 cm across and a stipe 3–6 cm long that bruises blue, and records it from eastern North America and Mexico. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia gives the name as derived from the Latin words caerulea (blue) and pes (foot). This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows solitary to cespitose in deciduous forests, on hardwood slash and debris, plant matter, and on or about decaying hardwood logs, birch, beech and maple. It gives the season as late May through December. Its stated range is eastern North America, from Nova Scotia to North Carolina and west to Michigan, and south to the Mexican states of Hidalgo and Veracruz, where it is found in cloud forests on Fagus. A GBIF description record lists a distribution of Vermont. The habitat and season details come from a single source, Wikipedia. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
