## Habitat and ecology

This comes from a single source cluster (Wikipedia, which iNaturalist reproduces). Wikipedia says that in southwest Finland the species grows in rows, groups or clusters in moist or occasionally dry coniferous heath forests, in Picea needle litter and sometimes in heaps of straw. It is often found alongside Pleurozium or Hylocomium mosses. In the eastern areas of southwest Finland it is reported growing throughout October. Wikipedia describes it as common throughout northwest Europe, where it grows from September to January, and additionally in nutrient-rich grasslands. *[wikipedia]*
