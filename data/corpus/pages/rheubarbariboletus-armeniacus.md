## Summary

Rheubarbariboletus armeniacus (Quél.) Vizzini, Simonini & Gelardi (2015) is a species in the family Boletaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a small mushroom native to Europe that was formerly placed in Boletus, Xerocomus and Xerocomellus. Index Fungorum currently lists Xerocomus armeniacus as the current name, so the generic placement is disputed. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Lucien Quélet described the species as Boletus armeniacus in 1885 and placed it in Xerocomus in his 1888 work Flore mycologique de la France et des pays limitrophes. It was later transferred to Xerocomellus, a genus described by Josef Šutara in 2008, and then to Rheubarbariboletus in 2015. This account comes from a single source, Wikipedia. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species has been recorded forming an ectomycorrhizal association with white poplar (Populus alba) in Hungary. It adds that the ectomycorrhizae cannot be reliably distinguished from those of Xerocomus subtomentosus. This is a single-source statement about one recorded association. *[wikipedia]*
