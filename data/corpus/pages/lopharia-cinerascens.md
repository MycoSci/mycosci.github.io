## Summary

Lopharia cinerascens (Schwein.) G. Cunn. is a crust fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Lewis David de Schweinitz first described it in 1832 as Thelephora cinerascens, and Gordon Herriot Cunningham transferred it to Lopharia in 1956. Wikipedia describes it as widely distributed in Africa, Asia, Australasia and North America, and less common in Europe and South America. *[wikipedia]*
