## Summary

Tulosesus angulatus (Peck) D. Wächter & A. Melzer, 2020, is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was first described by Charles Horton Peck in 1874 as Coprinus angulatus and has been moved between genera since. ## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by Charles Horton Peck in 1874 and placed in the genus Coprinus. After a 2001 phylogenetic study reorganized that genus, it was transferred to Coprinellus and was known as Coprinellus angulatus until 2020. In 2020 the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many species in the Psathyrellaceae on the basis of phylogenetic analysis, which produced the current name. *[wikipedia]*
