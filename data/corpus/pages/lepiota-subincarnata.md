## Summary

Lepiota subincarnata, known as the fatal dapperling and deadly parasol, is a gilled mushroom in the genus Lepiota (family Agaricaceae), described by J.E. Lange in 1940. Wikipedia reports that it contains alpha-amanitin and that eating it can be potentially lethal. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming
