## Summary

Lycoperdon excipuliforme (Scop.) Pers. (1801), the pestle puffball, is a species in the family Lycoperdaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a rather large puffball, up to 15 cm broad by 25 cm tall, widespread in northern temperate zones and found frequently on pastures and sandy heaths. Mushroom World gives smaller dimensions of 3-10 cm in diameter and 8-20 cm tall. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that this puffball has been variously placed in the genera Bovista, Handkea, Calvatia and Utraria. It says that in 1989 Hanns Kreisel described Handkea for species of Calvatia with a distinct type of capillitium (coarse, thick-walled hyphae with curvy slits instead of the usual pores). It adds that some authors accept that genus concept and others have rejected it. The supplied sources do not describe the material the species was originally described from. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species is common and widespread from late summer until autumn. It says the species grows singly or in small groups in soil, in both coniferous and broadleaf woodland and on short grassland, and that it is found frequently on pastures and sandy heaths. Mushroom World independently says it fruits most often singly or in very small groups in woodland habitats and gives its location as North America and Europe. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
