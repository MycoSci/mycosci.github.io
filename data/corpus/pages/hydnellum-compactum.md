## Summary

Hydnellum compactum (Pers.) P. Karst. (1879) is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae. Wikipedia describes it as rare and found in Europe, with top-shaped fruit bodies whose caps reach up to 10 cm across. The same source reports it as listed as vulnerable on the IUCN red list and as endangered in Switzerland. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus grows in deciduous forest in Europe, typically under beech, with fruit bodies growing singly or in groups. It describes the species as ectomycorrhizal with sessile oak (Quercus petraea), common oak (Q. robur) and beech (Fagus sylvatica), and occasionally with sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa). It is said to prefer nutrient-poor, dry to moist, loamy and sandy soils and sunny, mosaic, old-growth forests.
