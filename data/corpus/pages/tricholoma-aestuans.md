## Summary

Tricholoma aestuans (Fr.) Gillet is a mushroom in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as Acrid Knight or Bitter Yellow Knight. Mushroom World describes it as a medium-sized agaric with pale yellow gills, a conical to broadly convex sticky yellowish cap and a white spore print. *[mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described formally by Elias Magnus Fries in 1821 and transferred to the genus Tricholoma by Claude Casimir Gillet in 1874. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World (a single source) gives the location as Europe and states that the mushroom forms mycorrhiza with spruce and pine, rarely with deciduous trees, especially in sandy soils. *[mushroomworld]*
