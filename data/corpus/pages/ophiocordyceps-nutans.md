## Summary

Ophiocordyceps nutans (Pat.) G.H. Sung, J.M. Sung, Hywel-Jones & Spatafora (2007) is an entomopathogenic fungus in the family Ophiocordycipitaceae. According to Wikipedia, it parasitizes only Hemipterans, namely stinkbugs, and is one of the most common Cordyceps species in Korea. Wikipedia also states that its anamorph, Hymenostilbe nutans, is a potential selective biological control agent against stinkbugs. *[wikipedia]*
