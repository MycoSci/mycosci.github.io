## Summary

Lactarius plumbeus (Bull.) Gray, 1821, is a species of milk-cap in the family Russulaceae, order Russulales. GBIF records it in Europe, North America and Australia, among other countries. The only descriptive source available treats this name under another name, so this page is deliberately short. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the epithet plumbeus is derived from the Latin plumbeus, 'lead-coloured', and that it referred to the milk-cap's appearance. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the epithet plumbeus was coined by Pierre Bulliard, as Agaricus plumbeus in 1793. It also states that there is and was confusion about which mushrooms Bulliard's names were meant to cover. *[wikipedia]*
