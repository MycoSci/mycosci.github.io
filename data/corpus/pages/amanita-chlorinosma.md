## Summary

Amanita chlorinosma (Peck) Lloyd, 1898, is a species in the family Amanitaceae, also called the chlorine lepidella. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as one of several white Amanitas with powdery universal veil remnants and a strong odor reminiscent of chlorine, and says the name is often applied broadly to almost any species meeting that description. Wikipedia states it is strongly considered toxic, though little research has been done on this particular species. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and perhaps with pines, usually growing alone but sometimes scattered or gregarious, in summer and fall, widely distributed in eastern North America below the Great Lakes from about the Mississippi River eastward. Wikipedia reports it mainly from North Carolina (Piedmont and Coastal Plain), ranging from Massachusetts and Illinois south to Florida, in pine and oak woodland. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
