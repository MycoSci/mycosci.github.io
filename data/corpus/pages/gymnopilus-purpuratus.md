## Summary

Gymnopilus purpuratus (Cooke & Massee) Singer is an agaric in the family Hymenogastraceae. Wikipedia describes it as psychoactive, with psilocybin, psilocin and baeocystin reported from chemical analyses, and it has been recorded in Argentina, Australia, Chile, New Zealand, the UK and Germany. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia's external links point to a painting of Cooke's type specimen of Agaricus purpuratus, collected in Kew Gardens, in his Illustrations of British Fungi. No source supplied here narrates the later transfer to Gymnopilus. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows in clusters on dead wood, tree stumps and wood chip mulch. *[wikipedia]*
