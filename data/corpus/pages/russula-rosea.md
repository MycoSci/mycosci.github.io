## Summary

Russula rosea Pers. (1796) is a member of the Russulaceae, known in English as the Rosy Brittlegill. Wikipedia describes it as a commonly found north temperate mushroom with a convex to flat, mostly bright cinnabar to carmine red cap up to 10 cm across. It says the gills are pale straw-yellow and brittle, the spores pale cream, and the stem usually flushed carmine but sometimes pure white. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is commonly found in coniferous forests or near beech trees. *[wikipedia]*
