## Summary

Hygrocybe splendidissima (P.D. Orton) M.M. Moser, 1967, is a species of waxcap in the family Hygrophoraceae, given the recommended English name splendid waxcap. Wikipedia describes it as having a European distribution, occurring mainly in agriculturally unimproved grassland. Threats to that habitat have led to it being assessed as globally "vulnerable" on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1960 by the British mycologist P. D. Orton as a species of Hygrophorus. The type was collected by Orton in fields near Membury, Devon, in 1957. Meinhard Moser transferred it to Hygrocybe in 1967. Wikipedia also reports that molecular (DNA sequence) analysis has shown it to be distinct and to belong in Hygrocybe sensu stricto. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as widespread but generally rare throughout Europe, with the largest populations in the United Kingdom, Germany, Sweden, Denmark and Norway. It states that, like other waxcaps, it occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns).
