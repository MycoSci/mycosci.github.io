## Summary

Melanoleuca verrucipes (Fr.) Singer (1939) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, with GBIF occurrence records from many countries across Europe, North America and elsewhere. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as the one species in its genus that is not an identification nightmare, marked by prominent dark brown to black scabers over the stem surface. ## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in soil or woodchips, from spring through fall. In boreal and montane Europe it is reported there as found "naturally" in grassy areas, while elsewhere in Europe it is found in landscaping areas. For North America the same source reports it from the Pacific Northwest and from the northeast and mid-Atlantic states, in woodchip beds and other landscaping areas, or in grassy ditches and along roadsides, and says that on that continent it appears to be limited to urban areas. The described collection was from Pennsylvania. *[mushroomexpert]*
