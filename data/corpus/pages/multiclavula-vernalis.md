## Summary

Multiclavula vernalis (Schwein.) R.H. Petersen, 1967 is a clavarioid fungus in the family Hydnaceae, order Cantharellales. It was first described as Clavaria vernalis by Lewis David de Schweinitz in 1822 and moved to Multiclavula by Ronald H. Petersen in 1967. The sources supplied for this page hold little beyond nomenclature and a short distribution record. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Lewis David de Schweinitz named the species Clavaria vernalis in 1822 (Schr. naturf. Ges. Leipzig 1: 112). Ronald H. Petersen transferred it to Multiclavula in 1967, in a paper titled 'Notes on clavarioid fungi. VII. Redefinition of the Clavaria vernalis-C. mucida complex' (Amer. Midland Nat. 77: 205-221). Wikispecies also lists two other combinations: Clavulinopsis vernalis (Corner, 1950) and Stichoclavaria vernalis (Paechnitz, 1987). The supplied sources do not say why these transfers were made. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*
