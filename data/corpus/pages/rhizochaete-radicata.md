## Summary

Rhizochaete radicata (Henn.) Gresl., Nakasone & Rajchenb. (2004) is a species of Phanerochaetaceae in the order Polyporales. Its synonyms include Corticium radicatum, Peniophora radicata and Phanerochaete radicata. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen of trees, and the supplied sources say little else about it. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, it was first described as Corticium radicatum in 1895 by Paul Christoph Hennings. It was transferred to Rhizochaete in 2004 by Alina Greslebin, Karen Nakasone and Mario Rajchenberg. Wikipedia also notes that Index Fungorum and MycoBank disagree on the current name. MycoBank treats it as Phanerochaete radicata, and Index Fungorum as Rhizochaete radicata. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen infecting planes, beeches, pines, oaks and other trees.
