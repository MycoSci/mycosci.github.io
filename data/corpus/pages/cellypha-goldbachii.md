## Summary

Cellypha goldbachii (Weinm.) Donk, 1959, is a fungus in the family Tricholomataceae (order Agaricales) and the type species of the genus Cellypha. It was reported as a first record for Turkey in a 2018 paper. A 1991 catalogue of fungi from the Montseny massif in Catalonia also lists it. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*
