## Summary

Hypholoma marginatum J. Schröt. (1889) is a member of the Strophariaceae, known in English as the Snakeskin Brownie. Mushroom World, a single aggregator source, describes it as a small agaric with a cap 1.5-4 cm across and a stem with a silvery, snakeskin-like pattern. The same source says cap colour is highly variable and that the stem patterning is the more useful identification feature. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

That source gives its location as Europe and North America. The GBIF description record lists only DK, NO and SE. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
