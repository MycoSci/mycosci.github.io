## Summary

Hygrocybe insipida (J.E. Lange) M.M. Moser, 1967, is a waxcap in the family Hygrophoraceae, known in English as the Spangle Waxcap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tiny orange waxy cap with a thinly sticky cap and stem and gills that begin to run down the stem. The same source calls it a European species rarely reported in North America. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com says the precise ecological role of this species is uncertain. Its illustrated collection was growing gregariously under hardwoods in summer in Illinois. The same source reports that Largent (1985) found the species under coast redwood in fall and winter. Both habitat statements come from that single source. *[mushroomexpert]*
