## Summary

Hygrocybe acutoconica (Clem.) Singer, 1951, is a waxcap in the family Hygrophoraceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a medium-sized waxy cap with a pointed or conical cap and yellow to orange colors, which does not blacken with age or when bruised. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from Nebraska. It also states that European mycologists have applied the name to European material and have apparently relinquished use of Hygrocybe persistens, a competing European name. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as growing alone or scattered under oaks and other hardwoods, from late spring through fall (and over winter in warmer climates), and as apparently widely distributed in North America. It gives the precise ecological role as uncertain. The collections described and illustrated there are from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*
