## Summary

Calocybe chrysenteron (Bull.) Singer (1962) is a species in the family Lyophyllaceae. The site catalog gives its common name as Yellow Domecap. It has been recorded in many countries, mostly in Europe. A 2012 macrofungi survey of Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park in Nepal lists it among the species identified. *[lit_3]*

## Discovery and naming

A study of collections from several European countries concluded that the true Calocybe cerina is very close to C. chrysenteron and is best regarded as a variety. It proposed the new combination Calocybe chrysenteron var. cerina. The same study found that the name C. cerina had recently been applied to two different species, and that most collections identified as C. cerina belong to another fungus, described there as Callistosporium pinicola. A 1994 paper on Agaricales from maritime sands in peninsular Spain proposed the new combination Calocybe chrysenteron var. juncicola (R. Heim) G. Moreno. *[lit_1, lit_2]*
