## Summary

Agaricus litoralis (Wakef. & A. Pearson) Pilát, the coastal mushroom, is a species in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia reports it from across Europe, including Scandinavia and Great Britain, and says it may be quite common locally (for example on Öland) but is rarely found in most areas outside southern Europe. The same source gives its conservation status in Sweden as "near threatened". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by Wakefield and A. Pearson as Psalliota litoralis in 1946. The current combination in Agaricus is credited to Pilát (1952). *[wikipedia]*
