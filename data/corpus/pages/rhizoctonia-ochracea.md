## Discovery and naming

The type citation describes a collection from England (Kew), with the type in the Kew herbarium. Roberts (1999) cites the Massee specimen at Kew as holotype, but notes that according to Donk (1958) the specimen is missing. The synonymy of Thanatephorus orchidicola, described from England from roots of Orchis mascula, follows Roberts (1999). The source adds that another species Roberts listed under Thanatephorus ochraceus (Th. pennatus) appears to be distinct. It suggests the placement of Th. orchidicola be revisited by generating sequences from type material. *[gbif_desc]*
