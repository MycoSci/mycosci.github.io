## Summary

Hohenbuehelia angustata (Berk.) Singer, 1951, is a species in the family Pleurotaceae, recorded in GBIF occurrences from the United States, Canada, Mexico and many other countries. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a stemless, fan-shaped to spoon-shaped, whitish to dull brownish, rubbery pleurotoid mushroom with buff to pale tan gills and a white spore print.
