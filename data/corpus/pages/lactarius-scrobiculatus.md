## Summary

Lactarius scrobiculatus (Scop.) Fr. 1838 is a milk cap in the family Russulaceae, known in English as the scrobiculate milk cap or potholed milk cap. Wikipedia describes it as a large fungus with an orange to yellow, slimy cap and a short, hollow stem marked with small fluid-filled pits, and as occurring in Europe and North America. Wikipedia also says most authors regard it as inedible. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (single-source claim) states that it occurs primarily in coniferous and birch forests, forms mycorrhizal relationships, and appears to prefer damp, shady and boggy areas. Fruiting bodies appear in troops, sometimes forming fairy rings, and only rarely singly. The fruiting season is given as summer to autumn. *[wikipedia]*
