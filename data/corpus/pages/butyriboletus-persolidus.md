## Summary

Butyriboletus persolidus, recorded in English as the Brown Butter Bolete, is a pored mushroom in the family Boletaceae. It was described as new to science in 2014 by D. Arora and J.L. Frank, and the type locality is California. The available descriptive text comes from a single source cluster (Wikipedia and an iNaturalist entry derived from it). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it can be found on the West Coast from October to December, growing under hardwood trees, especially oak and tanoak. The iNaturalist taxon summary, which is declared a derivative of the Wikipedia text and so is not independent, places it in California in a mycorrhizal association with coast live oak (Quercus agrifolia). *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*
