## Summary

Mycena citricolor (Berk. & M.A. Curtis) Sacc. (1887) is a species of mushroom-forming fungus in the family Mycenaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen that produces leaf spots on coffee plants and causes the disease known as American Leaf Spot. It also states that small luminescent mushrooms can be seen on the brown, roughly circular leaf spots. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus affects coffee plants, primarily in Latin America, and can also grow on other plants. It says the fungus can grow on all parts of the coffee plant, including leaves, stems and fruits, and that infected leaves develop holes and often fall from the plant. This is a single-source claim (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a derivative of it). *[wikipedia]*
