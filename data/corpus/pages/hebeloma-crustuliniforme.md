## Summary

Hebeloma crustuliniforme (Bull.) Quél. (1872), known as poison pie or fairy cakes, is a gilled mushroom in the family Hymenogastraceae. Wikipedia describes it as poisonous and as occurring in both Europe and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific name derives from the Latin crustulum ('little biscuit'). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it from open woodland and heathland in summer and autumn, and also in winter in milder climates such as California. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods or conifers, growing gregariously or in loose clusters, sometimes in arcs or fairy rings, in grassy areas at woods' edges or in woods, in late summer and fall (winter and spring in California). Both describe it as widespread in North America; Wikipedia also records it from much of Europe. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
