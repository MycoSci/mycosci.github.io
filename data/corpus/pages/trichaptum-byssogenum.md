## Summary

Trichaptum byssogenum (Jungh.) Ryvarden, published in 1972 in Index of Fungi 4: 186, is a species in the order Hymenochaetales (Agaricomycetes). A single GBIF-hosted description characterizes it by a grayish pilear surface that is tomentose to hispid or strigose, large pores (1–2 per mm), and abundant apically incrusted cystidia. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The one available description states that it grows on deciduous wood and calls it a pantropical species. In Brazil it is listed for Acre, Amazonas, Bahia, Minas Gerais, Mato Grosso, Pará, Paraná, Pernambuco, Rio Grande do Sul, Rondônia, Roraima, Santa Catarina and São Paulo, with new records reported for Goiás and Tocantins. This is a single-source account. *[gbif_desc]*
