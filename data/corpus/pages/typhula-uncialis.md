## Summary

Typhula uncialis (Grev.) Berthier is a species of fungus in the family Typhulaceae, order Agaricales. It was first described by Robert Kaye Greville and given its current name by Jacques Berthier in 1976. It is recorded from Europe, North America and Asia. *[wikipedia]*
