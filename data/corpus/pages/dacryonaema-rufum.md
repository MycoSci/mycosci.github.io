## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the genus Dacryonaema was described in 1947 by the Swedish mycologist John Axel Nannfeldt, based on the single species Dacryonaema rufum. Wikipedia describes the immature basidiocarps of this species as elongated, conical and dark reddish brown, unlike those of other genera in the Dacrymycetes. It also reports that later molecular work confirmed the genus as monophyletic and added two further species to it. *[wikipedia]*
