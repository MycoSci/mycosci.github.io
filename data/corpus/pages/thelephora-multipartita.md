## Summary

Thelephora multipartita Schwein. (1828) is a fungus in the family Thelephoraceae, with North America as its type locality. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small, dark, fan-shaped, coral-like mushroom with multiple branches that often flatten with maturity. The same source notes that species concepts in Thelephora rest largely on Corner (1968) and that DNA studies suggest hidden species and significant morphological variability within species in the genus. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, and perhaps occasionally with conifers. It is said to grow alone, gregariously, or in small clusters on the ground in soil or in moss, from late spring through fall (and over winter in warmer climates). The same source calls it widely distributed in North America and says the illustrated and described collections are from Illinois. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
