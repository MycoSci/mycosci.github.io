## Summary

Amanita xanthocephala (Berk.) D.A. Reid & R.N. Hilton, 1980, is a ringless mushroom in the family Amanitaceae, known as the vermilion grisette, pretty grisette or vermilion amanita. Wikipedia places it in Australia in association with Eucalyptus and says it may be toxic to humans. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet xanthocephala derives from the Greek xanthos 'yellow' and kephale 'head'. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was at one stage known as A. pulchella, in a small genus that held the grisettes (ringless Amanita species). That genus was later sunk back into Amanita. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it from southwest Western Australia and from southeastern Australia, from around Adelaide to Southeast Queensland. It is described as particularly found in eucalypt forests, where it has an ectomycorrhizal relationship with Eucalyptus. *[wikipedia]*
