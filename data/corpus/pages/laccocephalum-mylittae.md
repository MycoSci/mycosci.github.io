## Summary

Laccocephalum mylittae (Cooke & Massee) Núñez & Ryvarden 1995 is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae, recorded in GBIF occurrences from Australia, New Zealand and several other countries. Wikipedia describes it as an Australian fungus known as native bread, whose hypogeous fruit body arises from a large underground tuber-like sclerotium that it reports can reach 60 cm in diameter. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described as Polyporus mylittae by Mordecai Cubitt Cooke and George Edward Massee in 1893, and was later placed in the small genus Laccocephalum by María Núñez and Leif Ryvarden in 1995. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
