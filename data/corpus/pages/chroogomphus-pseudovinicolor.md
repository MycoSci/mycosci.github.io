## Summary

Chroogomphus pseudovinicolor O.K. Mill. (1967) is a member of the Gomphidiaceae (Boletales), with its type locality in Idaho and occurrence records in the US, China and Canada. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a robust species of Chroogomphus with a very thick stem, up to 4 or 5 cm wide at the apex, distinctively covered with reddish fibers and scales. The same source describes a 5-15 cm dull red to dull orange cap, gills that run down the stem and develop olive shades, and a greenish to olive-black spore print. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as mycorrhizal with Ponderosa Pine and Douglas-Fir. It typically grows in small clusters of 2-3 mushrooms, but sometimes grows alone or scattered. The season is summer and fall (and early winter in coastal California). The source describes the distribution as western North America and the habitat as the western mountains. *[mushroomexpert]*
