## Summary

Gloeohypochnicium analogum (Bourdot & Galzin) Hjortstam is a crust fungus in the order Russulales, class Agaricomycetes, and the type species of the genus Gloeohypochnicium. The genus is described as wood-inhabiting crust fungi of uncertain familial placement. The catalog records it under the English name 'Fruity Crust'. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that G. analogum was described as new to science in 1913 by the French mycologists Hubert Bourdot and Amédée Galzin, as a species of Gloeocystidium. The genus Gloeohypochnicium was originally conceived by Erast Parmasto as a subgenus of Hypochnicium. Kurt Hjortstam considered it worthy of distinct generic status in 1987.
