## Summary

Clavulina rugosa (Bull.) J. Schröt. (1888) is a coral fungus in the family Hydnaceae, known in English as the wrinkled club. MushroomExpert.Com describes the fruiting body as white, unbranched or divided one to several times, 4-12 cm high and up to 1.5 cm wide, with a rugged surface and blunt branch tips. Wikipedia gives a similar maximum height of about 12 cm. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described as Clavaria rugosa by Jean Bulliard in 1790 and transferred to Clavulina by Joseph Schröter in 1888. On species limits, MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) notes that Clavulina rugosa and Clavulina cristata may be the extreme ends of a continuum, since their microscopic features are very similar, and that this might mean only one species is involved. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as presumably mycorrhizal, found under conifers and hardwoods, and growing alone, gregariously or in clusters. It gives the season as summer and fall (over winter in warm climates) and says the species is widely distributed in North America. Wikipedia separately says it can be found in Europe, growing near wooded paths from August to November. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
