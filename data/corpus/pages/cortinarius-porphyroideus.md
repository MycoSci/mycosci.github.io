## Summary

Cortinarius porphyroideus (Peintner & M.M. Moser, 2002) is a species in the family Cortinariaceae, known in English as the purple pouch fungus. The English Wikipedia article describes it as a secotioid species endemic to New Zealand, and says it appeared on a 2002 series of New Zealand postage stamps depicting native fungi. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to the English Wikipedia article, the species was first described in 1924 by Gordon Herriot Cunningham as Secotium porphyreum, from collections made in Wellington. In 1954 Rolf Singer transferred it to Thaxterogaster, a genus he erected for species with Cortinarius-like spores and either a reduced stipe with only partly exposed gills or a loculate hymenium. It received its current name in 2002, when molecular studies showed that Thaxterogaster was polyphyletic and nested within Cortinarius. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to the English Wikipedia article, C. porphyroideus is an ectomycorrhizal fungus associated with hard beech (Nothofagus truncata) and black beech (Nothofagus solandri). The same article says it has a highly restricted distribution, known only from East Harbour Regional Park in Lower Hutt, Wellington Region, New Zealand. *[wikipedia]*
