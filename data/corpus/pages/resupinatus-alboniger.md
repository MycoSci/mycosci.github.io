## Summary

Resupinatus alboniger (Pat.) Singer, 1978, is a species in the family Pleurotaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tiny pleurotoid mushroom with a flat black to dark gray-brown cap 2-10 mm across, black to dark blackish brown gills, and no stem. ## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on the recently dead wood of hardwoods, in summer and fall (or over winter in warmer climates or during warm spells), and widely distributed in eastern North America.
