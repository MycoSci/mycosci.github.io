## Summary

Chromocyphella muscicola (Fr.) Donk, 1959, is a fungus in the family Chromocyphellaceae (order Agaricales), listed in English as Moss Ear. Its basidiocarps (fruit bodies) are described as cyphelloid and cup-shaped, about 4 mm across, with an upper surface covered in fine hairs and a smooth underside. GBIF holds occurrence records from many countries, including several in Europe, Australia, New Zealand and the United States. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Cyphella muscicola by Elias Fries in 1822. It was transferred in 1959 by Marinus Anton Donk to the newly erected genus Chromocyphella. *[wikipedia]*
