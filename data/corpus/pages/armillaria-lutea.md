## Summary

Armillaria lutea Gillet (1874) is a species in the family Physalacriaceae. The supplied literature records it in pine and spruce plantations and in a mixed forest near Cracow, Poland, where it forms rhizomorph networks and is found in conifer stumps. *[lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

A 2007 study mapped A. lutea rhizomorphs in soil at a Pinus nigra plantation and a Picea abies plantation, with network attachments to Pinus stumps at the first site and to Picea roots and stumps at the second (lit_1). A 1988 study detected A. lutea in stumps in pure Norway spruce; in Norway spruce mixed with oak it sometimes produced extensive rhizomorph systems (lit_3). A 1990 experiment observed its fruitbodies on plots in a mixed forest near Cracow, Poland (lit_2). Each of these is a single study, and none states a season. *[lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*
