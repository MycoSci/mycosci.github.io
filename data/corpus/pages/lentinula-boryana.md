## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described as Agaricus boryanus by Miles Joseph Berkeley and Camille Montagne in 1849. David Pegler moved it to the genus Lentinula and gave it its current name in 1976. No source supplied describes the type material or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*
