## Summary

Melanophyllum haematospermum (Bull.) Kreisel is a species of Agaricaceae, known in English as the Redspored Dapperling and placed in its current genus in 1984. Wikipedia describes a cap 1–3 cm across with red gills and, despite the common name, an olive-green spore print. It is recorded in many countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described in 1793 as Agaricus haematospermus and was transferred to the genus Melanophyllum in 1984. It gives no further detail on the material or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat text, states that it can be found under trees and grows in humus-rich soil, often in disturbed areas. It reports the species near roads, in gardens, and in compost piles and greenhouses. The same source says it is rare in the Pacific Northwest and fruits in late summer and autumn. *[wikipedia]*
