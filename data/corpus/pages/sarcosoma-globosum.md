## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a detritivore living on decomposing plant matter, most commonly leaf litter, and states that it is found in spruce forests and along streams and brooks in Northern Europe. The same source says it prefers well-drained, nutrient-rich soil near rivers and streams within light spruce forests, and that dense forest is unsuitable. It reports that the species seems to grow well in selective logging zones but does not persist in forests that have ever been clear-cut. Wikipedia lists it as native to Canada, Czech Republic, Estonia, Finland, Latvia, Norway, Poland, Russia, Sweden, Ukraine and the United States. It reports the species as extirpated in Germany, Lithuania and Slovakia, and as rarely found in parts of northeastern North America, particularly the Great Lakes region. It also notes a 2021 record from Northern British Columbia. These habitat and range statements come from one source (Wikipedia) and are not independently corroborated here. No season is stated. *[wikipedia]*
