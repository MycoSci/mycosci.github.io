## Summary

Hydnum rufescens Pers. (1800) is a tooth fungus in the family Hydnaceae, commonly known as the terracotta hedgehog. Its fruit bodies bear spines or teeth on the underside of the cap instead of pores or gills. Sources describe it as similar to Hydnum repandum but smaller, with a russet to salmon-pink cap and spines that are not decurrent. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia notes that H. rufescens was previously sometimes considered a variety of Hydnum repandum. No source supplied here describes the original material or the reasons for the later separation. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World states that it grows on soil in broad-leaf or coniferous woods, with location given as Europe. Wikipedia says H. rufescens and H. repandum are both found on soil in European coniferous and deciduous forests. Wikipedia also says H. rufescens is reportedly ectomycorrhizal with Picea abies, Pinus sylvestris, Fagus sylvatica and Quercus robur. No supplied source states a fruiting season. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
