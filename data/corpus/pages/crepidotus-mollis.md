## Summary

Crepidotus mollis (Schaeff.) Staude, 1857, is a species in the family Crepidotaceae, known in English as the peeling oysterling, soft slipper toadstool, or jelly crep. It is a saprobic, stemless, kidney- or fan-shaped bracket fungus of hardwood deadwood with a flabby, gelatinous-textured flesh. Wikipedia regards it as inedible. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, inaturalist]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from Bavaria (Schaeffer 1774) and cites the combination by Staude (1857). It lists Crepidotus alabamensis and Crepidotus fraxinicola as synonyms. It also notes that some mycologists treat the otherwise near-identical Crepidotus calolepis as Crepidotus mollis var. calolepis. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on the deadwood of hardwoods, and reports it from Europe, Asia, North America, the Caribbean, Oceania and Africa. Wikipedia says it grows in groups or overlapping tiers on hardwood (tree trunks, fallen branches, sawdust) and rarely on conifers. It gives the season as July to September in the northern hemisphere. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
