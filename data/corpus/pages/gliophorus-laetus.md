## Summary

Gliophorus laetus (Pers.) Herink is a species of agaric fungus in the waxcap family Hygrophoraceae, listed under the common names Heath Waxcap and chameleon waxcap. Persoon described it in 1800, and it was transferred to Gliophorus in 1958. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small waxy cap with a usually pinkish orange, slimy cap, gills that begin to run down the stem, and often a somewhat fishy odor. The same source notes that, as currently defined, the name may represent a species group. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described as new to science by Christian Hendrik Persoon in 1800 and transferred to the genus Gliophorus in 1958. MushroomExpert.Com says it was originally described from France and lists Hygrophorus laetus and Hygrocybe laeta as synonyms. MushroomExpert.Com also cites a study (Lodge et al. 2013) of collections from Europe, Central America, eastern North America and western North America that showed potential phylogenetic differences, indicating that the name may represent a species group. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as growing scattered to gregariously in woods (especially in boggy, wet areas) or, in Europe, in grasslands and heaths.
