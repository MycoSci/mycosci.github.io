## Summary

Leratiomyces erythrocephalus, known as the red pouch fungus, is a species in the family Strophariaceae. It was first described in 1845 as Secotium erythrocephalum and was later transferred to Weraroa. It was given its current name in 2008. The one independent source available describes it as found in New Zealand. *[wikipedia]*
