## Summary

Otidea bufonia (Pers.) Boud., 1907, is an apothecial fungus in the family Otideaceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes the fruit body as a dark brown, deep cup, split down one side, up to 6 cm high and the same across. The same source says it can be told from other Otidea species by its narrow fusoid ascospores and by hyphae with striate resinous exudates in the medullary excipulum. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a rare European species that occurs singly or in small groups on soil in woodland, with the fruit body appearing from late summer to early autumn. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Belgium (including the Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden.
