## Summary

Lentinula raphanica (Murrill) Mata & R.H. Petersen, 2001 is a species in the family Omphalotaceae, with occurrence records from the United States, Brazil, Puerto Rico and several other countries in the Americas. Wikipedia describes the caps as convex, up to 7.5 cm wide, and pale with yellowish blotches, with a white spore print. It also describes a gill character and a smell reminiscent of radish or alliums, especially while drying, as the features that distinguish it from other Lentinula. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, William Alphonso Murrill described the material in 1943 as two species, Armillaria raphanica and Gymnopus alliaceus. Ron Petersen and J. L.
