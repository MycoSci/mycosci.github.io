## Summary

Gyromitra sphaerospora (Peck) Sacc. 1889 is a member of the Discinaceae (Pezizales), known in English as the Round-spored False Morel. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as uncommon but widely distributed in northern North America, with a cushion-shaped brown cap, a ribbed pinkish-to-purplish stem, and round spores that develop a prominent de Bary bubble at maturity. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously on moss-covered, well-rotted hardwood and conifer logs in late spring and early summer. It gives the range as northern North America from about the Great Lakes northward, and west to Alberta. GBIF's distribution entry for the species lists Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
