## Summary

Xerocomellus dryophilus (Thiers) N. Siegel, C.F. Schwarz & J.L. Frank (2014) is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, known in English as the oak-loving bolete. Wikipedia describes it as having a reddish cap up to 10 cm wide and yellowish pores that often bruise blue. iNaturalist, a derivative of the Wikipedia text and so not an independent source, calls it native to North America. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was formerly known as Boletus dryophilus and Xerocomus dryophilus, and was transferred to the new genus Xerocomellus in 2014. Wikipedia also states that the epithet dryophilus had previously been applied to a European species, now described as Xerocomellus redeuilhii. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species appears to occur only under Quercus agrifolia (coast live oak) and that it is commonly parasitized by Hypomyces chrysospermus. This is a single-source claim from one independent cluster. *[wikipedia]*
