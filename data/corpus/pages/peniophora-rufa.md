## Summary

Peniophora rufa (Fr.) Boidin, 1958 is a crust fungus in the family Peniophoraceae (Russulales); Stereum rufum is listed as a synonym and Red Tree Brain Fungus as a vernacular name. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as forming small, bright red, cushion-shaped resupinate fruiting bodies on dead, still bark-covered branches and trunks of aspens, each up to about 1 cm across and 2 mm high. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic and apparently sometimes pathogenic on the wood of aspens (quaking aspen and big-toothed aspen), and occasionally on the wood of other hardwoods, erupting through the still-attached bark. It gives the fruiting season as spring, summer and fall, and the distribution as fairly widespread where the host trees are present (northern and montane North America). The GBIF description record lists distribution entries for Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
