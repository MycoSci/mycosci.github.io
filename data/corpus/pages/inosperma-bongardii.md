## Summary

Inosperma bongardii (Weinm.) Matheny & Esteve-Rav. is an agaric fungus in the family Inocybaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a common, widespread species and says its fruit bodies are suspected to be toxic because they contain muscarine. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia says Johann Anton Weinmann originally described the species as an Agaricus in 1836. Lucien Quélet transferred it to Inocybe in 1872. A 2019 multigene phylogenetic study by Matheny and colleagues found that I. bongardii and its relatives in subgenus Inosperma were only distantly related to the other members of Inocybe. Inosperma was then raised to genus rank, and the species became Inosperma bongardii. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruit bodies grow on the ground, often in clay soils, and typically with broadleaf trees. It describes the species as common with a widespread distribution. *[wikipedia]*
