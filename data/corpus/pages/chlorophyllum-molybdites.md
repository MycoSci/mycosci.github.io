## Summary

Chlorophyllum molybdites (G. Mey.) Massee, 1898, is a large lawn-dwelling agaric in the family Agaricaceae, known as the green-spored parasol, false parasol and vomiter. Wikipedia describes it as poisonous, causing potentially serious vomiting and diarrhea, and as the most commonly consumed poisonous mushroom in North America. It is recorded from many countries, with the most records in the US. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it growing in meadows, lawns and parks across eastern North America and in temperate and subtropical regions elsewhere, with fruiting bodies generally appearing after summer and autumn rains. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered or gregariously in lawns and meadows, often in troops or fairy rings, summer through fall, and more common from the Great Plains eastward in North America. Mushroom World lists grassy areas, lawns, meadows and woodlands, often forming large fairy rings in summer and fall. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld]*
