## Summary

Inocybe hystrix (Fr.) P. Karst. 1879 is an agaric in the family Inocybaceae, originally described as Agaricus hystrix. It is described as a brown fungus with dense scales on both cap and stem, a combination the sources note is not often seen in the genus. Wikipedia states that it is poisonous and should not be consumed. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was first described in 1838 by Elias Fries as Agaricus hystrix, and that Petter Karsten transferred it to Inocybe in 1879. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as forming mycorrhiza with deciduous trees, with fruit bodies usually growing alone or in small groups on leaf litter in autumn. It states that in North America and Europe the species grows in deciduous forest, especially beech, and that in Costa Rica it is found in the Cordillera Talamanca in association with Quercus costaricensis at about 3,000 m. MushroomExpert.Com independently describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods, growing alone, scattered or gregariously in fall (and winter in California), and widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
