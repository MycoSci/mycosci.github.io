## Summary

Polyporus leprieurii Mont. (1840) is a poroid fungus in the family Polyporaceae, with its type locality in French Guiana. Wikipedia describes it as first described by the French mycologist Camille Montagne and as occurring in tropical to subtropical areas of America and Eastern Asia. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described scientifically by the French mycologist Camille Montagne. A 2021 PLoS ONE paper on rhizomorph-forming Polyporaceae in Yasuní National Park, Ecuador, described a new variety, Polyporus leprieurii var. yasuniensis. *[wikipedia, lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows on dead hardwood lying on the ground, or on hanging branches, in tropical to subtropical areas of America and Eastern Asia. A GBIF descriptions record (Spanish-language, with a distribution entry for Brazil) lists a gregarious habit and decomposing trunks or logs as substrate. A 1995 Mycotaxon survey of Polyporaceae from Ilha do Cardoso, Brazil, lists it as a new record for São Paulo State. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc, lit_3]*
