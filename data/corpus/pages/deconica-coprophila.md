## Summary

Deconica coprophila (Bull.) P. Karst. (1879) is a species in the family Strophariaceae, order Agaricales. Wikipedia lists the English common names dung-loving deconia, meadow muffin mushroom, dung-loving psilocybe and dung demon. The species is recorded across many countries, including the US, GB, MX, AU and ES. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus coprophilus by Jean Baptiste François Pierre Bulliard in 1793, and that Paul Kummer transferred it to Psilocybe in 1871. It also states that molecular studies in the first decade of the 2000s showed Psilocybe to be polyphyletic, and that the non-bluing (non-hallucinogenic) species were then transferred to Deconica. iNaturalist repeats this account and is a declared derivative of the Wikipedia text, so the two count as one source. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species grows on cattle dung in much of North America, generally from July to September, and from December to May on the West Coast. This is a single-source statement, and no supplied source gives habitat, substrate or season outside North America. *[wikipedia]*
