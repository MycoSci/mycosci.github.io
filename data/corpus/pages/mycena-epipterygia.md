## Summary

Mycena epipterygia (Scop.) Gray, 1821, is a species in the family Mycenaceae, known in English as the Yellowleg Bonnet. Wikipedia and Mushroom World describe it as a small mushroom with a sticky, striate cap and a yellowish to yellow-green stipe, which Wikipedia gives as an identifying feature. Wikipedia describes the mycelium as bioluminescent and the species as saprotrophic. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as common in Western Europe (naming the Netherlands and Belgium), growing on the ground in deciduous and coniferous woods, heather and acid grasslands, amongst grasses and mosses. It gives the British fruiting season as August to November. For the North American Pacific Northwest, Wikipedia says it appears in groups, in needle litter and on wood. Mushroom World independently describes it as growing in small troops on grasses, mosses and other debris in damp places, both in and out of woodlands, and lists its location as Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
