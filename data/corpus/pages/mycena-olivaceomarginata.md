## Summary

Mycena olivaceomarginata (Massee) Massee, 1893, is an agaric in the family Mycenaceae, known in English as the Brownedge Bonnet. Wikipedia reports that it is found in Europe and North America and that its mycelium is bioluminescent. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that it was originally described by the English mycologist George Edward Massee in 1890 as Agaricus olivaceomarginatus, that Massee transferred it to Mycena in 1893, and that it was described from Great Britain. This account comes from a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*
