## Summary

Rhizoctonia pseudocornigera (M.P. Christ.) Oberw., R. Bauer, Garnica & R. Kirschner (2013) is a fungus in the family Ceratobasidiaceae, order Cantharellales. The name is based on Ceratobasidium cornigerum var. pseudocornigerum. The only source available for this page is a database record of its type material and a short distribution note, so this page is deliberately minimal. *[gbif_desc]*
