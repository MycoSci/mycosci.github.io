## Summary

Agaricus bresadolanus Bohus (1969) is a species of fungus in the family Agaricaceae, described from Hungary. Wikipedia describes it as rare, recorded in Asia and southern Europe. Its spores are ellipsoid and lack a germ pore, measuring 5.5–7.5 by 4.0–5.0 μm. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

A 2023 Phytotaxa study of 23 specimens, combining morphology and ITS rDNA sequencing, found no diagnostic molecular characters separating A. bresadolanus, "A. infidus" (nom. inval.) and A. romagnesii. The authors concluded they are a single taxon whose correct name is A. bresadolanus, by priority over A. romagnesii. *[lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it fruits singly or in groups along paths and in grassy areas of deciduous woodland. *[wikipedia]*
