## Summary

Dermoloma cuneifolium (Fr.) Singer ex Bon is a species of fungus in the family Tricholomataceae and the type species of the genus Dermoloma. It was originally described as Agaricus cuneifolius by Fries in 1818 and transferred to Dermoloma by Bon in 1986. A description hosted at GBIF calls it probably the most common Dermoloma species in Europe. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a smallish, tricholoma-like mushroom with notched white gills, a white spore print, a mealy odor and a bald, gray-brown cap. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

In Europe, a description hosted at GBIF records it in semi-natural grasslands, dunes, forests, cemeteries and parks.
