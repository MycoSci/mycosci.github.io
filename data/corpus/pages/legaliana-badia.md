## Summary

Legaliana badia (Pers.) Van Vooren, 2020, is a cup fungus (apothecial ascomycete) in the family Pezizaceae, with the synonyms Peziza badia, Plicaria badia and Scodellina badia. Wikipedia describes it as one of the more familiar cup fungi of Europe, with irregular dark brown cups up to 8 cm wide and 2 cm tall, often in small groups. It is frequently observed in Europe, with scattered records from many other parts of the world. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it appearing from August to November on soil in woodland. It tends to be seen more frequently on sandy soils and favours bare ground, for example at the sides of paths. This description refers to Europe. *[wikipedia]*
