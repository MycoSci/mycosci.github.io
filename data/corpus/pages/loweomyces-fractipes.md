## Summary

Loweomyces fractipes (Berk. & M.A. Curtis) Jülich, 1982, is a poroid fungus in the family Steccherinaceae and the type species of the genus Loweomyces. Wikipedia describes it as widely distributed, reported from North America, Europe, Central America, South America and Korea. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the fungus was first described in 1872 by Miles Berkeley and Moses Ashley Curtis, from type specimens that the American botanist Henry William Ravenel had sent to Berkeley. It was later moved between several polypore genera. According to the same source, William Murrill placed it in Grifola in 1904. It then went to Abortiporus (Bondartsev, 1959), Heteroporus (Fidalgo, 1969) and Spongipellis (Kotlaba & Pouzar, 1976). In 1982 Walter Jülich transferred it to Loweomyces, which had been a subgenus of Spongipellis until Jülich raised it to genus rank. Wikipedia also lists Abortiporus tropicalis Murrill (1910), Polyporus delicatus Berk. & M.A.Curtis (1872) and Polyporus humilis Peck (1874) as heterotypic synonyms. All of this comes from a single encyclopedia source. *[wikipedia]*
