## Summary

Bovista plumbea Pers. (1795) is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae, with France as its type locality. Wikipedia describes it as a small puffball, white when young and greyish in age, with a fruiting body 1.5–3.5 cm broad that is attached to the substrate by a tuft of mycelium. Wikipedia gives its common names as tumbling puffball, tumbleball and paltry puffball. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia and Mushroom World both describe it as scattered to clustered in disturbed areas, especially in sparse grass; Mushroom World adds sparse grasslands and pastures. Both give its range as Western Europe and California. Wikipedia states that fruiting occurs throughout the mushroom season. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
