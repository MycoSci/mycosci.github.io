## Summary

Bondarzewia mesenterica (Schaeff.) Kreisel is a polypore fungus in the family Bondarzewiaceae (order Russulales), with the synonym Bondarzewia montana and the English vernacular name mountain polypore. Wikipedia describes a fruit body mass of overlapping, fan-shaped, tomentose caps up to 25 cm wide with brownish zones, growing from a shared base. It is recorded in GBIF from many countries across Europe, Asia and North America. *[wikipedia]*
