## Summary

Entoloma incarnatofuscescens (Britzelm.) Noordel., published in 1985 (Index of Fungi 5: 479), is a species in the family Entolomataceae (order Agaricales). It has synonyms in Agaricus, Leptonia, Omphaliopsis and Rhodophyllus, as well as Entoloma leptonipes. A 2007 taxonomic study of macrofungi collected in Babadağ District (Denizli), Turkey, in 2005-2006 presented it as one of five new records. *[lit_1]*
