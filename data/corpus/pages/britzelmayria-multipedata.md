## Summary

Britzelmayria multipedata (Peck) D. Wächter & A. Melzer is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, commonly known as the clustered brittlestem. Wikipedia describes it as a small brittlestem with a brown cap that is known for growing in dense clusters. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1905 by Charles Horton Peck as Psathyra multipedata. It was reclassified as Psathyrella multipedata in 1941 by Alexander H. Smith. In 2020 Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer moved it into the newly created genus Britzelmayria, as part of a reclassification of many Psathyrellaceae species based on phylogenetic analysis. Wikipedia adds that many field guides and websites still use the name Psathyrella multipedata. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, states that the species is found on soil among grass and in open grassy spaces within woodland. It describes the species as saprotrophic, growing on buried fallen trees from late summer to autumn. Wikipedia calls it widespread and found occasionally, with observations most common in the UK, western Europe and the East Coast of the United States. *[wikipedia]*
