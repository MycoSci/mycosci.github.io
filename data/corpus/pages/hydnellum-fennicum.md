## Summary

Hydnellum fennicum (P. Karst.) E. Larss., K.H. Larss. & Kõljalg is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae. Karsten first described it in 1882 as a variety of Sarcodon scabrosus and promoted it to species in 1887. Wikipedia describes a yellowish-brown, powdery-scaly cap with downward-pointing teeth that darken from whitish to purplish-brown, and says it is considered critically endangered in Switzerland. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Petter Karsten described it in 1882 as Sarcodon scabrosus var. fennicus, and promoted it to a distinct species in 1887 (Wikipedia). The GBIF record lists the 1882 variety as the basionym. It gives the type as not indicated, with a neotype in H designated by Maas Geesteranus & Nannfeldt in 1969. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as native to northern Europe and distributed primarily in continental Norway and eastwards. It says the fungus forms ectomycorrhizae with pine. The same source says it is considered critically endangered in Switzerland. The supplied sources give no substrate detail or fruiting season. *[wikipedia]*
