## Summary

Lindtneria chordulata (D.P. Rogers) Hjortstam, 1987, is a fungus in the family Stephanosporaceae (Agaricales), published in Index of Fungi 5: 596. A 1997 Mycotaxon paper on corticioid fungi from Colombia collected by Leif Ryvarden reports it for the first time from South America.
