## Summary

Conocybe velutipes (Velen.) Hauskn. & Svrček 1999 is a small brown gilled mushroom in the family Bolbitiaceae. It has a conic cap 1–2 cm across, a fragile stem and cinnamon-brown mature gills. Microscopic examination is probably needed to identify it with confidence (MushroomExpert.Com). *[mushroomexpert, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies lists the basionym as Galera velutipes Velen., published in Novitates mycologicae in 1939. It gives the combination in Conocybe as Hauskn. & Svrček, published in Czech Mycology 51(1): 68 (1999). Conocybe kuehneriana Singer (1969) is listed as a heterotypic synonym, and MushroomExpert.Com also calls it a synonym. *[wikispecies, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered or gregariously in leaf litter in woods or in disturbed soil in urban settings, from late spring through fall. The same source says its North American distribution is uncertain. It was previously reported from Massachusetts and California in online herbarium records, and the collection illustrated and described there is from Illinois. GBIF's description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
