## Summary

Dacrymyces capitatus Schwein. (1832) is a jelly fungus in the family Dacrymycetaceae, originally described from Pennsylvania. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small, orange to yellow jelly fungus with a fruiting body 2–6 mm across, cushion-shaped above a fairly clearly defined, whitish, stem-like structure that roots into the substrate. It is recorded from many countries. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing gregariously on the wood of hardwoods, from spring through fall. The same source says it is widely distributed in North America but apparently absent or uncommon in the southwestern United States, and is also found in Central America, South America, Europe, Oceania, and Asia. *[mushroomexpert]*
