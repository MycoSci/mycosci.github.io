## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Hygrophorus marzuolus as a mycorrhizal fungus, with associations noted with Abies alba and sometimes with Picea excelsa and Pinus sylvestris. Fruit bodies grow singly to scattered, or sometimes in clusters, on the forest floor in duff or in moss. It fruits under conifers, often fir and Engelmann spruce, typically at high elevations, and is described as a snowbank mushroom associated with melting snow, with fruiting initiated under snowbanks. Wikipedia records it from the mountains of Idaho, Montana, Oregon, Washington, northern Arizona and northern California. In Europe it is recorded from the French Pyrenees to the region of Prague, and in Asia it has been reported from Pakistan. All of this rests on a single source cluster. *[wikipedia]*
