## Summary

Homophron spadiceum (P. Kumm.) Örstadius & E. Larss. is a species of agaric fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, commonly called the chestnut brittlestem. Wikipedia also gives Psathyrella spadicea as a name for it, reflecting a 2015 move into the recreated genus Homophron. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Rolf Singer transferred the species to the genus Psathyrella in 1951, where it was placed in the section Spadiceae. In 2015 Örstadius & Larsson recreated the genus Homophron, a name used at sub-genus level since 1883, for a group of psathyrelloid mushrooms with no veil and light-coloured spores, and the species was moved into it. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a derivative of it). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states the species can be found near the base of hardwood trees. It reports the species from the Pacific Northwest, northern California and the east of North America, and also from Europe. The GBIF record lists a distribution of Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. No supplied source gives a season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
