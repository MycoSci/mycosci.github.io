## Summary

Rhodonia placenta (Fr.) Niemelä, K.H. Larss. & Schigel (2005) is a crust fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a brown rot species that grows on decaying conifer wood in China, Europe and North America. Its fruiting body is described as effused, poroid, fairly thick, juicy and soft, with a pale rose or white colouring. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described in the genus Polyporus by Elias Magnus Fries in 1861 and has since been moved through several polypore genera, including Oligoporus and Postia. According to the same source, molecular analysis showed it to be phylogenetically distant from those genera, in a separate clade near Antrodia. Wikipedia also lists an extensive synonymy, including Poria incarnata (Persoon, 1794), Bjerkandera roseomaculata (Karsten, 1891), Physisporus albolilacinus (1892), Poria monticola (Murrill), Poria carnicolor (Baxter, 1941) and Poria microspora (Overholts, 1943). *[wikipedia]*
