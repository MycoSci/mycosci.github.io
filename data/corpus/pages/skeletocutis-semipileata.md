## Summary

Skeletocutis semipileata (Peck) Miettinen & A. Korhonen (2018) is a polypore in the family Incrustoporiaceae, also known by the vernacular name Hazel Bracket and formerly placed in Polyporus and Tyromyces. The one supplied source describes the basidiocarps as annual, resupinate to half-resupinate, and hard when dry, with 8–9 pores per mm. It also states that the species is almost impossible to tell apart morphologically from several other species in the S. nivea complex, apart from S. (aff.) futilis. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The supplied source (a GBIF description record) gives the range as temperate holarctic, extending to the south-boreal zone at least in Fennoscandia. It states that the species grows on various angiosperm species, often on thin fallen branches but sometimes on coarse wood. Its list of examined specimens gives hosts including Corylus avellana, Prunus padus, Betula sp., Ulmus glabra, Tilia americana and Fagus grandifolia, from Finland, Norway, Poland, Russia, the United Kingdom and the U.S.A. *[gbif_desc]*
