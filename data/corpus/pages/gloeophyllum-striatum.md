## Summary

Gloeophyllum striatum (Fr.) Murrill, 1905, is a species in the family Gloeophyllaceae. A single GBIF-hosted description source characterises it as having a lamellate to daedaleoid pileate hymenophore, causing brown rot, and being pantropical, and it is recorded as widespread in Brazil. That source notes one examined specimen with a light-orange pileus, whereas the cited reference (Ryvarden 2015) describes the pileus as dark brown. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The source reports growth on deciduous wood (a Brazilian record from Maranhão). Habitat entries in the same source list Quercus humboldtii forest ("Bosque Quercus humboldtii"), and substrate entries list dead wood and a burnt trunk. The source also records a specimen from Honda, Tolima, Colombia. It gives no season. *[gbif_desc]*
