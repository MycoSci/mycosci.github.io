## Summary

Clavaria falcata Pers. (1794) is a species in the family Clavariaceae. A GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Brussels-Capital and Flemish regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. A 2026 paper on western North American Clavaria species with ornamented spores discusses a "Clavaria falcata complex" among two pale species that can produce ornamented spores. *[gbif_desc, lit_2]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the original publication as Persoon, Neues Magazin für die Botanik 1: 118 (1794). A 1954 paper on generic names proposed for conservation in the Hymenomycetes lists Holocoryne (Fr.) Bonord. as typified by Clavaria falcata Pers. ex Fr. *[wikispecies, lit_1]*
