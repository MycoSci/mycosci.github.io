## Summary

Byssoporia terrestris (Pers.) M.J. Larsen & Zak (1978) is a corticioid fungus in the family Albatrellaceae, order Russulales. It is the only species in the monotypic genus Byssoporia and is described as widespread. The sources supplied here contain little descriptive text beyond taxonomy and regional records. *[wikipedia]*
