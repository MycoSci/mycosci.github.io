## Summary

Oxyporus populinus (Schumach.) Donk, 1933, is a fungus in the family Oxyporaceae, known in English as the poplar bracket. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen that affects trees, and as typically white, more or less semicircular and 2.5–20 cm wide. GBIF distribution records list Belgium, Brazil, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
