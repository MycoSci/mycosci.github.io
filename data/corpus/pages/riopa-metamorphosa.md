## Summary

Riopa metamorphosa (Fuckel) Miettinen & Spirin (2016) is a species of Phanerochaetaceae (Polyporales). The only descriptive source available describes it as a resupinate polypore with white, cream or straw-coloured fruiting bodies that are fragile when dry, and with rounded-angular pores that split and become sinuous. The same source says it has an orange conidial anamorph, known as Sporotrichum aurantiacum, which may or may not be present. *[gbif_desc]*
