## Summary

Tyromyces galactinus (Berk.) Bondartsev ex J. Lowe (1975) is a poroid fungus in the family Incrustoporiaceae, order Polyporales. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen that causes a white rot in broad-leaved trees, and says it is found in North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described by Miles Joseph Berkeley in 1847, from a type collected near Waynesville, Ohio. Wikipedia says Appollinaris Semenovich Bondartsev placed it in Tyromyces in 1953, but that name was invalid because it did not conform to the rules for naming species. Josiah Lincoln Lowe then transferred it to Tyromyces validly in 1975. This account comes from a single independent source cluster. *[wikipedia]*
