## Summary

Aleurodiscus amorphus (Pers.) J. Schröt. (1888) is a crust fungus in the family Stereaceae, order Russulales. A 2007 Angewandte Chemie study by P. Spiteller and B. L. J. Kindler describes its fruiting bodies as only pinhead-sized and reports that injured fruiting bodies release hydrocyanic acid by an oxidative mechanism, presented as a chemical defense that deters predators. GBIF's description record lists its distribution as Denmark, Norway and Vermont. *[lit_1, lit_3, gbif_desc]*
