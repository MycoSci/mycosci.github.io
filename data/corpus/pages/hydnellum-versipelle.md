## Summary

Hydnellum versipelle (Fr.) E. Larss., K.H. Larss. & Kõljalg (2019) is a species of tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae. The basionym is Hydnum versipelle, described by Elias Fries in 1861. Wikipedia states that the species is found in Europe and North America; the GBIF description record lists distribution only as "Global; NO; SE". *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Elias Fries described the species in 1861 as Hydnum versipelle, in Öfversigt af Kongl. Svenska Vetenskaps-Akademiens Förhandlingar 18(1): 31. No type was indicated in the original publication. A neotype was later designated by Maas Geesteranus & Nannfeldt (1969): Sweden, Uppland, Danmark parish, collected by J. Eriksson & H. Nilsson, UPS F-013958. Wikipedia reports that Taisiya Lvovna Nikolayeva transferred the species to Sarcodon in 1961, and that Hydnum crassum, published by Kenneth A. Harrison in 1961, is a synonym. The GBIF record gives the combination in Hydnellum as a comb. nov. by Larsson, Larsson & Kõljalg. The sources supplied do not say why the species was moved between genera. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
