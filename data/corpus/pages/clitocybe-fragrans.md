## Summary

Clitocybe fragrans (With.) P. Kumm. is a small white agaric in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as the Fragrant Clitocybe or Fragrant Funnel. Wikipedia describes it as having a cap 1–5 cm across and a fragrant, aniseed-like odour. Mushroom World gives a similar account of the anise-like fragrance. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it grows throughout Europe in broadleaf woodland from August to December, and on North America's West Coast and in the Pacific Northwest under conifers from October to February. Mushroom World, a separate source, describes it as typically found in coniferous and mixed woodlands on nutrient-poor, acidic soils, growing in groups in late autumn to early winter. The two sources differ on the European woodland type. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
