## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic and possibly parasitic. It grows in clusters (rarely alone or scattered) on the wood of hardwoods in summer and fall, and is fairly widely distributed in North America. Wikipedia, a single source for the following details, says it is commonly found in late summer in the Great Lakes states, the Pacific Northwest and eastern North America, on the bark of hardwood trees. It says the species is rarely found in Europe, with the first Polish specimen found in 2010 at the Łężczok Nature Reserve. In the Great Lakes region it is said to decay logs of Acer saccharum and Tilia glabra. The GBIF descriptions record lists distribution entries for Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
