## Discovery and naming

The genus Butyrea was described as new by Miettinen in a 2014 revision of the polyphyletic polypore genera Antrodiella, Junghuhnia and Steccherinum, based on phylogenetic and morphological evidence. That paper also selected a lectotype for Physisporus flavicans P. Karst., which it equates with Butyrea luteoalba. Wikipedia lists Junghuhnia luteoalba (P. Karst.) Ryvarden, 1972 as a synonym. *[lit_1, wikipedia]*
