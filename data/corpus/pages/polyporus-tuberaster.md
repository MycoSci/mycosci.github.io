## Summary

Polyporus tuberaster (Jacq. ex Pers.) Fr. (1821) is a species in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as growing from a large sclerotium that can resemble buried wood or a potato, with a yellow-brown cap 4–15 cm wide that ranges from convex to flat and even funnel-shaped, and whitish stalks that can grow upwards of 10 cm high. The spores and spore print are described there as white. *[wikipedia]*
