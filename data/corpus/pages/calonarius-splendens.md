## Summary

Calonarius splendens (Rob. Henry) Niskanen & Liimat. is a species of fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, commonly known as the splendid webcap. It is recorded in many countries, mostly in Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was described in 1939 by the mycologist Robert Henry as Cortinarius splendens. In 2022 it was transferred to Calonarius, which Wikipedia says was based on genomic data. Wikipedia also notes it has been classed as conspecific with Cortinarius meinhardii. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says the species is native to Europe and associates it with European beech (Fagus sylvatica). It notes that Cortinarius meinhardii, with which it has been classed as conspecific, is associated with Norway spruce (Picea abies).
