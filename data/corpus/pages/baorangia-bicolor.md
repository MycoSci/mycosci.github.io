## Summary

Baorangia bicolor (Kuntze) G. Wu, Halling & Zhu L. Yang (2015) is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, known in English as the two-colored bolete or red and yellow bolete. Wikipedia and Mushroom World describe a medium to large bolete with a red cap and yellow pores that stains blue when injured. It is recorded from eastern North America and from China and Nepal. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the original name, Boletus bicolor, was derived from the Latin bōlētus, meaning "mushroom", and bicolor, meaning "having two colors". *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia says the names two-colored bolete and red and yellow bolete refer to the mushroom's two-tone coloring scheme. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia gives the distribution as southeastern Canada and the Great Lakes region, primarily east of the Rocky Mountains, south to the Florida peninsula and west to Wisconsin, with additional occurrences in China and Nepal. It describes the species as commonly found in deciduous woodland, usually under or close to broad-leaved trees, especially oak, growing singly or in groups or clusters, primarily from June through October. Mushroom World independently says it typically grows in hardwood forests, especially near oaks. The season is a single-source statement (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
