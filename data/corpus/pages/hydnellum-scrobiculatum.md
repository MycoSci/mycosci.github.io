## Summary

Hydnellum scrobiculatum (Fr.) P. Karst. (1879) is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, known in English as the ridged tooth. Wikipedia describes it as widely distributed in the Northern Hemisphere, found in Eurasia and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described as a species of Hydnum by Elias Magnus Fries in his 1815 work Observationes mycologicae, and Petter Karsten transferred it to Hydnellum in 1879. The same source says Kenneth A. Harrison published the variety H. scrobiculatum var. zonatum as a new combination in 1961, and that this variety is now considered synonymous with Hydnellum concrescens. This account comes from a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species fruits singly, in clusters, or in fused groups in both coniferous and mixed forests, and that it is found in Europe, Asia and North America. The same source states that in the United Kingdom it is considered vulnerable under the biodiversity action plan for stiped hydnoid fungi.
