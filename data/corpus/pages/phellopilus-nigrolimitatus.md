## Summary

Phellopilus nigrolimitatus (Romell) Niemelä, T. Wagner & M. Fisch. is a fungus in the family Hymenochaetaceae, order Hymenochaetales. It is the only species in the genus Phellopilus, which was circumscribed in 2001, and Wikipedia describes it as widespread. In a nuc-lsu rDNA phylogenetic study published in Mycologia, it was found to be closely related to Basidioradulum radula. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*
