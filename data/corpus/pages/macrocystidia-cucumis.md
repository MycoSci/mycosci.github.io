## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says it is often found in large numbers on needle litter or moist soil, and also in forests, disturbed ground and gardens, with wood debris as its preferred substrate. It says the species was originally described in Europe, where it is most common, and is also known from North America, Australia and New Zealand. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic and terrestrial, growing alone, gregariously or in troops in woods, disturbed ground, grassy areas and gardens, in summer and fall (or winter in warm climates).
