## Summary

Volvariella hypopithys (Fr.) Shaffer, 1957 is placed in the Pluteaceae (Agaricales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small white mushroom with a dry, silky cap 2–3.5 cm across, brownish-pink gills at maturity, a finely fuzzy stem without a ring, and a thick white sack-like volva at the base. GBIF holds occurrence records for it from many countries. *[mushroomexpert]*
