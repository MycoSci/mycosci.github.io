## Summary

Laetiporus conifericola Burds. & Banik (2001) is a polypore in the family Laetiporaceae, described from Alaska. Wikipedia describes it as growing as a plant pathogen on conifers in western North America, from California to Alaska. The same source notes that some people have reported gastrointestinal upset after eating it. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was described as new to science in 2001 by Harold H. Burdsall and Mark T. Banik, and that the type collection was made on the Kenai Peninsula, Alaska, in October 1999. It also states that, until it was confirmed as a distinct species, it was frequently misidentified as the eastern North American L. sulphureus, which grows on hardwoods. *[wikipedia]*
