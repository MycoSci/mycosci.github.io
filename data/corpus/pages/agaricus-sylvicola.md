## Summary

Agaricus sylvicola (Vittad.) Peck, 1872, is a species in the family Agaricaceae, commonly called the wood mushroom. Wikipedia describes it as related to the button mushroom and as a member of Agaricus section Arvenses. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the taxon was first described as the variety Agaricus campestris var. silvicola by Carlo Vittadini in 1832 and was later promoted to species rank by Charles Horton Peck. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows in both deciduous and coniferous woodland in Europe (including Britain) and North America, and has also been reported in South Asia (Bangladesh and Pakistan). It says the mushroom appears in autumn and is rarely seen in large numbers, usually a few or solitary. Mushroom World describes it differently, as growing in trooping groups on soil in coniferous woods, favouring spruce. GBIF description records list distribution entries for Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Madeira Island and Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
