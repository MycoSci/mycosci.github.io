MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as mycorrhizal, usually with pines (Pinus species). It grows scattered or gregariously in sand dunes in late summer and fall, on the East Coast, the Gulf Coast, and along the Great Lakes. The stems are often nearly entirely submerged in the sand.
