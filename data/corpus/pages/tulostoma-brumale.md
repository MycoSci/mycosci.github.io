## Summary

Tulostoma brumale Pers. (1794) is a species in the family Agaricaceae, known in English as the Winter Stalk Puffball. The one descriptive source supplied characterizes it by a membranous exoperidium, a circular-tubular mouth usually surrounded by a brown peristome, and abrupt widenings of the capillitial septa. That source separates it from Tulostoma simulans by exoperidial features, slightly smaller spores, and irregular crystals adhering to the capillitial walls. *[gbif_desc]*
