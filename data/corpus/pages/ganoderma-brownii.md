## Summary

Ganoderma brownii (Murrill) Gilb., 1962, is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae, with Elfvingia brownii and Fomes brownii listed as synonyms. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a brown polypore that appears to be limited to the West Coast of North America and grows on hardwood. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen and occasional saprotroph. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo, 2024) describes it as saprobic and sometimes parasitic, causing a white rot. It grows alone or in groups on the living or dead wood of hardwoods. It is perennial, found year-round, and distributed along the West Coast from California to British Columbia. Wikipedia states that in the San Francisco Bay Area it is very common on Umbellularia californica. It also says the fungus parasitizes both conifers and hardwoods, with a preference for hardwoods, which is a wider host range than the hardwood-only statement in MushroomExpert.Com. Wikipedia places it in the Pacific Northwest, primarily California. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
