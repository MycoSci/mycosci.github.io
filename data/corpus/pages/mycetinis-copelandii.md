## Summary

Mycetinis copelandii (Peck) A.W. Wilson & Desjardin (2005) is placed in the Omphalotaceae, with GBIF occurrences recorded in the US, Mexico, Brazil, Canada, India and Colombia. The one available description gives small but robust fruiting bodies, with a pileus mostly 8–24 mm broad and a stipe mostly 25–65 mm long. The same description reports an odor strongly of garlic but ephemeral, and a taste strongly alliaceous that persists in herbarium specimens. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The diagnosis in the single available source gives a fruiting preference on dead Lithocarpus leaves and a distribution in northern California. The specimens it lists were collected in Santa Cruz and San Mateo counties, California. The same record's distribution field reads only "Vermont", which does not agree with the northern California distribution in the diagnosis. *[gbif_desc]*
