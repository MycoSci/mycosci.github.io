## Summary

Tapinella panuoides (Fr.) E.-J. Gilbert, 1931, is a species in the family Tapinellaceae (order Boletales), known in English as oyster rollrim. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as lacking a stem or having only a stubby lateral attachment, with orangish gills that become crimped or corrugated near the base. Wikipedia gives caps up to 10 cm across and notes that the gills appear crimped, forked or crosshatched close to the base. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the name 'fan pax' comes from the species' former binomial, Paxillus panuoides. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on the deadwood of conifers (logs, sticks, stumps) and on lumber such as picnic tables and fenceposts. It gives the season as summer and fall, also over winter in warmer climates, and says the species is widely distributed in North America, with its illustrated collections from California. Wikipedia says it grows on conifer wood and in lignin-rich humus. Mushroom World says it grows solitarily or in clusters on conifer debris and lists its location as Europe and North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
