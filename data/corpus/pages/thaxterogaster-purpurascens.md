## Summary

Thaxterogaster purpurascens (Fr.) Niskanen & Liimat. is a mushroom-producing fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, commonly known as the bruising webcap. It was transferred to Thaxterogaster from Cortinarius in 2022 on the basis of genomic data. Records in the site catalog come from many countries across Europe, North America and Asia. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus purpurascens by Elias Magnus Fries in 1818. It was also placed in the segregate genus Phlegmacium by Ricken as P. purpurascens. In 2022 it was transferred from Cortinarius to Thaxterogaster based on genomic data. The same study reclassified three of its variants as the distinct species Thaxterogaster eumarginatus, T. occidentalis and T. subpurpurascens. This account rests on a single source cluster (Wikipedia, with iNaturalist as a derivative). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruit bodies grow in groups in coniferous forests, mainly on more acid soils, and describes the species as quite rare. It gives the distribution as the temperate zone of the Northern Hemisphere, with collections from Europe and North America.
