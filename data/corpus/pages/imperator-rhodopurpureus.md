## Summary

Imperator rhodopurpureus, the oldrose bolete, is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, transferred to the genus Imperator in 2015 from Boletus rhodopurpureus. It is recorded across many European countries. Wikipedia describes it as critically endangered in the Czech Republic and endangered in the United Kingdom, and very rare throughout. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states it was initially described as Boletus rhodopurpureus and transferred to the new genus Imperator in 2015. It also reports that a predominantly yellow form from Windsor Great Park in the UK had previously been identified as Boletus xanthocyaneus, until DNA studies found it to be a rare colour morph of I. rhodopurpureus. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it is found under deciduous trees including oak and beech in neutral soils. In the UK it is described as favouring old open woodland or parkland with plenty of sunlight on neutral soils, reported mainly from the south of England, such as parts of the New Forest and Windsor Great Park. Most UK reports are from Berkshire and Hampshire. Up to a hundred fruiting bodies have been recorded at a few sites. All of this comes from a single source (Wikipedia; iNaturalist copies its text). *[wikipedia]*
