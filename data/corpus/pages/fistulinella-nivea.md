## Summary

Fistulinella nivea (G. Stev.) Singer is a bolete in the family Boletaceae. The English Wikipedia article describes it as found in New Zealand, with a white cap up to 3 cm across and a pore surface that is white at first and changes to pale pink. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Greta Stevenson first described the species in 1962 as a member of Tylopilus. Rolf Singer later transferred it to Fistulinella. Stevenson had originally found the bolete in 1955 at Tōtaranui. The source does not give her reasons for the placement or Singer's reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
