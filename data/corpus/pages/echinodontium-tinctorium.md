## Summary

Echinodontium tinctorium (Ellis & Everh.) Ellis & Everh., 1900, is a fungus in the family Echinodontiaceae, known as the Indian paint fungus or toothed conk. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen with fruiting bodies 8–20 cm wide, identifiable by the grayish spines on its lower surface, and with reddish-brown, woody flesh. *[wikipedia]*

## Cultural and culinary history

Wikipedia reports that Native Americans used the red interior as a pigment, and that some Plateau Indian tribes applied the fungus to skin to prevent chapping. This is a single-source claim; the iNaturalist entry is a declared derivative of Wikipedia. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
