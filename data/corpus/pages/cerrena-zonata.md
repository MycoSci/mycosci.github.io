## Summary

Cerrena zonata (Berk.) H.S. Yuan is a species in the family Cerrenaceae (order Polyporales), recorded across Asia, Oceania, Europe, Africa and the Americas. Wikipedia describes it as a poroid white rot fungus. Two recent primary papers (2025, 2026) studied its wood-decay enzymes and its oxidation of model compounds in laboratory culture. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described by Miles Joseph Berkeley in 1854 as Irpex zonatus. Leif Ryvarden moved it to Antrodiella in 1992, and it was later transferred to Cerrena. This account comes from a single source cluster (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist entry only copies it). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Cerrena zonata as a white rot fungus growing on dead hardwoods. It lists Asian records from India to Thailand, Vietnam, China, Far East Russia and Japan, and also records from New Zealand, Australia and Argentina. In the GBIF specimen data for Yunnan Province, China, collections are labelled as on the trunk or stump of angiosperms, with one on a living angiosperm tree. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
