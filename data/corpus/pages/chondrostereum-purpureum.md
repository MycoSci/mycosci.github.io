## Summary

Chondrostereum purpureum (Pers.) Pouzar, 1959, is placed in the family Cyphellaceae (order Agaricales). Wikipedia describes it as a fungal plant pathogen that causes silver leaf disease of trees, attacking most species of the rose family Rosaceae, particularly the genus Prunus. The same source reports that it is commercially available as a mycoherbicide against forest 'weed trees'. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the common name (silver leaf) is taken from the progressive silvering of leaves on affected branches. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia notes that the name Stereum purpureum Pers. was widely used for this fungus in the past, but that under modern taxonomy it is only distantly related to Stereum. It belongs to the order Agaricales, whereas Stereum is in the order Russulales. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (a single independent source) describes it as a pathogen of various, mostly deciduous trees, including species of Acer, Aesculus, Alnus, Betula, Crataegus, Fagus, Larix, Malus, Ostrya, Picea, Populus, Prunus, Salix and Sorbus. It is often found on old stumps and dead wood but can also be a serious parasite of living trees. It occasionally infects conifers. The same source says its geographic spread is roughly as wide as that of its hosts, and that it is common in woods, orchards and tree plantations in temperate climates. *[wikipedia]*
