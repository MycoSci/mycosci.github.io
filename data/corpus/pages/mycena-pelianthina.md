## Summary

Mycena pelianthina (Fr.) Quél. (1872) is a species in the family Mycenaceae, order Agaricales, with the English vernacular name Blackedge Bonnet. Index Fungorum lists Prunulus pelianthinus as the current name, so the name is disputed in the spine. The GBIF description record lists it from Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Madeira and Vermont. Two red pyrroloquinoline alkaloids, pelianthinarubins A and B, have been isolated from its fruiting bodies. *[gbif_desc, lit_1]*
