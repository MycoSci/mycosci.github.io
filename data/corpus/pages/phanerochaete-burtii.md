## Summary

Phanerochaete burtii (Romell ex Burt) Parmasto, 1967, is a species of fungus in the family Phanerochaetaceae (order Polyporales). Wikipedia describes it as a thin, resupinate crust fungus with a smooth, pale orange to greyish-orange surface that becomes strongly cracked, exposing a white interior, with loosely attached hyphal strands along the margins. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that in 2008 Nakasone concluded that samples of Corticium lloydii represent the same species, with the correct modern name being Phanerochaete burtii. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
