## Summary

Ceraceomyces tessulatus (Cooke) Jülich 1972 is a member of the family Amylocorticiaceae. GBIF's descriptions record its distribution as Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. Wikipedia reports that a cultured strain known in Japan as Echigoshirayukidake (Basidiomycetes-X, or BDM-X) was identified as this species by ITS and D1/D2 rDNA sequence comparison, with 99% similarity to a C. tessulatus voucher. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Cultivation

Wikipedia describes the BDM-X strain as cultured on agar, liquid or sawdust media, with optimum growth at pH 5.0 to 6.0 and 22 to 26 °C. In culture it forms light pink colonies and hyphal masses (sclerotia) and does not form basidia. The hyphal masses are described as varying in shape with the culture environment. Large masses are reported on sawdust medium after one year. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia, with iNaturalist as a declared copy) and concerns the BDM-X strain. *[wikipedia]*
