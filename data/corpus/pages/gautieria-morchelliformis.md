## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was first described scientifically by the Italian Carlo Vittadini in 1831. It also states that three varieties have been described: var. globispora and var. stenospora by Albert Pilát in 1958, and var. microspora by Evžen Wichanský in 1962. It adds that none of these are considered to have independent taxonomical significance. Wikispecies records the original publication as Monographia Tuberacearum (Milano), p. 26 (1831), with the epithet spelled 'morchellaeformis' there. It also records that a lectotype (Tab. III, fig. VI G-H of Vittadini, 1831) was designated by Vidal et al. in Persoonia 50 (2023). *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*
