## Summary

Clitocybe vibecina (Fr.) Quél. 1872 is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, recorded from many countries, mainly in Europe. Wikipedia describes it as a common mushroom that often grows in rings on needle litter, usually late in the year. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the Latin vibicina (as spelled there) means "with weals or welts (vibices)" and would seem to describe the slightly raised striations of the cap. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it often grows in rings on needle litter, usually late in the year. The GBIF description record lists only distribution entries: Belgium, Flemish Region, DK, NO, Madeira Island, SE and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
