## Summary

Rigidoporus ulmarius (Sowerby) Imazeki, 1952, is a member of the family Meripilaceae, known in English as the Giant Elm Bracket. Wikipedia describes it as a fungal plant pathogen found mainly on broad-leaved trees, with elm considered particularly susceptible. A fruit body found at Kew Gardens, London, in 2003 was for a time the largest known fungal fruit body, measuring 150 by 133 centimetres. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen found mainly on broad-leaved trees, with elm considered particularly susceptible. It states that the fungus grows at the base of the trunks of deciduous trees, usually elm, that it is perennial, and that its season is all year. Wikipedia also states it is found in Europe.
