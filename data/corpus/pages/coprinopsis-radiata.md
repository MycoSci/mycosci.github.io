## Summary

Coprinopsis radiata (Bolton) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae, formerly known as Coprinus radiatus and, according to Wikipedia, commonly called the miniature woolly inky cap. It is described as a coprophilous fungus that grows on herbivore dung, and it has been used as a model organism to study basidiospore formation. GBIF's description record lists a distribution that includes Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally published in 1788 as Agaricus radiatus by the English naturalist James Bolton. Samuel Frederick Gray transferred it to Coprinus in his 1821 work A Natural Arrangement of British Plants. In 2001, following a reorganization of the genus Coprinus, it was moved to the newly created genus Coprinopsis. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
