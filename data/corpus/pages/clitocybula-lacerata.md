## Summary

Clitocybula lacerata (Scop.) Métrod, 1952, is a species in the family Marasmiaceae (order Agaricales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a collybioid mushroom with a streaked, gray-brown cap 1.5–4 cm across, white gills that are often cross-veined, a white spore print, and weakly amyloid spores. That description is a single-source account, based on collections from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com, the only source with descriptive text, describes this species as saprobic. It grows alone, scattered, gregariously, or in loose clusters on the wood of conifers and hardwoods, though it is often attached to buried wood and so appears terrestrial. The same source gives summer and fall as the season and says the species was originally described from what is now Slovenia. It calls the species fairly widely distributed in Europe and North America, and its illustrated and described collections are from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*
