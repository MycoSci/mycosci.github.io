## Summary

Geastrum minimum, the tiny earthstar, is a species of earthstar in the family Geastraceae, described by Schweinitz in 1822 with a type locality in North Carolina. Wikipedia describes the fruit body as opening into a star of 6–11 rays, 1–3 cm across, around a spherical or egg-shaped spore sac. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
