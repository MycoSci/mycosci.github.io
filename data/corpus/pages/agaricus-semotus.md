## Summary

Agaricus semotus Fr. (1863) is a species in the family Agaricaceae, with a type locality in Sweden. Wikipedia describes it as a woodland mushroom with a cap 2–7 cm across. A 2021 laboratory study purified a lectin from its fruiting bodies and tested it against Culex quinquefasciatus mosquito larvae. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology
