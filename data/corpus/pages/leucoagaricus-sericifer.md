## Summary

Leucoagaricus sericifer (Locq.) Vellinga, 2000, is a fungus in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia describes it as widespread in Europe. The current name is disputed: Index Fungorum and Wikipedia treat it as Leucocoprinus sericifer, following a 2024 reclassification. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described as Pseudobaeospora sericifera in 1952 by the French mycologist Marcel Locquin. Else Vellinga reclassified it as Leucoagaricus sericifer in 2000, and it was reclassified again as Leucocoprinus sericifer in 2024. The source gives no account of the type material or the reasons for the transfers. *[wikipedia]*
