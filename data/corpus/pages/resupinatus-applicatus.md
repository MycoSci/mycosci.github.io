## Summary

Resupinatus applicatus (Batsch) Gray, 1821, is a species in the family Pleurotaceae (order Agaricales), with the English vernacular name Smoked Oysterling. It is the type species of the genus Resupinatus. Its fruit bodies are described as small, stemless and cup-like to convex, grayish-blue to grayish-black, with a firm but gelatinous flesh. *[wikipedia]*
