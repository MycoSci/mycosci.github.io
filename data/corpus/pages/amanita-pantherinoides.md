## Summary

Amanita pantherinoides (Murrill) Murrill, 1912, known as the western panther or western American false panther, is a mushroom in the family Amanitaceae. Wikipedia describes it as poisonous, containing ibotenic acid and muscimol. Wikipedia places it in western North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that A. pantherinoides was long thought to be the same species as Amanita pantherina, but is now considered distinct. A. pantherina is reported from Europe and western Asia, while A. pantherinoides is reported from western North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes A. pantherinoides as mycorrhizal, growing under conifers, especially Douglas fir. It is usually found in forests but occasionally in cities. The same source places the species in western North America. No season or substrate is stated. *[wikipedia]*
