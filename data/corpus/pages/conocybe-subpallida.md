## Summary

Conocybe subpallida is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Bolbitiaceae, described by Manfred Enderle in 1991 with Germany as its type locality. Wikipedia's description gives a cap 3.5–5 cm wide that is very pale ochre or light cream with a darker brown centre, cinnamon-coloured gills, and 4-spored basidia. GBIF records it from a number of European countries. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was described in 1991 by the mycologist Manfred Enderle. It also reports that Conocybe subalpina was described in 1992 by Rolf Singer and Anton Hausknecht as a reclassification of Singer's 1989 Conocybe mesospora var. subalpina. That name is now treated as a synonym, because Everhardus Johannes Maria Arnolds reclassified the taxon as Conocybe subpallida var. subalpina in 2003. This account comes from a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to Wikipedia, the specimens Enderle studied were growing in a cow pasture, between the remains of old grass, wood and cow manure. Others were found at the side of roads by forests and in grass under trees.
