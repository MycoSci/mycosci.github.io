## Summary

Haploporus odorus (Sommerf.) Bondartsev & Singer (1944) is a fungus in the family Polyporaceae, recorded under the English names Aniseed Polypore and Diamond Willow Fungus. Wikipedia states that it is the type and, at the time of circumscription, only species of the genus Haploporus. It was first described as Polyporus odorus by Sommerfelt in 1826, and Wikispecies gives Norway as the type locality. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Polyporus odorus Sommerf., Suppl. Fl. lapp. (Oslo): 275 (1826), with the type locality recorded as "In Salix woodland: Norway". It was later placed in Trametes as Trametes odora (Sommerf.) Fr. (1838) and in Fomitopsis as Fomitopsis odora (P. Karst., 1881). Fomitopsis odoratissima Bondartsev (1950) is listed as a heterotypic synonym. The combination Haploporus odorus was published by Bondartsev & Singer in Singer, Mycologia 36(1): 68 (1944). Wikipedia states that the genus Haploporus was circumscribed in 1944 by Bondartsev and Singer with H. odorus as the type. A GBIF note records that Haploporus amarus, described from north-east China in 1993, was studied at the type level and found to agree morphologically with H. odorus, and it is listed there as a synonym. None of the supplied sources say why the successive transfers were made. *[wikispecies, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
