## Summary

Sowerbyella rhenana (Fuckel) J. Moravec, 1986 is a cup fungus in the family Pyronemataceae (order Pezizales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small cup fungus with a well-developed stem and a smooth, bright orange upper surface. GBIF's description record lists distribution entries for Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com notes that the species is often treated as an Aleuria ("Aleuria rhenana"), because it shares features with that genus, including bright colors and ornamented spores. It reports that Moravec (1986) argued the species was better placed in Sowerbyella, and that DNA evidence (Perry and collaborators, 2007) supports this placement. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single-source account), the species is probably mycorrhizal (citing Tedersoo et al. 2010). It grows gregariously or in loose clusters on the ground, usually in conifer woods. Fruiting is in summer and fall, or over winter on the West Coast. It is described as apparently widely distributed in North America but more common in the west, and the illustrated and described collections are from California and Michigan. *[mushroomexpert]*
