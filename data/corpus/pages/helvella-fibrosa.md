Helvella fibrosa (Wallr.) Korf, 2008, is an ascomycete in the family Helvellaceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes a small fruiting body with a cap 5–20 mm across on a stalk 10–35 mm tall. GBIF distribution records list Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Octospora villosa by the German botanist Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Wallroth in 1833. It was later placed in several genera, including Peziza, Fuckelina and Cyathipodia. Richard P. Korf proposed the current placement in Helvella in 2008. *[wikipedia]*
