## Summary

Lyophyllum decastes (Fr.) Singer is a gilled fungus in the family Lyophyllaceae, known as the clustered domecap in the United Kingdom and the fried chicken mushroom in North America. It forms dense clusters on the ground. MushroomExpert.Com cautions that the name may cover several species and that L. decastes is not as easily identified as field guides suggest, and Wikipedia notes that L. fumosum and L. loricatum are sometimes grouped with it as a species complex. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described from Sweden. *[wikipedia]*

## Cultivation

Wikipedia reports that the species is commercially cultivated in Japan, where it is known as hatake shimeji, and in China, where it is known as luronggu, and that it is grown on sawdust or bark compost. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Both Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com describe growth in dense, often huge clusters on the ground, usually where the ground has been disturbed (roadbeds, paths, landscaping areas; Wikipedia also lists gravel), and sometimes in woods. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic and says it occasionally grows alone or scattered, while Wikipedia says it is variously considered saprotrophic or ectomycorrhizal. MushroomExpert.Com gives the season as summer and fall (fall through spring on the West Coast) and describes it as widely distributed in North America. Wikipedia adds that it is widespread throughout Europe and north temperate regions and also occurs in eastern temperate Asia. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
