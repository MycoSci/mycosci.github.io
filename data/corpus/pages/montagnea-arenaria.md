## Summary

Montagnea arenaria (DC.) Zeller, 1943, is a secotioid fungus in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia describes it as commonly found in desert habitats and as characterized by a cap with an apical disc, radial gills, a hymenophore, and spores with a prominent germ pore. It is also known as the Desert Inkcap or gasteroid coprinus, although it is not classified as an inkcap. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the genus Montagnea is named for the mycologist C. Montagne. No source supplied discusses the derivation of the epithet arenaria. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that the species was originally named Agaricus arenarius by Augustin Pyramus de Candolle in 1815, and was transferred to the genus Montagnea by Sanford Myron Zeller in 1943. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species has been sampled in dry areas on every continent except Antarctica, and that it favors hot, desert and xeric terrain. It has also been found in temperate regions in dried pastures. This is a single-source description, and Wikipedia notes that the full range is not yet known. *[wikipedia]*
