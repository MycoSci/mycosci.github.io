## Summary

Conocybe mesospora Kühner ex Watling (1980) is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Bolbitiaceae, with a type locality in France. Wikipedia describes a small fruit body with a rust-brown to chestnut-brown, hygrophanous cap 0.9–2.5 cm wide. GBIF holds 899 occurrence records across many countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the French mycologist Robert Kühner described the species as Conocybe mesospora in 1935, but that the classification was invalid. The Scottish mycologist Roy Watling described it again in 1980. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, reports it in woods, meadows and fields, especially along paths in ditches and on the margins of fields. It also reports it in dry places among grass and shrubs. For Austria it gives a season of May until October, with a peak during September. *[wikipedia]*
