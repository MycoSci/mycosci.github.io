## Summary

Coprinellus deliquescens (Bull.) P. Karst. is a mushroom in the family Psathyrellaceae, commonly called the woodland inkcap. According to Wikipedia, it is the type species of its genus, was first described as Agaricus deliquescens in 1790 by Bulliard, and was transferred to Coprinellus by Petter Karsten in 1879. GBIF holds 107 occurrence records, from countries including Denmark, Sweden, Spain, France, the United Kingdom and the United States. *[wikipedia]*
