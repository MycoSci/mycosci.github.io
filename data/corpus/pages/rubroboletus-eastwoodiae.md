## Summary

Rubroboletus eastwoodiae (Murrill) G. Vasquez, Simonini, Svetash., Mikšík & Vizzini is a bolete in the family Boletaceae. Wikipedia describes a convex, dry, olive-colored cap 6–25 cm wide that turns pinkish with age, on a stalk 7–15 cm tall, with flesh that turns blue when cut and an olive-brown spore print. It is recorded from the West Coast of the United States. *[wikipedia]*
