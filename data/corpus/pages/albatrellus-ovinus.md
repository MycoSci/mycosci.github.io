## Summary

Albatrellus ovinus (Schaeff.) Kotl. & Pouzar (1957) is a species in the family Albatrellaceae, known in English as Sheep Polypore or Forest Lamb. Despite the common name, Wikipedia says it is not phylogenetically related to the shelf fungi (Polyporales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a ground-dwelling polypore that is mycorrhizal with conifers. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Cultural and culinary history

Wikipedia and iNaturalist (a declared derivative of Wikipedia, so one source) state that it is sold commercially in Finland. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers in a wide variety of ecosystems: under Engelmann spruce and subalpine fir in the Rocky Mountains, under Norway spruce and other conifers in the Appalachians, and under various firs, pines and spruces in northern and northeastern regions. It is usually gregarious, sometimes in dense clusters, and fruits in summer and fall, also over winter in coastal California, where manzanita is reported as a host along with conifers. Wikipedia reports it from northern Europe and, in North America, from the Pacific Northwest to Northern California, the Mountain states, the Great Lakes area, the Appalachians and the Northeast. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
