## Summary

Coprinellus truncorum (Scop.) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo (2001) is a species of mushroom-forming fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It belongs to the cluster of mushrooms morphologically related to Coprinellus micaceus. Wikipedia says it can be distinguished from C. micaceus by a smooth rather than pruinose (powdery) stipe and by more elliptical spores, and that it may be conspecific with C. micaceus, although this has not been conclusively proven. *[wikipedia]*
