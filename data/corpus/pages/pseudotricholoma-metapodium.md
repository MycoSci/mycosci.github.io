## Summary

Pseudotricholoma metapodium (Fr.) Sánchez-García & Matheny is a gilled mushroom in the family Tricholomataceae, with the recommended English name mealy meadowcap. Wikipedia describes its distribution as European, mainly in agriculturally unimproved grassland. It is assessed as globally "endangered" on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species because its habitat is under threat. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Elias Magnus Fries first described the species in 1818 as Agaricus metapodius. Rolf Singer transferred it to Porpoloma in 1973. Later molecular research, based on cladistic analysis of DNA sequences, found Porpoloma to be polyphyletic, with species belonging either to Porpoloma sensu stricto or to the new genus Pseudotricholoma. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as rare but widespread in northern and central Europe. It occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns) and is described as typical of waxcap grasslands, a habitat declining because of changing agricultural practices. No supplied source gives a fruiting season or host associations. *[wikipedia]*
