## Summary

Sparassis crispa (Wulfen) Fr., 1821, is a fungus in the family Sparassidaceae, known in English as cauliflower mushroom and wood cauliflower. Wikipedia describes it as an entangled globe up to 24 cm across, with flat, wavy lobes that are white to creamy yellow. GBIF holds occurrence records from many countries in Europe, North America and Asia. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only supplied source with habitat text, says the species is fairly common in Great Britain and temperate Europe (but not in the boreal zone), fruiting from July to November. It describes it as a brown rot fungus growing parasitically at the base of conifer trunks, often pines but also spruce, cedar, larch and others. GBIF's distribution note lists Belgium (including the Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
