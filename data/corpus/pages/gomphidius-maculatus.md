## Summary

Gomphidius maculatus (Scop.) Fr. is a mushroom in the family Gomphidiaceae (order Boletales), known in English as the larch spike; Wikipedia calls it the larch slime spike. Wikipedia describes a cap 2–9 cm across that is glutinous when damp, a species that stains black when damaged, and a stem with a bright yellow base. This description rests on a single independent source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet maculatus is derived from the Latin word for "spotted". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described scientifically by the naturalist Giovanni Antonio Scopoli in 1772. Elias Magnus Fries transferred it to the genus Gomphidius in 1838, giving it its current name. Wikipedia gives the type locality as Carniola, a historical region that now comprises parts of present-day Slovenia. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that Gomphidius maculatus forms mycorrhizal associations with larch trees. It describes the species as widely distributed in the northern hemisphere, in Europe and North America, and as introduced in New Zealand.
