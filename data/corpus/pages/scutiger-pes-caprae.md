## Summary

Scutiger pes-caprae (Pers.) Bondartsev & Singer 1941, known in English as the goat's foot polypore, is a species of fungus in the family Albatrellaceae, order Russulales. Wikipedia describes it as distributed in North America and Europe and associated with conifers and rotting wood. GBIF's description record lists its distribution as Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Christian Hendrik Persoon first described the species officially as a Polyporus in 1818. It was most commonly known as an Albatrellus in recent decades, until molecular research published in 2010 by Canadian mycologist Serge Audet showed it was more appropriate in an emended version of the genus Scutiger. iNaturalist repeats this account and is a declared derivative of Wikipedia, so it is not an independent source. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, states that it is found in western North America, under conifers and on rotting wood, from August to February. It also says the species is somewhat more common in the south than Albatrellus ellisii. No source supplied gives habitat detail for Europe. *[wikipedia]*
