## Summary

Lactarius chrysorrheus Fr. (1838) is a milkcap in the family Russulaceae. Wikipedia describes it as pale salmon with a white milk that turns bright sulphur yellow within seconds of exposure to air, and as poisonous and mycorrhizal with oak. MushroomExpert.Com describes the North American form as having a pale pinkish cap and a smooth stem without potholes. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet is derived from the Ancient Greek words chryso- "golden" and rheos "stream". *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports that it appears in summer and autumn and is mycorrhizal with oak in Britain. It says the species is frequent in the northern temperate zones, including Europe, North America and North Africa. MushroomExpert.Com describes the North American version as mycorrhizal with oaks from the Great Plains eastward, and possibly with other hardwoods. It gives the season as summer and fall and says the species is probably to be expected throughout the oak forests of eastern North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
