## Summary

Athelia bombacina (Link) Pers. (1822) is a species in the family Atheliaceae, order Atheliales. The supplied literature is agricultural rather than descriptive. A 2018 report identified it as the cause of postharvest fruit rot on pear in cold storage in Hebei Province, China, and a 2000 apple-orchard study used it as a known antagonist of the apple scab pathogen Venturia inaequalis. *[lit_1, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

A 2018 report isolated the fungus from diseased pear fruit during cold storage at several locations in Hebei Province, China, where disease incidence reached 10% and sometimes up to 20%. A 2024 study examined its interaction with 'Huangguan' pears. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark and Norway.
