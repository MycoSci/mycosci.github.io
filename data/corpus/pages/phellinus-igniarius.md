Phellinus igniarius (L.) Quél. (1886) is a fungus in the family Hymenochaetaceae, with a legitimate name in Index Fungorum. Wikipedia describes it as forming perennial, woody-hard bracket fruiting bodies and causing a white rot in its host tree. It is recorded in GBIF occurrences from many countries across Europe, North America and Asia. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruiting bodies rise from the bark of an infested living tree or from a dead log. It says the host is often willow but that the fungus may commonly be found on birch, alder and other broad-leaved trees. It also notes that woodpeckers are known to favour the site of the fungus for excavating nesting chambers, because the wood around it is softer and weaker.
