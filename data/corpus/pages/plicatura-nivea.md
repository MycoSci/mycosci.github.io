## Discovery and naming

A 1970 study (Canadian Journal of Botany) found differences in both basidiocarps and cultures between Plicatura nivea (≡ Merulius niveus Fr.) and Merulius tremellosus Fr., the type species of Merulius. On that basis P. nivea was excluded from Merulius. After a study of type specimens, P. alni Peck, M. rimosus Berk. and, for the first time, Radulum cuneatum Lloyd were placed in synonymy with P. nivea. *[lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology
