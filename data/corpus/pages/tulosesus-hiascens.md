## Discovery and naming

It was first described as Agaricus hiascens by Elias Magnus Fries in 1821. Following a 2001 phylogenetic study that reorganized the genus, it was transferred to Coprinellus, and it was known as Coprinellus hiascens until 2020. In that year Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many Psathyrellaceae species on the basis of phylogenetic analysis, giving the current name. *[wikipedia]*
