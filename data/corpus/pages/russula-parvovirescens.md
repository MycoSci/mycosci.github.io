## Summary

Russula parvovirescens Buyck, D. Mitch. & Parrent (2006) is a species of Russula in the family Russulaceae, with a type locality in North Carolina. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com describe it as a green to blue-green-capped species whose cap surface cracks into a quilted or crustose pattern, recorded from eastern North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes R. parvovirescens as recently separated from the traditional species Russula virescens, and says it was named in 2006. It differs from the classic concept of R. virescens in its blue-green colors, larger crustose patches, lined cap margin, generally smaller stature, and its pileipellis. Kuo also notes that illustrations of "Russula virescens" in several North American field guides published before the 2006 naming appear to represent R. parvovirescens. Wikipedia likewise says it was described as new to science in 2006. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single-source account) describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, and apparently with conifers. It grows alone, scattered, or gregariously in summer and fall, and is apparently widely distributed from Texas to Maine. Wikipedia states that it appears in the eastern United States from June to September. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
