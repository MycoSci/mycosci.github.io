## Summary

Amanita crocea, the saffron ringless amanita, is a ringless species of Amanita (Amanitaceae) that Wikipedia describes as widely distributed in Europe. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both describe an orange cap, a stem with pale orange fibrils and no ring, and a sacklike membranous volva at the base. Kuo notes that North American material is currently placed under this European name. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it as infrequent, occurring between July and October, mycorrhizal with hardwoods, particularly birch and beech in clearings. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods (especially oaks and birch), growing alone or scattered in summer and fall. Kuo's illustrated collections are from Illinois and Michigan, and he notes it is widely distributed in North America only as a species group and infrequently recorded there. Wikipedia also notes a report from Iran. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
