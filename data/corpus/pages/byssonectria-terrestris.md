## Summary

Byssonectria terrestris (Alb. & Schwein.) Pfister, 1994, is an ascomycete in the family Pyronemataceae (order Pezizales). GBIF's distribution description lists Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. A 2012 paper in Gayana. Botánica adds it as a new record for the Chilean mycobiota and describes and illustrates its diagnostic characters, and a 2012 Ascomycete.org paper lists it among rare or noteworthy Pezizales collected in 2011. A 2019 survey of Mycetophagidae (Coleoptera) in Almaty Region, Kazakhstan, records the beetle Typhaea stercorea on it. *[gbif_desc, lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*
