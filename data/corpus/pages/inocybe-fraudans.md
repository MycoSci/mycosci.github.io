## Summary

Inocybe fraudans (Britzelm.) Sacc. (1887), the pear fibrecap, is a species of the family Inocybaceae. Sources describe a whitish to yellowish-brown, fibrillose cap that bruises reddish and has a strong, distinctive odor. MushroomExpert.Com treats it as likely a group of closely related species rather than a single one. It is recorded in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that many authors have erroneously referred this species to Inocybe pyriodora. MushroomExpert.Com likewise notes that Inocybe pyriodora, in the sense of many North American authors, is a synonym of Inocybe fraudans. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as presumably mycorrhizal, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in diverse ecosystems, from high-elevation conifer forests to low-elevation oak-hickory forests. It fruits in summer and fall and is widely distributed in North America. The collections he illustrates and describes are from Illinois and Colorado. Wikipedia states that it grows in woodlands in Europe and North America.
