## Summary

Conocybe semiglobata Kühner & Watling (1980) is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae, with its type locality in France; the English vernacular name in the catalog is Roundhead Conecap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small brown-spored Conocybe that is recognized mainly through microscopic features, especially its large spores, cheilocystidia and caulocystidia. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (single source) describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on the ground in grassy areas and in woods, in summer and fall. It is described there as widespread in Europe, with North American distribution uncertain (most reports from the western United States and western Canada, with occasional reports east of the Rocky Mountains) and also reported in western Asia; the collection illustrated on that page is from Colorado. The GBIF description record lists distribution for Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
