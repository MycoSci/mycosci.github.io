## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single source), it is saprobic on the deadwood of hardwoods, usually recently fallen with the bark still adnate, and sometimes appears on living hardwood trees. It is annual and causes a white rot of the sapwood. It fruits from spring through fall, or over winter in warm spells or in warm climates. The same source calls it widespread in North America and Europe, and also recorded from the Caribbean, South America, Asia, Africa and Oceania. A GBIF description record lists distribution as DK; NO; Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
