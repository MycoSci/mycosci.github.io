## Summary

Agaricus moelleri Wasser (1976) is a species in the family Agaricaceae, with its type locality in Denmark. Wikipedia describes it as a large Agaricus with a greyish-brown scaly cap that yellows when bruised and an ink-like or iodoform smell. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that British authors for many years wrongly applied the name Agaricus placomyces to this species. That epithet was given by Peck in 1878 to a North American species. The current binomial was published in 1976 by the Ukrainian mycologist Solomon P. Wasser. *[wikipedia]*

## Cultural and culinary history

A 2019 interview-based case study in the State of Mexico (lit_1) named Agaricus moelleri as one of five fungi with potential to produce good-intensity colours for dyeing wool. The abstract describes this as potential use, not established practice. *[lit_1]*
