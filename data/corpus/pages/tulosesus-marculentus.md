## Summary

Tulosesus marculentus (Britzelm.) D. Wächter & A. Melzer is a mushroom-producing fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was first described as Coprinus marculentus by the German mycologist Max Britzelmayr in 1893. It was later moved to Coprinellus and, in 2020, to Tulosesus. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Max Britzelmayr first described the species as Coprinus marculentus in 1893. In 2001 a phylogenetic study reorganized that genus, and the species was transferred to Coprinellus. It was known as Coprinellus marculentus until 2020, when the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many Psathyrellaceae species on the basis of phylogenetic analysis.
