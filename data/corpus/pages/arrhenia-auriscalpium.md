## Habitat and ecology

Described as an arctic-alpine species with a circumpolar distribution (lit_1); in North America it is characterised as arctic-alpine (lit_2). In Colorado it was collected several times at high mountain localities at elevations of 3,350-3,670 m, the highest elevation yet recorded for the fungus, at a latitude of about 39° N (lit_1). A 2008 survey of the Rocky Mountain alpine zone (3,000-4,000 m) states that latitudes of 36-38° N likely include the southernmost extent of the species in the Northern Hemisphere (lit_3).
