## Summary

Laccaria pumila Fayod (1893) is a species in the family Hydnangiaceae, published in Saccardo's Sylloge fungorum XVII. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tiny Laccaria with a small, orangish brown cap that is often strongly lined, and a tiny stem with white basal mycelium. That source says it can be separated from visually near-identical Laccaria species only by microscopy, because it is the only species in that group with 2-spored basidia. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as mycorrhizal with willows, birches, and conifers, found under various high-elevation willows, birches, and conifers in arctic, boreal, and montane ecosystems of western and northern North America. It is reported growing scattered or gregariously, often in moss, in summer and fall. *[mushroomexpert]*
