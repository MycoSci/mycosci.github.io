## Summary

Coprinopsis picacea (Bull.) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo is a species of fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, commonly called the magpie inkcap, magpie fungus or magpie mushroom. Wikipedia describes a cap that is egg-shaped at first, then bell-shaped up to 8 cm wide, with white to grayish or cream velum remnants as flakes over a dark brown to black surface. Wikipedia also states that the species is inedible and causes digestive upset. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia and iNaturalist (which derives from the Wikipedia text and counts as one source) state that the species was first described in 1785 by the French mycologist Jean Baptiste François Pierre Bulliard as Agaricus picaceus. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species is common in Europe and Australia and also exists in North America, where it is uncommon. In Europe it is given as ranging from Great Britain and France in the west to Poland, Hungary and Romania in the east, south to Spain and the Balearic Islands, Italy and Greece, and north to Germany and Denmark. GBIF's description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Flemish, Walloon and Brussels-Capital regions), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. No supplied source gives a substrate, host or season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
