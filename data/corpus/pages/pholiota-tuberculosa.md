## Summary

Pholiota tuberculosa (Schaeff.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a species in the family Strophariaceae. Index Fungorum gives its current name as Pleuroflammula tuberculosa, so its placement differs between databases. In a 2008 culture study of conidiogenesis in Strophariaceae, it showed a mode of conidiogenesis that deviated from the two prevalent modes described in that study. GBIF holds occurrence records for it from many countries, including Sweden, France and the United Kingdom. *[lit_1]*
