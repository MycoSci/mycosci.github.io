## Summary

Muscinupta laevis (Fr.) Redhead, Lücking & Lawrey, 2009, is a fungus in the order Hymenochaetales, known in English as Minute Mossmaid. It is the only species in the monotypic genus Muscinupta, which Wikipedia describes as producing small white delicate fan-shaped to cupulate fruitbodies on mosses. GBIF's distribution list for the species includes Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia says the genus name Muscinupta refers both to its moss host and to an allusion to the marriage of the fungus with the moss, together with its veil-like properties on the moss. This is a single-source statement about the genus name; no supplied source discusses the species epithet. *[wikipedia]*
