## Summary

Russula compacta Frost (1879) is a species in the family Russulaceae, with its type locality in New York. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a sturdy Russula that bruises reddish brown on all parts, with a cap that starts almost white and discolors to reddish brown shades with age. The same source reports a characteristically foul odor that strengthens as the mushroom matures, and says it is found east of the Rocky Mountains. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (single source) describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods, and with conifers in northern areas. It grows scattered or gregariously in summer and fall, east of the Rocky Mountains, with a range extending as far south as Costa Rica. *[mushroomexpert]*
