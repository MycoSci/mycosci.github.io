## Summary

Hydnellum scabrosum (Fr.) E. Larss., K.H. Larss. & Kõljalg is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, also called bitter tooth or bitter hedgehog. It has a brown, scaly cap with spines rather than gills underneath. Wikipedia, the only source that gives a verdict, describes the flesh as bitter and calls the mushroom inedible. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Elias Fries originally described the species as Hydnum scabrosum in 1836. Petter Karsten moved it to Sarcodon in 1881, and it remained Sarcodon scabrosus until 2019. In 2019 a molecular analysis using nuclear DNA showed that this species and 11 others lay genetically within Hydnellum. The GBIF record for the 2019 combination gives the type as not indicated, with a neotype from Sweden (Småland, Femsjö, S. Lundell, UPS F-013954) designated by Maas Geesteranus & Nannfeldt in 1969. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says it is found in association with hardwood forests across Eurasia to Japan, and in North America. Mushroom World says it typically grows in coniferous forests, often forming symbiotic relationships with trees, and lists Europe, North America and Asia (Japan). The two sources disagree on forest type. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
