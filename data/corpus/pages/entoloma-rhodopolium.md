## Summary

Entoloma rhodopolium (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a member of the family Entolomataceae, commonly known as the wood pinkgill. Wikipedia describes it as a poisonous mushroom found in Eurasia. Its taxonomy is described as unclear, and it is uncertain whether North American mushrooms called by this name are the same species. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the European species was originally named by Elias Fries, and quotes Noordeloos (1981) that there is 'great need for neotypification of this classical Friesian species'. Wikipedia reports that Entoloma nidorosum, previously considered a separate species, is now classified as a variety of this fungus. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the Entoloma rhodopolium species group, not the European species specifically, as saprobic. It grows alone, scattered, or gregariously under hardwoods in late summer and fall (also over winter in California), and is widely distributed in North America. The collections it describes are from Illinois, Kentucky, and Missouri. Wikipedia says the species is found in Eurasia and is associated with specimens found in North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
