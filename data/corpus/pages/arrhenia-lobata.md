## Summary

Arrhenia lobata (Pers.) Kühner & Lamoure ex Redhead (1984) is an agaric in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes it as associated with mosses, possibly parasitically, and as occurring in the Iberian Peninsula, central Europe and North America.
