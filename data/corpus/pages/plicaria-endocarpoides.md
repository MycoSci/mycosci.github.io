## Summary

Plicaria endocarpoides (Berk.) Rifai (1968) is an apothecial fungus in the family Pyronemataceae. Wikipedia describes it as forming dark brownish cups up to 6 cm in diameter, usually in groups, which usually flatten with age. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (including the Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Madeira Island. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
