## Summary

Cortinarius evernius (Fr.) Fr. is a species of fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, known in English as the Silky Webcap. Wikipedia describes a cap 3–9 cm wide, conical when young then umbonate, reddish to violet-brown and possibly silky, with violet gills that turn brown and a violet-tinted stalk bearing whitish zig-zag veil remnants. GBIF's distribution listing names Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
