## Summary

Phaeoclavulina abietina (Pers.) Giachini, published in 2011, is a coral fungus in the family Gomphaceae, commonly called the green-staining coral or greening coral. Wikipedia describes it as characterized by a green staining reaction that develops when the fruit body is bruised or injured. It is recorded across Europe and North America, and the GBIF description record lists Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by Christian Hendrik Persoon in 1794 as Clavaria abietina. It was later placed in the genus Ramaria (subgenus Echinoramaria), until molecular phylogenetic work showed that Ramaria was polyphyletic. This account comes from a single independent source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that fruit bodies grow scattered or in groups, sometimes arranged in rows, on the ground in the duff of coniferous forests. For North America it reports the species from the United States, including the Pacific Northwest, and from Mexico, with fruiting mostly October–February on the West Coast and July–October inland. It also reports the species from Europe. The GBIF description record gives a distribution of DK, NO, SE and Vermont.
