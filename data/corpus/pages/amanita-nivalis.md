## Summary

Amanita nivalis Grev. is a species in the family Amanitaceae, listed in the catalog under the English names Mountain Grisette and Snowbed Amanita. English Wikipedia describes it as a small species, usually white when young and tinged grey or buff with age, that grows in subarctic and alpine conditions in Europe and Greenland. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

English Wikipedia states that Greville gave the epithet nivalis ("of the snow") to indicate the alpine type habitat in which he found it growing, rather than to describe its white colour. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to English Wikipedia, the Scottish mycologist Robert Kaye Greville described the species from specimens found growing at high altitudes in the Scottish Highlands. The publication year given in that source differs from the year in the verified record, so no year is stated here. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

English Wikipedia (a single source) states that the species grows in subarctic and alpine conditions in Europe and in Greenland, and is often associated with low-growing willows (Salix spp.) and birches (Betula spp.). It says the fruit bodies appear singly or in small groups, in summer, in both silicaceous and limestone areas. It also mentions reports of occurrence at high altitudes in the Rocky Mountains of North America. *[wikipedia]*
