## Summary

Hypomyces luteovirens (Fr.) Tul. & C. Tul. (1860) is a parasitic fungus in the family Hypocreaceae. It forms a yellow-green, mold-like covering on Russula mushrooms, and sometimes on Lactarius. Wikipedia gives "green langoustine" as a common name. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as parasitic on species of Russula, especially the purple and red species, fruiting in summer and fall, or over winter in warm climates. It is described as widespread in Europe and North America, and also reported from India and South Korea. The collections described and illustrated there are from Kentucky, Ohio, and Pennsylvania. Wikipedia likewise says it parasitises Russula, especially purple and red ones, and sometimes grows on Lactarius. Wikipedia also says it rarely covers the host cap and typically covers only the gills and upper stipe. MushroomExpert.Com says it usually covers only the upper stem and gills, and sometimes the cap surface. The fungus starts white or yellow, then turns light green and eventually dark green with age (Wikipedia). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as bright yellow-green, darkening to olive green or greenish brown with age. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
