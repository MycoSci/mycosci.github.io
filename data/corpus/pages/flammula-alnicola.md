## Summary

Flammula alnicola (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a species of mushroom in the family Hymenogastraceae, also known as Pholiota alnicola. Wikipedia describes the cap as about 3-8 cm across and gold or yellow, darkening with age, with gills that start pale yellowish and become reddish or orangish brown, and a brown spore print. The catalog records it from many countries, mainly in Europe, with some records from North America and Asia. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Flammula alnicola as a saprophytic fungus growing on rotting wood. It says the species usually grows on alder wood, sometimes on other hardwoods, and occasionally on conifer wood, and that it is found in North America, Asia and Europe. This is a single-source account; the iNaturalist entry only repeats Wikipedia. *[wikipedia]*
