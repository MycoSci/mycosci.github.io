## Summary

Coprinellus xanthothrix is a species of fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was first described as Coprinus xanthothrix by the French mycologist Henri Romagnesi in 1941 and transferred to the genus Coprinellus in 2001. A strain isolated from a polyphenol-polluted site in Greece was shown to have the enzymes laccase and manganese peroxidase and to decolorize the dye R-478. *[wikipedia]*
