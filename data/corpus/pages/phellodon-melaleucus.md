## Summary

Phellodon melaleucus (Sw. ex Fr.) P. Karst. (1881) is a tooth fungus, known in English as the grey tooth, placed in the family Thelephoraceae in the site catalog. Wikipedia describes it as widely distributed in Europe and North America and as an ectomycorrhizal associate of a wide range of host trees. Wikipedia also states that it is considered vulnerable in Switzerland, uncommon in the United Kingdom and very rare in Ireland. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described by Elias Magnus Fries in 1815 as a species of Hydnum. In 1881 Petter Karsten included it as one of the three original species in his newly circumscribed genus Phellodon.
