## Summary

Entoloma conferendum (Britzelm.) Noordel. is a species of agaric (gilled mushroom) in the family Entolomataceae, known in the UK by the recommended English name Star Pinkgill. Wikipedia describes it as widespread in Europe, occurring mainly in agriculturally unimproved grassland, and reports it from North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the recommended UK English name Star Pinkgill is based on the species' distinctive basidiospores. It describes the spores as multi-angled and stellate under a microscope. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by Max Britzelmayr in 1881 as Agaricus conferendus. Pier Andrea Saccardo transferred it to the genus Nolanea in 1887. When Nolanea was synonymized with Entoloma, Machiel Noordeloos transferred it to Entoloma in 1980. For many years it was known as Nolanea staurospora, now considered a later synonym. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as common and widespread in Europe. It says the species typically occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns), but may sometimes occur in woodland and bogs. It also says the species has been reported from North America. This habitat account comes from a single independent source cluster (Wikipedia); no season or host substrate is stated. *[wikipedia]*
