## Summary

Hygrocybe chlorophana (Fr.) Wünsche, the golden waxcap, is a gilled mushroom in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes a largely north temperate distribution: grassland in Europe, and woodland in North America and northern Asia. It also reports the species from mountainous areas of southern Australia. In a few European countries it appears on national red lists of threatened fungi. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia gives the specific epithet as coming from Greek χλωρός (pale green) + φαίνω (I appear). It adds that it is not clear why Fries chose this epithet, since he described the fruit bodies as "flavus" (Latin, yellow). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Per Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1821 by Elias Magnus Fries as Agaricus chlorophanus, from specimens collected in Sweden. In 1877 Friedrich Otto Wünsche moved it to the genus Hygrocybe. Wikipedia also states that recent DNA-sequence (cladistic) research places it within the core group of Hygrocybe sensu stricto. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that in Europe the species grows in old, unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns), and that in North America and Asia it grows in woodland. It typically produces fruit bodies in the autumn. Wikipedia also reports it collected from the alpine areas of Mount Wellington in Tasmania, Australia. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a copy of it). *[wikipedia]*
