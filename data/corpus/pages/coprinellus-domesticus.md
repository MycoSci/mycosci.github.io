## Summary

Coprinellus domesticus (Bolton) Vilgalys, Hopple & Jacq. Johnson is a species of mushroom in the family Psathyrellaceae, known in English as the firerug inkcap. It was first described as Agaricus domesticus by James Bolton in 1788 and transferred to Coprinellus in 2001. When present, a bright orange mat of hyphae called an ozonium around the fruiting bodies is a conspicuous feature, though it is not always present. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the name 'firerug inkcap' comes from the bright orange carpet of hyphae (an ozonium) that grows around the mushroom. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus domesticus by James Bolton in 1788, was later known as Coprinus domesticus, and was transferred to Coprinellus in 2001. MushroomExpert.Com adds that David Arora's Mushrooms Demystified (1986) applied the name Coprinus domesticus to what Michael Kuo considers probably a different mushroom. Kuo says that mushroom has slightly larger spores and less prominent veil remnants, and apparently lacks the ozonium. Arora himself noted confusion in the literature and called his use of the name tentative. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both describe it as saprobic, growing gregariously or in small clusters (occasionally alone) on decaying hardwood logs. Wikipedia says it is present worldwide and, in North America, grows on hardwood stumps, logs and debris from May to September in the East and from November to April near the West Coast. MushroomExpert.Com gives summer and fall (or over winter on the West Coast) and describes it as widely distributed in North America. MushroomExpert.Com also says it sometimes colonizes damp indoor substrates such as basement rafters or bathroom carpets, and that the ozonium may be less prominent or absent in indoor fruitings; Kuo notes this is based only on emailed reports and is not scientific evidence. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
