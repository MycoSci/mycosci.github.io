## Summary

Exidia cartilaginea S. Lundell & Neuhoff (1935) is a species in the family Auriculariaceae (order Auriculariales). English Wikipedia treats it under Exidia candida as the variety cartilaginea, and describes it as bicoloured, whitish and ochre to brown. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

English Wikipedia's account of Exidia candida says molecular research based on DNA sequences found E. cartilaginea to be a further synonym of E. candida. The same account says its distinctive morphology and habitat led to it being recognised at varietal rank as E. candida var. cartilaginea. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

English Wikipedia (the only source with habitat text) says E. candida var. cartilaginea grows preferentially on birch and alder. It says the taxon has a northerly distribution, occurring in North America, northern Europe and the Russian Far East. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
