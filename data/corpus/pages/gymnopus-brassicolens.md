## Summary

Gymnopus brassicolens (Romagn.) Antonín & Noordel. 1997 is a species in the family Omphalotaceae, known in English as the cabbage parachute. Wikipedia describes it as a relatively uncommon basidiomycete with a strong smell of rotten cabbage, buff-coloured gills, a cap 15-45 mm across, and a tapered stipe that is orange-yellow at the top and dark red-brown or black at the base. GBIF holds occurrence records from many countries across Europe, North America and elsewhere. *[wikipedia]*
