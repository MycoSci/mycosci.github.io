## Summary

Lactarius zonarius (Bull.) Fr. is a milk-cap in the family Russulaceae. It was first described in 1783 as Agaricus zonarius by Bulliard, and Wikispecies gives the current combination as (Bull.) Fr. 1838. GBIF distribution records for the species include Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, wikispecies, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia and Wikispecies both give the basionym as Agaricus zonarius Bull., 1783. Wikispecies lists these later names and combinations: Agaricus flexuosus var. zonarius (Bull.) Pers. (1801), Galorrheus zonarius (Bull.) P.Kumm. 1871, Lactifluus zonarius (Bull.) Kuntze 1891 and Lactarius zonarius (Bull.) Fr. 1838. It also lists Lactarius scrobipes Kühner & Romagn. 1953 and Lactarius insulsus sensu Neuhoff 1956 as taxonomic synonyms. It records that Lactarius zonarius sensu Neuhoff is L. evosmus and that Lactarius zonarius sensu Bres. is L. zonarioides.
