## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Peziza gilva Boud. in Cooke, Mycographia 1(6): 240, fig. 406 (1879). It lists Lachnea gilva (Gillet, 1881) and Tricharia gilva (Boudier, 1904) as homotypic synonyms, and the combination in Tricharina as Eckblad, Nytt Mag. Bot. 15(1–2): 60 (1968). A lectotype (PC 0167813) was designated by Van Vooren, Lindemann & Healy in Ascomycete.org 9(4): 106 (2017). The sources give no account of why the species was transferred. *[wikispecies]*
