## Summary

Russula adusta (Pers.) Fr., 1838, is a species in the family Russulaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a gilled mushroom in Russula subgenus Compactae, with a brown to gray, somewhat shiny cap, a mild taste, white spores, and a tendency to blacken when cut or bruised. Wikipedia gives its common names as blackening brittlegill and blackening russula. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it appears in late summer and autumn in deciduous and coniferous woodland, under conifers, across Britain, northern Europe and North America, and that in North America it appears in the Pacific Northwest and northern California from October to February. Mushroom World lists its location as Europe and says it usually fruits singly or in small scattered groups under thick leaf litter. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
