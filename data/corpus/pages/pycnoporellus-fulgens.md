## Summary

Pycnoporellus fulgens (Fr.) Donk (1971) is a polypore in the family Pycnoporellaceae, order Polyporales. Wikipedia describes it as native to Eurasia and North America, with tough, woody, shelf-like fruiting bodies that are typically orange or orange-red with a yellower leading edge. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (the only independent source with descriptive text) describes it as living in dead conifer logs as a brown-rot decayer, and gives dead logs of Norway Spruce and Douglas Fir as examples. It says the fruiting body is most commonly observed in July and August, on the sides of logs. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, France, Great Britain, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Slovenia and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
