## Summary

Tubaria dispersa (Berk. & Broome) Singer, 1961, is a species in the family Tubariaceae (Agaricales), known in English as the Hawthorn Twiglet. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small saprobic mushroom with a 1–2 cm buff to dull brownish yellow cap and dull yellow gills, originally described from England and widespread in Europe. Michael Kuo's 2023 page presents an Illinois collection as a good morphological match for the species. He notes that only two very old North American records (1897 and 1935) existed before it. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as saprobic, growing gregariously from the sometimes-buried fruits and litter of hawthorns, serviceberries, or fruit trees in the Rosaceae, from spring through fall. It is described as originally from England and widespread in Europe, with its North American range uncertain; the collection described on that page is from Illinois. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
