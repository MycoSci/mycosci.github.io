## Summary

Chroogomphus tomentosus (Murrill) O.K. Mill. is a species in the family Gomphidiaceae (order Boletales), known in English as the woolly pine spike. Wikipedia describes it as endemic to western North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found in forests with western hemlock and is commonly found near rotting logs. It describes an association with the admirable bolete (Aureoboletus mirabilis), a mycorrhizal fungus of hemlock, and says it is likely that C. tomentosus is parasitic on A. mirabilis. Fruiting is stated to start in summer and continue into winter. All of this comes from a single source (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist entry is a copy of its opening sentences). *[wikipedia]*
