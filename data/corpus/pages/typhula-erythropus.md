## Summary

Typhula erythropus (Pers.) Fr., 1818, is a fungus in the family Typhulaceae, known in English as Redleg Club. Wikipedia describes the fruiting bodies as 1.5 cm high, with spores measuring 9–10 × 3–4 μm. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with descriptive text, says it appears in North America, Europe and Asia, most often in Europe. It states that the fungus grows on petioles, stems and twigs of species such as Alnus, Populus, Acer, Fagus and Fraxinus, and rarely on grass, Urtica and Pteridium.
