## Summary

Tulosesus callinus (M. Lange & A.H. Sm.) D. Wächt. & A. Melzer, 2020, is a mushroom-producing fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was originally described as Coprinus callinus. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Denmark and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Coprinus callinus by Morten Lange and Alexander H. Smith in 1952. After a 2001 phylogenetic study reorganized that genus, it was transferred to Coprinellus, and it was known as Coprinellus callinus until 2020. In 2020 the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many species in the Psathyrellaceae based on phylogenetic analysis.
