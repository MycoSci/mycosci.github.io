## Summary

Coprotus sexdecimsporus (P. Crouan & H. Crouan) Kimbr. & Korf, 1967, is an ascomycete in the order Pezizales. The names Ascobolus sexdecimsporus, Ascophanus sexdecimsporus and Ryparobius sexdecimsporus are listed as synonyms. A single GBIF-hosted description gives its apothecia as minute, 0.1–0.5 mm across, whitish to translucent and with 16-spored asci, growing on dung. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single GBIF-hosted description states that the species is cosmopolitan and can be found on dung of various wild and domestic animals, mainly herbivores (especially ruminants and rodents), and that in the temperate zone it occurs from maritime to alpine zones. The specimens examined in that description, all from Croatia, were collected on dung of Equus asinus, Ovis aries and Lepus europaeus. *[gbif_desc]*
