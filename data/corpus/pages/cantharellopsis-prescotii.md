## Summary

Cantharellopsis prescotii (Weinm.) Kuyper, published in 1986, is placed in the Hymenochaetales (Agaricomycetes) and has records from Sweden, Norway, Finland and many other countries. Wikipedia describes it as the only species in the moss-dwelling (bryophilous) genus Cantharellopsis. Its fruit bodies are said to have a form intermediate between an Omphalina and a chanterelle (Cantharellus) because of forked, fold-like gills. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology
