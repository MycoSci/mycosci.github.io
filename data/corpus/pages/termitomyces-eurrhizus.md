## Summary

Termitomyces eurrhizus (Berk.) R. Heim (1942) is an agaric fungus in the family Lyophyllaceae. Wikipedia describes it as native to Pakistan, India, Sri Lanka, Burma, southwestern China and Malaysia, with a symbiotic relationship with termites; its mushrooms grow out of termite mounds after periods of rainfall. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Miles Joseph Berkeley named the fungus in 1847 as Agaricus eurrhizus, from material collected in Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. The French botanist Roger Heim reclassified it as Termitomyces eurrhizus in 1942. Its placement in Termitomyces was confirmed by a 2000 ribosomal DNA study showing that the termite mushrooms form a clade. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the mushrooms grow out of termite mounds, emerging after rain. It reports the fungus as associated with the termite Macrotermes gilvus in Selangor, Malaysia, and with Odontotermes badius in Sri Lanka.
