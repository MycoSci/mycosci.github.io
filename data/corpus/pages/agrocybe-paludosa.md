## Summary

Agrocybe paludosa (J.E. Lange) Kühner & Romagn. ex Bon is a species in the family Strophariaceae, with the English common name Marsh Fieldcap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a slender mushroom with a fragile stem, a persistent ring, a thin cap with a slightly lined margin and a brown spore print, associated with moist substrates. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com notes that some European sources (Nauta 2005, Ryman 2018) have begun calling this mushroom Agrocybe elatella. The site's author writes that they have been unable to locate sufficient and convincing documentation for that synonymy. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously from the ground in wet areas (marshes, spring run-off areas, etc.), appearing from spring through fall, and fairly widely distributed in North America and in Europe. The illustrated and described collection is from Illinois, where the author found it amid debris left by a nearby creek's spring flooding. This is a single-source description. *[mushroomexpert]*
