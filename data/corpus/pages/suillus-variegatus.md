## Summary

Suillus variegatus (Sw.) Richon & Roze (1888) is a bolete in the family Suillaceae, commonly called the velvet bolete. Sources describe it as forming a mycorrhizal relationship with pine and as occurring in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is mycorrhizal with two-needle pines and appears frequently in late summer. It is often found with heathers and other acid-loving plants, on sandy soils, and is described as widespread in Europe, nearer parts of Asia, and North America. Mushroom World, a separate source, describes it as typically growing in coniferous forests, often mycorrhizal with pine (Pinus spp.) and occasionally with spruce (Picea spp.) and fir (Abies spp.). The late-summer timing and the soil and companion-plant details each come from a single source. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
