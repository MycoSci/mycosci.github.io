## Summary

Byssocorticium atrovirens (Fr.) Bondartsev & Singer (1944) is a crust fungus in the family Atheliaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as forming thin, finely fuzzy, grayish blue resupinate patches 2–5 cm across on deadwood and forest debris. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from Sweden (Fries 1828) and neotypified from Great Britain (Hjortstam 1987). *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) states that it grows alone or gregariously on deadwood and forest debris, frequently on the undersides of well-rotted logs, from late summer through fall. It is described there as widely distributed in Europe and North America, and as possibly mycorrhizal (citing Tedersoo et al. 2010). The illustrated collection on that page is from New Hampshire. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
