## Summary

Fibroporia vaillantii (DC.) Parmasto, 1968, is a species of Fibroporiaceae in the order Polyporales. Wikipedia describes it as a wood-decaying fungus that can occur on timber in humid conditions and causes brown rot of pine wood, and lists "mine fungus" and "white pore fungus" among its other names. That description comes from a single independent source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus grows on wood from coniferous trees, such as pine, under damp conditions with a wood moisture content of 40 to 50 percent. In humid atmospheres, white cotton-like mycelium can grow on wood surfaces and cross inert materials. Wikipedia gives an optimal growth temperature of 28 °C (82 °F), with growth between 3 and 36 °C (37 and 97 °F). This habitat information is single-source (the iNaturalist text copies Wikipedia). A GBIF descriptions record lists distribution as Denmark, Norway, Madeira Island, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
