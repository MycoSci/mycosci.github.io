## Summary

Hydnellum gracilipes (P. Karst.) P. Karst., 1879, is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, order Thelephorales. Its recorded English name is Graceful Tooth. It has been recorded in GBIF occurrence data from countries including Sweden, Finland, Norway, the United Kingdom and Estonia. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the Finnish mycologist Petter Karsten first described the fungus as Hydnum gracilipes and later transferred it to the genus Hydnellum. Its dates for these two events are inconsistent, so none are given here. No source supplied describes the material examined or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as widely distributed in northern Europe, where it forms ectomycorrhizae with conifers. Its introduction names pine specifically. For Norway, the same source gives Finnmark as the northern extent of the distribution. It also reports that Scottish collections were found by lifting the dense ground cover of common heather (Calluna vulgaris). This is a single-source account. iNaturalist is a declared derivative of Wikipedia and does not corroborate it. *[wikipedia]*
