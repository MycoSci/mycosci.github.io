## Summary

Scleroderma citrinum (Pers., 1801), the common earthball or pigskin poison puffball, is a species of earthball in the family Sclerodermataceae. It has a hard, scaly, yellowish to yellow-brown fruiting body with a dark, purplish-black interior when young. English Wikipedia describes it as poisonous. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states the species was originally described from Europe in association with oaks (Persoon 1801). It lists Scleroderma aurantium and Scleroderma vulgare as synonyms. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, but often found in mixed woods, in mossy areas, and occasionally on well-rotted wood. It grows alone, scattered or gregariously, in summer and fall, or over winter in warm climates. It is reported as widely distributed in Europe and North America. Wikipedia describes it as the most common earthball in the United Kingdom, occurring in woods, heathland and short grass from autumn to winter. Both sources describe it as an ectomycorrhizal/mycorrhizal fungus. Wikipedia also notes that it can be parasitized by Pseudoboletus parasiticus. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
