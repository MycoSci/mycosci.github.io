## Summary

Cookeina speciosa (Fr.) Dennis is a species of fungus in the family Sarcoscyphaceae (order Pezizales), originally described by Elias Magnus Fries in 1822 as Peziza speciosa. Wikipedia calls it the most common species of its genus and says its apothecia vary in colour, with variants described as white over yellow, pinkish red, yellow-orange, and orange to chocolate brown.
