## Summary

Cortinarius uliginosus, known as the marsh webcap or orange willow webcap, is a species of mushroom in the family Cortinariaceae, first described by Miles Joseph Berkeley in 1860. Wikipedia describes the cap as orangish (ochre or reddish in some), about 1.5–4.5 cm across, on a yellowish stipe about 2.5–7.5 cm long, with a cortina and a rusty brown spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that C. uliginosus is found under willow trees in wet areas and also in alder carr forests, that it occurs in both North America and Europe, and that it is mycorrhizal. This habitat description comes from a single independent source. A GBIF description record lists distribution for Belgium (Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
