## Summary

Myriostoma capillisporum (V.J. Staněk) Suz, A.M. Ainsw., Baseia & M.P. Martín (2017) is a fungus in the family Geastraceae, listed in the catalog under the English name African Pepperpot and recorded in GBIF from Tanzania, South Africa, Kenya and Mozambique. Wikipedia describes the basidiocarps as resembling earthstars, but with the spore sac supported by multiple columns rather than a single column, and with multiple ostioles rather than a single apical ostiole. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the fungus was originally described as a variety of Myriostoma coliforme, based on the distinctive and conspicuous ornamentation of its basidiospores. It adds that recent molecular research, based on cladistic analysis of DNA sequences, has shown it to be a separate species. *[wikipedia]*
