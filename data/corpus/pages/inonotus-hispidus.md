## Summary

Inonotus hispidus (Bull.) P. Karst. (1879), in the family Hymenochaetaceae, is known in English as the shaggy bracket. Wikipedia describes it as a fungus and plant pathogen found in Europe, Asia and North America. The same source describes the fruit bodies as generally semicircular and lumpy, 10–20 cm across, orangish with a lighter margin when fresh and blackening with age, with orangish flesh and a brown spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found on oak and other hardwoods through eastern North America. GBIF description records list its distribution as Belgium (including the Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont; these records give no host, substrate or season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
