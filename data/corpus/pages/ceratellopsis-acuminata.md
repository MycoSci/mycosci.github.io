## Summary

## Discovery and naming

The name was combined by Corner in 1950 (Ann. Bot. Mem. 1: 202) from Pistillaria acuminata Fuckel. In the same work Corner chose C. aculeata as the type of Ceratellopsis. Konrad & Maublanc's original selection was C. queletii, which Donk (1954) accepted as type. Olariaga et al. (2020) reviewed the typification and accepted C. queletii as type. They treated it as a synonym of Pterulicium gracile and provisionally proposed C. acuminata as conserved type. The 2022 proposal repeats this to preserve the usage of Ceratellopsis and Pterulicium, and notes that Olariaga et al. found C. aculeata and C. acuminata formed a clade separate from other species then placed in Ceratellopsis. *[lit_1]*
