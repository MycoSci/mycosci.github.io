## Summary

Cellulariella warnieri (Durieu & Mont.) Zmitr. & Malysheva (2014) is a species of Polyporaceae in the order Polyporales. A 2022 study describes it as having a strictly lamelliform (lenzitoid) hymenophore, meaning gill-like rather than poroid. GBIF holds 707 occurrence records for it from many countries, including France, Spain, Italy and Germany. *[lit_2]*

## Discovery and naming

Lit_2 (2022) says polypores with a gill-like hymenophore were traditionally placed in Lenzites, and that this species was originally Lenzites warnieri Durieu & Mont. The genus Cellulariella Zmitr. & V. Malysheva was erected to hold L. warnieri and L. acuta. According to the same paper, that step did little to resolve cross-synonymies among lamellate polypores of the Trametes group. The paper combines five molecular markers (LSU, ITS, EF1-α, RPB1, RPB2) with morphology. In analyses of RPB1, RPB2 and EF1-α, L. warnieri has an unresolved position on poorly supported trees. Analyses of combined ITS+LSU sequences instead support a clade of L. warnieri and L. acuta, sister to a broader clade that includes Leiotrametes, Pycnoporus and the Trametes ljubarskyi–T. cingulata subclade. The paper redefines Cellulariella using the following characters: a moderate amount of scarcely branched, non-flagelliform binding hyphae in the context, pointed and fusiform hyphal ends protruding through the hymenium, and a strictly lamelliform hymenophore. It designates a lectotype for Cellulariella warnieri. *[lit_2]*
