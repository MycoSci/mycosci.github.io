## Summary

Cylindrobasidium laeve (Pers.) Chamuris, 1984, is a fungus in the family Physalacriaceae, listed in the catalog under the English name Tear Dropper. Wikipedia states that a product containing it as the active ingredient can be used as a mycoherbicide to control Acacia mearnsii (black wattle) in South Africa. The GBIF distribution entry lists Belgium (including its Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway, Madeira Island and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by Persoon in 1794 as Corticium laeve. The name currently accepted in Index Fungorum was published in 1984 by George Peter Chamuris. No supplied source gives the reasons for the transfers or the material described. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, describes the species as very common in Poland. There it is usually found in forests, bushes, parks, gardens and roadsides, on trunks and branches of deciduous trees. The tree species listed are maples, chestnut, alder, silver birch, hornbeam, hazel, hawthorn, beech, hairy ash, apple, black poplar, plum, Robinia pseudoacacia, willow and lime. Wikipedia says it occurs rarely on conifers. This is a single-source account and covers Poland only. *[wikipedia]*
