## Summary

Tomentella fuscocinerea (Pers.) Donk (1933) is a corticioid fungus in the family Thelephoraceae. In the supplied literature it is best known as the ectomycorrhizal fungal partner of two North American mycoheterotrophic orchids, Corallorhiza striata and Corallorhiza bentleyi. It was reported as new to Finland in 2015. *[lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

Described as an ectomycorrhizal Tomentella. An American Journal of Botany study (2010) sequenced fungi from 107 Corallorhiza striata individuals across 42 North American populations. Nearly all fungal sequences were most similar to T. fuscocinerea, and a clade of Californian orchids was subspecialized toward a narrow T. fuscocinerea clade. A Castanea study (2021) found that Corallorhiza bentleyi, known from five counties in Virginia and West Virginia, associated with a few genotypes of T. fuscocinerea across all sampling localities. A 2015 Czech Mycology paper reports it as new to Finland. GBIF lists distribution records for Denmark and Norway.
