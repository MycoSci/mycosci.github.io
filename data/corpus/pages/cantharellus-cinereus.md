## Summary

Cantharellus cinereus Pers. (1794) is a name in the family Hydnaceae, and Index Fungorum treats the current name as Craterellus cinereus. Wikipedia describes it as a greyish-black, funnel-shaped chanterelle with a cap 1.5–5 cm across, and gives the common names black chanterelle and ashen chanterelle. It has been recorded in Europe and North America, and the catalog also holds occurrence records from many other countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat detail, describes it as a mycorrhizal species growing on soil with leaf litter in broadleaf woods, found singly or in small groups. It adds that in Europe it is found in coniferous forests. It calls the species widespread but uncommon, found in winter and early spring in western North America. A separate survey of wild mushrooms in Poonch District, Azad Jammu and Kashmir lists it among the species identified there, but its abstract gives no habitat or substrate details. *[wikipedia, lit_2]*
