## Summary

Termitomyces umkowaan (Cooke & Massee) D.A. Reid is a species of agaric fungus in the family Lyophyllaceae, recorded from South Africa. It was described as new to science in 1889 by Mordecai Cubitt Cooke and George Edward Massee from collections made in Durban, and was transferred to the genus Termitomyces by Derek Reid in 1975. *[wikipedia]*
