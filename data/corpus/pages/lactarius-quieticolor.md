Lactarius quieticolor Romagn. is a milk-cap in the family Russulaceae, first described scientifically by the French mycologist Henri Romagnesi in 1958. GBIF's distribution record lists Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Brazil and Chile (Maule, Biobío and Los Lagos regions). *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
