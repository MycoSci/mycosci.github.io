## Summary

Trametes pubescens (Schumach.) Pilát, 1939, is a small, thin bracket fungus (polypore) in the family Polyporaceae. It has a cream-colored, finely velvety cap. Unlike most other turkey tail-like Trametes species, its cap lacks strongly contrasting zones of color. Its pore surface becomes yellowish with age and has 3-5 angular pores per mm (MushroomExpert.Com). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both describe it as an annual, saprobic decomposer of the deadwood of hardwoods, rarely reported on conifer wood. It grows in clusters on logs and stumps (Wikipedia adds downed branches). MushroomExpert.Com says it causes a white rot, fruits in summer and fall, and is widely distributed across North America. Wikipedia alone describes it as a purported plant pathogen infecting peach and nectarine trees. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
