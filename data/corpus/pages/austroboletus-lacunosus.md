## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Boletus lacunosus by Mordecai Cubitt Cooke and George Edward Massee in 1889, but that name was not valid because it had been used previously. Otto Kuntze proposed Suillus lacunosus in 1898 as a new name. Because it was published earlier, it takes precedence over the superfluous name Boletus cookei, proposed by Pier Andrea Saccardo and Hans Sydow in 1899. Australian mycologists Tom May and Alec Wood transferred the taxon to Austroboletus in 1995. This account comes from a single independent source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
