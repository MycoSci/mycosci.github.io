## Summary

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1902 by George Francis Atkinson as Amanitopsis albocreata, and was transferred to Amanita in 1941 by Jean-Edouard Gilbert. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
