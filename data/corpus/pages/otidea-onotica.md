## Summary

Otidea onotica (Pers.) Fuckel 1870 is a cup fungus in the Pezizales, placed in the family Otideaceae in the site catalog and recorded from many European and North American countries. Sources describe a yellow to orangish, sometimes pink-tinged cup that is split down one side and up to about 10 cm tall, and common names include hare's ear and donkey ear. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it occurs in Europe and North America, singly or in small groups on the soil of deciduous woodland, most often with beech, with fruiting bodies from spring to early autumn. MushroomExpert.Com (a separate source) describes it as saprobic and terrestrial in woods under hardwoods or conifers, often clustered but sometimes alone or scattered, in summer and fall (winter and spring in warmer areas), and widely distributed in North America. Mushroom World also reports growth on soil in broad-leaf and mixed woods from spring to early autumn, tending to be under beech. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld]*
