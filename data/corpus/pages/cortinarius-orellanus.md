## Summary

Cortinarius orellanus Fr. (1838), the fool's webcap, is a member of the family Cortinariaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a deadly fungus native to Europe and as part of the Orellani group, all of which are described as highly toxic, with kidney failure that is often irreversible. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by Elias Magnus Fries in his 1838 work Epicrisis Systematis Mycologici seu Synopsis Hymenomycetum. It is one of seven highly toxic species in the Orellani, a subgenus of Cortinarius. This is a single-cluster account. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it in central and eastern Europe (Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia), rare in Germany and occasionally found in southern Britain. It is said to grow in deciduous forests under beech, hornbeam and oak, often near pine trees. Mushroom World independently says it grows solitary or in scattered trooping groups with broad-leaf trees. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
