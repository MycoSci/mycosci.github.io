## Summary

Tricholoma sulphureum (Bull.) P. Kumm. is a member of the Tricholomataceae, recorded across Europe and in North America and China. Wikipedia describes it as a bright yellow mushroom with an unusual smell likened to coal gas, and classifies it as inedible or mildly poisonous. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia gives the specific epithet as derived from the Latin meaning 'of or pertaining to sulfur'. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described in 1784 by Pierre Bulliard as Agaricus sulphureus, and was placed in the genus Tricholoma by Paul Kummer in 1871. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found in deciduous woods, particularly beech, and can occur anytime from spring until autumn. It is reported across Europe, confirmed as far east as China, and is also distributed in North America, where it also grows with conifers. *[wikipedia]*
