## Summary

Peziza vesiculosa Bull. (1791) is an apothecial fungus in the family Pezizaceae, with France recorded as its type locality. Wikipedia gives its common name as the common dung cup and describes pale, cup-shaped ascocarps 1–6 cm wide that often form clusters, with a white spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species grows on nutrient-rich soils such as manure and compost. It says the species can be found year-round throughout North America, usually within a December–April season on the West Coast, that it is common in Europe, and that records elsewhere in the world are scattered. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a declared derivative of it). The GBIF description record lists distribution entries for Belgium, Norway, Sweden, Madeira Island and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
