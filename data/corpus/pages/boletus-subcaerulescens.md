## Summary

Boletus subcaerulescens (E.A. Dick & Snell) Both, Bessette & A.R. Bessette, published in 2000, is a bolete in the family Boletaceae. Wikipedia describes it as found in northeastern North America, with a brown cap up to 18 cm wide, yellow tubes that stain blue and later brown when bruised, and white to buff flesh that does not stain when cut. This description comes from a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*
