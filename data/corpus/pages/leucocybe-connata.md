## Summary

Leucocybe connata (Schumach.) Vizzini, P. Alvarado, G. Moreno & Consiglio (2015) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as White Domecap. Mushroom World describes it as a medium-sized agaric with a white cap 3–8 cm across and a white stem, with no volva or ring, whose fruitbodies often form compact clusters with fused stem bases. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[gbif_desc, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Mushroom World notes that Clitocybe connata was previously used for this mushroom in some Nordic countries (including Sweden), and that Lyophyllum connatum is an older name. *[mushroomworld]*
