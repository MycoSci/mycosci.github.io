## Summary

Skeletocutis alutacea (J. Lowe) Jean Keller, 1979, is a poroid fungus in the family Incrustoporiaceae (order Polyporales). Its basionym is Poria alutacea. It is recorded from North America, Europe and New Zealand. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was described as new to science in 1946 by the American mycologist Josiah Lincoln Lowe as Poria alutacea. Jean Keller transferred it to the genus Skeletocutis in 1979. This account comes from a single independent source cluster, and no reason for the transfer is given. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
