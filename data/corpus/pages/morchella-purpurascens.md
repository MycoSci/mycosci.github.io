## Summary

Morchella purpurascens, the purple morel, is an ascomycete fungus in the family Morchellaceae. It was first described as a variety of Morchella elata by Emile Boudier, based on an earlier plate by Julius Vincenz von Krombholz. Wikipedia describes the species as characterised by pinkish or purplish colours on the cap of its young fruit bodies. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the taxon was first described as a variety of Morchella elata by Emile Boudier in 1897, based on a plate previously illustrated by Julius Vincenz von Krombholz. It adds that, as with many other morels, the species' taxonomic status was debated for a long time. An extensive phylogenetic and nomenclatural study by Richard and colleagues in 2014 confirmed its autonomy and matched it to phylogenetic lineage Mel-20.
