## Summary

Hydnum repandum L. (1753) is a species in the family Hydnaceae, order Cantharellales, and is the type species of the genus Hydnum. Wikipedia describes fruit bodies whose spore-bearing structures are spines rather than gills, hanging from the underside of the cap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a European species associated with various trees, including spruce and beech. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet repandum means "bent back", referring to the wavy cap margin. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Linnaeus first described the species in his 1753 Species Plantarum, and Elias Fries sanctioned the name in 1821. It was placed in other genera over time: Hypothele (Paulet, 1812), Dentinum (Gray, 1821), Tyrodon (Karsten, 1881) and Sarcodon (Quélet, 1886). A 1977 nomenclatural proposal by Ronald H. Petersen was accepted, making H. repandum the type species of Hydnum. Wikipedia also reports that a 2016 worldwide genetic study of the genus found at least four species within the broad concept of H. repandum. MushroomExpert.Com adds that by current DNA-informed definitions H. repandum does not occur in North America, although the name has traditionally been used in North American field guides. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes H. repandum as a mycorrhizal fungus whose fruit bodies grow singly, scattered, or in groups on the ground or in leaf litter in both coniferous and deciduous forests, sometimes in fairy rings, fruiting from summer to autumn. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods or conifers, especially spruces and beech, growing gregariously on the ground in summer and fall and widely distributed in Europe. Its illustrated collection is from northern Italy. Wikipedia also reports the species in the Pacific Northwest and Rocky Mountains and with oaks in eastern North America. MushroomExpert.Com states that under current DNA-informed definitions it does not occur in North America, so the North American records are contested. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
