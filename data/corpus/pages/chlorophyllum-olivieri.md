## Summary

Chlorophyllum olivieri (Barla) Vellinga, 2002, is a species in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia lists it as one of three closely related species, with C. rhacodes and C. brunneum, that share the common name shaggy parasol. ## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that C. rhacodes, C. olivieri and C. brunneum were formerly placed in Macrolepiota or Lepiota, and that the genus was changed on the basis of molecular phylogenetic evidence showing a closer relationship to Chlorophyllum molybdites than to Macrolepiota procera. This is stated for the three species as a group, not for C. olivieri alone. *[wikipedia]*
