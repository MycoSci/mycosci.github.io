## Summary

Clavariadelphus ligula (Schaeff.) Donk, the strap coral or Ochre Club, is a club-shaped fungus in the family Clavariadelphaceae. Wikipedia describes the fruit bodies as buff to pale yellow or reddish, up to 12 cm tall, with spongy flesh, growing in groups on the forest floor. It is recorded in Eurasia and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet ligula is derived from the Latin word for "shoestring". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that Jacob Christian Schaeffer first described the species in 1774 as Clavaria ligula, and that Marinus Anton Donk first placed it in Clavariadelphus in 1933. Clavaria ophioglossoides, described by August Batsch in 1783, is considered a synonym. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states the fruit bodies grow gregariously on the ground in forest duff, and are widespread and common in coniferous forests, fruiting in summer and fall. A GBIF description (citing Methven 1990) gives a scattered to gregarious habit on the ground in mixed woods with Abies, Picea, Pinus, Thuja and Tsuga. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
