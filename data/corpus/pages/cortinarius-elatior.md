## Summary

Cortinarius elatior Fr. (1838) is a species of webcap in the family Cortinariaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a large webcap with white flesh, native to Europe. It gives the cap as 5-11 cm, starting conical and flattening with an umbo, and the stem as 5-8 cm, tapering to a pointed base. It also gives cream gills that turn reddish-brown with age, a reddish-brown spore print, and warted, almond- to lemon-shaped spores of 11-15 x 7.5-9 μm. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia gives the common name "wrinkled webcap" and attributes it to the cap's tendency to wrinkle with age. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as native to Europe. GBIF's description record lists its distribution as Denmark, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
