## Summary

Amanita rhopalopus Bas (1969), the American Club-Footed Lepidella, is a species of Amanita in the family Amanitaceae, described as found in North America. MushroomExpert.Com places it among the stinky, chlorine-smelling species of section Lepidella and distinguishes it by a very long, rooting stem, an ephemeral ring, and white to brownish patches or warts on the cap. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (single-source description) gives the ecology as mycorrhizal with oaks, usually growing alone or scattered but sometimes gregariously, in summer and fall. It is described as probably widely distributed in eastern North America below the Great Lakes, but more common in the Appalachians. *[mushroomexpert]*
