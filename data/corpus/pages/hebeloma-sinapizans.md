## Summary

Hebeloma sinapizans (Paulet) Gillet is a species in the family Hymenogastraceae. Wikipedia describes it as poisonous, with a strong radish-like smell and a prominent bulbous stem base. MushroomExpert.Com notes that the species called Hebeloma sinapizans in North America is probably not the true European species. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the European species was first described from France by Paulet in 1793. The same source says the North American concept of the name (sensu A. H. Smith, 1984) is probably a different species or species group. It has a darker cap and cheilocystidia unlike the European species. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states the fungus fruits on the ground in groups or fairy rings in deciduous and coniferous forests. MushroomExpert.Com describes the North American species group, which it says is probably not the true European species. That source reports it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods or conifers. It grows gregariously or in loose clusters, sometimes in arcs or fairy rings, in grassy areas at woods' edges or in woods. The season is late summer and fall (winter and spring in California). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
