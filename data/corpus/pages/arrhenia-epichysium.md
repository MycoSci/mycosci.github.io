## Summary

Arrhenia epichysium (Pers.) Redhead, Lutzoni, Moncalvo & Vilgalys (2002) is an agaric fungus in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes the fruit body as having small brown to dark gray caps 1–3 cm across, which change to light gray to tan when dry. It also describes narrow, thin, closely spaced gills that run down the stipe (decurrent), smooth ellipsoid spores measuring 6–7.5 μm, and a white spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, a single-source claim, states that it can be found on dead wood in North America (except for the Gulf Coast), lasting through May on the West Coast. A 2025 paper in the Ukrainian Botanical Journal lists it among eight species that are rare in Ukraine and newly reported from the Kholodnyi Yar National Nature Park (Cherkasy Region). The GBIF description record lists the distribution as DK; NO; SE; Vermont. *[wikipedia, lit_1, gbif_desc]*
