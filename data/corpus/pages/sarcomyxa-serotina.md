## Summary

Sarcomyxa serotina (Pers.) V. Papp is a species of fungus in the family Sarcomyxaceae. Its recommended English name in the UK is olive oysterling, and in North America it is known as late fall oyster or late oyster mushroom. The fruit bodies consist mainly of greenish, overlapping fan- or oyster-shaped caps up to 9 cm wide, with closely spaced, bright orange-yellow gills. *[wikipedia]*
