## Summary

Ganoderma orbiforme (Fr.) Ryvarden, 2000, is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as widespread across southeast Asia and as a plant pathogen that causes basal stem rot of the African oil palm (Elaeis guineensis). It is known in oil palm pathology mainly under the name G. boninense. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described scientifically in 1838 by Elias Magnus Fries from collections made in Guinea. Leif Ryvarden transferred it to the genus Ganoderma in 2000. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a pathogen of the African oil palm (Elaeis guineensis) that causes basal stem rot, with a hemibiotrophic lifestyle in that host. It states that the fungus is not a soil-borne pathogen and does not grow in soil, but is not killed by soil and can persist in dead, buried palm trunk material. Besides the type locality in Guinea, Wikipedia reports collections from the Bonin Islands in the Pacific, from Venezuela and from Puerto Rico. These habitat statements come from a single source cluster (Wikipedia, with iNaturalist as a derivative copy). *[wikipedia]*
