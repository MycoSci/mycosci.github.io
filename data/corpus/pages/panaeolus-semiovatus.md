## Summary

Panaeolus semiovatus (Sowerby) S. Lundell & Nannf. (1938) is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae. Sources describe it as a medium-sized to large, buff to whitish-capped, black-spored mushroom that grows on dung and bears a ring on the stem. The spine records it in many countries, mostly in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows on horse dung. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on horse dung in spring, summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. A 2026 Zenodo description gives pastures, on dung of horses and cows. A GBIF description of a Colombian collection records dung as substrate in a Quercus humboldtii forest. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, lit_3, gbif_desc]*
