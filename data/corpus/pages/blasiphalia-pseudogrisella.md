## Summary

Blasiphalia pseudogrisella (A.H. Sm.) Redhead is the only species in the monotypic genus Blasiphalia, which Wikipedia places in the family Repetobasidiaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a honey colored omphalinoid agaric that grows with the liverwort genus Blasia. The genus was recognized as distinct in 2007 on the basis of molecular analysis. *[wikipedia]*
