## Summary

Cantharellula umbonata (J.F. Gmel.) Singer, 1936, is a gilled fungus in the family Hygrophoraceae, known in English as the humpback or grayling. Sources describe it as a small to medium funnel-shaped agaric with a grey cap and repeatedly forked, decurrent, whitish gills, growing with Polytrichum moss. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies records the basionym as Merulius umbonatus J.F.Gmel., published in Systema Naturae (ed. 13) 2(2): 1430 (1792). The combination in Cantharellula was published by Singer in Revue de Mycologie 1(6): 281 (1936). No source supplied here explains the reasons for the transfer. *[wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it is common in eastern North America, particularly in summer and autumn, and that it is associated with Polytrichum and other mosses. It says the species commonly grows in boggy areas and under conifers. Mushroom World independently says it grows associated with Polytrichum moss and fruits in summer and fall, but lists its location only as Europe. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
