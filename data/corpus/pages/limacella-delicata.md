## Summary

Limacella delicata (Fr.) Earle ex Konrad & Maubl. (1930) is a species in the family Amanitaceae. Wikipedia describes a reddish cap up to 8 cm wide, whitish gills that are often adnexed and then become free, and a stalk up to 12 cm long and 1.5 cm thick with a ring, whitish above the ring.
