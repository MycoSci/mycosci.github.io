## Summary

Discina ancilis (Pers.) Sacc. 1889 is a species in the family Discinaceae, commonly known as pig's ears. Wikipedia describes it as a brown to tannish, wrinkled, cup- or ear-shaped fungus, sometimes with a short, stout stalk. It is recorded from North America and Europe, among other regions. *[wikipedia]*
