## Summary

Clitocybe catinus (Fr.) Quél. (1872) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae. English Wikipedia's treatment of Infundibulicybe gibba describes it as differing from C. gibba by a white cap, occasionally with some pink tonality, and a slight smell of flour. It was reported as a new record for Turkey in a 2007 study of macrofungi from Kayseri province. *[wikipedia, lit_3]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia (in its article on Infundibulicybe gibba) reports that Harmaja (2003) gave the species a new name, Infundibulicybe catinus, and that Vizzini et al. (2011) listed it as a colour variant of Infundibulicybe gibba. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
