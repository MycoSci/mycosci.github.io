## Summary

Mycena rosella, the pink bonnet, is a species in the family Mycenaceae, placed in Mycena by P. Kummer in 1871. Mushroom World describes it as a small woodland bonnet with a bright pink to salmon pink cap and a distinctly brighter gill edge, with a cap 0.5–2 cm across and a stem 2–5 cm tall. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World, a single aggregator source, states that it grows exclusively in moist, moss-rich coniferous or mixed woods, often in large groups on needle beds, and is typically encountered in autumn. It gives the location as Europe and North America. *[mushroomworld]*
