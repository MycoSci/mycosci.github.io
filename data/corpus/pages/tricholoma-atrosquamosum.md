## Summary

Tricholoma atrosquamosum Sacc. (1887) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, known in English as the Dark Scaled Knight. Wikipedia, the only source with descriptive text, describes a cap 4–10 cm wide covered with dark grey-brown scales, on a stout stipe with no ring. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruit bodies are generally found singly or in small groups in deciduous woodland on chalk-based soils. It also says they appear under deciduous and coniferous trees, particularly beech and spruce, on chalky (calcareous) soils. It gives the range as North America and Europe and describes the species as generally rare in Europe. For var. squarrulosum, Wikipedia gives an association with oak, pine and spruce on chalk soils, with fruit bodies appearing September to November. A GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flanders), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
