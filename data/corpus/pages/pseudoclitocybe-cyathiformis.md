## Summary

Pseudoclitocybe cyathiformis (Bull.) Singer is a species in the family Pseudoclitocybaceae (order Agaricales) and the type species of the genus Pseudoclitocybe. Wikipedia gives its English common name as the goblet funnel cap; the site catalog also lists "Goblet" and "The Goblet". It was described by Bulliard in 1786 and later transferred by Singer in 1956, and it is reported from North America and Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes the species as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously, usually in moss on well-rotted wood but sometimes on the ground in moss or leaf litter, and favoring mossy stumps and logs. It fruits in fall, or over winter in warm climates. Wikipedia gives a season of September to December and says it grows in woodland soil or rotting wood in North America and Europe. MushroomExpert.Com describes the North American distribution as widespread in northern and montane areas (including the Appalachians), the West Coast and Mexico, and says it is also known from Japan and is widely distributed in Europe. Its illustrated and described collections are from California. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
