## Summary

Chlorophyllum rachodes (Vittad.) Vellinga, 2002, is a species in the family Agaricaceae, with the English vernacular name Shaggy Parasol. Wikipedia notes that "shaggy parasol" is used as a common name for three closely related species (C. rhacodes, C. olivieri and C. brunneum), and that many reference works spell the epithet "rhacodes" rather than "rachodes". *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that Vittadini used the spelling "rachodes" when he first published the species in 1835, but that this was erroneous, because the Greek word rhakos 'piece of cloth' should be transcribed as rhacos. Index Fungorum keeps the original spelling "rachodes". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that Vittadini first published the species in 1835, and that it was formerly placed in Macrolepiota or Lepiota. It was moved to Chlorophyllum on molecular phylogenetic evidence of a closer relationship to Chlorophyllum molybdites than to Macrolepiota procera. A 2003 Mycotaxon type study (lit_1) examined the C. rachodes complex in Europe and North America and recognized five species: Chl. rachodes, Chl. olivieri, Chl. brunneum, Chl. subrhacodes and Macrolepiota venenata. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, in a comparison with Macrolepiota procera, says C. rhacodes prefers more shade and dislikes open pastures and fields. It describes the three shaggy parasol species together as found in North America, Europe and Southern Africa. A 2016 survey (lit_2) reports C. rachodes in the Pampa biome of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. The GBIF description record lists distribution in the Valparaíso, Biobío and Los Ríos regions (Chile) and Norway. No supplied source states a host tree, substrate or fruiting season. *[wikipedia, lit_2, gbif_desc]*
