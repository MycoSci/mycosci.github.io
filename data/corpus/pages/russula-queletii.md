## Summary

Russula queletii Fr. (1872) is a member of the Russulaceae. It is described in the sources as a purple-capped Russula associated with conifers, especially spruce, with white to cream gills and spore print and an acrid taste. GBIF descriptions list distribution records for Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as growing in groups, predominantly in spruce forest. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, especially spruces and 2-needled pines, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in summer and fall. It says the true Russula queletii of Europe is associated primarily with spruces and is probably distinct from North American versions, some of which associate with pines. The MushroomExpert.Com collection described is from northern California, and it reports the species as widely reported in western and northern North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
