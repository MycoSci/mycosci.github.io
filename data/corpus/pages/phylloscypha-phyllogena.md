## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from South Carolina (Cooke 1877), as Peziza phyllogena. It lists Peziza badioconfusa as a synonym and notes that this latter name was often used in field guides. According to that source, Pfister (1987) studied the 19th-century type collection of Peziza phyllogena and concluded it was the same as Peziza badioconfusa. Because phyllogena is the older name, it takes precedence. *[mushroomexpert]*
