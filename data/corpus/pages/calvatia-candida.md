## Summary

Calvatia candida (Rostk.) Hollós, 1902, is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae, and the site catalog records it from many countries. A study of a Sri Lankan collection describes a small, subglobose fruiting body about 35 mm high and 40 mm across, with a buttercup-yellow outer skin that becomes dull yellow with maturity, and small globose, spiny spores. That study reports it as a first record for Sri Lanka. *[gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Langermannia candida Rostk. 1839 and lists Lycoperdon candidum (Rostk.) Bonord as a synonym. *[wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

The one source that describes ecology says it grows solitary on clayey soil with leaf litter. That source reports the species from Australia, Costa Rica, Cuba, India, Israel, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, the USA and several European countries including Germany, Hungary and Italy. No season or host tree is given. *[gbif_desc]*
