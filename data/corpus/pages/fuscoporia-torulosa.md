## Summary

Fuscoporia torulosa (Pers.) T. Wagner & M. Fisch., the tufted bracket, is a bracket fungus in the family Hymenochaetaceae, combined in Fuscoporia in 2001. Wikipedia describes it as a wood-decay fungus that causes a white rot of heartwood. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was formerly known as Phellinus torulosus, and that a 2001 phylogenetic study split the genus Phellinus into five new genera, after which P. torulosus was renamed Fuscoporia torulosa. This account comes from a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes a wood-decay fungus causing a white rot of heartwood in dead and living hardwood trees in Europe, and in coniferous trees in North America. It gives Quercus as the preferred host and lists many other hardwood genera reported as hosts, among them Acer, Castanea, Fagus, Fraxinus, Olea, Populus, Salix and Ulmus. It also lists conifers as more rarely reported hosts: Cedrus, Cupressus, Larix, Picea and Pinus. A GBIF description record lists specimens examined from Yunnan Province, China, collected on trunks of angiosperms in a broad-leaved forest between 2017 and 2018 and noted as white rot. No source supplied here states a fruiting season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
