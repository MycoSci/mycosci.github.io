## Summary

Mycena galericulata (Scop.) Gray, 1821, known in English as the common bonnet, is a member of the family Mycenaceae and the type species of the genus Mycena. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com both describe it as a saprobic species that grows in clusters on decaying wood. Wikipedia describes it as common and widespread across the temperate zone of the Northern Hemisphere. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet galericulata is derived from the Latin galer and means "with a small hat". This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that the fungus was first described as Agaricus galericulatus by Giovanni Antonio Scopoli in 1772. Elias Magnus Fries sanctioned that name in his 1821 Systema Mycologicum, and Samuel Frederick Gray transferred the species to Mycena in the same year. MushroomExpert.Com states that it was originally described from Carniola, in present-day Slovenia. Wikipedia also identifies it as the type species of Mycena. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on well-decayed hardwood logs and stumps. It says the fungus not infrequently arises from subterranean wood and so appears terrestrial. It usually grows in loose or dense clusters, occasionally alone or scattered. It fruits in spring and fall, or over winter in warmer climates. Wikipedia (an independent cluster from MushroomExpert.Com) describes it as saprobic on decaying hardwood and softwood sticks, chips, logs and stumps. It says the mushroom can also grow from submerged wood, typically in small clusters or sometimes singly, and fruits from late spring to early winter. The two sources differ on whether softwood is a substrate: MushroomExpert.Com names only hardwood, while Wikipedia names both. For range, MushroomExpert.Com gives widely distributed in Europe, and in North America east of the Rocky Mountains, along the West Coast, and in Mexico, with reports also from Central and South America. Wikipedia describes it as found throughout the temperate zone of the Northern Hemisphere and notes a collection from Edo State, Nigeria. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
