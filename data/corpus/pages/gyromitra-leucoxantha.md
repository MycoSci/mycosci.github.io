## Summary

Gyromitra leucoxantha (Bres.) Harmaja, 1969 is a cup fungus in the family Discinaceae (Pezizales); the site catalog records Piscidiscina leucoxantha as its current name in Index Fungorum. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as closely related to the false morels, with a yellow to yellowish-brown or orangish-brown cup 4-10 cm across. That source says it is more or less indistinguishable by eye from other brownish springtime cup fungi, and that cup-like Gyromitra identifications should not be trusted without examining the spores. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com cites the name as Bresàdola, 1881, combined by Harmaja, 1969, and notes that field guides usually treat this species as "Discina leucoxantha". No supplied source describes the original material or the reasons for the transfer. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously, and terrestrial. It often appears near rotted wood and stumps, as many Gyromitra species do. It is found under hardwoods and conifers in spring, in northern, northeastern and montane North America. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*
