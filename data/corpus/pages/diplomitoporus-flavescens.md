## Summary

Diplomitoporus flavescens (Bres.) Domański, 1970, is a poroid crust fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as having a circumpolar distribution that extends to eastern Canada, and as rare in Northern Europe. The supplied sources contain no morphological description of the fungus. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the Italian mycologist Giacomo Bresadola first described the species in 1903 as Trametes flavescens. It also states that Stanislaw Domański made it the type species of his newly created genus Diplomitoporus in 1970. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that in Northern Europe the fungus is rare and restricted to old-growth forests dominated by spruce. There it tends to prefer host trees that have no or few other fungi growing on them and that fell in a relatively healthy state. Wikipedia also reports it from the Changbaishan region of northeastern China, and from Turkey in 2016, where it was found on a branch of black pine. A GBIF materials-examined record describes a specimen collected on the trunk of Pinus at Zixishan National Forest Park, Chuxiong, Yunnan Province, China, on 1 July 2018. No supplied source gives a fruiting season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
