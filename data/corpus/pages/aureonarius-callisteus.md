## Summary

Aureonarius callisteus (Fr.) Niskanen & Liimat. is a species of agaric fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, known by the common name tawny webcap. The name dates to 2022, when the species was reclassified from Cortinarius. GBIF lists a distribution for Denmark and Norway. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Elias Magnus Fries first described the species in 1818 as Agaricus callisteus. About two decades later he transferred it to the genus Cortinarius in his 1838 work Epicrisis Systematis Mycologici. In 2022 it was transferred from Cortinarius and reclassified as Aureonarius callisteus based on genomic data. This account comes from a single encyclopedia source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
