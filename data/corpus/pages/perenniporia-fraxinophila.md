## Summary

Perenniporia fraxinophila (Peck) Ryvarden, 1972, is a polypore fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Sources describe it as a pathogen of ash trees; MushroomExpert.Com calls it a tough, perennial polypore that causes a heart rot in living ash. GBIF occurrence records include the United States, Canada, Mexico, Cuba and Peru. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single-source account) describes it as parasitic on living ash trees (Fraxinus), especially white ash, and occasionally reported on other hardwoods. It is said to often appear high on the tree, causing an extensive white heart rot. The same source describes it as perennial, growing alone or gregariously, appearing year-round, and fairly widely distributed in North America where ash trees occur. Wikipedia independently describes it only as a plant pathogen infecting ash trees. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
