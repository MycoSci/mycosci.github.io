## Summary

Hebeloma vaccinum is a species of agaric fungus in the family Hymenogastraceae, described by Henri Romagnesi in 1965. It is recorded from many countries, mainly in Europe. A single GBIF-hosted description associates it with Salix (willow). *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

A GBIF-hosted description (a single source) states that H. vaccinum is known from low-elevation dunes and woodlands with Salix, and that it is widespread in Northern Europe. It gives other arctic-alpine collections from the European Alps, the Carpathians in Slovakia and Greenland, always with Salix species. The same source associates it with dwarf Salix in the Rocky Mountains. *[gbif_desc]*
