## Summary

Hydnellum suaveolens (Scop.) P. Karst. 1879 is a toothed fungus in the family Bankeraceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as having a whitish cap, a purplish blue stem and a strong fragrant odor, and as growing under conifers. Wikipedia gives the odor as anise or peppermint. GBIF description records list its distribution as Flanders, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers (especially spruces), growing alone or gregariously in summer and fall. It says the species is widely distributed in northern and montane North America, and on the West Coast from the San Francisco Bay Area northward, and that the illustrated and described collections are from Colorado and Oregon. Wikipedia separately says it is often found beneath conifers. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
