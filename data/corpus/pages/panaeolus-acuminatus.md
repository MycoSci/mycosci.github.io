## Summary

Panaeolus acuminatus (P. Kumm.) Quél., 1872, is a species of mushroom in the family Bolbitiaceae, also known as Panaeolus rickenii. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small dark-spored mushroom with a cap 1-2 cm across and a very long, thin stem (8-12 cm long, 1-2 mm thick) and a black or blackish spore print. Wikipedia describes the gills as dark purplish black and the spores as lemon-shaped. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that P. acuminatus grows in grass and dung and has been found throughout North America and Europe. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or scattered in grass, manured areas, and on dung in spring, summer, and fall, and as widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
