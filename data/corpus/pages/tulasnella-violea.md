## Summary

Tulasnella violea (Quél.) Bourdot & Galzin, 1909, is a species in the family Tulasnellaceae, order Cantharellales. Wikipedia describes its fruit bodies as smooth, waxy, and violet-pink or lilaceous to grey, growing on the underside of fallen branches and logs. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described in 1883 by Lucien Quélet, who emphasized the lilac-pink colour of the fruit bodies and gave basidiospore measurements. Quélet failed to notice the distinctive basidia and placed it among the corticioid fungi in the old form genus Hypochnus. Hubert Bourdot and Amédée Galzin transferred it to Tulasnella in 1909. In 1933 Donald P. Rogers treated T. violea as highly variable and synonymized several previously described species under it, including T. eichleriana and T. thelephorea. In 1994 Peter Roberts rejected that synonymy but noted that differences in spore sizes suggested it was possible that more than one taxon is involved under the name. Wikipedia adds that molecular work has confirmed T. violea is distinct from T. eichleriana, though type specimens have not been sequenced, and that a 2016 paper says it remains possible more than one taxon is involved. This account comes from a single independent source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
