## Summary

Punctularia strigosozonata (Schwein.) P.H.B. Talbot is a fungus in the family Punctulariaceae, order Corticiales. The current combination dates from 1958 and is published in Index of Fungi 2: 518. One source states that it produces the antibiotic phlebiarubrone. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

The species was originally described in 1832 by Lewis David de Schweinitz as a member of the genus Merulius. Patrick Talbot transferred it to the genus Punctularia in 1958. *[wikipedia]*
