## Summary

Entocybe nitida (Quél.) T.J. Baroni, Largent & V. Hofst. (2011) is a mushroom in the family Entolomataceae, and English Wikipedia gives its common name as the shining pinkgill. Wikipedia describes the cap as mostly dark blue, lightening with age, on a blue stipe about 3–8.5 cm long and 0.25–0.5 cm wide that is whitish at the base. The gills are whitish to pinkish and adnexed to notched, and the spore print is pinkish. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that Entocybe nitida is found under conifer trees, where it grows in moss and duff. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
