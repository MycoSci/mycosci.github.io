## Summary

Cortinarius tabularis (Fr.) Fr. (1838) is a species in the family Cortinariaceae, recorded in GBIF from a wide range of countries across Europe, Asia and North America. The one available description, a GBIF database record, characterises it as a greyish brown species with bluish tinges only at the lamellae and stipe apex, and with a whitish stipe that often carries a sparse universal veil. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Only one source, a GBIF description record, states habitat. It reports the species in northeast China in Betula platyphylla forests or mixed with Larix gmelinii, and in east China under broadleaf trees. It also describes the species as widespread in Europe and in North America, from northern Quebec and Alaska, under broadleaf and mixed forests including Picea, Betula, Alnus, Salix, Quercus and Fagus. The same record notes that the species is reported as common and widespread in China, but that its authors found it rare there, occurring only in Heilongjiang, Inner Mongolia and Zhejiang. It says the Chinese distribution needs further confirmation. The Chinese specimens cited in the record were collected in late August (Heilongjiang and Inner Mongolia) and in April (Zhejiang). *[gbif_desc]*
