## Summary

Otidea cochleata (L.) Fuckel (1870) is a species of Pezizales in the family Otideaceae, with occurrence records in GBIF from many countries. Wikipedia describes it as a rare European species that grows singly or in small groups on soil in woodland. It describes the fruiting body as a brown, irregularly shaped cup, split down one side, up to 5 cm high and the same across. This description comes from a single independent source; the iNaturalist text is a copy of it. *[wikipedia]*
