## Discovery and naming

According to Wikispecies, the basionym is Boletus chromipes Frost, published in the Bulletin of the Buffalo Society of Natural Sciences 2: 105 (1874). The epithet is given there as 'chromapes'. The species was later placed in Suillus (Kuntze, 1898), Ceriomyces (Murrill, 1909), Krombholzia (Singer, 1942), Leccinum (Singer, 1947) and Tylopilus (A.H. Smith & Thiers, 1968). It was moved to the new genus Harrya in Halling et al. (2012), Australian Systematic Botany 25(6): 418–431, a paper whose title says it describes the new genera Harrya and Australopilus. Wikispecies also lists Tylopilus cartagoensis Wolfe & Bougher (1993) as a heterotypic synonym. The supplied sources do not say why any of these transfers were made or what material Frost described. *[wikispecies]*
