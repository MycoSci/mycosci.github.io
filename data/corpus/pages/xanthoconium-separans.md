## Summary

Xanthoconium separans (Peck) Halling & Both is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, originally described by Charles Horton Peck as Boletus separans in 1873. It was transferred to Xanthoconium in 1998, although a 2013 molecular phylogenetic analysis places it closer to Boletus sensu stricto. English Wikipedia reports that it grows on the ground in the eastern United States. *[wikipedia]*
