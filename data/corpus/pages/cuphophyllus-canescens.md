## Summary

Cuphophyllus canescens (A.H. Sm. & Hesler) Bon is a species of gilled mushroom in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes it as known from North America. In its wide sense, which includes the recently separated C. atlanticus, it has been assessed as globally "vulnerable" on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species. No source supplied here gives a description of habitat, season or ecology. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described from North Carolina in 1942 by Alexander H. Smith and Lexemuel Ray Hesler as Hygrophorus canescens. Marcel Bon transferred it to Cuphophyllus in 1990, when it was thought also to occur in northern Europe. Wikipedia reports that molecular research based on DNA sequence analysis has since found it to be restricted to North America. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
