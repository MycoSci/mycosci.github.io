## Summary

Amphinema byssoides (Pers.) J. Erikss. 1958 is a species in the family Atheliaceae, order Atheliales, with the English vernacular name "Cratered Duster". Wikipedia describes it as a corticioid fungus known to form mycorrhizal relationships with spruce trees. Beyond that, the supplied sources are mostly ecological studies that mention it only as one member of a mycorrhizal community. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Thelephora byssoides by Christiaan Hendrik Persoon in 1801. John Eriksson transferred it to the genus Amphinema in 1958. The supplied sources give no further detail on the material studied or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is known to form mycorrhizal relationships with spruce trees. In a study of white spruce (Picea glauca) in Alaska, A. byssoides was identified among the ectomycorrhizal fungi on the roots. It was listed among the dominant taxa at low-nitrogen sites and was absent from the high-nitrogen sites (Ecology, 2002). A separate study of ornamental spruce in Calgary, Alberta, in a semiarid climate and slightly to moderately alkaline soils found that A. byssoides formed more than 5% of the mycorrhizae (Mycologia, 1989). GBIF's distribution listing for the species names Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Madeira Island, Faial Island, Terceira Island and Vermont. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2, gbif_desc]*
