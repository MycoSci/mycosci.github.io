## Summary

Hygrocybe ceracea (Sowerby) P. Kumm., 1871, is a waxcap in the family Hygrophoraceae, known in English as the Butter Waxcap. Mushroom World lists it from Europe and North America and describes it as small, with a cap 0.5–3.5 cm across that is yellow to orange-yellow, and a yellow stem 2–5 cm tall. A white spore print is also given. *[mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World, a single aggregator source, describes it as found in unimproved grasslands, meadows and mossy areas, and as typically encountered in late summer to autumn. It gives the location as Europe and North America. A GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (including the Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Sweden and Vermont. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
