## Summary

Conocybe tenera (Schaeff.) Kühner, 1935, is a member of the family Bolbitiaceae with occurrence records in many countries. Wikipedia describes it as the type species of the genus Conocybe and as a small saprotrophic mushroom with a conic to convex, smooth, orangish-brown cap up to 2.5 cm wide, striate almost to the center. The same source gives the stem as 3–9 cm long, hollow, without a ring, with adnexed pale brown gills and a rusty brown spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as saprotrophic, common in disturbed areas in North America and widely distributed across the world. A GBIF description record lists its distribution only as BR; SE; Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
