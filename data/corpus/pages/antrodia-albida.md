## Summary

Antrodia albida (Fr.) Donk is a species of fungus in the family Fomitopsidaceae, order Polyporales. One source describes it as growing on the dead wood of deciduous trees and as widely distributed, recorded from Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania, North America and South America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Elias Magnus Fries first described the fungus as Daedalea albida in his 1815 work Observationes mycologicae. Wikispecies lists the basionym's type details as 'Forsander s.n.; Tab. 38, figs E-F (Battarra, Fung.)', from trunks of Betula in Sweden. Marinus Anton Donk later transferred it to Antrodia. Wikispecies also records earlier transfers to Lenzites (1838) and Trametes (1849). *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*
