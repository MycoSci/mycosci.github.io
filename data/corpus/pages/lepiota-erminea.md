## Summary

Lepiota erminea (Fr.) P. Kumm. 1871, the dune dapperling, is a species of agaric fungus in the family Agaricaceae, and Wikipedia states that it is found in Europe and North America. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a medium-sized lepiotoid mushroom that is apparently fairly widespread in North America but not common, and rarely if ever included in North American field guides. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that Lepiota erminea is very similar to Lepiota clypeolaria in stature and in its long, fusiform spores, which led one nineteenth-century mycologist to describe it as a white variety of that species. The same page also lists the name Lepiota alba as an alternative. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in grassy areas and disturbed soil, in summer and fall.
