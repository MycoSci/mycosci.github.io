## Summary

Ramaria flava (Schaeff.) Quél. 1888 is a coral fungus in the family Gomphaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a yellow coral mushroom found in Europe, also native to temperate areas of southern Chile and the south of Brazil (state of Rio Grande do Sul), where it is known by the local name "changle". A GBIF distribution entry lists Belgium (Flemish and Walloon Regions), Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
