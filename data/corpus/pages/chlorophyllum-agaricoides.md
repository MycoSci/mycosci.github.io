## Summary

Chlorophyllum agaricoides (Czern.) Vellinga (2002) is a species in the family Agaricaceae; Endoptychum agaricoides is a synonym. Wikipedia describes it as a secotioid mushroom: the gleba of underdeveloped gills is completely enclosed by a cap that never fully opens. The same source says it is protected in Hungary and believed to be in decline across Europe. *[wikipedia]*
