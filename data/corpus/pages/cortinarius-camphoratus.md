## Summary

Cortinarius camphoratus (Fr.) Fr. is an agaric fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, known as the goatcheese webcap or stinking webcap. It is recorded in Europe and North America, and Wikipedia describes it as having pale blue-lilac colors when young and a strong, distinctive odor. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet camphoratus refers to the odor resembling camphor. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that Elias Fries first described the species as Agaricus camphoratus in his 1821 Systema Mycologicum, and transferred it to Cortinarius in 1838. Friedrich Otto Wünsche placed it in Inoloma in 1877. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to Wikipedia, the species grows on the ground in a mycorrhizal association with conifers, especially spruce and also firs, in Europe and North America. Mushrooms grow singly, scattered, or in groups, usually between September and October. *[wikipedia]*
