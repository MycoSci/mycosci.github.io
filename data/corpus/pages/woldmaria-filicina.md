## Summary

Woldmaria filicina (Peck) Knudsen (1996) is a species in the family Niaceae, published in Index of Fungi 6: 825. Wikipedia describes the genus Woldmaria as monotypic, with W. filicina as its only species, found in Europe and North America. ## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the genus Woldmaria was described by William Bridge Cooke in 1961 with Woldmaria crocea as the type species. That species was subsequently moved into synonymy with W. filicina. *[wikipedia]*
