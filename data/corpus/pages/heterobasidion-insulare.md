## Summary

Heterobasidion insulare (Murrill) Ryvarden, published in 1972, is a species in the family Bondarzewiaceae (order Russulales). It was originally described as Trametes insularis Murrill in 1908. GBIF occurrence records place it in Japan, India, China, Russia, the Philippines, South Korea, Pakistan, Nepal, Taiwan, Myanmar, Indonesia and North Korea. *[wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Trametes insularis Murrill, Bull. Torrey Bot. Club 35(8): 405 (1908), with holotype Murrill 5001 and lectotype NY 705030, from logs of Pinus insularis on Luzon, Philippines. The combination Heterobasidion insulare (Murrill) Ryvarden [as 'insularis'] was published in Norweg. Journ. Bot. 19(3–4): 237 (1972). Wikispecies also lists Fomitopsis insularis (Murrill) Imazeki (1943) as a homotypic synonym. It lists Heterobasidion ecrustosum Tokuda, T.Hatt. & Y.C.Dai (2009) as a heterotypic synonym, with holotype TFM F-18051 from the trunk of Pinus luchuensis on Okinawa, Japan. The supplied sources do not say why the species was transferred between genera. *[wikispecies]*
