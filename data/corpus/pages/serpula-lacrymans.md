## Summary

Serpula lacrymans (Wulfen) J. Schröt. is a species in the family Serpulaceae, order Boletales. Wikipedia describes it as a fungus known for causing dry rot, and as the most damaging destroyer of indoor wood construction materials in temperate regions. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet is derived from the Latin words serpula, for "creeping", and lacrymans, meaning "making tears". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Boletus lacrymans by Franz Xavier von Wulfen in 1781. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes S. lacrymans as a common indoor biodeterioration agent that has been found in only a few natural environments: the Himalayas, Northern California, the Czech Republic and east Asia. It says the fungus causes brown rot in wood. It reports a preference for 21 to 22 °C, survival between 3 and 26 °C, and an ideal wood moisture content of 30 to 40 percent for fruit body formation. It says the fungus uses calcium and iron ions extracted from plaster, brick and stone to help break down wood. These details come from a single encyclopedia source. *[wikipedia]*
