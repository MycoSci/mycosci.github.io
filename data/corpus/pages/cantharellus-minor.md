## Summary

Cantharellus minor Peck (1872) is a chanterelle in the family Hydnaceae, described from New York and known as the Small Chanterelle. Wikipedia describes it as native to eastern North America and one of the smallest species in its genus. MushroomExpert.Com likewise describes it as much smaller than the average chanterelle, with more slender proportions. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes C. minor as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, growing alone, scattered, or occasionally gregariously in moss, from late spring through fall, and widely distributed east of the Rocky Mountains; its described collections are from Illinois, Indiana, and Québec. Wikipedia says the species is native to eastern North America and fruits from June to September, and that it is only suspected of being mycorrhizal, in association with oaks and moss. Wikipedia also reports that it has recently been found in semi-evergreen to evergreen forests in the Western Ghats, Kerala, India, where it forms ectomycorrhizal associations with tree species including Vateria indica, Diospyros malabarica, Hopea parviflora and Myristica species. MushroomExpert.Com, citing Petersen (1979), also mentions a hemlock-associated form from Great Smoky Mountains National Park, Cantharellus minor f. intensissima, described as brilliant orange with usually copiously anastomosing lamellar folds. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
