## Summary

Aspropaxillus candidus (Bres.) M.M. Moser (1953) is a fungus in the family Tricholomataceae, order Agaricales, based on Bresadola's earlier name and also known as Leucopaxillus candidus. GBIF holds 569 occurrence records across many countries, mainly in Europe and North America. The English Wikipedia article on A. giganteus, the only descriptive source available, says A. candidus resembles that species but is darker in colour and smaller, with a cap 6 to 30 cm broad, and states that A. candidus is conspecific with A. giganteus. *[wikipedia]*
