## Discovery and naming

The English Wikipedia article that the name G. penetrans redirects to is titled for G. sapineus. It says that, citing Michael Kuo, Elias Magnus Fries made an arbitrary distinction between G. sapineus and G. penetrans. Fries first labelled G. penetrans merely a form of G. sapineus in 1815, then reversed himself and treated them as separate in 1821. The same article places G. penetrans and G. nevadensis in a G. sapineus complex with similar macroscopic morphology and ecology. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World (an aggregator, single source) describes G. penetrans as saprotrophic on decaying wood, especially coniferous stumps and fallen logs, in Europe and North America, fruiting through autumn and sometimes into early winter. A GBIF description attached to this name records Pakistani collections (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa) at 2250–2500 m, growing solitary or in small groups on mulch-rich soil under Pinus wallichiana and under Picea smithiana. The Wikipedia article is titled for G. sapineus, and its habitat statements (rotting wood under conifers in North America) are for that name, so they are not applied here. *[mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
