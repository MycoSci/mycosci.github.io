## Summary

Cortinarius bolaris (Pers.) Zaw. is a species of webcap in the family Cortinariaceae, with the English vernacular name dappled webcap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as fairly easily recognized for a Cortinarius by the bright, brownish red scales covering its cap and stem, and notes a cylindric stem, rusty orange bruising at the stem base, and a dry cap and stem. Like other Cortinarius species, it grows on the ground, has a cortina, and gives a rusty brown spore print. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods, perhaps exclusively with beech and oaks, though it is sometimes reported under conifers. It is often found in wet areas, growing alone, gregariously, or in small troops, in summer and fall. The same source gives its distribution as widely distributed in eastern North America and documented in Costa Rica. A GBIF description entry lists a distribution of Vermont. These habitat and season statements come from a single source (MushroomExpert.Com). *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
