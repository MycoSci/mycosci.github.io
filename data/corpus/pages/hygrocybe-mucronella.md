## Summary

Hygrocybe mucronella (Fr.) P. Karst. 1879, the bitter waxcap, is a gilled mushroom in the family Hygrophoraceae. Wikipedia describes it as a European species of grassland that fruits in autumn. It is assessed as globally vulnerable on the IUCN Red List. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described by Elias Magnus Fries as Hygrophorus mucronellus and was later moved to the genus Hygrocybe. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species is widespread throughout Europe. It typically grows in old, unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns) and produces fruit bodies in autumn. The same source says recent research suggests waxcaps are neither mycorrhizal nor saprotrophic but may be associated with mosses. It is described as typical of waxcap grasslands, a declining habitat. *[wikipedia]*
