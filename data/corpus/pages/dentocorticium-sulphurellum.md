## Summary

Dentocorticium sulphurellum (Peck) M.J. Larsen & Gilb. is a crust fungus in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as having a toothed surface and a sulphur-yellow colour, with dendrohyphidia in the hymenium under the microscope. The same source states that it is found in North America and Japan. This description comes from a single source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Charles Horton Peck originally described the species in 1879 as Hydnum sulphurellum, and it was transferred to Dentocorticium in 1974. No supplied source gives the type material or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*
