## Summary

Ramaria fennica (P. Karst.) Ricken, 1920, commonly called the bitter coral, is a coral mushroom in the family Gomphaceae. Wikipedia reports it from Australia, Europe and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet fennica refers to Finland, where the original collections were made. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Petter Karsten first described the species in 1868 as Clavaria fennica, from collections made in Finnish fir woods. Adalbert Ricken transferred it to Ramaria in 1920. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that fruit bodies grow on the ground singly, scattered, or in groups, often with tanoak in North America, and that the species is generally uncommon but sometimes fruits prolifically. It is also known from Europe and Australia. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) treats R. fennica as a species group. It says the strict R. fennica is a European species growing under conifers. It describes the illustrated Midwestern collections as matching R. fennica in morphology but R. fumigata in ecology (mycorrhizal with hardwoods, summer and fall, eastern North America). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
