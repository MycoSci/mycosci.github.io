## Summary

Climacocystis borealis (Fr.) Kotl. & Pouzar (1958) is a poroid fungus in the order Polyporales, placed in the family Fomitopsidaceae. It is described as widely distributed, with records from Eurasia, Oceania and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described in 1821 by the Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries, and that it has since acquired an extensive synonymy. It also states that the genus Climacocystis was circumscribed in 1958 by the Czech mycologists František Kotlaba and Zdeněk Pouzar, and that until 2014 C. borealis was the genus's only member. This is a single-source account, and no source gives the material or reasons behind the transfers. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as both a saprophyte and a secondary pathogen that causes a heart rot in the roots and bole of host trees. It says the species can be found on the base of conifer trees, and that in China it is recorded from Shanxi, Guangdong, Sichuan and Tibet. The GBIF description record lists distribution entries for Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. No supplied source gives a season. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
