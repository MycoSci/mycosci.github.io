## Summary

Phellodon tomentosus (L.) Banker (1906) is a tooth fungus in the family Thelephoraceae, known in English as Woolly Tooth. It was first described by Linnaeus as Hydnum tomentosum in 1753. Wikipedia and Mushroom World both give a cap 1.5–5 cm wide with spines on the underside, and both give its range as Asia, Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
