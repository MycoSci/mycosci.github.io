## Summary

Entoloma sericellum (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a species of Entolomataceae. Wikipedia describes a dry white cap up to 5 cm wide covered in tiny fibrils, white fragile gills, and a thin white stipe up to 5 cm long that is sometimes translucent. It says the cap and stipe yellow with age and the gills turn pinkish from the spores as they mature. *[wikipedia]*
