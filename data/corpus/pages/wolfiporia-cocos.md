## Summary

Wolfiporia cocos (F.A. Wolf) Ryvarden & Gilb. (1984) is a species in the family Fomitopsidaceae, order Polyporales, with the common name tuckahoe. Its synonyms include Poria cocos, Pachyma cocos, Macrohyporia cocos and Sclerotium cocos, and GBIF holds occurrence records from about 30 countries, including the US, China, Japan and Chile. One encyclopedia source describes it as a wood-decay fungus with a subterranean growth habit that forms a large, long-lasting underground sclerotium resembling a small coconut. *[wikipedia]*
