## Summary

Porphyrellus porphyrosporus (Fr.) E.-J. Gilbert, 1931, is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, commonly called the dusky bolete. Wikipedia describes it as a rare fungus with a dark, purple-brown cap and stem, and says it is not easy to spot despite its large size. Its tubes turn blue-green when cut or bruised, and its spore print is reddish-brown. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as widespread in Europe, especially in the north, but nowhere particularly common, and says it can be found in northern North America. It states that fruit bodies appear from late summer to autumn, often in small groups, associated with broad-leaved trees such as beech and oak. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Walloon Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
