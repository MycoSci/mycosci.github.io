## Summary

Entoloma pleopodium (Bull.) Noordel. (1985) is a species of mushroom in the family Entolomataceae, known in English as the olive pinkgill or aromatic pinkgill. Wikipedia states that it can be found in Europe and the Pacific Northwest. The same source describes a distinctive smell said to resemble apples or pear drops. *[wikipedia]*
