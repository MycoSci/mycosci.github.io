## Summary

Antella americana (Ryvarden & Gilb.) Ryvarden is a species in the family Steccherinaceae (order Polyporales). It was first described as Antrodiella americana in 1984 from a holotype collected in Pennsylvania, U.S.A., and was later placed in Antella. The catalog holds 362 GBIF occurrence records across several countries. *[wikispecies, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Antrodiella americana Ryvarden & Gilb., published in Mycotaxon 19: 138 (1984). The holotype is Overholts & White 14364, held at PAC, from Pennsylvania, U.S.A. In the same year Ginns published the combination Flaviporus americanus (Ryvarden & Gilb.) Ginns (Mycotaxon 21: 326), which Wikispecies lists as a homotypic synonym. The combination in Antella is credited to Miettinen in Miettinen & Ryvarden, Ann. Bot. Fenn. 53(3–4): 158 (2016). The supplied sources do not say why the species was moved between genera. *[wikispecies]*
