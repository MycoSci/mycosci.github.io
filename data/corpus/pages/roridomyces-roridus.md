## Summary

Roridomyces roridus (Fr.) Rexer, published in 1994 in Index of Fungi 7: 321, is an agaric in the family Mycenaceae. It is known in English as the dripping bonnet or the slippery mycena. Wikipedia describes the cap as tannish, fading to whitish or dirty yellow, broadly convex and 5–15 mm across, with a whitish stipe covered in a thick, slippery slime layer. The same source says the species can be bioluminescent and is one of several causes of foxfire. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. This is a distribution listing only. *[gbif_desc]*
