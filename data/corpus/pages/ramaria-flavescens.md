## Summary

Ramaria flavescens is a species of coral fungus in the family Gomphaceae. It was first described as Clavaria flavescens by Jacob Christian Schäffer in 1762 and was transferred to Ramaria by Ron Peterson in 1974. The IUCN Red List labels it Critically Endangered in Denmark and Data Deficient in the Czech Republic. *[wikipedia]*
