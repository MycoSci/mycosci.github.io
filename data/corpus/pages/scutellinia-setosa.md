## Summary

Scutellinia setosa (Nees) Kuntze, 1891, is an apothecial fungus in the family Pyronemataceae. Wikipedia describes its fruit bodies as disc-shaped, with thick black setae ("hairs") around the cup rim. Literature in the supplied sources records it from Spain (a 2003 paper calls it the second Spanish record) and Madeira (new to the Madeira catalogue in 2007). *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows on the rotting wood of deciduous trees, and gives its range as Europe (where it was originally described), North America and Central America. This is a single-source claim; the iNaturalist text is a declared copy of it. A 2007 Madeira forays paper lists the species as new to the Madeira catalogue, and GBIF distribution records list Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Madeira and Vermont. Neither of those sources states a substrate, host or season. *[wikipedia, lit_3, gbif_desc]*
