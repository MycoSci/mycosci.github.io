## Summary

Cortinarius flexipes (Pers.) Fr. is a species in the family Cortinariaceae, with its current name published in 1838. Wikipedia describes it as a small brown mushroom in the genus Cortinarius and gives Pelargonium webcap as its common name. Wikipedia also places it in the Telamonia group and describes it as hygrophanous, thin-fleshed, and dry in both cap and stipe. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that the species is called the Pelargonium webcap because of its unusual smell of Pelargonium (the household "geranium"). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia notes that Cortinarius paleaceus and Cortinarius paleiferus were commonly identified in Europe as separate species. It says these are now combined into one species and considered only varieties of C. flexipes. It gives the difference as C. paleiferus having more widely spaced gills and a pale violet mycelium at the stipe base. *[wikipedia]*
