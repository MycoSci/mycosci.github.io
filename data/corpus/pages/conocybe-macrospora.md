## Summary

Conocybe macrospora (G.F. Atk.) Hauskn. is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Bolbitiaceae. It was first described by George Francis Atkinson in 1918 as Galerula macrospora and moved to Conocybe by Anton Hausknecht in 2003. The Wikipedia article is the only independent source with descriptive text for this species. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was described in 1918 by the American mycologist George Francis Atkinson as Galerula macrospora. Wikipedia lists later names now treated as synonyms: Galera tenera f. bispora (John Eugene Sass, 1929), Conocybe ochracea f. macrospora (Robert Kühner and Roy Watling, 1971), Conocybe rubiginosa as described by Watling, and Conocybe pubescens var. macrospora (Erhard Ludwig, 2007). It says Sass's form differed from Galera tenera only in having two-spored basidia, and that examining the basidia was the only means noted of telling them apart. Wikipedia states that Hausknecht reclassified the species as Conocybe macrospora in 2003. This account rests on a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat text, says the specimens Atkinson studied grew on the ground among mixed grasses and moss at the edge of a coniferous wood near Stockholm, Sweden. It says Hausknecht described them as growing in grassy, nitrate-rich meadows or in dung, disturbed ground and compost, and rarely in leaf litter or on the edge of burnt areas. Wikipedia also states that the species is common in Europe. No supplied source gives a fruiting season. This habitat account rests on a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*
