## Summary

Russula foetens Pers. (1796) is a species in the family Russulaceae, known in English as the Foetid Russula and the Stinking Brittlegill. Wikipedia describes it as a common Russula found in deciduous and coniferous forests, with a cap up to 10 cm across that is very slimy when young. *[wikipedia]*
