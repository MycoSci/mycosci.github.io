## Summary

Strobilurus conigenoides (Ellis) Singer (1962) is a small agaric in the family Physalacriaceae, known in English as the magnolia-cone mushroom. Wikipedia describes the cap as white to yellowish and up to 12 mm wide, with a white spore print. It is recorded in the United States and several other countries, and is described as growing on fallen Magnolia cones. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, and the iNaturalist entry derived from it, state that the species grows on fallen 'cones' (follicles) of Magnolia species, naming M. fraseri, M. grandiflora, M. tripetala and M. acuminata. Wikipedia describes it as distributed mainly in southeastern North America and occurring most often in autumn. It says the species is widely reported to be specific to Magnolia follicles, but that a 1991 study reported it on fruits of Liquidambar styraciflua (American sweetgum).
