## Summary

Mycopan scabripes (Murrill) Redhead, Moncalvo & Vilgalys (2013) is the only species currently placed in the genus Mycopan, which was formerly classified in Hydropus or Mycena. It produces dusky-coloured, mycenoid fruit bodies with a cap up to 3 cm wide and a notably scruffy stipe. It is described as occurring in North America and Europe. *[wikipedia]*
