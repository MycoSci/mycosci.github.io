## Summary

Polyporus lipsiensis (Batsch) E.H.L. Krause, 1928 is a species in the family Polyporaceae; Ganoderma lipsiense is listed as a synonym. A 2022 laboratory study tested mycelial extracts of the species against the protozoan Giardia duodenalis and reported that it showed the highest anti-parasitic activity of the seven mushrooms tested.
