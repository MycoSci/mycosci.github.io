## Summary

Helvella elastica Bull. (1785) is a species of Helvellaceae in the order Pezizales, and it has been recorded in some 40 countries. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com describe it as having a saddle-shaped, brownish cap on a whitish to cream stem. MushroomExpert.Com separates it from Helvella latispora by cap margins that fold inward toward the stem and a bald undersurface. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says the fungus fruits singly, scattered or clustered on the ground or on wood in coniferous and deciduous woods, in summer and fall, and reports it from Europe, Japan, China and western North America. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as 'probably mycorrhizal', growing alone or gregariously under conifers or hardwoods, on the ground or rarely from rotting wood, in late summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. Mushroom World says it grows on soil in mixed woods. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld]*
