## Summary

Phlebia coccineofulva Schwein. (1832) is a crust fungus in the family Meruliaceae, known in English as the scarlet waxcrust. MushroomExpert.Com describes the fruiting body as a spreading patch that is very bright scarlet to scarlet orange when fresh and darkens with age to reddish brown. It is reported from North America, continental Europe and northern Asia. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, spreading in patches across decorticated deadwood of oaks and other hardwoods and causing a white rot. It is annual, fruiting in summer and fall (and over winter in warm climates), and is widely distributed in North America but much more common in the upper Midwest and the northeast. Wikipedia describes it as a saprophyte on decaying stumps and woody forest debris in North America, continental Europe and northern Asia. It has been recorded in Koygorodskiy National Park (Komi Republic, Russia), where it is one of seven species listed in the Red Book of the Komi Republic, and it was newly reported from India (Shimla District, Himachal Pradesh) in 2019. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*
