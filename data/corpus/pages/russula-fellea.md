## Summary

Russula fellea (Fr.) Fr. is a brittlegill in the family Russulaceae, recorded mainly from European countries. Wikipedia describes it as straw or honey coloured, with the common names geranium-scented russula and bitter russula, and as inedible. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet is derived from the Latin adjective felleus, meaning "biliary", in reference to the bitter taste, reminiscent of bile. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, it was first described by the Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries in 1821 as Agaricus felleus, and was placed in the genus Russula in 1838. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, says it appears in autumn and is found with beech (Fagus) in Britain, but in Europe sometimes occurs with spruce (Picea). It says the species is normally associated with well-drained acid soils and grows in deciduous and coniferous woods and forests. *[wikipedia]*
