## Summary

Bjerkandera fumosa (Pers.) P. Karst. is a poroid fungus in the family Phanerochaetaceae, order Polyporales, known in English as the big smoky bracket. Its basionym is Boletus fumosus, and the combination in Bjerkandera dates to 1879. Only a small amount of descriptive text was available for this page. *[wikipedia]*
