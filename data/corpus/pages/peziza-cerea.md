## Summary

Peziza cerea Fr., the Cellar Cup, is an ascomycete in the family Pezizaceae. Wikipedia describes it as usually less than 5 cm across, with a cup that is round when young and flattened and irregular when mature. Wikipedia also lists it as inedible. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia glosses the genus name Peziza as meaning 'a sort of mushroom without a root or stalk'. No supplied source discusses the derivation of the epithet cerea. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says P. cerea can be identified by growth in cellars, damp mortar, soil between pavement slabs, on rotting sandbags, plant material or manure, and that it is found all year round. It describes the species as saprobic, found on paths and in cellars, and widely distributed throughout America and Europe. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
