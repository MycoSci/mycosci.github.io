## Summary

Eonema pyriforme (M.P. Christ.) Redhead, Lücking & Lawrey (2009) is a corticioid fungus in the family Hygrophoraceae. It is the only species in the monotypic genus Eonema. It was previously classified in the genus Athelia, until molecular analysis showed it was nested within the Hygrophoraceae. *[wikipedia]*
