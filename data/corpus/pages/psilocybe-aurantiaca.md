## Discovery and naming

According to the Wikipedia article on Leratiomyces ceres (a single-source account), Mordecai Cubitt Cooke originated the names Agaricus squamosus f. aurantiacus and Agaricus thraustus var. aurantiacus around 1885, and these later gave rise to the name Stropharia aurantiaca. The article says the name is defined by Cooke's illustration in his Handbook of British Fungi. In 2004 Richard Fortey found that this illustration was not of L. ceres, as had generally been assumed, but of L. squamosus var. thaustus [sic]. The article therefore advises that the name aurantiaca be avoided when applied to L. ceres. *[wikipedia]*
