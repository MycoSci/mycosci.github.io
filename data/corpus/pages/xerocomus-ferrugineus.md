## Summary

Xerocomus ferrugineus (Schaeff.) Alessio, 1985 is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, published in Index of Fungi 5: 623. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a member of the Xerocomus subtomentosus group, with a distinctively ribbed stem and a cap that is finely velvety, at least when young. That source also says it is unclear whether the North American and European versions are the same species. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as mycorrhizal with spruces and other conifers, occasionally reported under hardwoods (especially in mixed conifer-hardwood forests) and shrubs. It grows alone, scattered, or gregariously, in summer and fall. The same source calls it widely distributed in northern and montane North America and widely distributed in Europe; its illustrated and described collections are from Colorado and Finland. *[mushroomexpert]*
