## Summary

Amaropostia stiptica (Pers.) B.K. Cui, L.L. Shen & Y.C. Dai (2018) is a polypore in the order Polyporales, known in English as the Bitter Bracket. Sources describe it as extremely bitter-tasting. A 2025 study isolated triterpene glucosides (oligoporins) from it that activate human bitter taste receptors. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*
