## Summary

Leccinum cyaneobasileucum is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, described by Lannoy & Estadès in 1991 (Index of Fungi 6: 149) with France as the type locality. Wikipedia describes fruit bodies with caps 5–15 cm wide in hazel, reddish-yellow or walnut-brown shades, and a white to grey stipe 7–20 cm long covered with brownish scales. It was originally found growing under silver birch. *[wikipedia]*
