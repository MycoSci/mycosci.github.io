## Summary

Candolleomyces candolleanus (Fr.) D. Wächt. & A. Melzer (2020) is a species of fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, formerly known as Psathyrella candolleana. It is called the pale brittlestem or common brittlestem. The sources supplied place it in Europe and North America, and one notes a 2014 report from Iraq. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

The species was formerly placed in Psathyrella as P. candolleana and was transferred to Candolleomyces by D. Wächter and A. Melzer in 2020. A recent phylogenetic study reported in the GBIF descriptions treats C. candolleanus as the name of a species complex of at least 20 proposed species, and lists a sequenced neotype for C. candolleanus itself. The supplied sources do not say why the transfer was made or from what original material the species was first described. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as commonly found growing in small groups around stumps and tree roots on lawns and pastures in North America and Europe. Mushroom World says it typically grows tufted on soil near broad-leaf trees, and on stumps and other woody debris. The two descriptions differ on whether the fruiting bodies are grouped or tufted. A phylogenetic study of the wider C. candolleanus complex characterizes its members as usually not caespitose and typically terrestrial, sometimes lignicolous. That statement describes the complex, not this species alone. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
