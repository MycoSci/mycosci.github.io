## Summary

Pholiota limonella (Peck) Sacc. is a species of Pholiota in the family Strophariaceae, based on Agaricus limonellus and recorded in North America and Europe. Mushroom World describes it as a medium to large, orange-yellow agaric with a slimy cap, growing in clusters on wood. It can reportedly be separated from P. adiposa and P. aurivella reliably only by spore size. *[mushroomworld, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Sources differ on the substrate. Wikipedia says it is commonly found on fallen beech trunks and other hardwoods. iNaturalist (a declared derivative of Wikipedia, so not independent) says it is saprobic on coniferous stumps and logs. Mushroom World says it grows in clusters on downed logs, standing dead trees and stumps, and gives its location as North America and Europe. No source gives a season. *[wikipedia, inaturalist, mushroomworld]*
