## Summary

Pholiota lucifera (Lasch) Quél. (1872) is a species in the family Strophariaceae. A 2014 survey of wood-decaying macrofungi on Vasilyovska Mountain, Forebalkan, Bulgaria, reported it as the second record for that country. A 2008 study of Strophariaceae grown in culture included it among species whose conidiogenesis follows a mode with hyaline, rod-shaped conidia. *[lit_1, lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

Recorded from the southwestern slopes of Vasilyovska Mountain (Forebalkan), Bulgaria, in a 2007–2011 survey of wood-decaying macrofungi, where it was the second national record. GBIF's distribution description lists Belgium (Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[lit_2, gbif_desc]*
