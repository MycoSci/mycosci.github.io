## Summary

Yuchengia narymica (Pilát) B.K. Cui, C.L. Zhao & R.B. Steffen, 2013, is a crust fungus in the family Polyporaceae and the only species in the genus Yuchengia. It was formerly placed in Perenniporia. Wikipedia describes it as having a cream to yellowish buff pore surface with angular pores. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming
