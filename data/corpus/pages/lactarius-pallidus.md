## Summary

Lactarius pallidus Pers. (1797), the pale milkcap, is a species of milkcap in the family Russulaceae. GBIF occurrence records place it across much of Europe, with scattered records elsewhere. One source describes it as growing mycorrhizally under beech, and less often under birch. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as growing mycorrhizally under beech, and also found under birch. It is typically half-buried in leaf litter, sometimes in large groups, and occurs throughout summer and autumn. The same source calls it common in Europe, much rarer in North America, and also present in Australia. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a declared derivative of it). *[wikipedia]*
