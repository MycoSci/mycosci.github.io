## Summary

Hydnellum geogenium (Fr.) Banker is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, order Thelephorales. Wikipedia describes it as occurring in Europe and North America, and reports that its fruitbodies contain a yellow pigment compound called geogenin. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus grows in coniferous woods in Europe and North America, forms mycorrhizae with Picea trees, and is widely distributed in temperate regions of the northern hemisphere. Its northern range extends to the Polar circle, and in Europe the northern extent is southeast Norway. The same source says it appears on the red lists of Germany, Poland and Sweden and is considered endangered in Switzerland. No supplied source gives a fruiting season. *[wikipedia]*
