## Summary

Tricholoma fulvum (DC.) Bigeard & H. Guill. (1909) is a member of the Tricholomataceae, which Mushroom World lists from Europe and North America. Wikipedia describes a pale brown to reddish-brown cap with crimped edges and a white spore powder, and Mushroom World gives the cap as 5-10 cm across. It is known in English as the Birch Knight. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
