## Discovery and naming

According to Wikispecies, the species was first published as Sphaeria eschscholtzii Ehrenb. in 1820 (Horae Physicae Berolinenses 8: 89), spelled 'eschscholzii' in the original. Fries (1823) treated it as Sphaeria concentrica variety b. eschscholtzii, and Starbäck (1901) treated it as Daldinia concentrica var. eschscholtzii. Rehm published the combination Daldinia eschscholtzii in 1904 (Annales Mycologici 2(2): 175). Wikispecies also lists three heterotypic synonyms: Hypoxylon stratosum Sacc. (1891), Daldinia stratosa (Sacc.) Sacc. & Trotter (1913) and Daldinia luzonensis Rehm (1913). The sources give no account of the type material or of the reasons for these transfers. *[wikispecies]*
