## Summary

Marchandiomyces corallinus (Roberge) Diederich & D. Hawksw., 1990, is a fungus in the family Corticiaceae (order Corticiales). The supplied sources describe it as a lichenicolous fungus, a parasite on living lichens. Wikipedia notes that it is a basidiomycete, whereas about 95% of lichen parasites belong to the Ascomycota. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Illosporium corallinum Roberge, published in Desmazières in 1847, and lists Aegerita physciae Vouaux (1914) as a synonym. It cites Diederich, Mycotaxon 37: 312 (1990), as the primary reference for the current combination. *[wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a parasite of lichens, particularly those in the genera Physcia, Parmelia, Flavoparmelia, Lepraria, Pertusaria, Lasallia and Lecanora, and says it is commonly found in eastern North America and Europe. Wikispecies gives its life habit as a parasite on living lichens, hosts "various lichens". GBIF distribution records list Denmark, Norway, Sweden, several regions of Portugal (Algarve, Trás-os-Montes e Alto Douro, Minho, Estremadura) and Vermont. *[wikipedia, wikispecies, gbif_desc]*
