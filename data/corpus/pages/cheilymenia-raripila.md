## Summary

Cheilymenia raripila (W. Phillips) Dennis, 1960, is a member of the Pyronemataceae (Pezizales). A 1986 description (lit_1) gives its apothecia as gregarious to closely crowded, 1–1.5 mm across, with a pale yellowish to yellowish-brown, cup- to saucer-shaped receptacle bearing only a few inconspicuous hairs, and a disc that is deep yellow when young and paler later. It is a small species known from a wide range of countries in the catalog record. *[lit_1]*

## Discovery and naming

A 2003 taxonomic revision of Cheilymenia in Czech Mycology (lit_2) treated C. raripila in detail, placing it in ser. Raripilosae. That revision reports that examination of the type material [K(M)] showed Lasiobolus dubius Starbäck to be a later synonym of C. raripila. *[lit_2]*
