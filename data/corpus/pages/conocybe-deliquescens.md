## Summary

Conocybe deliquescens Hauskn. & Krisai (2006) is a member of the Bolbitiaceae; Gastrocybe lateritia is listed as a synonym. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a slimy, flimsy mushroom with a rusty-brown cap and gills. Its weak stem bends over, and the cap and gills dissolve into mush very quickly, so it rarely lasts much past about noon. The same page cites researchers (Hallen, Watling & Adams 2003) who theorize that the consistently wilted features may be caused by a bacterial infection. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or scattered in grassy areas in summer and fall. It says the species is probably widely distributed east of the Great Plains and more common in the Great Lakes region; the described collections are from Illinois. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*
