## Summary

Leucocybe candicans (Pers.) Vizzini, P. Alvarado, G. Moreno & Consiglio (2015) is placed in the Tricholomataceae (Agaricales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a little clitocyboid mushroom whose young cap has a whitish, hoary coating that disappears as the mushroom fades to white. The same source gives a cap of 1–2 cm across and a white spore print. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single source), it is saprobic, growing scattered or gregariously in leaf litter under hardwoods or, less frequently, in needle duff under conifers. A GBIF distribution record lists DK, NO, SE and Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
