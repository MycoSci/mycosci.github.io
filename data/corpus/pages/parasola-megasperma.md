## Summary

Parasola megasperma (P.D. Orton) Redhead, Vilgalys & Hopple (2001) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae, with Coprinus megaspermus listed as a synonym. A GBIF-hosted morphological description gives a cap 15–18 mm across when mature, sulcate-striate and ochreous to dark yellow-brown at the centre, on a hollow stipe 48–71 mm long, with smooth, dark brown to almost black spores. The same record lists distribution in Belgium (Walloon Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[gbif_desc]*
