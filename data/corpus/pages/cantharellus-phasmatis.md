## Summary

Cantharellus phasmatis Foltz & T.J. Volk (2013) is a species of chanterelle in the family Hydnaceae. It was described as new to science in 2013 from a collection made in Hixon Forest Park, La Crosse County, Wisconsin. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

The species was described in 2013 alongside Cantharellus flavus and Cantharellus spectaculus, all three previously considered Cantharellus cibarius. The description rests on morphological differences and nuclear DNA sequence data (nLSU, ITS, TEF1) (lit_1). The description was based on a specimen collected in Hixon Forest Park, La Crosse County, Wisconsin (wikipedia). An earlier (2011) treatment of the same three species reports that C. phasmatis and C. flavus share a most recent common ancestor with Cantharellus tenuithrix, a species described from the southern United States (lit_2). *[lit_1, lit_2, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

The type specimen was collected in mixed deciduous forest in Hixon Forest Park, La Crosse County, Wisconsin (wikipedia). The 2013 description reports that the three new species it describes, including C. phasmatis, were all found under Quercus spp. in a small plot in that park (lit_1). No source supplied here states a season or a substrate beyond this. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*
