## Summary

The name is based on an earlier combination, and Athelia fuscostrata and Corticium fuscostratum are listed as synonyms. The only descriptive source available, a GBIF description record, lists its distribution as Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Terceira Island. *[gbif_desc]*
