## Summary

Clitocybe subcordispora is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, described by Harri Harmaja in 1969 with Finland as its type locality. Wikipedia describes it as a rare, saprotrophic species with a brownish, hygrophanous cap with red tints, a pure white spore print, and subcordiform (somewhat heart-shaped) spores 4.2–6.2 by 3.0–4.0 micrometres. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Per Wikipedia, the species was formally described by the Finnish mycologist Harri Harmaja in 1969. The holotype was collected on 21 September 1967 in a dryish heath forest dominated by Picea and Pinus near Päijärvi, Vehkalahti, in South Karelia, Finland. An isotype (duplicate) is preserved at the herbarium of the University of Michigan. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (single-source claim; iNaturalist's text is a copy of it) describes the species as saprotrophic, fruiting in rows or arcs in dry, nutrient-poor heath forests dominated by Pinus or Picea, occasionally under Larix sibirica or among mosses such as Pleurozium, Hylocomium and Dicranum. It gives the fruiting period as early September to early November. *[wikipedia]*
