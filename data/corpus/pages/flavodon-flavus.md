## Summary

The one supplied source, a GBIF description quoting a Brazilian specimen report that cites Ryvarden (2015), describes it as recognizable by a yellowish basidiome with a poroid to hydnoid hymenophore and a reddish reaction with KOH. *[gbif_desc]*
