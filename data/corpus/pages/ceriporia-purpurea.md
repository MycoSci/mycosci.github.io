## Summary

Ceriporia purpurea (Fr.) Donk is a crust fungus in the family Irpicaceae, order Polyporales. Fries described it in 1821 as Polyporus purpureus, and Donk gave it its current name in 1971 by transferring it to Ceriporia. Wikipedia reports it as widely distributed in the temperate zone of Eurasia and also in the American North-East. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries first described the fungus in 1821 as Polyporus purpureus. Marinus Anton Donk transferred it to the genus Ceriporia in 1971, which gave it its current name. Wikipedia also notes a 2016 study that identified six similar Ceriporia species, referred to as the Ceriporia purpurea group: C. bresadolae, the European C. torpida and C. triumphalis, and the North American C. manzanitae and C. occidentalis. *[wikipedia]*
