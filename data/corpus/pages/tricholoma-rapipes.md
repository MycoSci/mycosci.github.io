## Summary

Tricholoma rapipes (Krombh.) Heilm.-Claus. & Mort. Chr. is a species in the family Tricholomataceae. MushroomExpert.Com treats it as a member of the Tricholoma saponaceum species group, separated by its spruce/fir association, yellow to yellow-green colors, and tapered, rooting stem base. It describes the flesh in the stem base as turning reddish to orangish, and the odor as soap-like or not distinctive. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Denmark and Norway. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as mycorrhizal with spruces and firs, especially Engelmann spruce and subalpine fir, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously. In the Rocky Mountains it fruits in late summer and early fall (monsoon season). In Scandinavia and the Alps it appears in late summer and fall. The author's illustrated and described collections are from Colorado. He states that the 2013 authors do not document the species outside Scandinavia and France, and that applying the name to the yellow to yellow-green 'Tricholoma saponaceum' of the Rocky Mountain spruce-fir forests is his own judgement of a plausible match, not a documented identification. *[mushroomexpert]*
