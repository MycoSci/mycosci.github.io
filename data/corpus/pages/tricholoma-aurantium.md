## Summary

Tricholoma aurantium (Schaeff.) Ricken is an agaric in the family Tricholomataceae, recorded in English as Orange Knight and, per Wikipedia, the golden orange tricholoma. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as recognizable by a sticky orange cap, a mealy taste, and a stem with scaly orange girdles that end near the apex, leaving a white zone at the top. GBIF records it from many countries, mainly in northern Europe and North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described by Jacob Christian Schäffer in 1774 and later transferred to the genus Tricholoma by Adalbert Ricken; MushroomExpert.Com's reference line likewise credits Schaeffer 1774 and Ricken. Neither source says what material the description was based on or why the transfer was made. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, growing scattered or gregariously and sometimes in clusters, fairly widely distributed in northern and montane North America (including the Rocky Mountains and the Appalachians), fruiting in summer and fall. Its illustrated and described collections are from Colorado, Michigan, and Kentucky. The same source cites a 2017 DNA study (Heilmann-Clausen and collaborators) as indicating a circumboreal distribution. Wikipedia independently describes fruit bodies growing on the ground with various conifers, and reports ectomycorrhizae with Pinus wallichiana and Abies pindrow in Pakistan and with Abies alba (silver fir) in Italy. It also lists India and Pakistan as Asian records. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
