## Summary

Laccaria ochropurpurea (Berk.) Peck, 1897, is a species in the family Hydnangiaceae, known in English as the Purple-and-White Deceiver and the Purple Laccaria. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a robust mushroom with purple gills, a pale brownish to lilac cap that often fades to whitish, and a white spore print. Both MushroomExpert.Com and Wikipedia place it east of the Rocky Mountains. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with hardwoods and conifers, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously. It is described as especially partial to oaks and beech, and as also frequently found in young plantations of eastern white pine. It is reported as widely distributed east of the Rocky Mountains, fruiting in late summer and fall (MushroomExpert.Com). Wikipedia gives the fruiting period as July to October and also places it under hardwoods and conifers east of the Rocky Mountains. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
