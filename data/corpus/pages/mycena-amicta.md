## Summary

Mycena amicta (Fr.) Quél. is a species of mushroom in the family Mycenaceae, commonly known as the coldfoot bonnet. Wikipedia describes young specimens as blue, fading to brownish hues with age, with a cap that starts conical to convex, flattens, and typically reaches up to 1.8 cm across. The cap cuticle is described as peelable, the gills as close, and the stem as covered in powdery hairs. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the mushrooms appear in small groups on the trunks of broadleaved trees, and particularly in the Pacific Northwest around rotted conifer wood. It gives the season as May to November on the West Coast and July to September further east. This is a single-source account. GBIF's description records list the distribution as Belgium (Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
