## Summary

Tulosesus heterosetulosus (Locq. ex Watling) D. Wächter & A. Melzer, 2020, is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae. It is described as a coprophilous fungus that grows on sheep or goat dung. GBIF lists a distribution record for Denmark. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first classified as Coprinus heterosetulosus by the French mycologist Marcel Locquin in 1947. A phylogenetic study in 2001 led to a major reshuffling of that genus, and the species was transferred to Coprinellus. It was known as Coprinellus heterosetulosus until 2020, when the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many Psathyrellaceae species on the basis of phylogenetic analysis. *[wikipedia]*
