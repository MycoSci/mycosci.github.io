## Summary

Leucoagaricus nympharum (Kalchbr.) Bon, 1977, is a species in the family Agaricaceae, and Index Fungorum gives its current name as Leucocoprinus nympharum. Wikipedia describes it as a large white mushroom with a scaly cap 4-10 cm across, and says it is seldom recorded and little known. The GBIF description record lists its distribution as Brazil, Denmark, Norway, Madeira Island and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first classified as Agaricus nympharum by the Hungarian mycologist Károly Kalchbrenner in 1873. The French mycologist Marcel Bon reclassified it as Leucoagaricus nympharum in 1977, and it was reclassified as Leucocoprinus nympharum in 2024. The source gives no further account of the material examined or the reasons for the transfers. *[wikipedia]*
