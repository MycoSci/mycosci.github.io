## Summary

Wynnella silvicola Nannf. (1966) is placed in the family Helvellaceae, order Pezizales. Index Fungorum gives its current name as Midotis lingua, so the name is disputed in the catalog. The English Wikipedia article, the only source with descriptive text, describes upright, ear-shaped fruit bodies 5–10 cm high with a deep blood-red to purple-brown spore-bearing surface and a smooth outer surface that is reddish brown above and pale yellow at the base. That description rests on a single source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat text, reports that in Europe the species occurs in subalpine coniferous woods on calcareous soils (Austria, Sweden, Norway), and that in North America it is found on forest-floor plant litter under conifers, sometimes in post-fire habitats. It also mentions a single Swedish record from beneath oak. In Britain it is said to be known only from one early 20th-century collection at Roslin, Scotland. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
