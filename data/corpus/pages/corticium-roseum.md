## Summary

Corticium roseum Pers. (1794) is a species of fungus in the family Corticiaceae, with its type locality in Germany. Its fruit bodies are effused, smooth, corticioid and pink. Wikipedia describes it as having a wide north and south temperate distribution. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Persoon originally described Corticium roseum in 1794 as part of his new genus Corticium, and that it was later selected as the type species of that genus. Differences in morphology between collections suggested that C. roseum might be a species complex, and several new species were described. According to the same source, DNA-sequence (cladistic) research has partly confirmed this: Corticium boreoroseum, C. medioroseum and C. malagasoroseum are separate species, while C. erikssonii and C. lombardiae are synonyms of C. roseum. This account comes from a single source cluster. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that in Europe the species is typically found on dead, attached branches of Salix and Populus, and that its distribution is wide, in both the north and south temperate zones. No supplied source gives a season. *[wikipedia]*
