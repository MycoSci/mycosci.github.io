## Summary

Hygrophorus discoxanthus (Fr.) Rea, 1908, is a waxy cap in the family Hygrophoraceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a slender, beech-associated species that is white when young, often turns yellow to orangish brown with age, and has mealy flakes near the stem apex and gills that are attached but not running down the stem. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states the species was originally described from Sweden (Fries 1815) and cites the combination as C. Rea, 1908. It lists Hygrophorus chrysaspis as a synonym. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with American beech and with European beech, growing scattered or gregariously in summer and fall. It says the species is widely distributed in Europe and North America within the natural range of the host trees. The described collection is from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*
