## Summary

Gloeocystidiellum porosum (Berk. & M.A. Curtis) Donk, 1931, is a species in the family Stereaceae (order Russulales). Wikipedia describes it as a corticioid homobasidiomycete that grows on various types of dead wood. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows on various types of dead wood. A specimen record in the GBIF descriptions (Zixishan National Forest Park, Chuxiong, Yunnan Province, China) was collected on a fallen branch of an angiosperm on 1 July 2018.
