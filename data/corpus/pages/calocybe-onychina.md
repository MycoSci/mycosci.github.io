## Summary

Calocybe onychina (Fr.) Donk, 1962, is a species in the family Lyophyllaceae, known in English as the Lilac Domecap. MushroomExpert.Com calls it an attractive but fairly rarely encountered mushroom. It describes a white spore print, yellow gills and a pinkish to purplish stem, with a deep dusky purple to purplish red or purple-brown cap 2–5 cm across. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single source), the species is probably saprobic. It grows alone, scattered, or gregariously under conifers, especially subalpine fir, Engelmann spruce and Douglas-fir, in summer and fall. In Europe it is described as distributed in northern and montane regions. In North America it is described as distributed from central Idaho to the Four Corners and west to the Pacific Northwest. The collections illustrated and described there are from Colorado and British Columbia. *[mushroomexpert]*
