## Summary

Pluteus salicinus (Pers.) P. Kumm. is a wood-growing mushroom in the family Pluteaceae, known in English as Willow Shield. Wikipedia describes it as a European psychedelic species, and a 1984 Norwegian screening detected psilocybin and psilocin in it. GBIF records it from many European countries and from North America. *[wikipedia, lit_2, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Christian Hendrik Persoon originally described the species as Agaricus salicinus in 1798, and that Paul Kummer transferred it to Pluteus in 1871. No supplied source gives the material examined or the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species is widely distributed across western Europe and Siberia and is always found growing on wood. It reports the species on hardwoods (Alnus, Eucalyptus, Fagus, Populus and Quercus), summer to fall, solitary or gregarious on dead hardwood in damp flood-plain forests.
