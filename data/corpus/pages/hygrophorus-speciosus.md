## Summary

Hygrophorus speciosus Peck (1878), the larch waxy cap, is a member of the Hygrophoraceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a waxy cap with a sticky, orangish red cap that fades to bright yellow and a whitish stem sheathed in gluten. Both MushroomExpert.Com and Wikipedia associate it with larch. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, including larches and ponderosa pine. It says the fungus grows scattered, gregariously, or in loose troops in late summer and fall, and is apparently widely distributed in North America; the collections it illustrates and describes are from Colorado and Québec. Wikipedia states that it can be found inland within the Pacific Northwest, in areas where larch is plentiful. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
