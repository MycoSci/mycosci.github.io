## Summary

Melanoleuca cognata (Fr.) Konrad & Maubl. 1927, the spring cavalier, is an agaric in the family Tricholomataceae. Wikipedia describes it as fairly tall for its genus, with a cap 5–13 cm wide and a stipe 6–12 cm long, and says it may be difficult to identify without microscopic examination. MushroomExpert.Com gives the same cap and stipe size ranges. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says the species is found in Europe and North America in forests, meadows and parks. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as probably saprobic, found in woods (often under conifers) or in grassy waste areas, in spring, summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. Mushroom World says it often fruits in spring, growing solitary or scattered on soil and needle litter in coniferous woodlands, and occasionally in litter-rich grassland, woodchip or garden compost heaps. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld]*
