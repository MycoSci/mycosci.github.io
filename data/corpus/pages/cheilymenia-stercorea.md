## Summary

Cheilymenia stercorea (Pers.) Boud. (1907) is a small cup fungus in the family Pyronemataceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes it as orange-red discs up to 3 mm across on dung, while MushroomExpert.Com gives a size of 0.5–4 mm and calls it an "eyelash cup", one of several cup fungi with tiny hairs. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says it grows clustered on dung, usually from cows, and is found in Europe and North America. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on the dung of domestic animals (primarily horses and cows), occasionally reported on the dung of wild animals, and growing alone or gregariously. It gives the season as spring through fall, or winter in warmer climates, and calls it widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
