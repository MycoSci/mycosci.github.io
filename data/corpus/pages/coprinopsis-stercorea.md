## Summary

Coprinopsis stercorea (Fr.) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo (2001) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a coprophilous fungus commonly known as the dung-loving inky cap. It gives the cap as about 4–10 mm across, egg-shaped when young and flattening with age, with gills that start grayish, turn black and deliquesce. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described in 1782 as Agaricus stercoreus, and that it has since carried other names, including Agaricus ephemerus var. stercoreus and Coprinus stercoreus. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it is found on dung. It usually grows on herbivore dung and is occasionally found on dog and bear dung, and it names sheep, goat and donkey dung. GBIF's description record lists a distribution that includes Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Terceira Island. No source supplied here gives a season, a host tree or any other substrate. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
