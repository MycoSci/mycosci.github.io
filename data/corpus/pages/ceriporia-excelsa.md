## Summary

Ceriporia excelsa (Parmasto, 1959) is a crust fungus in the family Irpicaceae (order Polyporales); Index Fungorum lists the name as invalid. Wikipedia describes soft, crust-like fruit bodies with a pink to reddish-orange pore surface. A 2017 study reported it as a new record for Sicily, and a 2006 study redescribed it among little-known polypores of Mexico. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*
