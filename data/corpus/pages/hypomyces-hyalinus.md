## Summary

Hypomyces hyalinus (Schwein.) Tul. & C. Tul. is a parasitic ascomycete in the family Hypocreaceae, known as the Amanita Mold. It attacks the fruiting bodies of Amanita species, covering and disfiguring them. It has been recorded in North America, including Vermont and the southeastern United States, and in Asia. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia gives the earliest recording of this parasite as 1822 in Salem, North Carolina, and says microscopic descriptions do not appear in the literature until 1886. MushroomExpert.Com likewise gives the original description as from North Carolina (von Schweinitz 1822). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Parasitic on Amanita species, attaching to the host's fruiting body. MushroomExpert.Com names Amanita rubescens and other Amanita species as hosts. A Binghamton University specimen record (2021) lists likely A. rubescens and A. frostiana, found under Pinus and Tsuga. Wikipedia reports records from eastern Canada, the northern and southeastern United States, and eastern Asia including China. MushroomExpert.Com describes distribution in North America primarily east of the Great Plains and in central Mexico, with some Pacific Northwest records, and also records from Asia. *[mushroomexpert, lit_1, wikipedia]*
