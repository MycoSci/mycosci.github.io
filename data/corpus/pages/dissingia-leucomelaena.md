## Summary

Dissingia leucomelaena (Pers.) K. Hansen & X.H. Wang (2019) is a species of fungus in the family Helvellaceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia gives its common name as the white-footed elf cup and describes a deeply cup-shaped fruiting body up to 3 cm broad, with a blackish inner cup surface and a white stem that can reach 4 cm long and carries ribs or folds extending onto the undersurface of the cap. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent descriptive source, states that the fungus typically grows in coniferous forests, with the white stipe possibly hidden or obscured by leaves or partially buried in the soil, and that it can be found from spring to early summer. It reports the species as rare in North America, where it has been collected in California, Alaska and the Rocky Mountains, and as also found in South America and Europe. GBIF's description record lists distribution in Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
