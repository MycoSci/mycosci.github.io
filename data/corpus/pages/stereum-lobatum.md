## Summary

Stereum lobatum (Kunze ex Fr.) Fr. (1838) is a species in the family Stereaceae, order Russulales, known as false turkeytail. Wikipedia describes it as a basidiomycete crust fungus that grows flat or in shelf-like, fan-shaped forms on wood and has no gills or stem. The same source says its pale brownish-yellow surface turns bright yellow when bruised. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that in North America this species was long thought to be Stereum ostrea. It says DNA sequencing has since shown the two to be distinct, and that S. ostrea is native to Indonesia and is not found in North America. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia; the iNaturalist text is a derivative of it). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
