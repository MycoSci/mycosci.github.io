## Summary

Tulosesus congregatus (Bull.) D. Wächter & A. Melzer (2020) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae, known as Coprinellus congregatus until 2020. Wikipedia states that it is found in North America and Europe, and the GBIF description record lists its distribution as Denmark and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1782 as Agaricus congregatus by the French mycologist Jean Baptiste François Pierre Bulliard. The Finnish mycologist Petter Adolf Karsten later transferred it to Coprinellus in 1879. In 2020 the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many species in the Psathyrellaceae based on phylogenetic analysis, and the species became Tulosesus congregatus. This account rests on a single encyclopedia source. *[wikipedia]*
