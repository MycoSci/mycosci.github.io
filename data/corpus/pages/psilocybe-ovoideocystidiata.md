## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (a single-source account) describes the species as mainly native to the Eastern United States, with a range stretching from Missouri east to Rhode Island and from Georgia north to Michigan, and as particularly common in the Ohio River valley. It also reports the species in Western North America, from Southern California north to British Columbia. It is said to grow on woody debris, along rivers and streams (usually in overflow areas), and in man-made mulch and wood chips in urban and suburban environments. It is sometimes found alongside Japanese knotweed and tends to prefer shady areas over direct sunlight. It is typically gregarious, in groups of several hundred individuals or in several small clusters close together, though solitary specimens also occur. In the northeastern US, Wikipedia gives the main fruiting period as spring, from mid-April to late June and peaking in late May, especially after several consecutive days of heavy rain. Occasional fruiting as late as November is reported. Seasonality is said to vary considerably by region. *[wikipedia]*
