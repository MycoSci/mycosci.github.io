## Summary

Agaricus subperonatus (J.E. Lange) Singer, 1951, is a species in the family Agaricaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a brown-capped species with free gills and a dark brown spore print, found near conifers (usually pines) in urban settings, in Europe and North America. ## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as saprobic, growing gregariously in urban areas (parks, yards and gardens) in association with conifers, usually pines, often but not always in grass, in summer and fall.
