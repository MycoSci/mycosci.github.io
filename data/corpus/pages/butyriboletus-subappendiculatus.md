## Summary

Butyriboletus subappendiculatus (Dermek, Lazebn. & J. Veselský) D. Arora & J.L. Frank, 2014, is a pored mushroom in the family Boletaceae. Wikipedia, the only source that describes it, gives medium-sized fruiting bodies with brownish-orange to buff caps up to 8 cm across, lemon-yellow tubes, and pores that do not bruise blue. It is recorded across Europe, and both Wikipedia and a 2022 Beskydy Museum (Czech Republic) fungi-exhibition report list it in the Endangered red-list category. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (a single source) states that the species fruits in upper-montane coniferous or mixed forests, most commonly under Norway spruce (Picea abies) or silver fir (Abies alba), on neutral to calcareous soils at elevations of 1,200–1,700 m, in midsummer (typically July through August). It gives Bulgaria's Pirin and Rila mountains as high-elevation localities. It lists records across Europe (Austria, Czech Republic, Germany, Spain, United Kingdom, Greece, France, Italy, Montenegro, Norway, Poland, Serbia, Slovenia and Slovakia) and into Turkey. *[wikipedia]*
