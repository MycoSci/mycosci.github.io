## Summary

Pluteus atromarginatus (Konrad) Kühner, 1935, is a member of the Pluteaceae with the English vernacular name Blackedged Shield. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a wood-rotting saprobe with a pink spore print, gills free from the stem, and gill edges that are dark brown or black. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on decaying conifer wood, growing alone or scattered in summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America; its illustrated collection is from Arkansas. A 2017 macrofungi survey of the Apuseni Mountains, Romania, lists it in coniferous stands and treats it as an indicator of old, undisturbed forest. It is also listed among the taxa in a 1998 paper on the mycological catalogue of the Balearic Islands. A 2013 thesis on the Basidiomycetes of District Mansehra reports it as a first record for Pakistan and confirms it by ITS sequence alignment. *[mushroomexpert, lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*
