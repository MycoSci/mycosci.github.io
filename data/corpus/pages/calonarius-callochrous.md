## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Agaricus callochrous Pers., published in Synopsis Methodica Fungorum 2: 282 (1801). It was later combined as Cortinarius callochrous by Gray (1821) and as Phlegmacium callochroum by Wünsche (1877). The current combination in Calonarius was made by Niskanen & Liimatainen in Liimatainen et al., Fungal Diversity 112: 129 (2022).
