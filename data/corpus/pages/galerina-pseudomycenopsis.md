## Summary

Galerina pseudomycenopsis Pilát (1954) is a species in the family Hymenogastraceae, with its type locality in Sweden and a spine-recorded substrate of amongst moss. A 2001 molecular study could not separate it from Galerina marginata using ribosomal DNA sequences, but the authors kept it as a distinct species, and a 2005 study reached a similar result. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to the English Wikipedia article on Galerina marginata, Gulden and colleagues' 2001 study found that G. pseudomycenopsis could not be distinguished from G. marginata by ribosomal DNA sequences and restriction fragment length polymorphism analyses. They preferred to keep G. pseudomycenopsis as a distinct species because of differences in ecology, fruit body color and spore size, combined with inadequate sampling. A 2005 study again failed to separate the two species by molecular methods, but reported that incompatibility in mating experiments suggests they are distinct.
