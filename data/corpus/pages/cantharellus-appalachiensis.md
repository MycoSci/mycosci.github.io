## Summary

Cantharellus appalachiensis R.H. Petersen (1971) is a chanterelle in the family Hydnaceae, with its type locality in Tennessee. Wikipedia describes it as native to eastern North America, with a cap that varies from brown to yellow, often with a brown spot at maturity. MushroomExpert.Com adds that its flesh and undersurface stain dull red with iron salts, which it says is apparently unique among North American Cantharellus. Journal articles report it from China (2011) and from the northwestern Himalayas of India (2013). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, lit_1, lit_2]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the scientific name refers to the Appalachian Mountains. This is a single-source statement. MushroomExpert.Com notes that, despite the name, the species appears to be distributed throughout eastern North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as mycorrhizal and found in hardwood forests of eastern North America. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, growing alone, gregariously, or in small clusters, in summer, and apparently widely distributed east of the Great Plains. The collections MushroomExpert.Com illustrates and describes are from Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Indiana, and Illinois. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
