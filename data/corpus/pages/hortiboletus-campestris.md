## Summary

Hortiboletus campestris (A.H. Sm. & Thiers) Biketova & Wasser (2015) is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, recorded in the US, Canada and Mexico and known by the English name field bolete. ## Discovery and naming

According to the GBIF description record, the species was first published as Boletus campestris A.H. Sm. & Thiers in Boletes of Michigan (1971, p. 266) and was transferred to Hortiboletus by Biketova & Wasser in Index Fungorum 257 (2015). The holotype (MICH 4999, A.H. Smith 72961) was collected and determined by A.H. Smith at Ann Arbor, Washtenaw Co., Michigan, on 16 July 1966. The same source lists Boletus harrisonii A.H. Sm. & Thiers (Boletes of Michigan, p. 267) as conspecific. It reports that multilocus and single-locus phylogenetic analyses of the two holotypes (MICH 4999 and MICH 10011) proved this, and that they also fall in one species-level clade in the phylogenomic tree of Tremble et al. (2024). The record also states that several specimens and sequences previously identified as H. campestris or B. harrisonii actually belong to Hortiboletus flavorubellus. It adds that one collection's LSU sequence belongs to Xerocomus tenax, and that no correctly identified H. campestris collections are represented by ITS sequences in INSDC. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The GBIF description record gives habitat information only for the two Michigan type collections, both made at Ann Arbor. The holotype (collected 16 July 1966) was recorded as gregarious on a lawn. The holotype of Boletus harrisonii (collected 1 July 1967), now treated as the same species by that source, was recorded as gregarious on a lawn near Picea sp. The source states that further studies on the morphology and distribution of the species are needed. *[gbif_desc]*
