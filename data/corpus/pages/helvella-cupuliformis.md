## Summary

Helvella cupuliformis Dissing & Nannf. (1966) is a cup fungus in the family Helvellaceae (order Pezizales), with its type locality in Sweden. MushroomExpert.Com describes the cap as cup-shaped to disc-shaped, 0.5-3.5 cm across, on a short stem 2-30 mm long. Wikipedia notes a variant, var. crassa, described from China with larger fruit bodies and wider spores. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Single-source account from MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo): probably mycorrhizal, growing alone or gregariously on the ground or in moss under hardwoods (especially oaks), from early summer through fall, and widely distributed in North America. The same source says it appears to be associated with hardwoods in North America but often grows under conifers in Europe, and that in the lower Midwest it appears in early summer. Wikipedia states only that the species has been found in China and Jalisco (Mexico). *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
