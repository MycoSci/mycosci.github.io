## Summary

Hydnellum mirabile (Fr.) P. Karst. (1879) is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, order Thelephorales. Wikipedia describes it as inedible and as Vulnerable under IUCN criteria because of habitat loss, and says it is used as an indicator species for forest health. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that in Europe it grows in old-growth coniferous forests on base-rich or chalky soils. It forms ectomycorrhizal associations primarily with Norway spruce (Picea abies) and occasionally with Scots pine (Pinus sylvestris).
