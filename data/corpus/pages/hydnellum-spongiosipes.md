## Summary

Hydnellum spongiosipes (Peck) Pouzar, 1960 is a tooth fungus in the family Bankeraceae, order Thelephorales, known in English as the velvet tooth. Wikipedia reports it from Europe and North America and says it is considered a vulnerable species in Switzerland. MushroomExpert.Com describes a brown, velvety cap with brownish teeth on the underside and a spongy, swollen stem. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

MushroomExpert.Com states that the spongy, swollen stem is what gives the species its Latin name. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was originally described by the American mycologist Charles Horton Peck, who placed it in the genus Hydnum. Zdeněk Pouzar transferred it to Hydnellum in 1960. Wikipedia lists Hydnellum nuttallii (Howard James Banker, 1906) and Hydnellum velutinum var. spongiosipes (Rudolph Arnold Maas Geesteranus, 1957) as synonyms. MushroomExpert.Com adds that H. velutinum, in the sense of some North American authors (e.g. Coker & Beers, 1951), is a synonym. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with oaks (especially white oak) and other hardwoods. It grows alone or gregariously in summer and fall, and is widely distributed east of the Rocky Mountains. Wikipedia likewise says that in North America it appears in the east, from July to September. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
