## Summary

Hydnomerulius pinastri (Fr.) Jarosch & Besl (2001) is a species in the family Paxillaceae, order Boletales, and is described as the sole member of the genus Hydnomerulius. Wikipedia describes it as a saprophyte that causes a brown rot in wood, and notes that the species has not been thoroughly studied outside taxonomic work. It may be called "spiny dry rot" or "spiny dry rot fungus" colloquially. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was described as a member of Leucogyrophana in 1976. Jarosch and Besl later erected Hydnomerulius in 2001 as a monospecific genus for it. According to the same source, sequencing of the 28S gene and staining characteristics indicated a closer relationship to members of the Paxillaceae than to other Leucogyrophana. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes H. pinastri as a saprophyte and wood-decaying agent that produces a brown rot through decay of cellulose and hemicellulose, and says its ecological role beyond this is unclear. The GBIF descriptions record lists its distribution only as "BR; DK; DK; NO; SE; Vermont".
