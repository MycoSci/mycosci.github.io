## Summary

Russula fragilis Fr. (Fragile Brittlegill) is a member of the Russulaceae, recorded across Europe, Asia and North America. Wikipedia describes it as a small, fragile, long-stemmed and variably coloured brittlegill found in mixed forests and woods. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, it was first described as Agaricus fragilis by Christian Hendrik Persoon in 1801 and later placed in the genus Russula by Elias Magnus Fries in 1838. Wikispecies lists Agaricus fragilis Pers. 1801 among the synonyms. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with descriptive text, says it appears in late summer and autumn, usually growing in small groups, in mixed forests and woods. It is described as probably mycorrhizal with a variety of trees, including birch and oak, and as often appearing on or near rotted wood. *[wikipedia]*
