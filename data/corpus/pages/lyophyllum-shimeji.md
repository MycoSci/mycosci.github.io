## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that Lyophyllum shimeji grows in pine forests, often near man-made roads. It says the species was originally known only from Japan, Korea and China, and that its known distribution now includes Russia, Estonia, Scandinavia, Czechia, Spain, the USA and Canada. The same source describes it as particularly widespread in lichen pine forests in Sweden, Finland and Norway, after being confirmed from these countries as late as 2011.
