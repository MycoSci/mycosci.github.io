## Summary

Russula paludosa Britzelm. (1891) is a mushroom in the family Russulaceae. Sources describe it as occurring in Europe and North America, and GBIF lists it for several European countries and for Vermont. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia and Mushroom World both describe it as mycorrhizal, occurring in coniferous woodlands and in peat bogs of Europe and North America, preferably under pine trees, where it forms mycorrhizae. Both say it can be very common locally. Mushroom World adds a caution that mushrooms resembling Russula paludosa in North America may not necessarily be this exact species. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
