## Summary

Leratiomyces squamosus (Pers.) Bridge & Spooner 2008 is a mushroom in the family Strophariaceae, known in English as the slender roundhead. Wikipedia describes it as inedible. The cap is about 2-6 cm across and the gills darken from grayish to purplish black, with a purplish black spore print. *[wikipedia]*
