## Summary

Pleurotus calyptratus (Lindblad ex Fr.) Sacc., 1887, is a species of fungus in the family Pleurotaceae. Wikipedia describes a distinctive delicate veil on young fruiting bodies. It also states that phylogenetic research places the species in the P. djamor-cornucopiae clade, where it forms its own intersterility group. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (the only independent source with ecological text) describes the species as mainly inhabiting trunks, stumps and fallen branches of poplars, especially white and trembling poplars, and as very rare on willows and "rowanberries" (as worded in the source). It says the fungus attacks trees weakened by drought or waterlogging, can continue as a saprobe for some time after the host dies, and causes intense white rot in the heartwood. For distribution, Wikipedia says it lives in Europe in temperate to subarctic climate zones. It lists finds from Germany (only isolated finds in Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg), Croatia, Poland, Liechtenstein, Austria, Slovakia, the Czech Republic and Hungary. In northern Europe it names only Finland and Sweden. *[wikipedia]*
