## Summary

Trametes hirsuta (Wulfen) Lloyd, 1924, is a bracket fungus in the family Polyporaceae, known in English as hairy bracket. Wikipedia gives the cap as up to 15 cm wide, whitish gray, with short hairs and subtle zoning. MushroomExpert.Com describes a very densely hairy, semicircular to kidney-shaped cap with a often brownish to blackish margin, and 3-4 pores per mm on the pore surface. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it is found on dead wood of deciduous trees, especially beechwood, and is found all year round. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on the deadwood of hardwoods (very rarely reported on conifer wood), annual, causing a white rot, growing in clusters on logs and stumps, in summer and fall, and widely distributed across North America. These two sources differ on season (all year versus summer and fall). GBIF description records list specimens from fallen angiosperm trunks in Yunnan Province, China, and a Spanish-language record gives the substrate as dead wood. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
