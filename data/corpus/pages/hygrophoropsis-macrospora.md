## Summary

Hygrophoropsis macrospora (D.A. Reid) Kuyper, 1996, is a species of fungus in the family Hygrophoropsidaceae (order Boletales). Wikipedia describes the fruit bodies as having a cream- to tan-coloured cap with a felt-like surface and forked, yellowish, decurrent gills. The same source says it differs from H. aurantiaca mainly in its larger spores, measuring 8.0–11.0 (sometimes up to 13.0) by 3.0–4.5 (5.0) μm. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Derek Reid formally described the taxon in 1972 as a variety of Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca, and Thomas Kuyper raised it to distinct species status in 1996. Wikipedia also notes that Hygrophoropsis pallida (Peck) Kreisel is a synonym that was never formally published. It is based on Charles Horton Peck's Cantharellus aurantiatus var. pallidus, published in 1896. *[wikipedia]*
