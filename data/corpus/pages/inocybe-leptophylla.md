## Summary

Inocybe leptophylla G.F. Atk. (1918) is a species in the family Inocybaceae, with its type locality in New York. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a scaly, dark brown-capped Inocybe with a cap 1-4 cm across, associated with conifers. Distinguishing it from close relatives such as Inocybe lanuginosa is described there as needing microscopy. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that Inocybe casimiri is a synonym of this species. It also states that Atkinson (1918) originally described Inocybe leptophylla var. cystomarginata, thinking it differed in having cheilocystidia. It adds that Matheny & Kropp (2001) "do not consider var. cystomarginata as an autonomous taxon." This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (the only supplied source with habitat text), this species is mycorrhizal with conifers, possibly at least facultatively saprobic. Fruiting is given as spring, summer, and fall, and the species is described as widely distributed in North America. That source does not address habitat in Europe. *[mushroomexpert]*
