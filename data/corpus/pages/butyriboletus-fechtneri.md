## Summary

Butyriboletus fechtneri (Velen.) D. Arora & J.L. Frank is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, known in English as the Pale Bolete. It was originally described as Boletus fechtneri and transferred to Butyriboletus in 2014 on the basis of molecular data. Wikipedia describes it as native to Europe and reports that regional populations are genetically highly variable. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Boletus fechtneri Velen., published in České Houby in 1922. It lists as heterotypic synonyms Boletus appendiculatus subsp. pallescens Konrad (1929) and Boletus romellii Kallenbach (1931), among others. Wikipedia states that the species was moved to the newly erected genus Butyriboletus in 2014 (Mycologia 106(3): 466), after molecular data showed it to belong to the "Regius" clade, named after B. regius. *[wikipedia, wikispecies]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that B. fechtneri is native to Europe and forms ectomycorrhizal associations with various broad-leaved trees of the family Fagaceae, particularly oak (Quercus), beech (Fagus) and chestnut (Castanea). The same source lists molecular confirmation from Austria, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Estonia, France, Spain and Sweden, and says it is considered endangered in the Czech Republic. GBIF's description record lists distribution in Denmark, Norway and Sweden. These statements come from a single independent source cluster (Wikipedia and its derivative iNaturalist) plus a database distribution record; no source supplied gives a season or substrate. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
