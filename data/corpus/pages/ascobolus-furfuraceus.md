## Discovery and naming

A 2004 study in Fungal Genetics Reports mated strains grown from 30-40 year old ascospores of the Ascobolus stercorarius - furfuraceus complex. All pairings within the stercorarius group and within the furfuraceus group were fertile, and all pairings between the two groups were sterile. The author concluded from this reproductive isolation that the complex consists of two species, A. stercorarius and A. furfuraceus. *[lit_3]*
