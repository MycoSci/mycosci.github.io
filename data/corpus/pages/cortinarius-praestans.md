## Summary

Cortinarius praestans (Cordier) Gillet, known as the Goliath Webcap, is a species in the family Cortinariaceae. Wikipedia describes mature caps as typically 10–20 cm across, on a solid, club-shaped stem 10–15 cm long and 3–5 cm thick, with a cobwebby partial veil (cortina). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus praestans by the French botanist François Simon Cordier in 1870. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fruit bodies grow in deciduous forests, mainly on calcareous soils, and describes the species as common in western Europe, particularly England and France. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
