## Summary

Psilocybe azurescens Stamets & Gartz (1995) is a species in the family Hymenogastraceae, with its type locality in Oregon. Wikipedia describes it as a psychedelic mushroom whose main active compounds are psilocybin and psilocin. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports that it occurs naturally along a small area of the US West Coast, with the first type collections made at Hammond, Oregon. It describes the preferred environment as caespitose to gregarious on deciduous wood-chips and/or in sandy soils rich in woody debris. Fruiting is reported to begin in late September and continue until late December and early January, according to Paul Stamets.
