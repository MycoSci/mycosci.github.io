## Summary

Macrolepiota fuliginosa (Barla) Bon (1977) is a member of the Agaricaceae, based on Lepiota procera var. fuliginosa, and is known in English as the Sooty Parasol. GBIF records it from many European countries, and its GBIF distribution entry lists Belgium, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. Two 2013 fungal surveys from Rupandehi District, Nepal also list it among the dominant fungi collected. *[gbif_desc, lit_1, lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

Two 2013 surveys in Rupandehi District, Nepal list M. fuliginosa among the dominant species collected in tropical deciduous riverine forest. One survey covered 110–165 m above sea level (Banko Janakari) and the other covered 165–200 m at Sankarnagar Community Forest (Nepal Journal of Science and Technology). Both are lowland surveys, and neither gives a host tree, substrate or season for this species. *[lit_1, lit_2]*
