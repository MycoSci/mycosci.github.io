## Summary

Strobilurus esculentus (Wulfen) Singer, 1962 is a species in the family Physalacriaceae (order Agaricales), published in Index of Fungi 3: 226 and known in English as the Sprucecone Cap. GBIF records it from many European countries and from North America and Asia. Wikipedia and Mushroom World both describe it as appearing in spring on or around fallen spruce cones. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (whose text iNaturalist reproduces) and Mushroom World, which is independent of it, both describe fruiting on or around fallen spruce cones in spring. Mushroom World describes this as early spring, says the species is specific to spruce, and says it is never found on pine cones. That last claim comes from Mushroom World alone. Mushroom World lists Europe as the location. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
