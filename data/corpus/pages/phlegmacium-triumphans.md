## Summary

Phlegmacium triumphans (Fr.) A. Blytt (1905) is a species in the family Cortinariaceae, recorded from Europe, Asia and North America. Wikipedia gives its English names as the birch webcap and the yellow girdled webcap. It was long known as Cortinarius triumphans and was moved to Phlegmacium in a 2022 genomic study. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia glosses the specific epithet triumphans as "triumphing". It gives no further account of how the name arose. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by Elias Magnus Fries in 1838. Axel Gudbrand Blytt reclassified it as Phlegmacium triumphans in 1905. It nonetheless remained known as Cortinarius triumphans until 2022, when a genomic study moved it and many other Cortinarius species into Phlegmacium. Wikipedia also says some authorities treat it as the same species as Cortinarius crocolitus Quélet. This is a single-source account, and no primary literature was supplied. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the fungus appears under birch trees in autumn in Europe and in Asia, where it is described as widespread but local. It also reports it from north-eastern North America and describes it as mycorrhizal with birch (Betula).
