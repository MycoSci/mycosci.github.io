## Summary

Peziza echinospora P. Karst. (1866) is a cup fungus (apothecial fungus) in the family Pezizaceae, with its type locality in Finland. Wikipedia describes the cups as up to 10 cm across, with a dark brown, smooth inner surface and a pale, sometimes almost white, rough outer surface. It is recorded on burnt soil and at old fire sites. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as found at old fire sites, with cups appearing from late spring to early autumn, and calls it a European fungus. The GBIF description record lists distribution entries for Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
