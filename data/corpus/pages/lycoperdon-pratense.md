## Summary

Lycoperdon pratense Pers. (1794), the meadow puffball, is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae, recorded on soil. Wikipedia describes it as a small puffball reaching about 4 cm wide and 5 cm tall, with a stump-like stem. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it from Great Britain and Ireland (including the Outer Hebrides), mainland Europe and occasionally North America. It is described as commonly seen in sand dune systems, where it can be abundant in dune slacks, as well as in grassland and lawns. Mushroom World describes it as growing typically in small troops on lawns and in other places with short grass, occasionally on roadside verges. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
