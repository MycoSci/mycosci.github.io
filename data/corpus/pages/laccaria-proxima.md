## Summary

Laccaria proxima (Boud.) Pat., 1887, is a species in the family Hydnangiaceae, order Agaricales, known in English as the Scurfy Deceiver. It is recorded from many countries across Europe and North America, among other places. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a mycorrhizal associate of pines that can only be reliably separated from the very similar Laccaria laccata by microscopic features. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with pines (Pinus species), especially in young plantations, growing scattered or gregariously in summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America. The same source states that it grows only under pines, and that mushrooms not found under pines are therefore not this species. This is a single-source claim. Wikipedia separately describes it as occurring in conifer forest of California and in eastern and northern North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
