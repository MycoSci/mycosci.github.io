## Summary

Russula ochroleuca Fr. (1838) is a species in the family Russulaceae. Wikipedia places it among the brittlegills and gives its common names as common yellow russula and, more recently, ochre brittlegill. The same source describes it as widespread and common in mixed woodland. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows in deciduous and coniferous forest, where it is very common, at least in Northwestern Europe. For the USA, the same source describes it as fairly common under conifers, birch and aspen in the Northern States.
