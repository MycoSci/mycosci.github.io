## Summary

Inocybe corydalina Quél. (1875) is a member of the family Inocybaceae, commonly called the greenflush fibrecap. Wikipedia, the only source with descriptive text, describes it as a small mycorrhizal mushroom reported to contain a small amount of psilocybin. That source describes the variety I. corydalina var. corydalina. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, describing I. corydalina var. corydalina, states that it is mycorrhizal and widespread across Europe and the British Isles, usually fruiting in the fall under deciduous trees but also found under conifers. GBIF's descriptions record a distribution including Belgium (Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
