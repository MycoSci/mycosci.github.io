## Summary

Mycena interrupta (Berk.) Sacc. (1887) is a species of mushroom in the family Mycenaceae, commonly known as the pixie's parasol. Wikipedia, the only independent source with descriptive text, describes it as having a Gondwanan distribution, recorded from Australia, New Zealand, New Caledonia and Chile. The same source describes brilliant cyan-blue caps 0.8 to 2 cm across and states that, unlike some other Mycena species, it is not bioluminescent. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the pixie's parasol appears in small colonies on rotting, moist wood in rainforests, and in southern beech or eucalypt forests. It reports the species in Australia from Victoria, Tasmania, New South Wales, South Australia and Queensland, where its Queensland distribution is limited to Lamington National Park. *[wikipedia]*
