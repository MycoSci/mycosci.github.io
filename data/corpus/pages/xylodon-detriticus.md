## Summary

Xylodon detriticus (Bourdot) K.H. Larss., Viner & Spirin (2018) is a fungus in the family Schizoporaceae, order Hymenochaetales. It is listed under the synonym Peniophora detritica. The one source available describes its basidiocarps as effused, up to 5 cm across, with a white, smooth or warted, farinaceous hymenial surface. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The single available source (a GBIF description record) gives the distribution as Europe (Czech Republic, France, Italy). It reports the species on herbaceous remnants, and once from pine bark at the same spot where it was found on fern remains. *[gbif_desc]*
