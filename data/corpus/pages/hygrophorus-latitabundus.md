## Summary

Hygrophorus latitabundus Britzelm. (1899) is a species in the family Hygrophoraceae, with its type locality in Hungary. Wikipedia describes it as a large agaric with a slimy, grey-brown to olivaceous cap and a thick, slime-covered stem, found in pine forests in Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it in coniferous forests, forming ectomycorrhizal associations exclusively with pines (Pinus), fruiting in autumn and preferring calcareous soils. The same source calls its occurrence rare but abundant where it is established. It gives the distribution as southern and central Europe, including Austria, Germany, France, Italy, Slovenia and Spain, and says it is also known from the Republic of Macedonia, Greece and Turkey. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
