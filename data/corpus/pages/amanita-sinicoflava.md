## Summary

Amanita sinicoflava, the mandarin yellow ringless amanita, was described by Tulloss in 1988 and belongs to the family Amanitaceae. MushroomExpert.Com places it in the Amanita vaginata group, which lack a ring and have a strongly lined cap margin and a sacklike volva. It describes the cap as 2.5-7 cm across and olive tan to olive yellow, and says the white volva usually stains gray to black by maturity. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet sinicoflava means "Chinese-yellow", referring to the cap color. MushroomExpert.Com likewise describes the cap color as "Chinese yellow" to olive tan or olive yellow. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows in sandy soil under Quercus, Pinus rigida, Acer rubrum, Quercus alba and Quercus velutina. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with various conifers and hardwoods, growing alone or gregariously in summer and fall, and fairly widely distributed east of the Great Plains. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
