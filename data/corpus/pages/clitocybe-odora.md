## Summary

Clitocybe odora (Bull.) P. Kumm. 1871 is a gilled mushroom in the family Tricholomataceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes fresh, unfaded specimens as bluish green with a strong anise smell. It notes that both the green colour and the odour fade with age, and older specimens sometimes lack the odour entirely. Wikipedia also describes it as blue-green and strongly anise-scented. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet odora is from the Latin meaning "perfumed". This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia says the species was first described by the French mycologist Jean Baptiste Francois Pierre Bulliard. MushroomExpert.Com's reference line gives the original description as J. B. F. Bulliard, 1784, with the combination by P. Kummer in 1871, and says it was originally described from France. Neither source says what material the description was based on or why the name was transferred. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing scattered or gregariously on the litter of hardwoods or conifers, in summer and fall. It calls it widespread in Europe. In North America it is widely distributed in northern and montane areas and in the Midwestern United States, but apparently rare or absent in the southeastern and southwestern United States. It is also reported from Japan. Wikipedia describes it as occurring in both deciduous and coniferous woods in temperate zones of Asia, Europe (August to December) and North America (around August to November). It says the species favours oak woodland on the East Coast of North America and is often abundant in the coniferous forests of the Pacific Northwest. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
