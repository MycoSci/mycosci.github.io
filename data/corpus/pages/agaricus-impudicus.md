## Summary

Agaricus impudicus (Rea) Pilát, 1951, is a species in the family Agaricaceae, known in English as the tufted wood mushroom. Wikipedia gives the same common name and lists a number of synonyms, including Psalliota impudica Rea 1932. A 2025 Water Science & Technology study used the fungus's biomass as one of two adsorbents (the other was lateritic soil) for recovering ammonium nitrogen from human urine. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (single-source) says the species is known from Western and Southern Europe and New Zealand. It calls the species uncommon and says it is found in deciduous or coniferous forest in autumn. The GBIF descriptions record distribution in Denmark, Norway, Madeira Island and Sweden. No supplied source gives a host tree, substrate or more specific habitat. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
