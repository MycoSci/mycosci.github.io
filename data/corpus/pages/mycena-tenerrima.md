## Summary

Mycena tenerrima (Berk.) Quél. is a species in the family Mycenaceae, with the English name "Frosty Bonnet". Its name is listed as legitimate, and Index Fungorum gives the current name as Basidopus tenerrimus. The one descriptive source supplied, Wikipedia, covers it under the name Mycena adscendens, and it notes that some authorities treat M. tenerrima as a separate species. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the epithet tenerrima derives from the Latin tener, meaning "tender" or "delicate". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that, according to Maas Geesteranus, Miles Berkeley's 1836 Agaricus tenerrimus is the same species as Mycena adscendens (originally Agaricus adscendens, Lasch 1829), and that Mycena tenerrima, published by Lucien Quélet in 1872, is a later name based on Berkeley's basionym. The same source says other authorities treat the species as independent. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
