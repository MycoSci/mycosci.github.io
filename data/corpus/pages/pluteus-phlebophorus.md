## Summary

Pluteus phlebophorus (Ditmar) P. Kumm. is a species in the family Pluteaceae, known in English as the Wrinkled Shield. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small Pluteus with a thin, fragile, dark pinkish brown cap 1.5–4 cm across that is sometimes radially veined over the center, and says that identification requires microscopic examination. GBIF's description record lists its distribution as Belgium, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on decaying hardwood logs, growing alone or gregariously, in summer and fall in temperate climates.
