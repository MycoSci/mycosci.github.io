## Summary

Cantharellus subalbidus, the white chanterelle, was described by A.H. Smith & Morse in 1947 and is placed in the family Hydnaceae. Sources describe it as a large, white to cream chanterelle of western North America whose surfaces bruise yellowish to orange. MushroomExpert.Com gives a spore print of white and describes the undersurface as false gills running down the stem. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as native to California and the Pacific Northwest, where it is found from August to October; MushroomExpert.Com gives fall and winter for the Pacific Northwest and northern California. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, especially Douglas-fir, growing alone or scattered. Wikipedia lists possible mycorrhizal associations with pine, hemlock, Douglas-fir and Pacific madrone. Both sources report it as more common in old-growth forests than in younger forests; MushroomExpert.Com attributes this to a study by Dunham and collaborators (2006). *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
