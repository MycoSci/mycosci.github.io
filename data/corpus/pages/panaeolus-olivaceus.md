## Summary

Panaeolus olivaceus F.H. Møller (1945) is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae. English Wikipedia describes it as a widely distributed, rarely identified little brown mushroom that contains the hallucinogen psilocybin. A 2019 paper reports it as a first record for Iran, from Kermanshah Province. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows scattered to gregariously in rich grassy areas, from late summer through December, across North and South America, and is likely more widely distributed. It lists collections from the U.S. states of Washington, Oregon, Florida, Georgia, South Carolina and Minnesota, from Quebec in Canada, from New Zealand and from the United Kingdom. This is a single-source account (Wikipedia, which cites Stamets 1996). A survey paper separately reports the species from Kermanshah Province, Iran, but the supplied abstract gives no habitat detail. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*
