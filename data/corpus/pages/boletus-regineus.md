## Summary

Boletus regineus D. Arora & Simonini (2008), the queen bolete, is a member of the Boletaceae with California as its type locality. Wikipedia describes the cap as 5–18 cm wide, convex then flat, brown with a whitish dusting when young, and the stalk as 5–15 cm long and 3–6 cm wide, clavate then equal, and whitish tan. This description comes from a single source. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, B. regineus was considered a variant of B. edulis for many years until it was declared a unique species in 2008. Wikipedia adds that phylogenetic analysis places it in a clade with B. subcaerulescens, Gastroboletus subalpinus, B. pinophilus, B. fibrillosus, and B. rex-veris. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species inhabits southwestern North America. *[wikipedia]*
