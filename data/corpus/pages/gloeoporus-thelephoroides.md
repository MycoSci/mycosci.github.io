## Summary

Gloeoporus thelephoroides (Hook.) G. Cunn., 1965, is a species in the family Irpicaceae (order Polyporales). It was originally described as Boletus thelephoroides, with a holotype from Ecuador. One database note separates it from other Gloeoporus species by its white to pinkish hymenophore and, microscopically, by simple septate generative hyphae. *[gbif_desc, wikispecies]*

## Discovery and naming

The basionym Boletus thelephoroides was published by Hooker in Kunth's Synopsis Plantarum (1822). Its holotype is K(M) 159287, and the recorded locality is Ecuador. The species was later moved to Polyporus (Fries, 1838), Polystictus (Berkeley, 1851) and Microporus (Kuntze, 1898). It was combined in Gloeoporus by G. Cunningham in 1965 (Bull. N.Z. Dept. Sci. Industr. Res. 164: 111). Gloeoporus conchoides Mont. (1842) and Leptoporus conchoides (Mont.) Pat. (1900) are listed as heterotypic synonyms. The supplied sources do not say why any of these transfers were made. *[wikispecies]*
