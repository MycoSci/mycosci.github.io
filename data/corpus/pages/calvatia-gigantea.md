## Summary

Calvatia gigantea (Batsch) Lloyd, 1904, the giant puffball, is a puffball in the family Lycoperdaceae. Wikipedia describes it as found in meadows, fields and deciduous forests in late summer and autumn, with fruiting bodies reported from 10–50 cm wide and high, and occasionally larger. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Lycoperdon giganteum Batsch, 1786, with Langermannia gigantea (Batsch) Rostk. as a synonym. Wikipedia says the species has also been placed in Lycoperdon and Langermannia, and that the current view is that it belongs in Calvatia. MushroomExpert.Com says the Langermannia placement reflects a taxonomic argument that DNA studies have yet to resolve. *[wikispecies, wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it is commonly found in meadows, fields and deciduous forests in late summer and autumn, in temperate areas throughout the world. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic and terrestrial, growing alone or gregariously in woods or in grass, often at the edges of meadows, in drainage ditches or under brush, in late summer and early fall. Kuo describes it as fairly widely distributed from the Great Plains eastward, with occasional, possibly erroneous, reports from the Southwest and West Coast; his own collections are from central Illinois. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
