## Summary

Lysurus cruciatus (Lepr. & Mont.) Henn. is a stinkhorn in the family Phallaceae, order Phallales. Its fruit bodies have a stem topped by a head of several short, pointed, hollow arms, and the arms are coated with foul-smelling spore slime (gleba). It is recorded from many countries across several continents. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described scientifically in 1845 by the French botanists François Mathias René Leprieur and Camille Montagne as Aserophallus cruciatus. Paul Christoph Hennings transferred it to the genus Lysurus in 1902. In 1901 the mushroom was rediscovered in Inanda, Natal, and this 'new' find was named L. woodii. That name was later corrected to the previously described L. cruciatus. *[wikipedia]*
