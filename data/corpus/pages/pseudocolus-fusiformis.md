## Summary

Pseudocolus fusiformis (E. Fisch.) Lloyd, 1898, is a stinkhorn in the family Phallaceae. Wikipedia describes it as the most widely distributed member of its genus, with three or four orange arms joined at the top and a dark greenish slimy gleba that smells foul at maturity. It is recorded in many countries, including the United States, Australia, Japan and Turkey. *[wikipedia]*

## Where the common name comes from

Wikipedia states that it is commonly known as the stinky squid because of its fetid odor and its three or four upright arms, which are connected at the top. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows scattered or in groups on disturbed soil in coniferous or mixed forests, and on wood chips used as mulch in gardens or landscaping. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously, often in urban settings but sometimes in woods, and fruiting year-round depending on climate. Wikipedia says it is thought to have been introduced to North America from southeast Asia, where it is found from May to September. A 2019 Ukrainian Botanical Journal paper treats it as an alien species in Ukraine, recorded there only once, in the Nikita Botanical Garden. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, lit_3]*
