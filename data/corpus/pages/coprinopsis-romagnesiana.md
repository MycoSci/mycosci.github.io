## Summary

Coprinopsis romagnesiana (Singer) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo (2001) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as, more or less, a scaly version of the inky cap Coprinopsis atramentaria. Its cap carries brown to orangish brown scales, and its gills turn black with age and eventually liquefy. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com states that the species was originally described from northern Italy, citing Bresadola 1931. It lists Coprinus romagnesianus and Coprinus atramentarius squamosus (described there as a taxonomically invalid previous name) as synonyms. The page reproduces Bresadola's 1931 illustration of Coprinus atramentarius squamosus. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes the species as saprobic, growing in clusters on stumps or from senescent roots around stumps, in spring and summer. It gives the distribution as Europe and North America, especially northern and montane areas, and also reports it from Argentina; the collection illustrated and described there is from Michigan. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
