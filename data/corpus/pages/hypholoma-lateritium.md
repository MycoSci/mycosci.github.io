## Summary

Hypholoma lateritium (Schaeff.) P. Kumm. 1871 is a species in the family Strophariaceae, commonly called brick cap. Wikipedia describes a cap 3.5–9 cm across, usually brick-red in the centre with a paler margin, and a purple-brown spore print. Mushroom World describes it as growing densely clustered. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and West Virginia it is found in dense clusters on stumps and roots from October until long after frosts. Mushroom World describes it as growing densely clustered on stumps and debris of broad-leaf trees. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
