## Summary

Psathyrella piluliformis (Bull.) P.D. Orton, 1969, is an agaric in the family Psathyrellaceae, with GBIF occurrence records from the United Kingdom, Denmark, the United States and many other countries. Sources describe brown to reddish-brown convex caps that fade as they dry, with white veil tissue along the margin of young caps. ## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing in small to large clusters on wood or deadwood of hardwoods or on woody debris. It sometimes appears terrestrial, but then probably arises from buried wood. That source gives the season as spring through fall, or over winter in warm climates, and says it is widely distributed in North America and in Europe. The description there is based on a collection from California. Wikipedia likewise says fruiting occurs in clusters at the base of hardwood stumps. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
