## Summary

Gymnopilus dilepis (Berk. & Broome) Singer, 1951, is a species in the family Hymenogastraceae (order Agaricales). Sources place it in India, Malaysia and North America, and a 2023 study reports it from Brazil. One encyclopedia source and a laboratory study report psilocybin in it. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_2]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Rolf Singer gave the species its current name in 1951. A 2023 Karstenia paper says it was originally described from Sri Lanka. That paper also asks whether G. lepidotus should be treated as a synonym of G. dilepis. Its authors prefer to keep them as distinct entities. *[wikipedia, lit_2]*

## Habitat and ecology

A 2023 Karstenia paper reports specimens of G. dilepis growing with the grass Zoysia japonica (Poaceae) in an urban area in the State of Paraíba, Brazil. A separate 2023 study in Letters in Applied Microbiology isolated G. dilepis among white rot fungi from decayed wood. That study does not describe the collection sites in the text supplied here. *[lit_2, lit_3]*
