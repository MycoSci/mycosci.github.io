## Summary

Lentinus arcularius (Batsch) Zmitr., known as the spring polypore, is a species of fungus in the family Polyporaceae. One source describes the cap as 1–8 cm across, convex to depressed, with a decurrent hymenium of hexagonal pores and a central stipe. It has been reported from all continents, primarily the United States, Austria, Mexico, Australia and Japan. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was first documented in 1783 by the German naturalist August Batsch as Boletus arcularius. It was renamed Polyporus arcularius in 1821 by the Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries, and was transferred to the genus Lentinus in 2010. *[wikipedia]*
