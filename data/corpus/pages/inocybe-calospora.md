## Summary

Inocybe calospora Quél. (1881) is a species in the family Inocybaceae, with its type locality in France. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as one of many brownish Inocybe species, with a small, scaly, dark brown to brown cap. That source says it can only be identified with certainty under a microscope, because the spores are covered in prominent spines 2-3 µm long. *[mushroomexpert]*
