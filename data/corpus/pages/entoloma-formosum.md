## Summary

Entoloma formosum (Fr.) Noordel. is a mushroom in the family Entolomataceae, published in Index of Fungi 5: 479 (1985). Wikipedia describes it as an inconspicuous yellowish-brown mushroom with a depressed yellowish-to-brownish cap 1–5 cm wide, with darker radial lines. GBIF's distribution record for the species lists Belgium (including the Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
