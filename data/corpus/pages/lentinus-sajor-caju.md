## Summary

Lentinus sajor-caju (Fr.) Fr. (1838) is a species in the family Polyporaceae, formerly placed in Pleurotus as Pleurotus sajor-caju. Wikipedia describes it with a funnel-shaped (infundibuliform) cap 4–8 cm across, decurrent gills, a white spore print, and a short white stipe bearing a ring (annulus). It is described as saprophytic on rotten wood. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the current name Lentinus sajor-caju was published by Elias Magnus Fries in 1838, and that the species was formerly known as Pleurotus sajor-caju. It also states that the name Pleurotus sajor-caju is often incorrectly used by cultivator-mycologists for Pleurotus pulmonarius, and that the two are separated by the ring on the stipe (present in L. sajor-caju, absent in P. pulmonarius) and by hyphal system (trimitic or dimitic in L. sajor-caju, monomitic in P. pulmonarius). This is a single-source account (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
