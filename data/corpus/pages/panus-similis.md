## Summary

Panus similis (Berk. & Broome) T.W. May & A.E. Wood, 1995, is a species in the family Panaceae (Polyporales). The only usable source, a GBIF-hosted description, gives it a solitary, medium to large fruiting body with a thin, coriaceous, funnel- to cup-shaped cap 3–5.5 cm across that is cinnamon-brown or pale brown and radially grooved. It also describes crowded, decurrent buff to pale brown gills and a central, chestnut-brown, solid stipe 7.5–9 cm long. That source lists the distribution as Angola, Australia, Brunei, China, Congo, India, Ivory Coast, Kenya, Malay Peninsula, Papua New Guinea, Philippines, Sabah, Sarawak, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, Thailand, Uganda, Vietnam and Zanzibar. *[gbif_desc]*
