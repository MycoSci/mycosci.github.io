## Summary

Psathyrella corrugis (Pers.) Konrad & Maubl. (1949) is a member of the family Psathyrellaceae, order Agaricales. Wikipedia describes it as the type species of the genus Psathyrella and as common in North America. Wikipedia's description gives a cap 1–4 cm wide that is bell-shaped and translucent when young, a whitish stalk 4–12 cm tall, and a dark brown to black spore print. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states the species was originally described from Europe as Agaricus corrugis. It adds that the lectotype of Psathyrella is P. gracilis, but naming priority goes to P. corrugis, published in 1794, which is 27 years earlier than P. gracilis. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it can be found growing around areas of dead wood and grass throughout North America, and that it sometimes fruits with Tubaria furfuracea. The GBIF record lists distribution entries for Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Vermont, without further habitat detail. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
