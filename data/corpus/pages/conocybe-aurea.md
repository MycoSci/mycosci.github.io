## Summary

Conocybe aurea (Jul. Schäffer) Hongo, 1963, is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae. Wikipedia describes it as having an orangish-yellow cap up to 5 cm across, with beige gills and stipe. The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia, the only source that discusses the name, states that the specific epithet aurea is Latin for golden yellow. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the fungus was first described in 1930 by the German mycologist Julius Schäffer as Galera aurea, and Tsuguo Hongo transferred it to Conocybe in 1963. Wikipedia adds that Anton Hausknecht published the variety C. aurea var. hololeuca in 2000, and that Index Fungorum does not consider this taxon to have independent taxonomic significance. This account comes from a single source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, as the only source with habitat text, describes C. aurea as a saprobic fungus that prefers nitrate-rich soils, fields, woodchip mulch, old compost and greenhouses. It calls the species rare but widespread, found in Europe, Asia, North America, South America and New Zealand. This is a single-source claim. *[wikipedia]*
