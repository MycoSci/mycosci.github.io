## Summary

Agrocybe firma (Peck) Singer, 1940, is a member of the Strophariaceae (Agaricales). MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a brown-spored, ringless species with a dark brown cap and a stem marked by whitish-then-brown fibrils that aggregate into vague longitudinal lines. A 2025 study reported it as a previously unrecorded species in Korea. *[mushroomexpert, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single source) describes it as saprobic, growing in small to large clusters (rarely alone) on the deadwood of hardwoods, from spring through fall. It is described as widely distributed and fairly common east of the Great Plains, very rarely reported from Colorado, California and the Pacific Northwest, and also found in Europe. The described and illustrated collection is from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*
