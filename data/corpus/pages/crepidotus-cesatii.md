## Summary

Crepidotus cesatii (Rabenh.) Sacc. (1877) is a species in the family Crepidotaceae, commonly called the roundspored oysterling. Wikipedia describes it as a saprophytic fungus with a stipeless, sessile cap. GBIF holds more than 10,000 occurrence records, mostly from European countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as saprophytic, found on woody and herbaceous plant debris from many different hosts including conifers. It is said to appear from late summer to winter, usually in small scattered groups. This habitat description comes from a single independent source cluster (Wikipedia, which iNaturalist copies). The GBIF description record lists a distribution of Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
