## Summary

Agaricus dulcidulus Schulzer (1874) is a small mushroom in the family Agaricaceae, known in English as the rosy wood mushroom. Wikipedia describes it as found in deciduous woodlands of Europe and also recorded in North America, North Africa and Asia. A 2020 Mycotaxon paper reported it for the first time from Turkey, based on collections from Tokat and Yozgat provinces. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia notes that some sources tend to synonymize this species with Agaricus semotus, but states that it is a distinct taxon. This is a single-source statement, and no source supplied here gives the story of the original description. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (single independent source) describes Agaricus dulcidulus as a mycorrhizal species in close relation with different oak species. It says the fungus prefers humid soil with rich decomposed leaves as a substrate, and that fruiting bodies appear from late July to early October depending on microclimatic conditions. It also describes the species as found in deciduous woodlands of Europe. *[wikipedia]*
