## Summary

Lycoperdon marginatum Vittad. (1842), the peeling puffball, is a puffball in the family Agaricaceae. Its spiny outer layer sloughs off in patches or sheets, exposing a smooth, brown inner skin. Fruit bodies are described as 1–5 cm wide, and GBIF holds occurrence records from many countries. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as saprobic, growing on the ground singly, scattered, or in groups, often on sandy soil, in nutrient-poor locations, or in oak-pine woods, fruiting between June and December, and reports it from Europe, North and South America, and Australia. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered, or gregariously in woods under hardwoods or conifers, but also along roadsides and in urban settings, in summer and fall, and widely distributed in North America; its described collections are from Illinois. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
