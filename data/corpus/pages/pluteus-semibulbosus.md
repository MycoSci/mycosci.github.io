## Summary

Pluteus semibulbosus (Lasch) Quél. (1875) is a species in the family Pluteaceae, based on Agaricus semibulbosus. The only supplied source, a GBIF-hosted description, gives a hemispherical pileus about 43 mm across that is pale yellow toward the margin.
