## Summary

Psathyrella pennata (Fr.) A. Pearson & Dennis (1948) is a species of mushroom in the family Psathyrellaceae. Wikipedia gives its common names as carbon brittlestem and bonfire brittlestem, and states that it usually fruits during the spring. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that Psathyrella pennata usually fruits in spring, and sometimes in fall and winter. It grows in soil in burned areas after forest fires and at firepits. Wikipedia also says it is often found near Morchella, Crassisporium funariophilum and Geopyxis carbonaria, as well as other fungi.
