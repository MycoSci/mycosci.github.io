## Summary

Trichaptum sector (Ehrenb.) Kreisel, 1971 is a polypore in the order Hymenochaetales, and Index Fungorum lists Nigrohirschioporus sector as its current name. A GBIF-hosted description says it is recognizable by a dark grayish to purplish-gray pore surface and flabelliform white to grayish-brown basidiomes. Both that description and MushroomExpert.Com place it on hardwood (deciduous) wood. *[gbif_desc, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on the deadwood of hardwoods and causing a white rot. It grows alone or gregariously, often in overlapping clusters, is annual, and appears year-round. That source says it is widely distributed in Mexico and found in the United States from eastern Texas to North Carolina, and that its illustrated and described collections are from Texas. It also quotes Overholts (1953) as calling the species the "tropical analogue" of Trichaptum biforme. A description hosted by GBIF states that it grows on deciduous wood and is a Neotropical species, widespread from the southeastern United States to northern Argentina (citing Ryvarden 2016). *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
