## Summary

Scleroderma cepa Pers. (1801), the onion earthball or smooth earthball, is a gasteroid fungus in the family Sclerodermataceae. Sources describe it as poisonous, and one reports a worldwide distribution. MushroomExpert.Com notes that confident identification within this genus requires microscopic analysis. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc, mushroomexpert]*

## Cultural and culinary history

Wikipedia states that it is used as a soil inoculant in agriculture and horticulture. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Sources agree that it is ectomycorrhizal or mycorrhizal, but they differ in detail. Wikipedia says it occurs solitary, scattered or in groups, in sparse grass, woodlands, landscaped areas or on disturbed ground, fruiting in summer and fall in watered areas or after rain. MushroomExpert.Com says it appears primarily under oaks in woodland, and also in parks and gardens near oaks. It describes the species as mycorrhizal with oaks and other hardwoods, growing gregariously from spring through fall, or over winter in warm climates. A GBIF-hosted description (Bau et al. 2024, as cited there) says it is gregarious on soil, usually in subtropical forests, associated with Fagaceae, Juglandaceae and Pinaceae. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
