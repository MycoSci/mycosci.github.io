## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com notes that Boekhout (1986, 1990) treated Volvariella taylorii as a variety of Volvariella pusilla, since it differed only in its darker colors and slightly larger size. The same page states that a more recent DNA study (Justo et al. 2011b) clearly separates the two taxa at the species level. It also lists "Volvariella taylori" (with one i) as an alternate spelling. *[mushroomexpert]*
