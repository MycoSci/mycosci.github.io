## Summary

Stereum gausapatum (Fr.) Fr. (1874) is a member of the family Stereaceae, known in English as Bleeding Oak Crust. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a crust fungus that usually forms shelving, cap-like fruiting bodies on the deadwood of oaks, with a yellowish to orange-brown undersurface that turns dark red where scratched. GBIF distribution records list Belgium, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Madeira and Vermont, among other places. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic on the deadwood of oaks, and says it is apparently limited to association with oak deadwood. It grows densely gregariously, often from gaps in the bark, and causes a white rot of the heartwood. The fruiting body is annual and is reported in spring, summer, fall and winter. It is described as widely distributed in North America, and the illustrated and described collections are from Illinois. This is a single-source description. *[mushroomexpert]*
