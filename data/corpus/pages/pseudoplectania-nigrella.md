## Summary

Pseudoplectania nigrella (Pers.) Fuckel, the ebony cup, is a cup fungus in the family Sarcosomataceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes its fruit bodies as small blackish cups, typically up to 2 cm broad. GBIF holds occurrence records for it from many countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Christian Hendrik Persoon named the species Peziza nigrella in 1801, and Fries sanctioned that name in 1821. In 1870 Fuckel transferred it to his newly described genus Pseudoplectania and made it the type species. Wikipedia adds that Hazslinszky placed it in Crouania and Karsten (1885) placed it in Plectania, but says neither placement is considered correct. This account comes from a single source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as saprobic, growing in groups on the ground or on moss-covered decaying wood, especially amongst fallen pine needles. It reports that in North America fruit bodies appear in spring and summer and are fairly common. In Britain it says the fungus fruits from winter to spring and is rare. Mushroom World, a separate source, says it is typically found in both deciduous and coniferous woodlands, often in early spring. Only that source makes the deciduous-woodland statement. Wikipedia lists a worldwide distribution including North America, the Caribbean, Europe, India, Madagascar, New Zealand, Israel and Japan. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*
