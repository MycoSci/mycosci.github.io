## Summary

Cantharellus roseocanus (Redhead, Norvell & Danell) Redhead, Norvell & Moncalvo, 2012, is a chanterelle in the family Hydnaceae, known in English as the rainbow chanterelle. It was first described as a variety of Cantharellus cibarius and later raised to species rank. It is recorded from the United States, Canada, Mexico and Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as having bright orange false gills that contrast with a duller cap, and young caps often covered with a pinkish bloom. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Scott A. Redhead first described the taxon in 1997 as a variety of Cantharellus cibarius, and that it became its own species in 2012. MushroomExpert.Com attributes the original description to Redhead and collaborators (1997), from the Pacific Northwest. It adds that a 2013 paper by Foltz and collaborators published the peer-reviewed data that allowed promotion from variety to species. Neither source describes the type material. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (a single-source account) describes it as mycorrhizal with spruces (including sitka spruce and Engelmann spruce) and with two-needled pines (including Bishop pine, lodgepole pine and jack pine). It grows alone, gregariously or in loose clusters in summer and fall. Its range is given as the Rocky Mountains westward, with reports from Québec to Maine; the described collections are from Colorado and Wyoming. Wikipedia places it in the Pacific Northwest region of North America. A 2024 study in Revista Chapingo recorded it in pine forests (Pinus hartwegii, P. montezumae and P. pseudostrobus) in Pico de Orizaba National Park, Veracruz, Mexico. Collecting there ran from June to November over four years (2019-2022). *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, lit_3]*
