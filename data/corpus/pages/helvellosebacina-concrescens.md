## Summary

Helvellosebacina concrescens (Schwein.) Oberw., Garnica & K. Riess (2014) is a jelly fungus in the family Sebacinaceae, order Sebacinales. MushroomExpert.Com describes its fruiting body as an amorphous, vaguely lobed, whitish to pale watery gray mass 2–10 cm across that climbs up plant stems from the ground. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

MushroomExpert.Com lists Tremella concrescens and Sebacina concrescens as former names of this species, and cites Schweinitz (1822) and Oberwinkler, Garnica & Riess (2014) as the naming references. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single field-guide source), it is mycorrhizal with hardwoods and grows in amorphous masses that climb from the ground up the stems of plants, fruiting in summer and fall, or occasionally over winter in warm climates. The same source describes it as widely distributed in North America from the Great Plains eastward and southward into Mexico, and also known from South America; its described collections are from Illinois. GBIF's description record lists a distribution of Vermont. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
