## Summary

Ramaria subbotrytis (Coker) Corner, 1950, is a coral fungus in the family Gomphaceae, order Gomphales. It was first described as Clavaria subbotrytis by William Chambers Coker in 1923 from collections made in North Carolina. Records in the site catalog span North America, Europe and several other regions. *[wikipedia]*
