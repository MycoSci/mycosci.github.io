## Summary

Tulosesus ephemerus (Bull.) D. Wächter & A. Melzer (2020) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. It was first described as Agaricus ephemerus by Bulliard in 1786, and it was known as Coprinellus ephemerus until the 2020 reclassification by Wächter and Melzer. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

First described as Agaricus ephemerus by the French mycologist Jean Baptiste François Pierre Bulliard in 1786. Wikipedia reports that a 2001 phylogenetic study led to a major reorganization and reshuffling of the genus, and this species was transferred to Coprinellus. It was known as Coprinellus ephemerus until 2020, when the German mycologists Dieter Wächter and Andreas Melzer reclassified many species in the Psathyrellaceae based on phylogenetic analysis.
