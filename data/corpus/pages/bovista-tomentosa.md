## Summary

Bovista tomentosa (Vittad.) De Toni is a puffball fungus in the family Lycoperdaceae. It was first described by Carlo Vittadini and given its current name by Giovanni Battista de Toni in 1888. It is recorded from Europe, Asia, Africa and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia (the only independent source with habitat text; iNaturalist copies its summary but not this passage) says the species appears in North America, Europe, Africa and Asia, most often in Europe. It says it usually grows outside forests, in sunny places, among xerothermic vegetation, on calcareous soils among grasses, and on pastures. It adds that it sometimes grows on rocks and less often in pine forests, parks and gardens. It also lists Juniperus communis thickets, abandoned farmlands and industrial waste. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
