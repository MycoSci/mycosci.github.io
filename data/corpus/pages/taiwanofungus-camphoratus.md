## Summary

Taiwanofungus camphoratus (M. Zang & C.H. Su) Sheng H. Wu, Z.H. Yu, Y.C. Dai & C.H. Su, 2004, is a fungus in the family Fomitopsidaceae (order Polyporales). Wikipedia gives the common name stout camphor fungus and describes it as endemic to Taiwan, growing only on the tree Cinnamomum kanehirae and causing a brown heart rot. *[wikipedia]*

## Cultural and culinary history

Wikipedia states that the fungus is used in Taiwanese traditional medicine as a purported remedy, and that its fruiting bodies can fetch high prices; it reports good-quality fruiting bodies costing as much as US$15,000/kg in 1997. This is a single-cluster claim (Wikipedia, mirrored by iNaturalist for the opening sentence only). *[wikipedia]*

## Cultivation

Wikipedia states that artificial cultivation methods were developed after 1997, and that some people have illegally farmed the fungus in Taiwanese forests by hollowing out Cinnamomum kanehirae trees. No cultivation method is described in the supplied sources. *[wikipedia]*
