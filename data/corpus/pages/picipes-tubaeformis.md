## Summary

Picipes tubaeformis (P. Karst.) Zmitr. & Kovalenko, 2016, is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae; Polyporellus varius subsp. tubaeformis is listed as a synonym. Mushroom World, an aggregator and the only source with descriptive text, calls it the Trumpet Polypore and describes it as a tough, funnel-shaped polypore with a cap 2-12 cm across. ## Habitat and ecology

Mushroom World states that it grows on decaying wood of both deciduous and coniferous trees, appearing from spring through autumn.
