## Summary

Chroogomphus fulmineus (R. Heim) Courtec. 1988 is a member of the Gomphidiaceae (Boletales), with occurrence records from Spain, France, Austria, Portugal, Italy and several other countries. A 2018 revision of European Chroogomphus recognises it as one of eight species in Europe. It was one of twelve mycorrhizal mushroom species from Northeast Portugal included in a 2011 chemical and antioxidant characterization study. *[lit_1, lit_3]*

## Discovery and naming

A 2018 revision of European Chroogomphus chose an epitype for C. fulmineus. A 2016 barcoding study says C. mediterraneus shows macroscopic features similar to C. fulmineus and C. rutilus, and had been confused with them in the literature. *[lit_2, lit_3]*
