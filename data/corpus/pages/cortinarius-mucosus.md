Cortinarius mucosus (Bull.) E. Berger, the orange webcap, is a species in the family Cortinariaceae. Descriptions in the supplied sources give it a slimy, brownish-orange cap, a slimy whitish stem and a rusty-brown spore print. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as widely distributed in North America, Mushroom World lists Europe and North America, and the GBIF description record lists Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*

It places the species in subgenus Myxacium, section Myxacium, under the infrageneric classification of Cortinarius proposed by Moser in Singer (1986). ## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says the species is commonly found under birch and coniferous trees, especially pine, and prefers acidic, sandy soils. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as mycorrhizal with pines (especially, but not exclusively, 2- and 3-needled pines) and with other conifers, growing alone, scattered or gregariously in late summer and fall, and apparently widely distributed in North America. Mushroom World states that it typically grows with pine or birch, prefers acidic, sandy soils, and occurs in Europe and North America. The GBIF description record lists distribution as Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, mushroomworld, gbif_desc]*
