## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Suillus bellinii as a thermophilic fungus occurring in the Mediterranean region of southern Europe, in coastal pine forests. It forms ectomycorrhizal associations with pines, including Aleppo pine (Pinus halepensis), maritime pine (Pinus pinaster) and stone pine (Pinus pinea).
