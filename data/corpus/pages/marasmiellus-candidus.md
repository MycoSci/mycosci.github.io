Marasmiellus candidus (Fr.) Singer, 1948, is a species in the family Omphalotaceae. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a tiny marasmioid mushroom with a white to whitish cap 6–18 mm across, found on sticks, canes, small logs, and similar forest litter. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on twigs, canes, small logs, and similar litter in hardwood and conifer forests. It grows alone, scattered, or gregariously, in summer and fall, or over winter in warm climates.
