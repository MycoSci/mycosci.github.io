## Summary

Coprinopsis martinii (P.D. Orton) Redhead, Vilgalys & Moncalvo (2001) is a species of fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, originally described as Coprinus martinii. Wikipedia describes it as a small inkcap that grows in wetland environments. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described in 1960 by the English mycologist Peter Darbishire Orton as Coprinus martinii. In 2001, after phylogenetic analysis restructured the genus Coprinus, it was reclassified as Coprinopsis martinii by Scott Alan Redhead, Rytas J. Vilgalys and Jean-Marc Moncalvo. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it grows trooping in small groups on rotting sedges and rushes of the genera Carex, Scirpus and Juncus, in marshes and wetland environments from spring through autumn, and calls it widespread but seldom recorded. This is a single-source account. GBIF's description record separately lists the distribution as DK, NO and SE. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
