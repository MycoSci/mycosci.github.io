## Summary

Leucocoprinus ianthinus (Sacc.) P. Mohr (1994) is a species of mushroom-producing fungus in the family Agaricaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a small dapperling with a whitish cap bearing a dark purple to reddish-brown centre and purplish scales, and a stem with a violet to lilac base. GBIF description records list its distribution as Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Denmark, France, Great Britain, the Netherlands, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that, like several other Leucocoprinus species, it may have originated in a tropical climate but is now found in plant pots, greenhouses and compost piles in many countries. The same source says it is seen less often in plant pots than Leucocoprinus birnbaumii and less often in the wild than Leucocoprinus brebissonii. This is a single-source claim; iNaturalist repeats the same text and is not independent. *[wikipedia]*
