## Summary

Cortinarius gentilis, the Conifer Webcap, is a fungus in the family Cortinariaceae, described by Fries. Wikipedia places it in the subgenus Telamonia and reports it from North America and Europe. Its toxicity is contested: it was historically reported as poisonous, but a 2003 Finnish study found no signs of toxicity. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states it grows separately or in groups in moss under conifer trees, and can be found in North America's Pacific Northwest and Rocky Mountains. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
