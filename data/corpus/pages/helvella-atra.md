## Summary

Helvella atra, commonly known as the dark elfin saddle, is a species of Helvellaceae (order Pezizales) described by Oeder in 1770. Wikipedia describes a gray, saddle-shaped cap up to 2 cm tall and wide on a stalk up to 3.5 cm long and 5 mm thick. Wikipedia reports it from China and Japan, and GBIF distribution records list Belgium (Flanders and Wallonia) and Vermont. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
