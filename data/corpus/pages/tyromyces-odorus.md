## Summary

Tyromyces odorus (Sacc.) Zmitr. is a wood-growing shelf fungus in the family Incrustoporiaceae (order Polyporales). The combination in Tyromyces dates to 2018. It has been recorded in Sweden, Finland, Estonia, Norway, Russia, the United States and many other countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

When first scientifically described, the species was included in the wastebasket genus Poria. It has since been placed in Antrodia and in Skeletocutis before its current placement in Tyromyces. Wikipedia lists the synonyms Poria odora Sacc., 1888; Polyporus odorus Peck, 1885 (nom. illeg.); Antrodia odora (Sacc.) Gilb. & Ryvarden, 1985; Skeletocutis odora (Sacc.) Ginns, 1984; and Skeletocutis odora f. investiens Zmitr. & Malysheva, 2015. This account comes from a single encyclopedia source. *[wikipedia]*
