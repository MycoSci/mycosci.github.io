## Summary

Deconica horizontalis (Bull.) Noordel. (2009) is a species of agaric fungus in the family Strophariaceae, known in English as the wood oysterling. Wikipedia describes the fruit bodies as kidney-shaped when viewed from above, with a short, usually off-center stipe, and the cap, stipe and lamellae all brownish. The only descriptive text supplied is that single Wikipedia entry. *[wikipedia]*
