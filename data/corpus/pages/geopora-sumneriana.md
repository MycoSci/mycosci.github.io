## Summary

Geopora sumneriana (Cooke ex W. Phillips) M. Torre 1976 is a member of the Pyronemataceae, known in English as the Cedar Cup. Wikipedia describes a rounded, brown, roughly hairy ascocarp that forms underground, then breaks the surface in spring as a cream-coloured cup up to 7 cm across and 5 cm tall. This description rests on a single independent source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that the species occurs in small groups and is exclusively found associated with cedar trees. It also states that the fruit body stays subterranean for most of the year and breaks the surface in spring. The GBIF description record lists distribution for Belgium (Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon Regions), Germany, Denmark, France, Great Britain, the Netherlands, Norway and Slovenia. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
