## Summary

Parasola misera (P. Karst.) Redhead, Vilgalys & Hopple (2001) is a species of coprophilous fungus in the family Psathyrellaceae, formerly placed as Coprinus miser and known in English as the Least Inkcap. Wikipedia states that it grows on the dung of goats and possibly on that of sheep. The GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
