GBIF distribution records list Belgium (including the Flemish and Walloon Regions), Norway and Vermont. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a shell- or fan-shaped, stemless mushroom with brown fibrils or scales on the cap and orangish immature gills. *[gbif_desc, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology
