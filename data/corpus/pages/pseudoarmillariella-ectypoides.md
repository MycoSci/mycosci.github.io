## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies records that Peck described the species as Agaricus ectypoides in Annual Report of the New York State Museum 24: 61 (1872) [1871], with the typification given as "Peck s.n.". It lists later combinations in Clitocybe (Saccardo, 1887), Cantharellula (Singer, 1942), Clitocybula (Raithelhuber, 1980) and Omphalina (Bigelow, 1982). Singer's Pseudoarmillariella combination appeared in Mycologia 48(5): 725 (1956), where Wikispecies notes the epithet was printed as 'ectyloides'. The sources give no reasons for any of these transfers. *[wikispecies]*
