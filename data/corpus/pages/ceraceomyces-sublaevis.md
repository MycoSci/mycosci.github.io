## Summary

Ceraceomyces sublaevis (Bres.) Jülich, 1972, is a species of corticioid fungus in the family Amylocorticiaceae. A 1996 Korean survey of wood-rotting Aphyllophorales reported it as new to Korea and registered it with a description. *[lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

In Korea it was recorded from wood-rotting fungi collected on field trips to mountain areas between October 1994 and June 1995. A separate variety, Ceraceomyces sublaevis var. grandisporus, is reported from tree species in Chandigarh, India, in a study of wood-rotting corticioid fungi of deciduous trees. That record is for the variety, not the nominal species. *[lit_1, lit_3]*
