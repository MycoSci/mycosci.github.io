## Summary

Ascobolus brassicae P. Crouan & H. Crouan (1857) is an apothecial fungus in the family Ascobolaceae (Pezizales). Wikipedia describes it as a European species with tiny off-white discs, later turning purple, with toothed edges and up to 1 mm across.
