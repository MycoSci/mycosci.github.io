## Summary

Hortiboletus engelii (Hlaváček) Biketova & Wasser (2015) is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, with GBIF occurrence records from 32 countries, mostly in Europe. A GBIF-hosted description gives the cap as (4–)5–13(–15) cm broad, nearly hemispherical at first and then convex to flattened, with a dry, finely felty surface that often cracks, and colours that vary widely from dark brown through ochre and grey to pinkish-red. The same description gives yellow tubes that are unchanging to weakly bluing when injured, large pores, a yellow-topped stipe that is sometimes partly or wholly red, and an olive-brown spore print. *[gbif_desc]*
