## Summary

Imperator torosus (Fr.) Assyov et al. 2015, the brawny bolete, is a species in the family Boletaceae (order Boletales). One source describes it as native to southern Europe east to the Caucasus and Israel and as generally rare in Europe. It was transferred to Imperator in 2015, having been known for many years as Boletus torosus. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

One source states that the specific epithet torosus derives from Latin and means "muscular". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that Louis Secretan described the species as Boletus pachypus in 1833, but many of his names have been rejected for nomenclatural purposes. Elias Magnus Fries and Christopher Theodor Hök described Boletus torosus in 1835 based on Secretan's B. pachypus. Fries wrote in 1838 that he had not actually observed the species, and no type specimen or illustration was designated. Lucien Quélet transferred it to Dictyopus in 1886. In 2013, Valerio Bertolini and Giampaolo Simonini noted that the original description was so brief that some later papers applied the name B. torosus to specimens closer to Boletus rhodopurpureus or B. luteocupreus, and they selected a neotype from the French-Swiss border region. Molecular phylogenetic work published in 2013 placed it well away from Boletus edulis, the type species of Boletus, and it was made the type species of the new genus Imperator in 2015. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a mycorrhizal species, generally associated with deciduous trees such as hornbeam (Carpinus), oak (Quercus) and beech (Fagus) in warm, dry locales. Fruitings are reported typically from July to September in calcareous (chalky) soil, with fruit bodies appearing singly or in small groups. The same source records it under Quercus coccifera trees in the Bar'am forest of Upper Galilee, and says it is generally rare in Europe but appears to be relatively common in Hungary. This is a single-source account. The iNaturalist text is a declared copy of the Wikipedia text. *[wikipedia]*
