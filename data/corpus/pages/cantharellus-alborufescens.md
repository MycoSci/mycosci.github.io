## Summary

Cantharellus alborufescens (Malençon) Papetti & S. Alberti (1999) is a species in the family Hydnaceae, with the basionym Cantharellus cibarius var. alborufescens. It has been recorded in southern and central Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. A 2018 paper in Cryptogamie Mycologie reported it as new to Iran, from northern Iranian forests. *[lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as growing in oak forest among Quercus ilex and Quercus pubescens. In northern Iran, a 2018 study collected it in slightly acidic soils (Cryptogamie Mycologie), and a 2020 plot study in a plain forest at Noor, Mazandaran, found it in oak-hornbeam stands on clay-loam soils with pH 5.7-7.2. That study also found it associated with higher soil carbon content, a higher C:N ratio and more sand and clay than plots without it. No source supplied here states a fruiting season. *[wikipedia, lit_2, lit_3]*
