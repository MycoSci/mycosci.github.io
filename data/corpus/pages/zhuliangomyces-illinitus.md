## Summary

Zhuliangomyces illinitus (Fr.) Redhead is a species in the family Amanitaceae (order Agaricales), known in English as the Dripping Slimecap. Wikipedia and the iNaturalist entry derived from it describe it as a mushroom-forming fungus with a distinctive slimy cap. The site catalog and GBIF record it from many countries. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that a 2018 study proposed splitting the genus Limacella into three genera, and that this species was reclassified as Myxoderma illinitum. Because the name Myxoderma was already used for a cyanobacteria clade, Scott A. Redhead renamed the genus Zhuliangomyces, which gave the current name Zhuliangomyces illinitus. This account comes from a single source cluster (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source cluster with habitat text, says the species is typically found scattered in temperate mixed forests, swamps and grass lawns. Its distribution section, which uses the older name Limacella illinita, adds woods, fields, lawns, roadsides and sand dunes, growing singly, scattered or in groups. For North America it gives a season from July or August to October or November. Wikipedia also says the species was expected to be facultatively mycorrhizal but did not form ectomycorrhizal relationships with plant roots in a laboratory setting. It says the species is assumed to be saprobic, although more research is required. All of this is single-source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*
