## Summary

Hygrocybe glutinipes (J.E. Lange) R. Haller Aar., 1956, is a waxcap in the family Hygrophoraceae, known in English as the Glutinous Waxcap. GBIF's description record lists it for Belgium, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. MushroomExpert.Com describes Illinois collections that it considers a good match for the species as small, slimy, bright orange waxcaps with caps 5-30 mm across and a white spore print. *[gbif_desc, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes Illinois collections, which it treats as a good match for the European species, as appearing in late spring in oak-hickory forests of central Illinois. They grew usually in moss beds and occasionally on bare soil, scattered or gregariously, often on mossy ridgetops. The same source says the precise ecological role is uncertain and the North American distribution is uncertain. It notes that the Illinois material differs from the European species in its consistently bright orange colors and in its spring appearance. It says the European species is orange or, more commonly, yellow, and appears in summer and fall. This is a single-source account. *[mushroomexpert]*
