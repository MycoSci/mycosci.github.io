## Summary

Crustomyces subabruptus (Bourdot & Galzin) Jülich 1978 is a species of toothed crust fungus in the family Cystostereaceae. It is described as a wood-decay fungus that is widely distributed in Europe and was reported from Iran in 2012. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Hubert Bourdot and Amédée Galzin described the fungus as Odontia subabrupta in their work Hyménomycètes de France, basing it on the similar species Odontia artocreas, a South American crust fungus originally described by Giacomo Bresadola in 1896. Walter Jülich transferred it to the genus Crustomyces in 1978. George Chamuris suggested that the western North American Cystostereum pini-canadensis was the same species and proposed the new taxon C. pini-canadensis subspecies subabruptum. This account comes from a single independent source (Wikipedia). *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a wood-decay fungus that grows on both deciduous and coniferous wood, typically occurring in dry and warm localities. It states that the species is widely distributed in Europe and was reported from Iran in 2012. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
