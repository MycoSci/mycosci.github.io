## Summary

Sarcosphaera coronaria (Jacq.) J. Schröt., the violet crowncup, is a cup fungus in the family Pezizaceae. It is described as whitish to grayish, with a fruit body that starts as a partly buried hollow ball and splits from the top downward into rays, leaving a lilac-gray inner surface. Wikipedia notes that recent research has found several species in what was treated as one complex. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia gives the Greek genus name as meaning "flesh ball" and says the Latin specific epithet, coronaria, refers to the crown-like form of the open fruit body. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first named Peziza coronaria by Jacquin in 1778 and underwent several name changes before reaching its current name. The genus Sarcosphaera was first described by Bernhard Auerswald in 1869 to hold the species then known as Peziza macrocalyx. The same source reports that Caulocarpa, a genus first thought to be a truffle, is now considered a generic synonym of Sarcosphaera, and that S. crassa was once considered the correct name for S. coronaria. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Sources differ on host and season. Wikipedia describes fruit bodies found singly, scattered or clustered in broad-leaf woods favoring beech, less frequently with conifers, with a noted preference for calcareous soil (though also on acidic bedrock). It says they are more common in mountainous locations and occur most often in spring, often near melting snow. Its introduction also mentions coniferous woods under humus. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, growing alone or more commonly in clusters, partially submerged in the ground in spring, summer or fall, and common from the Rocky Mountains westward but reported in Michigan and New York. Wikipedia reports the fungus from 23 European countries, North Africa, and North America from British Columbia east to Michigan and New York and south to Veracruz, Mexico, and also from Palestine and the Asian part of Turkey. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
