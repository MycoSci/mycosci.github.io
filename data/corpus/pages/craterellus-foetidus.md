## Summary

Craterellus foetidus A.H. Sm. (1968) is a member of the family Hydnaceae, described from Michigan and recorded in the United States and Canada. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as an eastern North American mushroom with a trumpet-shaped fruiting body 4-10 cm tall and 3-7 cm wide. The upper surface is pale to dark watery gray with blackish scales. The outer surface is wrinkled and veined with gill-like folds and cross-veins over the upper third. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single source), it is mycorrhizal with oaks. It grows gregariously or, more commonly, in fused clusters of 2-5 mushrooms. It is described as fairly widely distributed east of the Great Plains, fruiting from late spring through early fall. The collections described and illustrated there are from Illinois. *[mushroomexpert]*
