## Summary

Lactarius trivialis (Fr.) Fr., 1838, is a species in the family Russulaceae, with the English name Tacked Milkcap. Wikipedia states it is most commonly found in Scandinavia and that the cap colour ranges from light brown to dark purple. Mushroom World describes a large fleshy cap that exudes white milk, with pale gills and stem. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies lists the basionym as Agaricus trivialis Fr. 1815, with the name treated in Lactarius by Fries in 1838 (Epicrisis Systematis Mycologici, p. 337). It records later transfers to Galorrheus (Kummer, 1871) and Lactifluus (Kuntze, 1891), plus several taxonomic synonyms including Lactarius curtus Britzelm. (1885), L. deflexus Lindblad (1855), L. homaemus Britzelm. (1885) and L. trivialis var. maculatus Peck (1885). Wikipedia instead says the species was discovered and first recorded in 1838 by Elias Magnus Fries in Epicrisis Systematis Mycologici, which does not account for the 1815 basionym. No supplied source explains the reasons for the transfers. *[wikispecies, wikipedia]*
