## Summary

Lepiota helveola Bres. (1882) is a gilled mushroom in the family Agaricaceae, described by the Italian mycologist Giacomo Bresadola. Wikipedia states that it contains amatoxins, which may cause potentially fatal liver injury. A 1994 clinical report describes a patient with fulminant hepatic failure from poisoning by this species who was treated with liver transplantation. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia credits Giacomo Bresadola with describing the species in 1882. A 1962 paper (Naturalis repository record) compares L. helveola with neighbouring species and reports that L. helveola sensu Joss. and most recent authors is attributed to Lepiota subincarnata J. E. Lange. The same paper describes L. ochraceofulva P. D. Orton and a new species, L. roseo-lanata Huijsm. *[wikipedia, lit_2]*
