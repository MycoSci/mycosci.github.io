## Summary

Cheilymenia fimicola (Bagl.) Dennis, 1978, is an apothecial fungus in the family Pyronemataceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes it as growing as flat orange saucers without stipes, with eyelash-like hairs, reddish-orange when young and lightening with age. It is recorded from many countries in GBIF occurrence data. *[wikipedia]*
