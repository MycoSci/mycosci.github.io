## Discovery and naming

According to the GBIF notes, Roberts (1999) stated that the species lacks a type collection and should be neotypified, and no neotype has been designated. Representative sequences for the taxon were selected from Hibbett & Binder (2002). Those sequences are identical to sequences that Currah et al. (1990) and Taylor & McCormick (2008) treated as Cr. goodyerae-repentis, and the phylogenetic analyses in that study clustered them together. The notes say the two may be conspecific. Andersen & Stalpers (1994) treated Cr. goodyerae-repentis and R. goodyerae-repentis as nomina dubia, and this treatment follows them pending typification of the names. *[gbif_desc]*
