## Summary

Mycena urania, the violet bonnet, is a small mushroom in the family Mycenaceae. Wikipedia describes the cap as initially conic, expanding to hemispheric with maturity, and typically 0.4 to 1 cm across; Mushroom World gives 0.5 to 1.6 cm. Wikipedia calls it a rare species. *[wikipedia, mushroomworld]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first named Agaricus uranius in 1818 by the Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries and was assigned its current name in 1872 by the French naturalist Lucien Quélet.
