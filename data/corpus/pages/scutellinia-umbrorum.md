## Summary

Scutellinia umbrorum (Fr.) Lambotte, 1887, is an apothecial fungus in the family Pyronemataceae, order Pezizales. Wikipedia describes it as a common European species that forms clusters of orange discs up to 1.5 cm in diameter. The same source says it is very similar to congeners such as Scutellinia olivascens and can only be reliably identified by microscopic features. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology
