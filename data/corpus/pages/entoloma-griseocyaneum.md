## Summary

Entoloma griseocyaneum (Fr.) P. Kumm. (1871) is a gilled mushroom in the family Entolomataceae, with the recommended English name Felted Pinkgill. Wikipedia describes it as a European species of agriculturally unimproved grassland. It reports the species as globally assessed as "vulnerable" on the IUCN Red List because of threats to that habitat. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described by Elias Magnus Fries in 1821 as Agaricus griseocyaneus, and Paul Kummer transferred it to Entoloma in 1871. The same source says Entoloma scabropellis, which lacks blue colours on the stipe, is said to be a synonym based on DNA analysis. This comes from a single independent source and gives no detail on the type material. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as rare but widespread in Europe. It says the species occurs in old, agriculturally unimproved, short-sward grassland (pastures and lawns) and is typical of waxcap grasslands. This is a single-source account (iNaturalist reproduces part of the same text). *[wikipedia]*
