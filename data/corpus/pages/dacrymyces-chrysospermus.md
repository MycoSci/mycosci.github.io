## Summary

Dacrymyces chrysospermus Berk. & M.A. Curtis (1873) is a jelly fungus in the family Dacrymycetaceae. Wikipedia gives its recommended UK English name as orange jelly spot, and says that in North America it is called orange jelly or orange witch's butter. The same source describes the fruit bodies as gelatinous, bright orange and extremely variable in shape. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as saprotrophic on dead coniferous wood. It says the species is now understood to be distributed worldwide on a variety of conifers, is considered common across North America, and has been collected in South America, Europe, Asia and Australia.
