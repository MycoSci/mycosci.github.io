## Summary

Scleroderma bovista Fr. (1829) is a species in the family Sclerodermataceae, order Boletales. The single description record supplied describes small to medium-sized, subglobose to tuberiform basidiomata 19–58 mm in diameter with a leathery peridium bearing flaky to granular brown squamules, and a gleba that is compact at first and becomes pulverulent. The same record notes that the recently described S. griseobrunneum could be a morphotype of S. bovista. *[gbif_desc]*
