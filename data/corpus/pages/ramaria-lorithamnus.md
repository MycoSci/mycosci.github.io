## Summary

Ramaria lorithamnus (Berk.) R.H. Petersen, 1982, is a fungus in the family Gomphaceae, order Gomphales. It has been recorded in Australia, New Zealand, Chile and China. Synonyms include Clavaria lorithamnus, Clavaria sinapicolor, Ramaria sinapicolor and Ramariopsis lorithamnus.
