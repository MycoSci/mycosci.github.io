## Summary

Chaetocalathus craterellus (Durieu & Lév.) Singer, 1943, is a species in the family Marasmiaceae. A 1993 paper reports it from Albania for the first time and gives a description, and a 2002 paper describes and illustrates a collection from the Macchiagrande WWF oasis in the Roman coastal woodlands. A 1981 paper on Neotropical cyphellaceous fungi discusses similarities and differences between this species and the newly described Cyphellocalathus cecropiae, among other taxa. *[lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*
