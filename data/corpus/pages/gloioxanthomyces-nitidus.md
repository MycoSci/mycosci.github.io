## Summary

Gloioxanthomyces nitidus, the shining waxcap, is a species in the family Hygrophoraceae. It was first described by Berkeley and Curtis in 1853 as a Hygrophorus and moved into the newly created genus Gloioxanthomyces in 2013. The fruitbodies are described as having convex, apricot-yellow to orange caps roughly 1–4 cm across, with pale to bright yellow, waxy, decurrent gills. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
