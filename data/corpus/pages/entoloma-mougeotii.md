## Summary

Entoloma mougeotii (Fr.) Hesler, 1967, is a species in the family Entolomataceae, and GBIF descriptions list Belgium, Denmark, Norway and Sweden in its distribution. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a small entolomatoid mushroom with a grayish purple, finely scaly cap and a grayish purple stem with a white apex. It says the gills are white at first and turn pinkish as the spores mature, and that the spore print is pink. That source describes it as a European species and notes that North American collections identified under this name may only be a best-fit assignment. *[mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology
