## Summary

Clitocybe marginella Harmaja (1969; Index of Fungi 3: 548) is a species in the family Tricholomataceae, with its type locality in Finland. English Wikipedia describes it as a saprotrophic fungus of coniferous litter in southern Scandinavia and Finland, where it is not common. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to English Wikipedia, the holotype was collected on 17 September 1967 in a coniferous wood near Suoniini, Somero, in the province of Etelä-Häme, Finland. An isotype (duplicate) is preserved at the herbarium of the University of Michigan. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

English Wikipedia, the only independent source with habitat text, describes it as saprotrophic, fruiting solitarily or in small arcs and rings on litter under Pinus or Picea, occasionally among Juniperus, alder, birch or goat willow, and often associated with mosses such as Pleurozium and Dicranum. It reports fruiting from late August to late November. It gives the range as the temperate to northern boreal zones of southern Scandinavia and Finland, up to about 250 m elevation, where it is not common. *[wikipedia]*
