## Summary

Tricholoma virgatum (Fr.) P. Kumm. 1871, the ashen knight, is a gilled mushroom in the family Tricholomataceae. Sources describe a grey, sharply conical cap with darker radiating fibres and a white spore print. It is recorded from Europe and North America, and Wikipedia also lists China. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that Elias Fries first described the species as Agaricus virgatus in 1818, and that Paul Kummer transferred it to Tricholoma in 1871. No source given here explains the reasons for the transfer. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with conifers, growing alone, scattered or gregariously, in fall (and over winter on the West Coast), and fairly widely distributed in North America; the collections it describes are from Colorado. Wikipedia more broadly says it is found in deciduous and coniferous forests of China, Europe and North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
