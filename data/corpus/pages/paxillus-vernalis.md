## Summary

Paxillus vernalis Watling (1969) is a species in the family Paxillaceae (Boletales), with Michigan as its type locality. Wikipedia describes it as a basidiomycete found in montane forests in northern North America, described as new to science by the Scottish mycologist Roy Watling in 1969. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as mycorrhizal with quaking aspen or paper birch, apparently also capable of existing as a saprobe on wood and woody debris. It grows scattered or gregariously in late summer and fall, and is fairly widely distributed in boreal and montane North America; the same source says it can fruit in stunning numbers under quaking aspen and paper birch. Wikipedia independently describes it as found in montane forests in northern North America. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
