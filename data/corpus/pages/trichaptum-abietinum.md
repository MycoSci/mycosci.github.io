## Summary

Trichaptum abietinum (Pers. ex J.F. Gmel.) Ryvarden, 1972, is a poroid fungus in the order Hymenochaetales that grows on dead conifer wood. The cap is fan-shaped and 1–4 cm wide, and the pore surface is purple when fresh. It is recorded in many countries, including Sweden, the Netherlands, Finland, Norway, the United Kingdom and the United States. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on the dead or dying sapwood of conifers, especially firs, growing in overlapping clusters on logs and stumps from spring through fall, and widely distributed in North America. The collections illustrated there are from Colorado. Wikipedia likewise describes it as saprophytic on dead conifer wood. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
