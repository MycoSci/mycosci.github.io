## Summary

Schizophyllum amplum (Lév.) Nakasone, 1996, is a fungus in the family Schizophyllaceae, also known as poplar bells. Wikipedia describes it as a small bell-shaped fungus with a cap 5–15 mm across. It is recorded from many countries, including several in Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming
