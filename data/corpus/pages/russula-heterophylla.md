## Summary

Russula heterophylla (Fr.) Fr. (1838) is a brittlegill in the family Russulaceae, known in English as the Greasy Green Brittlegill. Wikipedia describes it as a variably coloured species that usually has a greenish cap, 5 to 10 cm across, with white adnexed gills and a white spore print. GBIF records it from many countries, mostly in Europe. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only independent source with ecological text (iNaturalist copies it), says the species is found in deciduous forests and woods in Britain, Europe and Scandinavia. It appears in summer to early autumn, usually in small groups, and is described as occasional. It grows with several species of broad-leaved trees, on whose roots it is ectomycorrhizal. *[wikipedia]*
