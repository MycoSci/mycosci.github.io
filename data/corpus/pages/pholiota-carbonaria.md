## Summary

Pholiota carbonaria (Fr.) Singer (1951) is a species in the family Strophariaceae, order Agaricales. A 2021 laboratory study describes it as a post-fire fungus and examines its physical interactions with the moss Polytrichum commune. *[lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

A 2021 study (Life) describes P. carbonaria as a post-fire fungus. In that study, done under laboratory conditions, fungal appressoria formed on germinating spores and living protonemata of the moss Polytrichum commune, and fungal hyphae were found in moss rhizoids after six months. The authors take this as support for the fungus having multiple ways of obtaining nutrients. It is an in vitro observation, not a field substrate record. The GBIF description record gives the distribution as Vermont. *[lit_1, gbif_desc]*
