## Summary

Cheilymenia theleboloides (Alb. & Schwein.) Boud. (1904) is a cup fungus in the family Pyronemataceae (order Pezizales), with occurrence records from many countries. A 2015 macrofungal survey of the Atatürk Dam Lake basin in Şanlıurfa province reported it as new to the mycobiota of Turkey. A single 2014 histological study describes its apothecia as 1–11 mm across, yellow to orange at first and darkening to brown with age, clothed with short, comparatively few hairs. *[lit_2, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

One study (lit_3) describes the apothecia as sessile, scattered to gregarious on waste soil. In that study they began to appear in January and February and continued to 29 March. The source as supplied does not state the country or region of the collections. *[lit_3]*
