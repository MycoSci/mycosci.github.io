## Summary

Bjerkandera adusta (Willd.) P. Karst., 1879, is a polypore in the family Phanerochaetaceae, commonly called the smoky polypore or smoky bracket. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen that causes white rot in live trees but most commonly appears on dead wood. MushroomExpert.Com describes its dark gray to black pore surface as contrasting with a pale cap surface. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described scientifically as Boletus adustus by Carl Ludwig Willdenow in 1787. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia says the species is often found on decaying wood and describes it as causing white rot in live trees, though most commonly appearing on dead wood. MushroomExpert.Com (a single-author field guide) describes it as saprobic on the deadwood of hardwoods and, rarely, conifers, causing a white rot, annual, appearing year-round, and widely distributed throughout North America. GBIF-supplied specimen records list collections from Yunnan Province, China, on trunks, stumps and fallen branches of angiosperms, and in one case a fallen branch of Pinus. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert, gbif_desc]*
