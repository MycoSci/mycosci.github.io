## Summary

Inocybe whitei (Berk. & Broome) Sacc. is a species of agaric fungus in the family Inocybaceae, recorded in the site catalog under the English name Blushing Fibrecap. Wikipedia describes the mushroom as initially white, developing reddish stains as it matures. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the epithet whitei was given in honour of Dr. Buchanan White, a naturalist of Perthshire. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described as Agaricus whitei by Miles Joseph Berkeley and Christopher Edmund Broome in 1876 and transferred to Inocybe by Pier Andrea Saccardo in 1887. Robert Kühner independently described it as Inocybe pudica in 1947. The two names are now treated as synonyms, with Berkeley and Broome's epithet taking precedence. *[wikipedia]*
