## Summary

Agrocybe pediades (Fr.) Fayod, 1889 is a species in the family Strophariaceae, known in English as the Common Fieldcap. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a cosmopolitan grass-dwelling mushroom with a thin yellow-brown cap, a skinny stem that lacks a ring, and a medium brown spore print. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was first described as Agaricus pediades by the Swedish mycologist Elias Magnus Fries in 1821 and moved to the genus Agrocybe by Victor Fayod in 1889. MushroomExpert.Com's reference line gives the same authorship and years, (Fries, 1821) Fayod, 1889, and lists Agrocybe semiorbicularis as a synonym. Wikipedia adds that some guides list A. semiorbicularis as a separate species. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously in lawns, meadows and other grassy areas, and sometimes on woodchips, manure or compost. It gives the season as summer, or nearly year-round in warm climates. Mushroom World likewise lists grassy areas, cultivated or disturbed soil, and pastures. Wikipedia mentions lawns and other grassland and mulch containing horse manure, and says it appears year-round in North America. MushroomExpert.Com says it was originally described from Europe, is common and widely distributed in North America, and is also found in the Caribbean, South America, Asia and Oceania. *[mushroomexpert, mushroomworld, wikipedia]*
