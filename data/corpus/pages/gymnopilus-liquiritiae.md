## Summary

Gymnopilus liquiritiae (Pers.) P. Karst. 1879 is a mushroom in the family Hymenogastraceae, with occurrence records in many countries. Wikipedia and MushroomExpert.Com describe it as a wood-rotting mushroom with an orange to rusty brown cap, a bitter taste and no partial veil. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic on the rotting wood of fallen hardwoods (especially in the south) and conifers (especially in the north and west), growing alone or gregariously in summer and fall, and apparently widely distributed in North America. Its illustrated collections are from Missouri, Maryland and Québec. Wikipedia describes it as subcaespitose on conifer, and says it seems to prefer dead hardwood in the southern regions. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
