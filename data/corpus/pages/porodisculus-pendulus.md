## Summary

A 1997 Canadian Journal of Botany paper reports the first documented Canadian records, from southern Ontario and southwestern Quebec, and describes its cultural characters for the first time. A 1965 laboratory study found that it produced L-malic acid as a main metabolic product in shake cultures with CaCO3 added. *[lit_1, lit_2]*
