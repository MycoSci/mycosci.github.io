## Discovery and naming

Wikispecies gives the basionym as Agaricus aurantiorugosus Trog, published in Mitt. Naturf. Ges. Bern in 1857. The typification is given as Otth s.n., with the type recorded on Populus in Switzerland. The combination in Pluteus is credited to Saccardo, 1896 (Hedwigia 35, Suppl.), where the epithet is spelled 'aurantio-rugosus'. MushroomExpert.Com likewise says the species was originally described from Switzerland (Trog 1857). ## Habitat and ecology

According to MushroomExpert.Com (a single source), the species is saprobic on decaying hardwood logs and stumps. It grows alone or in small groups, in summer and fall. The source calls it fairly widely distributed in Europe and in North America, where it is more common east of the Rocky Mountains than in western regions. Its illustrated and described collections come from Illinois and Pennsylvania. Wikispecies records the type as found on Populus in Switzerland. GBIF's description record lists distribution only, naming Belgium (including its Brussels-Capital, Flemish and Walloon regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[mushroomexpert, wikispecies, gbif_desc]*
