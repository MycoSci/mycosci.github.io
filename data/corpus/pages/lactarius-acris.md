## Summary

Lactarius acris (Bolton) Gray 1821 is a milkcap in the family Russulaceae, known in English as the Raspberry Milkcap. Wikipedia reports it from Europe and describes a chestnut-brown cap 5–8 cm across that becomes sticky when wet. The same source says the milk turns pink after a few moments of exposure to air. *[wikipedia]*
