## Summary

Suillus ochraceoroseus, known in English as the Rosy Larch Bolete, is a species in the family Suillaceae (order Boletales). It was originally described as Boletinus ochraceoroseus and is now placed in Suillus by (Snell) Singer, 1973. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Wally Snell first described the species as Boletinus ochraceoroseus in 1941, from specimens he collected near Smith Creek in Idaho. René Pomerleau and Alexander H. Smith transferred it to Fuscoboletinus in 1962, and Rolf Singer moved it to Suillus in 1973.
