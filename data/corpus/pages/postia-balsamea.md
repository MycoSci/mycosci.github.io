## Summary

Postia balsamea (Peck) Jülich, 1982, is a polypore in the family Dacryobolaceae (order Polyporales). GBIF's description record lists its distribution as Belgium (Flemish Region), Norway and Sweden. It is reported as a causal agent of root rot and butt rot in balsam fir, and the available literature consists of studies of its chemical constituents. *[gbif_desc, lit_1, lit_2, lit_3]*

## Habitat and ecology

Reported as a causal agent of root rot and butt rot in balsam fir (Abies balsamea); one source states that mechanical or insect-caused wounds to the roots or basal areas of trees provide entry for the fungus. *[lit_1, lit_3]*
