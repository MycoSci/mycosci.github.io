## Summary

Bogbodia uda (Pers.) Redhead (2013) is a species in the family Strophariaceae (order Agaricales), known by the common name Peat Brownie. According to Wikipedia, it is the only species in the genus Bogbodia, a bog-inhabiting group of agarics that colonizes peat and Sphagnum and produces tan-colored fruit bodies. The same source describes the genus as forming chrysocystidia and rather large, finely roughened, violaceous basidiospores, and as phylogenetically distinct from Hypholoma, Pholiota and Leratiomyces. *[wikipedia]*
