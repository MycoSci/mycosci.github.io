## Summary

Parasola plicatilis (Curtis) Redhead, Vilgalys & Hopple, the pleated inkcap, is a small saprotrophic mushroom in the family Psathyrellaceae. MushroomExpert.Com gives the mature cap as 10-35 mm across, ovoid at first, then convex or bell-shaped, then flat, and deeply grooved from the margin nearly to the center. It lacks a universal veil and has no ring. *[mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes it as a decomposer found in grassy areas, alone, scattered or in small groups, with fruiting bodies that grow at night after rain. It describes the species as widely distributed in Europe and North America. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as growing alone or scattered in grassy areas, usually in direct sunlight, in summer and fall (and over winter in warmer climates), and widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
