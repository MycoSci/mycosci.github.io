## Summary

Hygrophoropsis rufa (D.A. Reid) Knudsen, 2008, is a species in the family Hygrophoropsidaceae (order Boletales). Wikipedia describes it as a fungus of Europe that grows on woodchips or near conifer stumps. ## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, Derek Reid originally described it in 1972 as a variety of Hygrophoropsis aurantiaca. It was raised to distinct species status in 2008. The same source states that molecular analysis of DNA sequences confirms its genetic uniqueness and its status as sister species to H. aurantiaca. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
