## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that it fruits in autumn in forests, especially conifer forests. A GBIF description based on Chinese material says it grows sparse to gregarious in Larix gmelinii forests or in mixed forests with Betula platyphylla, and sometimes in broadleaf forests of Betula and Populus. That description reports it from Heilongjiang, Inner Mongolia, Sichuan and Xinjiang, calls it a newly recorded species in China, and says it is widespread in Europe and North America under coniferous forests. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
