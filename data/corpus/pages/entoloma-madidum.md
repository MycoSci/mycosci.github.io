## Summary

Entoloma madidum Gillet (1876) is a species in the family Entolomataceae. It is recorded in Norway, the UK, Denmark, Sweden and the US, among other countries. The only descriptive source available is the English Wikipedia article on the related Entoloma bloxamii, which says E. madidum is a deeper, slightly violet blue than E. bloxamii, keeps some of its colour when old, and has smaller spores (6 to 7.5 μm). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

The English Wikipedia article on Entoloma bloxamii reports that recent DNA-based work found E. bloxamii sensu lato to be a group of distinct blue taxa in Europe. E. madidum, previously considered a synonym, is listed among them. The same article says collections from the west coast of North America, formerly referred to E. bloxamii or E. madidum, are distinct and are now referred to Entoloma medianox. This is a single-source account, and it appears in an article about a different species. *[wikipedia]*
