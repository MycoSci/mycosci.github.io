## Summary

Peniophora albobadia (Schwein.) Boidin is a species of crust fungus in the family Peniophoraceae. It was first described by Lewis David de Schweinitz in 1822 and transferred to Peniophora by Jacques Boidin in 1961. Wikipedia describes the fruiting body as 1–12 cm across, with brown zones in the centre and a fuzzy white margin, and says it is commonly found in the United States. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the species epithet is derived from albo-, meaning white, and badi-, meaning reddish-brown, and that it describes the contrast between the fertile area and the margin. This is a single-source statement. *[wikipedia]*
