## Summary

Sidera vulgaris (Fr.) Miettinen, 2011, is a species in the family Rickenellaceae, order Hymenochaetales. A description hosted by GBIF gives it as a resupinate, annual to biennial fungus that forms a poroid crust, white to cream when fresh, on the under-surface of decaying logs. *[gbif_desc]*

## Habitat and ecology

The GBIF-hosted description reports the species on the under-surface of decaying logs. It lists the following hosts: broadleaved trees (Alnus, Eucalyptus, Fagus, Populus, Quercus, Sorbus and Ulmus), and coniferous trees (Picea, Pinus including P. halepensis, P. nigra ssp. laricio, P. pinaster and P. sylvestris, Juniperus, and Abies cephalonica). It is reported from Mediterranean Europe (Portugal, Spain, France, Italy, Croatia and Greece), Germany, Slovakia, Poland, Estonia, Sweden, Belarus and Russia, and also from Armenia, Georgia, Iran, Kazakhstan, China, the USA and Canada. This is a single-source account. No season is stated. *[gbif_desc]*
