## Summary

Hortiboletus bubalinus (Oolbekk. & Duin) L. Albert & Dima is a bolete in the family Boletaceae, also known by the English name Ascot Hat and formerly placed as Xerocomus bubalinus. A GBIF-hosted description gives the cap as (2.9–)3.8–10.3(–13.5) cm broad, at first dark garnet red or dark reddish-brown and fading with age to ochraceous tones. The same description gives yellow to olive-yellow tubes that blue on cutting, and an olive-brown spore print. *[gbif_desc]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia states that the species was originally described in 1991 as a species of Boletus, was transferred to Xerocomus in 1993, and was transferred to Hortiboletus by Bálint Dima in 2015. This is a single-cluster account; the iNaturalist text is a declared derivative of it. *[wikipedia]*
