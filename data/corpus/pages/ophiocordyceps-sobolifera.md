## Summary

Ophiocordyceps sobolifera (Hill ex Watson) G.H. Sung, J.M. Sung, Hywel-Jones & Spatafora, 2007, is a fungus in the family Ophiocordycipitaceae (order Hypocreales, phylum Ascomycota). It was previously placed in Cordyceps, and Index Fungorum also lists Clavaria sobolifera, Sphaeria sobolifera and Torrubia sobolifera as synonyms.
