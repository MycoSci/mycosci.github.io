## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was originally described in 1801 by Christiaan Hendrik Persoon as a form of the common yellow morel (Morchella esculenta) and was later recombined as a distinct species by Samuel Gray. Because of its high morphological plasticity, its taxonomic status was long in flux. It was sometimes treated as a variety of, or conspecific with, M. esculenta, and several forms and varieties of M. vulgaris itself were described. Wikipedia reports that a phylogenetic and nomenclatural study by Richard and colleagues in 2014 confirmed M. vulgaris as a distinct species and resolved several of its synonymies.
