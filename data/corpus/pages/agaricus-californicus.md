## Summary

Agaricus californicus Peck (1895) is a species in the family Agaricaceae, placed in section Xanthodermati of the genus Agaricus. Wikipedia gives the common names mock meadow mushroom and California agaricus. It describes the species as mildly poisonous, often causing gastrointestinal upset. *[wikipedia, inaturalist]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing gregariously in lawns and other grassy areas, sometimes near oaks or Monterey cypress, in fall and winter. It reports the species as originally described from Pasadena, fairly widely distributed in California, and also found in central Mexico. *[mushroomexpert]*
