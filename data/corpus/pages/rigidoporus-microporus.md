## Summary

Rigidoporus microporus (Sw.) Overeem (1924) is a fungus in the family Meripilaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a plant pathogen that causes white root rot disease on various tropical crops, such as cacao, cassava and tea, and that has economic importance on the para rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis). Most of the supplied descriptive text is written under the synonym Rigidoporus lignosus. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

A single GBIF description record gives the habitat as "Robledal" (Spanish for oak woodland), the substrate as "Sobre madera muerta" (on dead wood), and an altitude of 2600 with no unit stated. Its notes call it the first record of the species for the Department of Tolima. Wikipedia, writing mostly under the name R. lignosus, describes a root pathogen with a wide host range: more than 100 woody species in the Ivory Coast are recorded as susceptible, with the greatest losses in Hevea brasiliensis plantations and lesser losses in teak (Tectona grandis). It states that orange-yellow sporophores form mainly during the rainy season at the base of heavily attacked trees, that the bracket form is most common, and that a resupinate form also exists.
