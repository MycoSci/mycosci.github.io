## Summary

Clitopaxillus alexandri (Gillet) G. Moreno, Vizzini, Consiglio & P. Alvarado is a fungus in the family Pseudoclitocybaceae, with the recommended English name Alexander's funnel. Wikipedia describes its fruit bodies as agaricoid and resembling those of Clitocybe species. It was originally described from France and is mainly known from Europe. Wikipedia reports it as assessed 'critically endangered' on the Red Data List of Threatened British Fungi, 'vulnerable' on the Dutch red list, and 'threatened' or 'near threatened' on some other European red lists. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as saprotrophic and states that it typically occurs in leaf litter with pine, oak, and cedar. It says the species is mostly known from southern, western, and central Europe, extending into North Africa. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
