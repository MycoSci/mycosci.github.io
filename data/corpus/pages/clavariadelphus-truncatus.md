## Summary

Clavariadelphus truncatus Donk (1933) is a club-shaped fungus in the family Clavariadelphaceae, known in English as the Truncated Club. The English Wikipedia article describes a yellow-orange, club-shaped fruiting body with a flat top, white flesh that is thin and hollow at the top, and a pale yellow to ochre spore print. Occurrence records exist from many countries, mainly in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia]*
