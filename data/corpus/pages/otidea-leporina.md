## Summary

Otidea leporina (Batsch) Fuckel, 1870, is a cup fungus in the family Otideaceae, in the order Pezizales. MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a pale brownish to yellowish brown species that often looks like rabbit ears emerging from the ground. A single encyclopedia source states that it contains toxins that may cause serious gastric upset. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic and terrestrial, growing in woods under hardwoods or conifers. It is often clustered but occasionally grows alone or scattered. It fruits in summer and fall (winter and spring in warmer areas) and is described as widely distributed in North America. *[mushroomexpert]*
