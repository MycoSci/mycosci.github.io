## Summary

Parasola kuehneri (Uljé & Bas) Redhead, Vilgalys & Hopple (2001) is a species in the family Psathyrellaceae. The GBIF description gives the cap as 7–9 × 9–14 mm when still closed and up to 19 mm when mature, ochreous to red-brown at the centre with gray shades at the margin, and sulcate-striate. The stipe is 46–105 × 2 mm, cylindrical and hollow. The same record lists its distribution as Belgium (Flemish Region), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. *[gbif_desc]*
