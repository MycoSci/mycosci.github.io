## Summary

Ganoderma lobatum (Cooke) G.F. Atk. (1908) is a polypore in the family Polyporaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a fungal plant pathogen, while MushroomExpert.Com describes it as a stemless bracket that grows a new cap below the previous year's cap, giving a stacked appearance, and whose cap crust is soft enough to be punctured with a thumb. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes G. lobatum as saprobic, growing alone or gregariously on decaying logs and stumps of various hardwoods and causing a white rot. It reports the species as widely distributed in North America east of the Rocky Mountains, also found in Arizona and in Mexico. The described collection is from Michigan. Wikipedia, by contrast, describes it only as a fungal plant pathogen and gives no host or substrate. GBIF's description record lists the distribution as BR and Vermont. The ecology and range details come from a single independent source. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
