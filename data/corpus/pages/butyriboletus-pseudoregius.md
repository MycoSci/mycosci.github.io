## Summary

Butyriboletus pseudoregius (Heinr. Huber) D. Arora & J.L. Frank (2014) is a pored mushroom in the family Boletaceae. Index Fungorum lists its current name as Butyriboletus fuscoroseus, and the English Wikipedia article on that species treats B. pseudoregius as a synonym. Wikipedia also states that B. fuscoroseus is considered critically endangered in the Czech Republic. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the taxon was originally described in 1927 as a subspecies of Boletus appendiculatus, and Boletus pseudoregius is treated there as a European synonym of Butyriboletus fuscoroseus. Wikipedia says the species was formerly placed in Boletus and was transferred in 2014 to the newly created genus Butyriboletus. The supplied source does not say who made the 1927 description, what material it was based on, or why the transfer was made. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, which treats this taxon as a synonym of Butyriboletus fuscoroseus, describes fruiting bodies in warm, dry to moderately moist broadleaf woodlands, most often under oak (Quercus spp.) and frequently on calcareous soils, from near sea level up to about 800 m. For Bulgaria it gives a fruiting period of June through September and lists localities along the Southern Black Sea coast, the Eastern Forebalkan, the western and eastern flanks of the Stara Planina, the Sofia region and the Eastern Rhodope. It also lists occurrence across much of southern and central Europe and into Turkey. This is a single-source account, and the season is stated for Bulgaria only. *[wikipedia]*
