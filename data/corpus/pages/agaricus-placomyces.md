## Summary

Agaricus placomyces Peck (1878) is a member of the Agaricaceae, described from New York. Wikipedia describes it as a toxic species of North America and says Eurasian populations formerly given this name are now called A. moelleri. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

MushroomExpert.Com describes it as saprobic, growing scattered or gregariously under hardwoods or conifers, usually in woods but also under planted trees in lawns and other urban settings, in summer and fall. It is found east of the Rocky Mountains, and the collections MushroomExpert.Com illustrates and describes are from Minnesota and Illinois. Wikipedia likewise describes it as saprobic, growing in groups under hardwoods and in mixed woods in summer and fall, generally east of the Rocky Mountains. *[mushroomexpert, wikipedia]*
