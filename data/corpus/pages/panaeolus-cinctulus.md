## Summary

Panaeolus cinctulus (Bolton) Sacc. (1887) is a species in the family Bolbitiaceae, order Agaricales, with the English vernacular name Banded Mottlegill. Wikipedia describes it as very common and widely distributed, with a hygrophanous cap 1.5–6 cm wide, gills mottled brown to black, and a black spore print. GBIF holds occurrence records from dozens of countries. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only source with habitat text, calls it cosmopolitan. It reports the species growing solitary to gregarious to densely clumped on compost piles and well-fertilized lawns and gardens, and rarely directly on horse dung. It gives the season as spring to fall, with abundant fruiting after rain. The same source lists many countries and regions where it has been recorded, and says it is known from all 50 US states. This is a single-source account. *[wikipedia]*
