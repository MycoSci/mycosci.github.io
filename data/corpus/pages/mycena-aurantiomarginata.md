## Summary

Mycena aurantiomarginata (Fr.) Quél., the golden-edge bonnet, is a species of agaric fungus in the family Mycenaceae. Wikipedia describes it as saprobic, fruiting on the floor of coniferous forests, and widely distributed in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia states that the specific epithet aurantiomarginata is Latin and refers to the orange edges of the gills (aurantius, "orange"; marginata, "bordered"). *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first named Agaricus marginatus by the Danish naturalist Heinrich Christian Friedrich Schumacher in 1803. Elias Magnus Fries renamed it Agaricus aurantio-marginatus in his 1821 Systema Mycologicum, and Christiaan Hendrik Persoon called it Agaricus schumacheri in 1828. Although Schumacher's name has the earliest publication date, Fries's name is sanctioned, so his epithet takes nomenclatural precedence. Lucien Quélet transferred the species to Mycena in 1872. In 1930 Karel Cejp treated it as a variety of Mycena elegans. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes M. aurantiomarginata as a saprobic fungus that draws nutrients from decomposing organic matter on the forest floor, such as needle carpets. Fruit bodies grow scattered, in groups or in tufts under conifers (usually spruce and fir) and are often found on moss. Wikipedia lists records from California, Washington, Oregon and British Columbia, and says the species is widely distributed in western and northern Europe and has also been recorded from North Africa. In Central America it was collected on the summit of Cerro de la Muerte, Costa Rica, on leaf litter of Comarostaphylis arbutoides. A 2010 report from Hokkaido, Japan, found it growing on Picea glehnii forest litter in early winter. A GBIF distribution record lists Belgium (Flemish and Walloon Regions), Denmark, Norway and Sweden. These habitat details come from a single independent source (Wikipedia); the GBIF record gives distribution only. *[wikipedia, gbif_desc]*
