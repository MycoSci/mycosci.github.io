## Summary

A 2010 study in Proceedings of the Royal Society B describes it as a modern saprotrophic fungus and measured carbon isotope variability in specimens from the Washington Cascades, USA, as a modern comparison for the Devonian fossil Prototaxites. *[lit_1, gbif_desc]*
