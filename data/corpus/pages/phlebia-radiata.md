## Summary

Phlebia radiata Fr. (1821), the wrinkled crust, is a crust fungus in the family Meruliaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a wrinkled, orange to pinkish waxy crust on decaying wood that causes a white rot. Its range is given as widespread in the Northern Hemisphere. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes Phlebia radiata as a saprophytic species that causes a white rot in fallen logs and branches of both coniferous and hardwood trees, and calls it widespread in the Northern Hemisphere. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, spreading across logs and stumps of hardwoods or conifers, annual, causing a white rot, fruiting in spring, summer, fall and winter, and widely distributed in North America. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
