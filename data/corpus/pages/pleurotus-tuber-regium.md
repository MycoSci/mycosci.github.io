## Summary

Pleurotus tuber-regium (Fr.) Singer, published in 1951, is a species in the family Pleurotaceae. Wikipedia describes it as a gilled fungus native to the tropics, including Africa, Asia and Australasia, and as a saprotroph that forms a sclerotium (a storage tuber). This page rests on that single encyclopedia source. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia, the only supplied source, describes P. tuber-regium as a saprotroph on dead wood, including Daniellia trees in Africa. It states that as the fungus consumes the wood it produces a sclerotium, either within the decaying wood or in the underlying soil. The sclerotia are described as round, dark brown with white interiors, and up to 30 cm wide, and the fruiting bodies emerge from the sclerotium. The source gives no season. *[wikipedia]*
