## Summary

Clitocybe nebularis (Batsch) P. Kumm. is a gilled fungus in the family Tricholomataceae, commonly called the clouded agaric, clouded funnel or cloudy clitocybe. Wikipedia describes it as abundant in both conifer-dominated and broad-leaved woodland in Europe and North America. *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

According to Wikipedia, the species was first described as Agaricus nebularis by Batsch in 1789 and placed in Clitocybe by Kummer in 1871. It was later placed for periods in Lepista and Gymnopus before being returned to Clitocybe. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia reports it in conifer-dominated forests and broad-leaved woodland. MushroomExpert.Com (Michael Kuo) describes it as saprobic, growing alone, scattered or gregariously under conifers or occasionally hardwoods, in summer and fall, or over winter in warm climates. The same source says it is most common in the Pacific Northwest in North America, and that it is unclear whether North American collections are the same species as the European one. *[wikipedia, mushroomexpert]*
