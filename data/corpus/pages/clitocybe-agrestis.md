## Summary

Clitocybe agrestis Harmaja (1969) is an agaric in the family Tricholomataceae, recorded across much of Europe. Wikipedia states that its fruitbodies are poisonous because they contain the toxin muscarine. *[wikipedia]*

## Etymology

Wikipedia gives the species epithet agrestis as meaning "of fields". *[wikipedia]*

## Discovery and naming

Wikipedia reports that the species was formally described by the Finnish mycologist Harri Harmaja in 1969. The holotype was collected on 11 October 1968 along a roadside near Läby in Uppland, Sweden, and an isotype is preserved at the herbarium of the University of Michigan. *[wikipedia]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia describes the species as saprotrophic, fruiting gregariously or in small tufts in man-made open habitats such as stubble fields (oats, rye), pastures and roadsides, always on bare, nutrient-rich soil. It may occur among grasses, herbs or mosses such as Ceratodon purpureus and Brachythecium albicans. Fruiting bodies appear from mid-August to early November. Wikipedia gives its known range as the hemiboreal to middle boreal zones of southern Sweden and Finland, up to about 250 m elevation, and says it is generally regarded as rare. *[wikipedia]*
