## Summary

Anthracobia melaloma (Alb. & Schwein.) Arnould (1893) is a species of apothecial fungus in the family Pyronemataceae, recorded in the site catalog under the English name Charcoal Eyelash. Wikipedia describes it as producing orangish cup- or disc-like fruit bodies with small brown hairs around the edge. A 1956 Mycologia developmental study calls it a small operculate discomycete. *[wikipedia, lit_1]*

## Habitat and ecology

Wikipedia states that fruit bodies occur in burn sites. A 1956 Mycologia study describes the species as found most frequently on burnt wood. A 1987 Canadian Journal of Botany study of fungal succession at Mount St. Helens reports it as the first fungus observed fruiting on tephra deposits after the May 1980 eruption, where it was common in both anamorph and teleomorph states. *[wikipedia, lit_1, lit_3]*
